## 4. Draft lens charter

*Status: **partial lock** — bullets 1–4, 8–11 **locked** in `SETTING-SPELL-TECH-TREE.md` §1; era/law/openers **draft**.*

1. **Not Prohibition NYC.** Do not use 1931, Volstead, Repeal, Hoover, speakeasy/gin bootleg, or OTR/BCC as default framing unless explicitly reintroduced by a new lock.
2. **Magic baseline = 18th-century D&D.** Spells, gods, guilds, and temples are the **primary** technology tree; iteration is craft, oath, and patronage — not scientific-industrial modernity.
3. **Industrial growth is diegetic.** The city is **moving** from guild/parish magic toward **early industrial / steampunk-esque** brass, clockwork, foundries, and rail — uneven by ward.
4. **No magitech-on-modern-NYC.** Do not solve problems with interwar or contemporary mundane tech (Tommy guns, Packards, radio, rotary phone, subway-as-1930) unless a new lock lists allowed mundane band.
5. **Automata are existential threat.** Construct labor, siege engines, or autonomous guildworks are **dangerous at civilization scale** — not rare garnish (`automaton=rare` is void).
6. **Multicultural humans first.** Real communities remain human; fantasy ancestries are **additional** diasporas — never 1:1 ethnicity allegory (`SETTING-ANCESTRIES-WARDS.md` principle survives).
7. **Ward-tiered visibility stays.** Open wards vs polite-denial Midtown is still valid social grammar; retheme institutions, not the tier logic.
8. **Five Families keep names.** Territory and racket **shapes** persist; blurbs must pass era/lens lint before promote.
9. **Contraband has a new legal metaphor.** Licensed vs bootleg services (healing, luck, links, **construct parts**, etc.) follow GM-chosen law — not Volstead parallel by default.
10. **Invention procedure:** Need → 18th-century magical plausibility → cost class → failure mode + legal status → describe with materials/patron/sensory detail — **no** smartphone/cloud/CCTV vocabulary.
11. **Skyline default:** Low brick, guild spires, factory smoke, clock towers — not glass curtain walls or 1930s noir skyline as identity.
12. **War and trauma** are pre-industrial / early-industrial, not WWI trench garnish unless re-locked.
13. **Press and denial** may still euphemize raids — but headlines must match the **fictional** era, not Tribune/Prohibition clichés.
14. **Phase E/F 1931 artifacts** are **pre-lens** until reskinned or marked `superseded` in stroke frontmatter.
15. **Before new infrastructure:** Log lens seeds in report header (e.g. `lens: industrial=rising, automata=threat, comms=guild-tubes`) and stay consistent within a thread.
