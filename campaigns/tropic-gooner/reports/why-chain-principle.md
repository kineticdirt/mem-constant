# Why-chain principle — every detail earns a cause

**GM law (2026-07-05):** There is **always a why**, no matter how miniscule. A neon sign, a gang tattoo, a hotel policy, a NPC pausing at a door — if it appears in canon, it traces to **belief → pressure → choice** (or **history → incentive → present**).

**Reference:** Cuzzolin et al., *Knowing me, knowing you: theory of mind in AI* ([PMC7253617](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7253617/), *Psychol Med* 2020). Cold pattern-matching fails; **goals and motivations** explain behavior and link appearance to essence.

---

## For agents (hard rule)

Before adding **any** lore atom (org, place, NPC beat, policy):

1. Write one line: **What happened?** (observable)
2. Write one line: **Why did they do that?** (belief/desire/fear/money/shame — can be wrong belief)
3. If no honest why → **do not add** the detail; or post taste question to Inbox

**Forbidden:** flavor without cause ("cool SWAT gear", "mysterious cult" with no incentive).

**Allowed:** irrational actors — but the why is *their* skewed belief (Tversky/Kahneman), not author whim. Armstrong & Mindermann (2018): behavior alone underdetermines preference — so cite **context** (hunger, heat, debt, supernatural hunger).

---

## Why-chain template (copy into org/NPC/scene drafts)

```text
OBSERVE: …
BELIEF: They think … (may be false)
DESIRE: They want …
PRESSURE: Money / heat / blackmail / Creed / hunger / tourism quota
CHOICE: Therefore they …
WHY MINISCULE (optional): Even the small thing — e.g. pink hair — because …
```

BDI (Belief-Desire-Intention) is a **scaffold**, not psychology homework — three lines max.

## Theory of Mind ↔ researcher lane

**Marco Reyes (Ash List)** annotates incidents with **belief/desire fields** — in-world mimic of the paper's call for mental-state labels on behavior data.

Example Ash entry:

```text
ASH-2025-051 | Nelly | BELIEF: "customer wants book" | DESIRE: finish research | CHOICE: door open late | WHY-HAIR: stayed up — nightmare, not fashion
```

Agents append **one** why-chain per tick when not writing full org file.
