## How to read this (SCP-style lens, not SCP IP)

| Concept | On Primavera |
|---------|----------------|
| **Multiple GOIs** | Dozens of groups claim the same vice, cleanup, or "safety" niche — some know about each other, some think they're alone |
| **Classification** | `PRI-####` file numbers = in-world **Paradisio Registry Index** (leaked USD grad-student list, not official) |
| **Containment** | Stevens & Co., CRT raids, casino back rooms — *management* not solution |
| **Mixed truth** | Public face often mundane; inner circle supernatural; street level criminal |

**Secrecy tiers (for agents):**

- **S0** — Tourist knows (Java Jump, CiDance)
- **S1** — Local gossip (gangs, escort licenses)
- **S2** — Insiders (Stevens teams, fixers, Hunter cells)
- **S3** — Monster courts, federal black files
- **S4** — Island-specific "why is the humidity wrong on Tuesdays" — one group knows
