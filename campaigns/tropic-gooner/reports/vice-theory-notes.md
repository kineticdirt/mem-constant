## 003 — Prostitution zoning: why the law moved, not where (adjunct professor)

**Working title:** *Three closures, one pattern — containment fails when the audience changes*

Entry 001 named Storyville as a **boundary**; this slice asks **why legislators chose zoning at all**, and **why each regime ended**.

### 1. New Orleans Storyville (1897 ordinance → 1917 shutdown)

**Why passed (1897):** Not moral victory — **municipal order + public health theater**. Baseline City Ordinance 13032 responded to syphilis panic, mixed-race brothel districts visible to "respectable" commerce, and **lost tax revenue** from unregulated houses. Aldermen **believed** a labeled district would (a) concentrate medical inspection fiction, (b) keep Basin Street hotels clean for Carnival donors. **Pressure:** 1890s Progressive hygiene + port economy. **Choice:** legalize *inside* 38 blocks, criminalize outside — therefore today's Paradisio **companion license** stack rhymes with "registered in-zone" without admitting health outcomes.

**Why repealed (1917):** WWI **Naval Station proximity** — Secretary of War Baker threatened base closure if brothels stayed within marching distance. **Belief shift:** vice wasn't a zoning problem anymore; it was **federal security + racial respectability** for white sailors. **Effect:** vice didn't vanish — it went **indoors, unlicensed, rebranded** (see Renta-Girl alleys post-Jackedsonville). **Island map:** when Sierra Dorado hosts a federal audit or naval cruise week, county **pre-announces sweeps** on Jackedsonville surface — same incentive, different uniform.

### 2. Hamburg St. Pauli / Reeperbahn (post-war regulation cycles)

**Why tolerated (1950s–80s):** West German **local-option** politics — Hamburg's port unions and nightlife tax base **believed** an outright ban would only push trade to uninspectable apartments (St. Pauli had already survived bombing). **Pressure:** reconstruction tourism + dockworker culture vs Christian Democratic federal moral campaigns. **Choice:** **street-visible tolerance** with periodic **Sperrbezirk** (restricted zone) rules — license windows, noise caps, **keep it on Herbertstraße**, not residential Altona.

**Why tightened (2000s–2017 ProstSchG):** Trafficking narratives + EU harmonization. **Belief:** registration protects workers ( contested). **Paradisio rhyme:** CiDance **discretion clause** (2016 ordinance 44-B) is Hamburg logic inverted — **hide the registry** instead of publish it, because donors fear FOIA more than sin.

### 3. Nevada county brothel licensing (1971 onward, Clark County exempt)

**Why county-level:** State delegated to **counties** because Las Vegas casino investors **feared** brothels competing with VIP host culture + Mormon-adjacent federal scrutiny on the Strip. Rural Nye / Lyon counties **desired** tax + employment on I-80 corridors with no other industry. **Belief (rural):** licensed house = controlled STI narrative + sheriff campaign donations. **Belief (Clark):** illegality + **in-room** escort via hotels preserves higher margins and cleaner press.

**Map:** Paradise / Lagoona = Clark County logic (luxury, off-menu); Jackedsonville **Renta-Girl co-op shell** = rural Nevada **licensed front + alley roster** — paperwork for the county, cash for the alley.

### Synthesis hook

Zoning laws pass when **someone needs to see order** (health board, Navy, EU commissioner, county assessor). They fail or mutate when **the viewer changes** (sailors → tourists → Instagram → federal capital). Paradisio's **Visibility Board** (PRI-0701) exists to **manage who the viewer is**, not to reduce vice.

**Counter:** Amsterdam **window** model (1980s–2000s) chose **display** as honesty — Paradisio rejected that as bad for cruise optics; only Porto Lujara Carnaval gets brief "display" before sweep.
