## Sensory

Pulsing EDM, interactive light rigs, heat from bodies before AC catches up; neon spill into the street when doors open between sets. Quay Nights (spring EDM / performance-art festival) makes Neon Flux an **anchor room** — party continues on the sidewalk; CRT Tier-1 corridor watches the crowd, not the VIP booths. Smell: sugared drinks, ozone from fog machines, cigarette heat in the alley behind.

## Who owns / who runs the night

- **Public face:** licensed Jackedsonville nightclub (venue named in region vault — Neon Flux Nightclub).
- **Street layer:** Quay Rojo (PRI-0201) — slot-fraud / protection / unlicensed dealing; **truce with licensed clubs during Quay Nights**.
- **Kindred tax:** Crimson Tithe (PRI-0602) taxes street vice; Quay Nights “casualties” (drunk, alone, apps mis-paired) are Tithe scavenger weather — club is loud mortal cover, not Anchor yacht philanthropy.
- **CRT:** festival standard deployment (PRI-0402 peak years); optics clear the boardwalk for cameras.

Exact deed holder unnamed in corpus — do not invent a corporate owner.

## Scene seed

Opening set of Quay Nights: Neon Flux hits capacity; Quay Rojo collectors pause outside because the licensed-club truce is live; a Tithe-adjacent fixer wants a drunk tourist walked out the **back** alley (not the CRT-facing front).
