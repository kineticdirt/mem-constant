## Design principle: the line is **visibility**

Isla Primavera sells **freedom** to tourists but manages **optics** for federal oversight (Sierra Dorado). Law is written so most vices are **tolerated behind doors**, **criminal in the street**. That gap is where money, blackmail, noncon cover-ups, and SWAT theater live.

| Context | Prostitution / escort | Drugs & intoxicants |
|---------|----------------------|---------------------|
| **Private** (home, licensed suite, club back room, hotel "companion package") | **Semi-legal** — licensed agency or registered independent; consenting adults; taxes optional | **Decriminalized personal use** — possession for self in private; "wellness" framing |
| **Licensed venue** (strip club, casino VIP, dispensary, RG's-style pharmacy) | **Regulated** — house cut, health checks on paper, bouncers log IDs | **Licensed sale** — cannabis, psilocybin-lite, designer party chems; harder stuff "compounded" off-menu |
| **Public / street / unlicensed** | **Illegal** — solicitation, unregistered pimping, trafficking rumors | **Illegal** — street dealing, public intox display, school zones, federal land |
| **Tourist zones** (Paradise boardwalk, cruise pier) | **Heavily policed surface** — performers flirt; explicit trade = arrest optics | **Zero-tolerance display** — open use is bait for riot line |

**Semi-legal** = not quite Amsterdam; more **Nevada county + pharma grift + don't-ask-don't-tell**.

## Prostitution & adult services

### Legal-ish stack

1. **Companion licenses** — escort agencies (Jackedsonville `Renta-Girl`, Paradise yacht comps, Porto Lujara cruise packages). Outcall/in-suite only; no street roster.
2. **Club-attached** — dancers, "private dance" booths, VIP hostessing; house takes 40%; NDAs standard.
3. **Creator economy overlap** — OF/streaming (Ellaine canon); legally "content," practically sex work — county pretends not to notice if taxes flow.
4. **Unlicensed** — street, motels, gang-run — **real crime** when it threatens tourism or skips kickbacks.

### The private/public line (playable)

- **Fine in private:** two+ adults, closed door, no minors, no cameras to press — cops rarely enter without a warrant someone bought.
- **Crosses the line:** sidewalk negotiation, hotel lobby, viral clip, minor-adjacent venue, **non-payment/coercion** (still prosecuted on paper; often buried if donor is rich).
- **WoD layer:** ghouls, succubi clubs, blood-as-fetish — licensed fronts; **real** predation hides as "extreme CNC scene."

| Region | Vice face |

## Drugs & intoxicants

### Tier system (county code, loosely enforced)

| Tier | Examples | Where |
|------|----------|--------|
| **T0 Tourist** | THC gummies, beach cocktails, kratom bars | Boardwalk kiosks, licensed |
| **T1 Party** | Coke-adjacent stims, MDMA, designer psychedelics | Clubs, dispensary "event packs," private |
| **T2 Hard** | Opioids, meth, bespoke synths | Private or gang dens; **RG's won't boof** but knows a guy |
| **T3 Federal felony** | Trafficking qty, military base, school zone | SWAT triggers |

### Private vs public

- **Private use:** decriminalized — OD call is "medical event," not arrest, if no press.
- **Public intox / dealing:** misdemeanor → riot line for crowds; felony → **Paradisio SWAT**.
- **Tourism math:** OD stats reclassified (`Laguna` canon); Stevens & Co. sanitizes scenes.

## Law enforcement: militant surface, selective depth

### Two-tier policing

| Force | Jurisdiction | Vibe |
|-------|--------------|------|
| **Paradisio County Sheriff + Tourism Police** | Bay tri-cities, beaches | Riot gear at festivals; pepper-ball for crowd control; **stage raids** on small dens |
| **Paradisio SWAT / CRT** (*Crisis Response Team*) | County-wide | **Highly militant** — bearcats, flashbangs, no-knock on **wrong-address poor blocks**; kid gloves for casino shareholders |
| **Sierra Dorado Capitol Police** | Capital, federal shores | Cold, procedural; blackmail files not bullets |
| **Base Fuerte Luna MP** | Military buffer | Hard line; drugs = federal |

### "Highly militant riot and SWAT" — how it plays

- **Riot line:** Carnaval, Quay Nights, cruise disembark — shoulder-to-shoulder shields, sonic cannons, **optics first** (clear the boardwalk for cameras, not justice).
- **SWAT:** Called for **street dealing**, alleged trafficking, shooter at casino (rare), or when someone **embarrasses a donor**. Tactics are aggressive; rules of engagement loose in **San Aurelio / Jackedsonville back blocks**.
- **Performative:** Monthly **made-for-TV** bust — unlicensed bordello or migrant stash house; bosses tipped off to move product.
- **Corruption:** Kickbacks from licensed clubs/dispensaries; SWAT commander owns a security firm casinos hire.

### Stevens & Co. Sanitation and Liquidation

Mundane + supernatural cleanup after SWAT/riot: biohazard, NDAs, "liquidation" of evidence. Ties vice enforcement to **cover-up economy**.
