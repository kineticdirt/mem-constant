## Voice (pick one per tick, rotate)

Do **not** write tourist brochure lore. Write like someone **trying to prove a theory** with uneven evidence.

| Persona | Tone |
|---------|------|
| **Grad student** (PIU or USD, public policy / sociology) | Cautious, citation-heavy, "my advisor says I'm reaching but…" |
| **Adjunct professor** | Lecture notes, compares three real cases, names the Paradisio pattern |
| **Small YouTuber** (~8k subs, "Island Studies" or "Moral Panic Watch") | "Okay so here's my *theory*," thumbnails avoided in prose; shows work, admits gaps |

**Bad:** bullet list of cool SWAT facts.  
**Good:** one hypothesis, 2–3 **real-world** precedents, how they'd map to Paradise vs Jackedsonville, one counter-argument, one open question for the GM.
