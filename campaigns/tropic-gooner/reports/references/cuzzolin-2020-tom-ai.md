# Reference extract — Cuzzolin et al. 2020 (ToM in AI)

**Source:** [PMC7253617](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7253617/) · *Psychol Med* 50(7):1057-1061 · CC BY 4.0

## Abstract (use in Marco / theory notes)

AI excels at cold cognition but not **hot cognition** (emotion-influenced thought). **Theory of Mind** — attributing mental states to others — is needed for safe human–machine shared environments. Interdisciplinary work (CS + psychiatry/neuroscience) required.

## Quotes useful for Primavera writing

1. **Ice cream van / child crossing:** Motion patterns deceive; humans change plans from **motivation and context** — not past trajectory alone.
2. **Appearance ↔ essence:** Machine ToM can **explain** behavior, not only predict it.
3. **BDI limits:** Belief-Desire-Intention models are simplistic; mental states shouldn't be **predetermined** — they must update from experience.
4. **Irrationality:** Preferences can't be recovered from behavior alone without assumptions (Armstrong & Mindermann 2018) — write **wrong beliefs** for NPCs.
5. **Simulation:** Humans model others by **internal simulation** (what would I do?) + rough social categories — useful for stereotype **and** subversion in play.
