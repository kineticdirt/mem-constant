## What the island grows and catches

Tourism is the big money. Food still has to move. The south and inland slopes hold cane and smaller farms. Ports move ice, fish, and rum. Villa Miel sells the friendly harvest face and hides a fertility cult in the committee (`REGIONS.md`, `HUNTER.md`).
