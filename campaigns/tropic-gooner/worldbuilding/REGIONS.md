## The three that matter right now

**r01 — Paradise.** North shore of the middle bay, the postcard the island sells: towers, marina, boardwalk, PIU hospitality interns holding the whole machine up. Sasha's home base by locked decision, and the Gilded Anchor's feeding ground by older arrangement. Chapter in `LORE-BIBLE.md`.

**r02 — Porto Lujara (Lujuria region).** South curve of the bay, colonial bones under neon, the port that works so the other two can play. Carnaval de Lujuria is the one week a year the county looks the other way on purpose. Chapter in `LORE-BIBLE.md`.

**r03 — Jackedsonville (Crimson Quay region).** West of the bay, casino money and the least filtered vice on the island. The 2014 rebrand to "Crimson Quay" failed for the city and stuck to the harbor. Quay Rojo runs the margins and pays the Tithe; Rough Ride guards the legitimate money and is not the good guys. Chapter in `LORE-BIBLE.md`.
