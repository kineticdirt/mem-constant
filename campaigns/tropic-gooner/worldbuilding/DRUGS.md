## The rule in one sentence

The county sells freedom by the week and taxes what it can brand. Street display is the crime. A closed door is a product.

That is the **visibility tariff** from `island-vice-and-enforcement.md`. Drugs follow the same line as escorts: licensed "wellness," private use, and tourist-zone zero-tolerance for open dealing.

## History — what influenced what

Write the cause chain, not a brochure timeline.

### Real-world rhymes (why the county wrote the law this way)

1. **Portugal 2001 (personal possession → health board)** — Paradisio copied the *private possession* half and skipped the national clinic half. Tourism prefers a dispensary aisle over a county nurse. [vice-theory 001]
2. **Storyville / Nevada county split** — zone and license the profitable room; criminalize the sidewalk for optics. Companion licenses and hotel suites are the Nevada/Clark pattern. Jackedsonville alleys are the Storyville afterlife. [vice-theory 001, 003]
3. **Post-1992 surplus militarization** — CRT gear and festival riot lines are optics doctrine, not therapy. [vice-theory 001, 002]
4. **Caribbean / Pacific tourism math** — cruise ports worldwide learned that OD stats and viral clips cost berth contracts. Stevens & Co. exists because two norovirus cover-ups failed before the 2016 contract. [canon]

### In-world cause chain (belief → pressure → choice)

| When | Belief | Pressure | Choice | Result |
|------|--------|----------|--------|--------|
| Postwar boom | Ports + GIs = cash | Mainland moral press vs dock unions | Tolerated dens, no written code | Unlicensed T1/T2 under casino and quay |
| 1998–2010s | Tourism can outgrow sugar | Cruise lines threaten pullout after alley clips | Safe Paradise Initiative → Visibility Board | Public/private line becomes policy, not habit [canon] |
| **2009** [proposal] | License the soft stuff or lose the youth weekend | Boardwalk OD cluster + YouTube clip | Draft Paradisio Vice & Wellness Act (soft T0 language) | Kratom bars and THC kiosks legal with tax stamp |
| **2014** | Rebrand Jackedsonville to clean the postcard | Viral alley clip nearly costs a cruise contract [canon] | Failed city rename; Tithe formalizes street tax same year [canon] | Surface cleaner; alley product moves indoors |
| **2016** | Hotel guest lists are FOIA poison | Donor panic after suite raid leak | Ordinance **44-B** shields companion / suite registries [canon] | T1 moves upstairs; sidewalk stays bait |
| **2017** | Visible force = donor confidence | Boardwalk tourist riot [canon] | Create **CRT** | Festival sonic line; wrong-door Ash List entries later |
| **2019 present** | Keep the postcard | Federal Sierra Dorado audits + Base Fuerte Luna hard line | Monthly made-for-TV bust; private use stays quiet | Status quo players inherit |

**Chicken/egg still open [open]:** Did the Act create the 2.8M tourist figure, or did the spike buy the Act? Do not invent the answer.

## Architecture — how buildings hide or enable the trade

### Hotel / tower (Paradise pattern) — [canon 44-B + proposal detail]

- Public floors: buffet, gym, wristbands. Smells like chlorine and fry oil.
- Floors 14+: keycard elevators; guest list shielded. Service elevators open to a linen closet that is also a handoff shelf.
- Suite minibars stock T0 stamped product. T1 arrives in room-service cloches labeled "wellness amenity."
- Balconies face the bay for Instagram. The deal faces the corridor camera that "malfunctions" on paid nights.

### Pharmacy counter (RG's / Casa / La / El) — [canon brands + proposal]

- Front: fluorescent OTC, sunscreen, blood-pressure cuff demos.
- Consultation window with frosted glass — compounding talk, not street talk.
- Back room: humidity cabinet for "compounded" soft chems; cardboard boxes labeled seasonal vitamins.
- Alley door painted the same coral as CRT trucks on purpose. Donors focus-grouped the stripe; runners learned to use it as camouflage. [proposal, builds on vice-theory 002 CRT color]

### Dock cold-chain — [canon IFC + proposal]

- Reefer units set to produce temperatures. Product rides under okra or shrimp.
- False bulkhead behind ice: 18-inch gap, enough for vacuum bricks.
- Foreman's office has two clipboards. The clean one hangs on the wall. The real one lives in the drawer under the tide table.

### Casino / club — [canon]

- VIP booths with curtains that meet fire code and block phone video.
- "Employee only" stairs that open onto the alley roster, not the kitchen.
- Drop ceilings with cable trays wide enough for a backpack. Neon Flux deep-dive already owns the club face; product uses the same bones.

### Float barn (Carnaval) — [proposal]

- Off-season: corrugated steel, bird nests, generator smell.
- Parade week: shrink-wrap shells become temporary vaults. The county looks the other way on purpose for seven days. Then the sweep. Anything still sealed after the sweep is a story seed (see STORIES.md float hook).

### Railhead / San Aurelio — [canon hook + proposal]

- Prefab sheds, chain-link, diesel. Precursors look like industrial cleaner until someone steals the right drum.
- CRT practice range nearby — optics theater next to the chemistry.

## Logistics in practice (one paragraph playbook)

Import prefers **Lujara Docks** cold-chain. Reseal happens in marina or quay annexes. Books and raid forecasts live on **Ledger Row**. Retail T0 sits on the **Boardwalk**. T1 sits in **clubs and CiDance suites**. T2 stays in private dens and alley back rooms in **Jackedsonville** and poor blocks CRT likes for TV. Enforcement is loud on the postcard and quiet in the suite. Stevens arrives if someone dies wrong. Base buffer is a career-ender, not a market.
