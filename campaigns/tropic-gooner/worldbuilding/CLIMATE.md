## Baseline

Isla Primavera sits in warm water. The bay cities (Paradise, Porto Lujara, Jackedsonville) run hot and humid most of the year. Trade winds keep the postcard days dry enough for boardwalks. Afternoon storms are normal in wet months. The highland capital, Sierra Dorado, is cooler and clearer. East-coast humidity can feel wrong on purpose in play — see Dry Contract notes in `HUNTER.md` / `LORE-BIBLE.md`. That is not ordinary weather.
