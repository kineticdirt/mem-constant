# Isla Primavera — groups roster (three cities)

Twelve groups across Paradise, Porto Lujara, and Jackedsonville: criminal outfits, fronts with letterhead, civic institutions, hunter-adjacent cells, and the fringe that eats. This is a roster, not a novel — the deep dives (rise/fall, why-chain, the works) live in `reports/organizations/`, and promotion to the vault `Organizations/` tree happens only with GM sign-off, per that tree's convention.

**Canon status:** entries marked **[canon]** exist in the registry/vault with their faces named. Faces marked **[proposal]** are mine — the org is real, the person is a casting suggestion. PRI numbers are the in-world Paradisio Registry Index, a leaked grad-student list, not an official document.

**The standing lock:** `wb-tg-factions` (closed 2026-08-01). Former PCs are ambient NPCs. No favor-debt anchors, no obligation quests, no "the party owes Quay Rojo one." Factions here have tensions with *each other*.

## Paradise

### CiDance Hotels — PRI-0101 [canon org / proposal face]

- **Turf:** Paradise tower district (flagship); towers in Lagooni Seika, an express going up in Porto Lujara.
- **Day-to-day:** runs the discretion economy. Cheap famous lunch buffet for locals below, companion packages above floor 14, guest lists sealed without a warrant under ordinance 44-B. Security reads incident reports before the police ever see a form. Leadership hunts whistleblowers as policy, per locked GM taste.
- **Face:** **Sol Varela**, regional director of the Paradise flagship — smiles through the buffet line, remembers every regular's omelet, reads every incident report twice.
- **Tension:** the **Lagoona Sirens** poached talent out of a "wellness retreat" package and two Sirens turned up beaten; nobody filed anything, and everybody knows.

### The Lunch Regulars — PRI-0205 [canon org / proposal face]

- **Turf:** table 6, CiDance Paradise buffet, lunch hours.
- **Day-to-day:** retired cops, ex-fixers, one guy nobody will identify. They eat, they trade. Not a gang and not for hire — they sell *who to ask*, which is more valuable and harder to subpoena. Half the county's quiet resolutions start as a joke told at this table.
- **Face:** **Ines "Nes" Bautista**, retired detective — files nothing, forgets nothing, still says "allegedly" out of habit.
- **Tension:** the **Visibility Board** — the Regulars retail exactly the gossip the Board would rather keep wholesale.

### The Gilded Anchor — PRI-0601 [canon]

- **Turf:** the Paradise marina; the yacht club; the gala circuit.
- **Day-to-day:** philanthropy on the surface, a Camarilla-flavored court underneath. Elysium wears a charity auction. Feeding runs off a VIP cruise-disembark roster obtained well in advance, because hunting is what poor predators do.
- **Face:** Elder **Marisol del Castillo** — never seen eating in public, and patient the way old money is patient.
- **Tension:** the **Crimson Tithe** — a Tithe collector once worked marina parking and left with a broken wrist from an Anchor ghoul. Both courts called it nothing. It was not nothing.

### Paradisio CRT — PRI-0402 [canon]

- **Turf:** county-wide; the Paradise substation is the showcase, festival crates pre-staged for the cameras.
- **Day-to-day:** two jobs in one uniform — the riot line at festivals (shields, sonic, optics first) and Tier 3 raids on poor blocks (bearcats, flashbangs, wrong doors). Never hits a CiDance tower. Locked as horror in play: frightening, consequential, no satire filter.
- **Face:** Capt. **Renata Voss**, director — ex-Miami liaison, smiles for cruise press and means none of it. (Sgt. **Dale "Hardstop" Mercado** runs ops and the Carnaval overtime spreadsheet; he also leaks.)
- **Tension:** the **Ash List** — one blurred wrong-door clip away from a subpoena season, and both sides know the clip count.

### Stevens & Co. Sanitation and Liquidation — PRI-0301 [canon]

- **Turf:** mobile, county-wide dispatch; Paradise marina and hotel floors are the priority clients.
- **Day-to-day:** fifty-six staff, sixteen three-person teams — driver, hazmat primary, documentation. Biohazard, evidence handling, and things that shouldn't drain into the bay, all billed as "sanitation." Team 7's wetwork reputation is real, per locked GM taste. Unmarked vans, tiny barcode, because the lawyers decided logos are subpoena bait.
- **Face:** **E. Stevens**, founder — a mortal who wanted a military pension without deployment and built a company out of other people's worst nights.
- **Tension:** the **Ash List** again — the List has a redacted photo of a humidity-proof bag Team 4 carried out of a casino corridor, and Stevens would like the negative.

## Porto Lujara

### Night Ledger — PRI-0304 [canon org / proposal face]

- **Turf:** Ledger Row, Porto Lujara — fluorescent offices between the union halls and the chandlers.
- **Day-to-day:** off-books accounting for clubs, casinos, and festival committees. Knows who pays CRT overtime before Carnaval, which makes its raid forecasts the most reliable weather report on the island. Mercado's spreadsheet and Night Ledger's books agree suspiciously often.
- **Face:** **Vera Lash**, senior ledger clerk — prices a favor in her head, never writes a name twice, tips in exact change.
- **Tension:** the **Visibility Board** — both sell foreknowledge of sweeps, and the Board does not enjoy a competitor with better data and worse letterhead.

### Porto Lujara Development Consortium — PRI-0605 [canon org / proposal face]

- **Turf:** the Porto hills, behind the bay — the "green condos" rising over bought-out blocks.
- **Day-to-day:** buys hillside property from elderly owners, rezones through county friends, publishes renderings, never publishes soil studies. Wyrm-tainted money if you ask the right people; not full Pentex, more like a local subsidiary of the idea of Pentex.
- **Face:** **Hollis Brandt**, project lead, mainland transplant — says "revitalization" with a straight face and means "margin."
- **Tension:** the **Black Sand Pact** — the preserve pack's quiet war with this money is canon; the Pact has started calling the condos "the leak."

## Jackedsonville (Crimson Quay)

### Quay Rojo — PRI-0201 [canon org / proposal face]

- **Turf:** the Row and the alleys behind it.
- **Day-to-day:** slot-fraud crews, protection on the unlicensed margin, dealing where the cameras thin out. Honors the licensed-club truce during Quay Nights because festival weeks are good for business and bad for arrests. Diplomatic by island gang standards — a duchy, not a warband.
- **Face:** **Rudy "Slots" Marron** — shakes your hand while counting your chips, and his count is right.
- **Tension:** the **Crimson Tithe** — tribute went up after the marina wrist incident, and Rudy's ledger and the Tithe's ledger disagree about what "the night owes."

### The Crimson Tithe — PRI-0602 [canon]

- **Turf:** the Quay — casinos, alleys, anywhere the night is loud.
- **Day-to-day:** an Anarch-flavored collective that taxes street vice and scavenges the festival casualties: the drunk, the alone, the app-mis-paired. Wants the night economy loud and stable, which is a harder balance than it sounds. Recognized by three off-island princes, which the Anchor finds gauche.
- **Face:** the envoy called **"Kingside"** — gender unclear, speaks for the collective, and has never once claimed to be a prince.
- **Tension:** the **Gilded Anchor** — shared blood sources, no shared trust; the border is a marina parking lot and everyone knows the space number.

### Renta-Girl Cooperative — PRI-0106 [canon org / proposal face]

- **Turf:** the alley cluster off the Quay, plus outcall anywhere a hotel will look away.
- **Day-to-day:** a licensed outcall shell on paper — "tour guides" — with an unlicensed roster in practice. Half co-op, half racket; the licensed books are clean enough to frame. A permanent Stevens priority client, for the nights that end early.
- **Face:** **Yolanda "Yoyo" Ferra**, license-holder of record — keeps the licensed roster in a drawer and the real one in her head, and mothers both.
- **Tension:** the **Lagoona Sirens** — the talent-poaching war is canon and the alley has long memories about it.

### Rough Ride [canon org, vault gangs file / proposal face]

- **Turf:** Jackedsonville, but the client list is island-wide — the biggest legitimate money on Primavera.
- **Day-to-day:** personal security and investigation: armored cars, full details, wiretapped lines (their own phrase, apparently). The island's gangs grew up diplomatic — sports clubs, scholarships, restaurants — and Rough Ride is what that diplomacy looks like with a payroll. People who move against them disappear for a while, and the rumors after are gruesome and unsourced.
- **Face:** **Wes Kaimi**, founder — ex-port police, unfailingly polite, never raises his voice, tips like a local.
- **Tension:** **Quay Rojo** — Rough Ride sells licensed protection on the same blocks where Rojo sells the other kind, and both claim they don't poach.

## Hunter-adjacent (county-wide, orbiting the three cities)

### The Ash List — PRI-0502 [canon]

- **Turf:** the USD library stacks in Sierra Dorado and an encrypted folder with mirrors; its subjects live in the tri-city.
- **Day-to-day:** a shared bibliography of Paradisio's ugly — redacted CRT overtime, van sightings, sonar PDFs, parking-lot incidents. Entry rule: two independent artifacts, or one body photo plus a Coral Trace denial. Half the moderation queue is meme traffic about "Object 14," and the serious editors burn out on schedule.
- **Face:** **Marco Reyes**, 28, public-policy grad student, this semester's editor — cites his sources once, then expects you to keep up. Recurring NPC, per locked GM taste. Not a Hunter; could become one contact away from it.
- **Tension:** **Coral Trace LLP** — one ignored cease-and-desist so far, and the firm's 48-hour NDA machine is very patient.
