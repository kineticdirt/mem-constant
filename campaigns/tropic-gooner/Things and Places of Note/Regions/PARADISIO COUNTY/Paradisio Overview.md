

#### 1. **[[Tropic Gooner/Things and Places of Note/Regions/PARADISIO COUNTY/Paradise]]** (North Central City)

- **Overview:**
    - A sun-soaked metropolis that fully embodies the island’s “live for the moment” ethos.
    - Glamorous beach resorts, upscale clubs, and neon-lit boardwalks define its nightlife.
- **Key Features:**
    - Luxury hotels, an emerging tech expo center, and yacht marinas.
    - Tourist slogan: “Live the Dream.”

#### 2. **[[Porto Lujara]]** *(alias Porto Lujuria)* (West Central City)

- **Overview:**
    - Located on the eastern arc of the middle bay, blending commercial shipping with pulsating nightlife.
- **Key Features:**
    - Booming club district, waterfront conventions, and a synergy of finance/tech gatherings.
    - Known for the annual “Carnaval de Lujuria,” featuring flamboyant parades and all-night raves.

#### 3. **[[Jackedsonville]]** *(alias Crimson Quay)* (South Central City)

- **Overview:**
    - Western middle-bay city (vibes.png spelling). Lore alias **Crimson Quay** is a later agent rebrand, not the map label.
- **Key Features:**
    - A rowdy casino district, diverse street markets, and risqué adult-themed shows.
    - Hosts “Quay Nights,” an EDM and performance-art festival each spring.

_(Together, Regions 1, 2, and 3 form the bustling **Paradisio bay tri-city** zone.)_

# Lore
The county of Paradisio formed as the three cities served different purposes, each coming into being during different parts of the islands exists. As the older settlements, they have similar levels of development as the relatively new capital region. Most of the economic activity and tourism occurs here, making it the main draw of Paradise! In County Records, it is refered to North Central, West Central, or South central depending on the city.
