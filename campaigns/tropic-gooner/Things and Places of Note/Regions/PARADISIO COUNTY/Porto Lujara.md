**Porto Lujara: South-bay carnival port (R2)**
*(alias **Porto Lujuria** — Misc Notes / older lore; vibes + INDEX spelling = **Porto Lujara**)*

Porto Lujara is the south-bay tri-city sibling of Paradise (R1) and Jackedsonville (R3). Waterfront carnival energy, cruise unload docks, culinary college annex — party town surface with CRT sweeps after the parade and Kindred/club-book shadows under the neon.

Region overview also in `Paradisio Overview.md`. Depth bar for vault pages: use **Jackedsonville.md** (fuller urban profile); this page is the **usable play stub** for beta (venues + turf + seeds), not a novel.

In-game year **2019**. Masquerade: mortal party town — monsters hidden.

---

## Sensory pass

- **Day:** Carnival route prep and vendor stalls; CCC Culinary Annex students hauling catering crates; docks busy with cruise/party-boat unload; Night Ledger clerks in fluorescent offices forecasting overtime.
- **Night:** Parade nights turn the waterfront into a river of floats and CRT sonic drills afterward; clubs borrow Porto crowds when Quay is full; dock shadows after the last ferry.
- **Smell / sound:** Fried dough + diesel + salt; brass from floats; radio chatter on CRT sweep vans.
