## Food Culture & Local Eateries

- **Street Food Markets:**  
    Daytime vendors set up along Main Street selling fusion dishes that mix island flavors with global influences—think tropical tacos with spicy mango salsa, seafood ceviche, and freshly grilled street fare that draws a constant flow of locals and tourists.
    
- **Mar de Sabores:**  
    A well-loved local restaurant that specializes in fresh seafood, serving dishes that highlight the best catch of the day alongside tropical fruits and island spices.
    
- **Bodega Roja:**  
    This urban diner offers a twist on traditional street food, with Caribbean-inspired sandwiches, vibrant sides, and a casual setting where digital billboards advertise daily specials and upcoming events.
    
- **Coffee Shops & Bakeries:**  
    Scattered around busy intersections, these spots serve artisanal coffee, pastries, and light meals—ideal for a quick break during a day of exploring or for early morning business meetings.

## Everyday Observations

- #### **Daytime Activity:**  
    Streets are busy with a mix of office workers heading to modern corporate offices, vendors managing colorful stalls, and tourists snapping photos of the glitzy casino fronts. Smart traffic systems and digital kiosks provide live updates, making navigation seamless.

    At dusk, the city lights up. Neon signs, illuminated storefronts, and vibrant street art create an urban canvas. The energy shifts as nightclubs, bars, and pop-up food stalls take over, while discreet police patrols ensure that even the hidden corners remain under watch.
    
- #### **Transportation & Connectivity:**  
    Modern shuttle services and water taxis run along the waterfront, offering both convenience and a glimpse of the city’s blend of high-tech infrastructure with its timeless urban pulse.
