**Paradise: North-central bay city (R1)**
*(Sasha home base — `wb-tg-sasha-base` → Paradise; in-game year **2019**)*

Paradise is the sun-soaked north-central city of the Paradisio bay tri-city (with Porto Lujara and Jackedsonville). Tourist slogan: **“Live the Dream.”** Glamorous beach resorts, neon boardwalks, yacht marinas, and CiDance towers sell the mortal party town; monsters stay hidden (`wb-tg-masquerade`).

County records call it **North Central**. History and colony/statehood arc live in `Organizations/Paradisio/Paradise.md` — this page is the **play region** note (venues, turf, seeds). Overview bullets also in `Paradisio Overview.md`.

---

## Sensory pass

- **Day:** White-collar mornings at Java Jump; PIU hospitality interns on CiDance floors; marina philanthropy calendars; boardwalk vendors under tourism-police smiles.
- **Night:** Yacht-club galas and discreet hotel floors; CRT festival crates staged near the Paradise substation; boardwalk optics first — street solicitation is the arrest theater, private suites are not.
- **Smell / sound:** Salt + sunscreen + buffet steam; ferry horns; CRT sonic drills on festival weeks (not every night).
