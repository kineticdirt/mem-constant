# Archive template policy (Tableslop / Isla Primavera)

**Locked (dual-app roadmap §2.5 / principle 2):** scenario, region, and sheet **templates** are **archived references**. Live scenario development is **GM-owned**.

- Agents may scaffold empty boards or stub files.
- Do **not** treat archived starters as live canon.
- Do **not** auto-fill story from templates into play SoT.
- Promote template → live only when the GM explicitly owns the content.

Starters / examples stay under archive or clearly marked template paths (e.g. `characters/_pilot-*-example.md`, org `_template.md`). Live region pages, places-directory, and registry rows are GM chronicle — not template output.
