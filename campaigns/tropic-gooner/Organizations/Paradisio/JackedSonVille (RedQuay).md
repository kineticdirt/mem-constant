# Gangs
Gangs are a part of any city, resulting in a mess of loyalties and power blocks within the city. 

Formed in the aftermath of war on drugs in the US, a more formal method for smuggling large amounts of contraband as well as keeping up appearances came hand in hand. Thanks to the booming cash and tourism industry, prefaced with a lax view of sex work, meant that gangs were quickly able to mobilize and stake their claim. Unlike the US gangs, these one were far less violent and more diplomatic. Acting like little duchies or businesses first rather than violent entities. Easier to stay under the radar that way. Sports club, martial arts schools, restaurants and the like all became flourishing hotspots for gangs and their members to grow. Some even sponsored clubs and scholarships, blending in with the various grey businesses. 


**Rough Ride**

Some of the more famous gangs, which has achieved a good amount of success in the personal security and investigation world. Rough Ride is famed for providing security for the largest and best companies on the island. Armored cars, wirtapped phone lines, full details, they do it all. With a penchant for making those who try and go against them...disappear for a while, with only gruesome rumors to follow.
