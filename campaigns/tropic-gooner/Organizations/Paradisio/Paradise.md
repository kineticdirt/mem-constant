# History
The history of Paradise is as a former trading colony of the Pacific Trading company. There enslaved peoples worked on sugar plantations as well as at the port before moving goods to the America's and Spanish colonies through the privateer system. This resulted in a large majority of Asian and British indentured servants which formed the core of the population. Enslaved Africans were also present, but due to the distance it was far easier to import East and South Asians (British East India Company, Hong Kong). 

The island was given independence after the American Independence war, staying that way as a minor trade hub for sailors in the pacific till the. Spanish American war. During that time, the island was given the option of statehood, which it voted overwhelmingly for. However, some Independence figures disagreed with the ruling and sought a radical solution, leading to the brief Statehood War. 

It lasted about a year and a half, with the US army dispatching two divisions to deal with pockets of resistance on the south and northmost parts of the islands. The overwhelming force resulted in heavy casualties for those who disagreed, and the island becoming a state a year later. Paradise, which was originally the main city, was then stripped of this role, and a new administrative capital in the highlands was created. 

## 20th Century to Now
Due to this changeup, the state found that it was mainly used as a trade hub, with most cargo going to the main port cities of JackedSonVille, Porto Lujara, and Paradise, with each developing their own unique culture. After a heavy bombing by the Japanese in WW2, the resulting military buildup further expanded its capabilities as a one stop shop for boats heading to the US, resulting in a booming economy. Mainlanders, former service men, and those seeking an easier life in the tropics flocked to the island, swelling the population, resulting in large cultural changes. 

This did not change, save for a small niche tech center, dwarfed by that in the mainland US, but bolstered by its port capabilities. FAB's for making sensors and chips, were the goal, as well as advancements in robotics and maritime business's. 

Nowadays, the island hasn't changed much. Tourism has become an excised part of the island revenue, causing laws to become more relaxed.
