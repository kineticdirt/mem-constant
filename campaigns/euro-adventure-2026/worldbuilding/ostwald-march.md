## Ethnic mix (how it plays)

Ostwald is not one race in a hat. At the table it should feel like **several peoples under one green law**:

1. **Basin bog-folk** — peat, reed, sinkhole knowledge; speak soft; keep old drown-taboos  
2. **Hill clans** — timber, game, blood-feuds; supply muscle for “hunts”  
3. **Circle-blood / horned lineages** — druid families; tattoos of thorn and antler; intermarry for politics  
4. **River / caravan minorities** — tin, salt, gossip; sometimes double as scouts into kingdom roads  
5. **Gone-native smith-folk** — former kingdom settlers who renounced charter law; prove Ostwald’s “we take the willing” story

Semi-evil lands here: Ostwald will **sacrifice its own fringe clients** if a vanishing teaches SmithsVille fear. Multiethnic ≠ kind.

---

## Why semi-evil (not cartoon demon nation)

Ostwald believes the wet remembers. Settlement without circle leave is **theft**. Their cruelty is **policy with theology**:

- Quiet removals look like “monsters / swamp bad luck” so SmithsVille’s guild cannot call **open war**  
- Public massacre would invite kingdom banners; deniable green war does not  
- They will bargain, host guests, trade reed-mats — then vanish a surveyor who stakes a claim  
- They keep **hostage truths**: some missing people are alive in Ostwald as wards / converts / labor; others are feed for rite or beast

Player-facing rumor should stay muddled (“swamp people,” “green vengeance,” “don’t walk that cut”) until hard proof.

## Druid streak — how it operates

### Circles

| Circle (working) | Job |
|------------------|-----|
| **Ash-Root** | Local politics; deals with fringe settlers who “respect the wet” |
| **Thorn-Hunt** | Enforcement — Wild Hunt proxies, herded beasts, night drives |
| **Cold-Ledger** (optional tool) | Bound-spirit / death-rite specialists — *reuses* prior NEXT-ARC wraith texture as a **weapon**, not the root cause |

Circles argue. Thorn-Hunt wants pressure now. Ash-Root wants Halkin emptied by fear without kingdom notice. Cold-Ledger is the faction that makes frost and neat boots if you keep that tool.

### Toolkit (deniable)

- **Beasts as proxies** — wolves, bog-crawlers, herded “monster” attacks that match the guild brief’s *monsters* line  
- **Wards / fingerpost hexes** — twist signs; dusk cuts that refuse to name themselves; iron-sick travelers  
- **Blight / fever** — standing water that “goes wrong” near new houses  
- **Green vengeance rites** — sacrifice of claim-stakes, survey chains, sometimes a living charter-holder  
- **Wild Hunt proxies** — not always literal riders; often a **driven pack** + one circle agent with a horn token  
- **Bound spirit (optional)** — prior “Cold Ledger” wraith = deniable assassin if you promote that sub-tool

**Rule:** if a monster appears, ask *who pointed it*. Random MotW is off-spine.

## Why SmithsVille / Smithville = encroachment

**[canon texture]** SmithsVille = hot industrial city (metals, saddles, carriages); guild HQ hub. Swamps are being **settled**. Halkin-Gaul is a named frontier contract town on a rise past the mountains.

**[proposal map]**

1. **Guild road** — stone-paved spine through empty marches = kingdom logistics toward the basin  
2. **Frontier charters** — settler companies claim peat, timber, and hard rises; Halkin is one such charter toehold  
3. **Logging / peat cuts** — commercial wet-harvest that Ostwald reads as desecration  
4. **Halkin-Gaul as buffer** — outcrop town between kingdom road-spine and Ostwald rim; if Halkin holds, more villages follow (“dozen villages” already flavor on the Blackdoor road)  
5. **Guard stone money** — artisanal cut stone on the shack = settler-company / SmithsVille capital priority, not local craft alone

Ostwald does not need to hate every smith. They hate **permanence** — plaster walls on a rise that used to flood clean.

## Clues PCs can find (hall steps onward)

Soft → hard. Do not open with the nation name.

1. Incomplete missing list vs family chin-points (hall / guard)  
2. Fancy guard stone → follow the money to a **settler charter seal**  
3. Locals flinch at talk of **green law / horns / far-rim folk**  
4. Fingerpost with **thorn-scored ward** under mildew  
5. Beast kill that looks **herded** (no scramble pattern; victims driven to soft ground)  
6. Belongings neat / quiet absence — optional frost if Cold-Ledger tool is on  
7. A peat cutter with **wrong accent** + horn-token charm (agent or convert)  
8. Hall tax book names that match a **crossed list** on a scrap in Ostwald script  
9. Hard proof: intercepted Thorn-Hunt order, captive Ash-Root agent, or charter map marked “remove”
