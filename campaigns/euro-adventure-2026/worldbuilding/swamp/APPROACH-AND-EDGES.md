# Approach and edges — SmithsVille to Halkin-Gaul

How the party got here, and what borders the swamp basin.  
Tags: **[canon]** / **[proposal]**. Citations: `../analysis/discord-swamp-extract.md`.

---

## Spine of the journey [canon]

```text
SmithsVille (guild city)
    │  ~10 days horse cart (guildmaster)
    │  traffic thins: carts of people/supplies → almost none
    │  weather worsens: cold, dark, overcast, rain
    │  stone-paved road through barren swamp/moor views
    ▼
Blackdoor Inn (multi-story road rest stop)
    │  "dozen villages … to the frontier"
    │  (party detained after Vesper's murder; guards by pigeon)
    ▼
Hills → snowy mountain peaks → valley with stream
    ▼
Mountain crossing
    ▼
Swamp basin: bogs, marshes, tree cover to horizon
    │  wooden road signs through bog
    │  mildew; donkey cart; squelch
    ▼
Halkin-Gaul on a slight raised outcropping
```

**SmithsVille** [canon]: hot industrial city — metals, saddles, carriages; breadbasket of explorer gear; many guild HQs. SorePunks is a small new shop there.

**Fenrir Hills** [canon, side geography]: northeast of the city; early goblin job country. Not the Halkin swamp. Do not collapse them.

---

## Blackdoor Inn — road node [canon]

Large multi-story inn on the thinning road. Name from GM line “The blackdoor in.” — treat as **Blackdoor Inn** until corrected.

Texture already played:

- Gnomish kitchen crew (incl. card-playing Biffens)
- Stables / hay / atrium / maids
- Room tiers (silver prices; party took a ~2 gp package with meals/hay)
- Three outhouses; many footprints the night of the murder
- Innkeeper can pigeon for guards; morning response expected
- Settling frontier: hard to get necessities without traders; inn “dirt broke”

**Dramatic load:** Vesper (Chelcins) assassinated — stab, sulfur scent, little struggle. Party left afterward toward the mountains. This place is **behind** them on the map, but not emotionally finished.

**[proposal]** Blackdoor sits on the last reliable stone road before the true wet. Beyond it, “villages” are smaller, wetter, and worse-signposted. A dozen named hamlets can exist as rumor without statting them.

## Mountain wall [canon]

After Blackdoor: hills swell; **snowy peaks** come into view; a **valley stream**; then the crossing. On the far side the world opens into swamp.

**[proposal]** Call the pass **[unnamed]** until a local or map labels it. North face holds snow longer; south face dumps melt into the basin and keeps the marshes fed. Travelers who die on the pass are not the same phenomenon as Halkin disappearances — unless you decide weather-wights later.

---

## Basin edges [proposal on canon basin]

Once over the mountains, the eye gets **bogs, marshes, tree cover**. Halkin-Gaul is one rise in that flat wet.

| Edge | Idea | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| **Mountain foot** | Drier shelves, runoff streams, last stone scrap | Matches descent narration |
| **Open marsh** | Reeds, standing water, bad cart | Matches “sea of mud” table fear |
| **Wooded bog** | Canopy, mildew, muffled sound | Matches tree-cover horizon |
| **Settler fringe** | Half-finished plaster, timber piles, failed dikes | Matches “settling the swamps” |
| **Older wet** | Peat cuts, unmarked posts, people who never go to hall | For epistemic fog |

Do not invent a second named swamp town as equal to Halkin-Gaul without GM say-so. Satellite hamlets can be **[proposal]** stubs: a landing, a charcoal camp, a drowned chapel foundation.

## Travel times (diegetic, soft) [proposal]

| Leg | Fair weather | Bad weather |
|-----|--------------|-------------|
| SmithsVille → Blackdoor | most of the “10 days” | add days; washouts |
| Blackdoor → mountain saddle | 1–2 days | stuck waiting freeze/thaw |
| Saddle → Halkin-Gaul signs | half day–1 day | donkey drama |
| Hall → deep reeds | walk hours | overnight or turn back |

These are pacing tools, not spreadsheet canon. Adjust to session length.

## What touches what

- **Kingdom periphery** [canon phrasing]: carts haul to/from “periphery regions” before the road empties.
- **Frontier** [canon]: innkeeper’s word — Halkin-Gaul is past the comfortable map.
- **Guild politics** [canon]: Chelcins want a branch out here; SorePunks are on a discretion contract from a small hall back in SmithsVille.
- **Not Tropic / NYC** — separate campaign tree; no Isla Primavera bleed.
