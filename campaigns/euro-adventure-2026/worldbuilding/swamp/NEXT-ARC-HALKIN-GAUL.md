## Foil conditions

### Full win

- Destroy or consecrate the **focus**
- Kill / capture / break Binder’s casting
- Turn or destroy Cold Ledger (or free it if you want a mercy beat)
- Hard clue trail documented for guild (discretion still possible)

### Partial wins (still pay / still messy)

| Outcome | Result |
|---------|--------|
| Kill Binder, wraith free | Murders continue until spirit dealt with |
| Destroy focus, Binder flees | Summons stop; wraith weakens; manhunt later |
| Drive Binder off without proof | Guild pay half; town still hostile; Chelcins may claim credit |
| Burn peat cut only | Temporary quiet; Binder relocates |

### Fail states

- Party leaves early → town empties over weeks; Binder owns the basin
- Party announces “necromancer!” with no proof → locals stonewall; Binder accelerates
