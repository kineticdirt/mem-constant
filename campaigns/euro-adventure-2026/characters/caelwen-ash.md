### Portrait / gallery

## Speech

- **Register:** warm, slow, priestly; never raises voice first
- **Lexicon / tells:** “leave,” “remember the wet,” “permanence,” “child” (for anyone under fifty)
- **Emotion → voice:** fury → stillness and shorter words
- **Signature lines:** “We do not hate your people. We hate your plaster.”
- **Terms of address:** Branek = “Captain”; Aldric = “Master Fenn”; strangers = “guest” until they stake a claim
- **Avoid:** comic villain monologues; she sells reason

## Act

- **Temperament:** patient strategist; will sacrifice fringe clients for the green law
- **Goals:** push settlers off the rise without kingdom war; grow converts; keep Thorn-Hunt on a leash
- **Conflict style:** envoys, blight, bargains, then Branek; personal violence rare
- **Hard limits:** open declaration of war; harming children for spectacle (quiet takes of adult charter-holders are another matter)
- **Agency:** decides and dispatches; does not ask PCs what she should do
- **Flaws:** overconfident that “monster” cover will hold forever

## Think

- **Worldview:** land-memory theology; multiethnic Ostwald proves green law can hold many peoples — smith-charters prove the opposite poison
- **Core drives:** preserve the wet; win without banners
- **Blind spots:** underestimates how hard guild investigators dig when paid
- **Secrets:** how many Halkin missing are alive as wards; whether Cold Ledger is hers to spend
