# Branek Holt

**[proposal]** Ostwald March — Thorn-Hunt war-leader / field captain. Not a Discord lock.

- **Full name:** Branek Holt
- **Also known as:** Hunt-Captain; “the Horn” (among peat folk who will not say his name loud)
- **Age / life stage:** 41 — veteran hill fighter, not a green boy
- **Pronouns / gender presentation:** he/him; broad masculine hill-clan presentation
- **Ethnicity / origin:** Ostwald **hill clan** (timber/game stock); mixed with circle-blood through a grandmother — enough for Thorn-Hunt rank, not Ash-Root soft politics
- **Languages:** Common with a gravel hill accent; Ostwald green-cant under stress (short bark-commands)
- **Role / occupation:** Thorn-Hunt captain — drives beasts, night hunts, deniable pressure on settler rises
- **Campaign presence:** unmet (off the Halkin rise; may be heard as distant driven howls before seen)
- **Registry / Pixi id:** branek-holt

- **Skin tone / texture:** wind-roughened, mid-tan; peat-stain under nails that never fully leaves
- **Overall silhouette / impression:** a man built to shove things into soft ground

- Square jaw; broken-and-set nose; deep-set hazel eyes; short-cropped iron-brown hair going grey at the temples; beard kept short enough not to catch in brush

## Speech

- **Register:** clipped orders; rare full sentences; contempt for “plaster people”
- **Lexicon / tells:** “drive,” “cut,” “leave the boots,” “green law,” “charter filth”
- **Emotion → voice:** anger → quieter, not louder; amusement → short bark
- **Signature lines:** “If they wanted to live, they would not build on a rise that remembers water.”
- **Terms of address:** strangers = “road meat” or by trade; circle equals by role; never “sir”
- **English-focused:** yes; green-cant only as short command words

## Act

- **Temperament:** decisive, cruel when useful, patient when hunting
- **Goals:** empty Halkin’s permanence without kingdom banners; prove Thorn-Hunt still bites
- **Social circle:** answers to circle politics grudgingly; respects Caelwen’s restraint until it costs hunts; uses Sile as a knife; distrusts Aldric’s smiles
- **Conflict style:** herded beasts first; personal steel last; never a fair duel in town square
- **Hard limits:** will not openly plant Ostwald banners on Halkin hall (deniability)
- **Soft limits:** ransom / live take if Ash-Root orders a convert
- **Agency:** acts; does not explain. If probed, he moves the pack or leaves a body-shaped warning
- **Flaws:** underestimates guild “shop” adventurers; pride in clean drives

## Think

- **Worldview:** settlement without leave is theft; fear is cheaper than war
- **Core drives:** land-memory, hunt prestige, proving hill blood still matters
- **Beliefs:** monsters are tools; confessing politics is weakness
- **What they notice first:** footing, wind, whether a target can run
- **Blind spots:** thinks SmithsVille only understands force or silence — not clever investigation
- **Knowledge bounds:** does not know SorePunks’ names yet; knows the guard stone was company-paid
- **Decision style:** tactical, short horizon (tonight’s drive)
- **Secrets / hooks:** which Halkin nights he ordered; whether he okayed any “alive takes”
