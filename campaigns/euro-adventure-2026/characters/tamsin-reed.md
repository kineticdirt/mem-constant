# Tamsin Reed

**[proposal]** Ostwald March — sympathetic defector / mixed-blood local contact. Former Ash-Root student. Will trade true names for a way out — or fold back if scared. Grey, not saint.

## Speech

- **Emotion → voice:** fear → whispers; anger (rare) → bog curses
- **Signature:** “I can show you a post. I will not say the name in the square.”
- **Terms of address:** PCs by trade (“smith-girl,” “big one”); Aldric = “salt man”; Caelwen = “Mother” only when terrified

## Act

- **Temperament:** survivor; helpful in fragments; will vanish if loud guild glory starts
- **Goals:** stay alive; maybe hurt Thorn-Hunt; maybe just sell a path and run
- **Conflict style:** information for coin/protection; not a fighter
- **Hard limits:** will not walk into Branek’s camp; will not publicly accuse Ostwald without an exit
- **Agency:** offers a chin-point or a night meet — then decides whether to show
- **Flaws:** half-lies to stay useful to both sides; may have fed Sile a name once

## Think

- **Worldview:** green law can be true and still be evil in the hands that hold it
- **Drives:** guilt, fear, thin hope of a life that is neither charter nor circle
- **Blind spots:** romanticizes “adventurers” as exits
- **Secrets:** saw Veyra score a post; knows Aldric’s smile; may know Sile’s fringe room
