## 1. Executive summary

Cherish is written as a **deliberate hybrid**: multiple ancestries implied (Dru-Nkar features, Tyreen eyes/psionics, Vocar/Cree/Preeva “reads,” “old ones corruption” tone). She is **socially radioactive**—xeno-haters and xenos both may reject her. In play she leans into **performance and appetite**: strip-club energy, pole work, **indifference to operational secrecy**. She is the party’s **id**—what the mission says not to do, she may do loudly.
