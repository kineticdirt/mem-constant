**Role:** Tiefling **Artificer**; **Steel Defender** “Endo”; ex-wife arc **Lady in Purple** / **Francessca’s Pole Party** brand. [A]

---

## 1. Executive summary

Red is **industry + intimacy** welded together: she built **sex androids**, married into **money**, suffered **cult-adjacent scandal** (children murdered; ex exposed). She **pivoted publicity** toward **“sex horror”** entertainment that doesn’t harm—**Francessca’s Pole Party** as **brand rehabilitation**. In play she is **hands-forward**, comic, **Endo-mediated** (measurements, shopping, salute to Smolder). She **anchors** on Aurora as **playmate / project**—erotic comedy with **real boundary conflict**.
