# Aurora — dossier

**Role:** Human Rogue (**Assassin**); **guild artisan** background; **short** stature; heavy **tech** id. [A]

---

## 1. Executive summary

Aurora is **competence-first** in a horny room: **holographic keyboard**, **glasses-as-display**, **gaming** during the meet. She **weaponizes sarcasm** and **personal space**—especially vs Red. She frames the mission as **“free money”** but cares about **gear quality** (roasts Smolder’s ship, Endo’s parts mismatch). **Corpo station:** **hacker/thief** path—keypad, **staff-only** doors, **“master”** at restricted loot. She is the party’s **cynic-engineer**—trust is **earned through proof**, not touch.
