## 1. Executive summary

Smolder is the **moral and logistical spine** of the crew: **Lawful Good**, **fame-averse**, **blunt**, **protective of ship and procedure**. She endured **docking queues**, **grimy stations**, and **Lucky Gal** debauchery with **grumpy professionalism**. She **paid** for competence—**million-credit** job, **brief introductions only** for security. She **knows** the mission is dirty but frames **crew safety** and **house rules** (medbay, nursery, hot water tub). **Relationship** with authority: she **is** authority but **dislikes** merchants and **crowds**—corpo: **shopping lists**, **tools for ruins**, **chem protection**, negotiates with **Geli** on social weakness.
