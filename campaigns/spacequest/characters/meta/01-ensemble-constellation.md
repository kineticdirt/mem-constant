# Ensemble constellation (Space Base party)

**Metaphor:** Treat the party as a **small social graph** (Constella-style): nodes = PCs, edges = recurring interaction patterns. The campaign’s “texture” comes from **who pulls focus** and **who stabilizes** when the mission tries to stay secret.

**Sources:** Exported `#rp`, `#corpo-station`, `#characters`; `#loredoc` for Concordium frame.

## Edges (relationship dynamics)

### Smolder ↔ Aurora

- **Pattern:** Authority vs competence challenge. Aurora questions Smolder’s “captain” look; Smolder answers with **mission relevance** and **controlled access** (cockpit: allowed, but **tailed**).
- **Pressure:** If Aurora’s hacks escalate, Smolder’s LG spine may **clash** with illegal gains unless framed as mission-critical.
- **Evidence grade:** A (`#rp`, `#corpo-station`).

### Red ↔ Aurora

- **Pattern:** Pursuit / boundary comedy with **real stakes** (clothes, Endo as hostage, latex outfit arc). Red uses **touch** as language; Aurora uses **threats to Endo** as leverage.
- **Thematic:** Control vs being “productized” (mirrors Red’s **android industry** past).
- **Evidence grade:** A.

### Smolder ↔ Geli

- **Pattern:** Practical partnership — naming (“Geli”), **merchant anxiety** (Smolder admits weakness), **sleep/crowding** concerns (Geli hates crowds; Smolder suggests sleep aids).
- **Pressure:** Geli’s telepathy + reserve vs Smolder’s blunt honesty — room for **trust episodes**.
- **Evidence grade:** A.

### Cherish ↔ group

- **Pattern:** **Anti-secrecy** energy — treats the meet as a show. Others must **react** (Smolder shrugs off “not my fault if secrecy fails”).
- **Pressure:** In a black op, Cherish is a **living breach** of operational security — great for scenes, dangerous for mission tone.
- **Evidence grade:** A.

### Daji ↔ group

- **Pattern:** **Low spotlight** early (bunny suit cover, background listening). Feb 22+ **guilt/repair** beat toward Smolder (“make up for her fuck up”) — suggests **emerging** edge with captain.
- **Evidence grade:** B (motivation clear in text, full arc in threads may add).

### Geli ↔ Red (corpo)

- **Pattern:** Geli **doesn’t know** Red’s full deal; reserved vs Red’s spectacle — contrast in **how desire is performed**.
- **Evidence grade:** A (brief corpo).

## Faction pull (external nodes)

- **Corporate principal (recording):** Sets **deadline + payday**; anonymized — good for paranoia and double-cross rumors.
- **Concordium decay:** Explains **why** gigs like this exist (see `#loredoc`); use as **ambient** class politics, not necessarily immediate combat.
- **Corpo station culture:** Uniforms, live commodity boards, **guards** — pressure toward **performance** (everyone “on display”).

---

## Balance notes (for GM / writer)

- **Spotlight risk:** Red–Aurora dyad can **eat** scenes; Smolder’s **LG** and Geli’s **slow burn** need **timed** beats so Daji/Cherish don’t flatten into gag roles.
- **Pacing:** Shopping / infiltration scenes (`#corpo-station`) already split **goal lines** (Smolder shops tools; Aurora steals; Geli screens boards). Good **parallel** structure — document in timeline when expanding.
