# Analysis framework (Space Base)

These notes apply a **hybrid** methodology so notes stay **human-readable** and **machine-friendly** (inspired by CHARML-style structure, Obsidian RPG Manager metadata ideas, and “constellation” ensemble thinking from Constella-style research).

---

## 1. Lajos Egri (adapted): spine, need, contradiction

For each PC we track:

- **Spine** — One sentence: what they *do* under pressure (habit of action).
- **Psychological need** — What would make them whole (often unspoken).
- **Contradiction** — The gap between mask and truth; where drama lives.

Egri’s “bone structure” idea: scenes test the contradiction until the character changes or breaks.

---

## 2. John Truby / ensemble “orchestration”

- **Weakness / fear** — What they avoid.
- **Desire line** — Concrete want in the campaign (money, anonymity, control, sensation).
- **Opposition web** — Who or what forces choices (party, corps, past scandal, biology).

Constella’s “interconnected web”: no PC is an island; edges are **obligations, lust, rivalry, shame**.

---

## 3. Dan Harmon’s Story Circle (eight beats) — *where they are now*

Rough mapping for **this arc** (black op → station → ship → corpo crawl):

1. **You** — Comfort / identity
2. **Need** — Money, escape, thrill, mission
3. **Go** — Enter bar, board ship, enter market
4. **Search** — Negotiate, hack, shop, perform
5. **Find** — Crew dynamics, corp excess, each other’s limits
6. **Take** — Pay, data, intimacy, dominance
7. **Return** — Bring loot / trauma back to the ship
8. **Change** — New alliances or scars (open — campaign ongoing)

Per-character dossiers note **which beat they keep circling** (e.g. “Search” for Aurora’s heists, “Take” for Cherish’s appetite).

---

## 4. Kishōtenketsu (light touch)

For sandbox-friendly scenes: **ki** (introduction), **shō** (development), **ten** (twist), **ketsu** (conclusion). Used in dossiers as **optional** scene recipe when a PC’s beat feels episodic (shopping, infiltration).

---

## 5. CHARML-inspired machine block

Each dossier ends with a `**<character>`** block: compact tags for tools, parsers, or future Hivemind-style “truth anchor” workflows—not a full CHARML spec, but **compatible in spirit** with tag-based character sheets.

---

## 6. Evidence grading

- **A** — Direct from `#characters` or clear play post.  
- **B** — Strong inference from play.  
- **C** — Thematic extrapolation; may need player confirmation.
