## 1. One-paragraph state of the story

Space Base V2 is a six-PC, adult **erotic-horror salvage** game set in the
**Concordium** (a decaying interstellar humanoid civilization; "feigned anarchy,"
petty fiefdoms, a bioengineered plague that made fertile men/futas rare). A
**remote corporation** has hired the party — deniable outside "cunt-sultants" — to
salvage dataterms from a **decades-abandoned blacksite research station** for **1M
gold credits each**, on a **~one-month** window, with the jump point held by the
captain. **Played so far** (`#rp` export): the meet at **Station Theta-H22** /
Lucky Gal bar, the corporate briefing recording, boarding and touring the ship,
and a **corpo mall** shopping/provisioning act. **Not yet reached in play:** the
blacksite itself, the **Amalgam** (a Halo-Flood-style biological mass that ate the
station), and the four-level asteroid crawl — all of which exist as **design docs
ahead of the table**.
