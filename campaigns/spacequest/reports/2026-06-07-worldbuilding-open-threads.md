## 4. Dispel-field vs PC magic (draft rules seed)

`story/station-systems.md` §8 frames dispel zones as **engineering, not hand-wave**
(emitters, maintenance hatches; destroy emitter → zone falls). Draft table rule:

- **Inside an active dispel zone:** spell attacks/saves still work but
  **concentration** and **ritual/circle** effects are suppressed; warlock/cleric
  "always-on" features flicker (narrative tension for Smolder/Geli).
- **Counterplay:** Athletics/Tech check (DC 14–16, as Session-1 already uses) to
  pull an **emitter hatch**; destroying it drops the zone *and* may unleash a
  hostile **circle** the field was containing — risk/reward.
- **Why it matters:** the Amalgam's hostile **magical circles** and the corporate
  **dispel fields** are opposing systems; the party can weaponize one against the
  other. This is the crawl's core puzzle layer.
