# Concordium — setting glossary (light)

*Distilled from `#loredoc` and timeline anchors. Expand with factions as needed.*

## Big picture

**Concordium** — loose umbrella for known interstellar civilization among humanoids. **Past prosperity, present decay.** Outwardly unified, inwardly **fractured**: petty fiefdoms, **feigned anarchy**, corps and warlords doing “diplomacy” with money and guns.

## Party’s place

Shipboard **explorers for hire** — “handymen of the wastes and outer planets.” The campaign’s gig is **deniable**: outside contractors for a **corporate blacksite** (salvage / retrieval fiction).

## Pressures (background texture)

- **Robots** common in workforce; **anti-machine slurs** and class tension.  
- **Bio-plague** — fertile men and futas **rare**; drives scarcity politics and body commodification (informs themes, not deterministic for any PC).  
- **Space cops** — tone note (strip searches, etc.); *subject to change at table.*

## Mission frame (employer)

Remote **research station** objective; **high pay**, **tight window**, **jump point** held by mission captain — see [[timeline]] and [[../story/station-and-antagonist]].
