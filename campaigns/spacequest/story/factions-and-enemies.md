# Factions & enemy roster — Flood-amalgam station (draft)

*Multiple **types** and **factions**; some **fight** each other. Tone: **serious** stakes with **over-the-top** sexual pressure from the hive — pleasure as **lubricant**, not the only genre.*

**Shell:** the place was a **corpo mining + R&D** installation before **one experiment** — the **biological Flood** — **ate** it and remade the rock into a **monster factory** and playground. See [[station-and-antagonist]] **Canon premise**.

---

## Primary threat — **the Amalgam** (working name)

Halo **Flood**-style **mass of biological material**: not a clean species, a **propagating corruption** that defines “corruption” on its own terms — **the R&D program that cannibalized its own station**.

| Trait | Story function |
|-------|------------------|
| **Hive broadcast** | Suggestions, urges, **wet** intrusive intimacy — esp. sexualized; degrades judgment under stress |
| **Absorption + release** | Takes a person, **spits out** someone *different* (identity horror; cult recruitment / sleeper agents) |
| **Spawn** | “**Monsters of yore**” and other entities — mythic shapes as **templates** (dragons, tentacle-beasts, etc.) — not ecology, **performance** of fear |
| **Replication** | Not standard biology; **spread** via biomass, fluids, compromised systems, **agreement** |
| **Traps** | Tentacle breeding snares, lure chambers — **perpetuate** the network, farm panic and desire |

**Magical circles** — The thing (or its priests) **casts** persistent circles; **objectives** include **power restoration** + **breaking anchors** + **dispel fields** where corporate tech still works.

## Human / former-human layers

| Faction | Motivation | Versus others |
|---------|------------|----------------|
| **Changed** | Partly assimilated crew; may believe they’re “helping” | Often vs pure survivors |
| **Cult (discovered mid-delve)** | Worship / negotiate with the Amalgam; **ritual sex** as sacrament (tone: serious, not slapstick) | May collaborate with hive until sacrificed |
| **Holdouts** | Barricaded labs, dock unions, security remnants | Uneasy alliances with party; may demand escort |
| **Rival spawn factions** | Different **templates** of monsters territorial in the maze | Environmental hazard; boss bait |

## “Not the first team” (employer conspiracy)

- **Salvage contractors** (party) sent by **company** that owned the station — public story: dataterms + asset recovery.
- **Evidence** of prior teams: stripped gear, **partial logs**, “success” markers that are **worse** than failure.
- **Payload ideas:** prior team **merged** into broadcast; employer wants **live samples** or **broadcast carriers**.

---

## Design rules (keep play manageable)

1. **One** primary supernatural logic (Amalgam); cult and factions are **expressions** of it.  
2. **Two** ongoing human tensions (e.g. cult vs holdouts + corporate secret).  
3. **Three** encounter buckets: **traps**, **changed**, **spawn** — rotate so scenes don’t blur.
