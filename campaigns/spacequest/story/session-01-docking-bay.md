## 1. Situation (what the table knows)

- The party **docks** on a **corporate research installation** carved into rock: **Level 1 — Skin** (dock, reception, outer hab).  
- **Expectation:** dead station, salvage run, weird silence.  
- **Immediate punch:** automated **defense / lockdown** still **tries** to kill them — but the station is **too broken** to finish the job. That wrongness is the hook: *something wanted visitors dead, and something else ate the machine before it could spend its last bullet.*

#### Dock (Z0) — atmosphere

Mag field **hum** at the south edge; **distant** pump cycling in the rock; turret servos **whine** then **catch** (dry fire). **Smell:** cold ozone at the barrier; past the airlock, **recycled** air with a **sweet** scrubber note (hooks deeper levels). **Light:** harsh white on the open deck; **turret alcoves** in contrast — redundant targeting lasers **paint** the berth, then **die** mid-sweep when the feed cuts.

#### Dock — plot threads (pins **#1–#7**)

1. **Who fed the guns?** Crane crates (**#4 / #5**) don’t match the **batch** on the turret hopper — mixed **training** ball and **AP** labels, or a **re-routed** shipment after lockdown.  
2. **Not the first visitors.** Scuffs at the inner airlock (**#3**) show **repeat** cycles — doors were **used** until the quiet. This party isn’t the first contract to land; they’re the first **since** the silence.  
3. **Machine vs meat.** Turrets **want** to kill; **something** starved them of ammo or power **before** your hull touched the field — foreshadow [[factions-and-enemies]] / hive elsewhere.

#### Cover symbols on the dock grid (`^` / `T` / `H`)

- **`^` cranes / stacked cargo:** half or three-quarters cover from deck level; **overpen** through **crate stacks** only if **rifle** semi (or AP) and GM allows — §3.1.2.  
- **`T` alcoves:** occupants **peek** from the **inboard** edge — **half** cover toward the berth; **automatic** spray from the **ship** side can ignore half-cover **Dex save** bonus per overlay.  
- **`H` ship hull:** **full** cover against cross-bay fire; **no** small-arms overpen through the hull.

#### Z1 gantries — why, read-aloud, hooks

**Why they exist:** loadmasters and ordnance tech kept eyes on the cradle without standing in the line of fire; Concordium grey, **decommission** stripe someone never finished scraping. **Spent casings** on grating (oil-glued **9mm**) read as **sidearm** panic or retreat — not turret feed.

**Read-aloud:** scrubbers pull a **constant** draft through the grating; the void below is a **grey** grid. Along row **11**, the **crane** bridge reads like a **knife** over the ship — exposed, one **shove** from a long fall.

**Hooks:** body on **`G`** with **security** badge but **wrong-size** boots (impostor / borrowed kit); from turret-overlook catwalks, **cut** feed lines visible (Athletics / Tech **DC 14–16**); **winch** cable at row **11** west, **sheared** end — something **heavy** lowered into the berth after lockdown.

**Vertical fire:** half cover for **prone** on **`G`**; standing on Z1 is exposed from Z0; burst/semi **lines** per §3.1.2; overpen **down** through grating only if **AP** + called shot (GM).

#### Reception / decon lobby (rows 22–31 on continuous map)

**Atmosphere:** polished floor reflects the ceiling **too** cleanly — victims weren’t dragged from a dock fight; they **walked** in calm (escorted? gas? order to kneel?). Walls **pristine** — no stray shots; combat here was **one-sided** or **not ballistic**. **Branding:** faded logo; slogan **scratched** out mid-letter (**nail** marks, not claws). **Clock:** digital **month** behind the desk **frozen** before the employer’s “last contact” window — possible lie about how stale the site is ([[station-and-antagonist]] **month clock**).

**Plot beats (with Beat B):** **#10** datapad — partial roster, redacted names, one extra login that never clocked out; **#11** — badges scan valid, nonlethal gear prominent, **no** obvious gunfight wall damage; visible clues allow gas / neuro / internal **stand down** / hive **suggestion**; **#12** north — corridor lights **sequence** off-on in a **slow** wave (power **rationed**, something deeper draws priority).

**Tactical:** **`@`** desk — three-quarters cover from **south** (dock); flank from **east/west** aisles. **Bodies** — difficult terrain; **half** cover if prone behind (morally charged — table check).

## 3. Beat A — “They’re shooting” (broken)

**Concrete default:** each turret emplacement has only **~5–6 effective kinetic rounds** before **dry fire** (starved hoppers). See [[../sessions/session-01/session-start-beat-plan]] for area breakdown and GM checklist.

**Trigger:** bay doors seal / mag field establishes / first foot on gantry — **weapon targeting** overlay hits ship HUD and/or station PA: *UNAUTHORIZED VESSEL — LETHAL FORCE AUTHORIZED.*

**What actually happens (pick one primary, optional secondary):**

1. **Dry kinetic:** Turrets **track**, **one** short burst (warning shot or panicked operator subroutine), then **click-click** — hoppers **empty**, belts **jammed**, servos **scream**.  
2. **Power fault:** First turret **fires**, second **explodes** its capacitor (shrapnel hazard, no endless fight).  
3. **Mixed:** **Spray** from one emplacement + **claymores** on the approach gantry **arm** (whine, red lattice) — then **dispel** residue or **broken circle** makes them **dud** / **single pop** only.

**Clues embedded in the failure:**

- Maintenance log at a turret kiosk (or smeared blood on the panel): **last manual override** days/weeks ago — *not* centuries of drift.  
- Ammo **types** don’t match current corporate loadout (someone **fed** the guns wrong on purpose — cult? sabotage? automated mistake?).  
- Hive **broadcast** not active yet — or a **faint** wrongness only the sensitive notice (save full **suggestion** pressure for deeper levels per [[narrative-synthesis]]).

**Table goal:** laughter + unease — *the station is hostile and incompetent at the same time.*

## 4. Beat B — “Too clean” (lobby)

Past the inner ring:

- **Lighting:** full spectrum, **no** flicker here (power **prioritized** to this node — why?).  
- **Bodies:** 3–6 **staged** — security in **corporate** armor, **nonlethal** gear prominent; one **research** badge; **no** obvious gunfight wall damage.  
- **Read:** could read as **gas**, **neuro**, **internal order** “stand down,” or **something that doesn’t need bullet holes**. Leave **multiple** interpretations until investigation.

**Investigation hooks (scatter; don’t require all):**

- Badges **still** scan as **valid** — mesh thinks they’re **alive** (IT ghost / hive mimic teaser per [[station-systems]] §5).  
- One corpse **clutching** a **dataterm** fragment or **child’s toy** — humanize before horror escalates.  
- **Smell:** *too* neutral — scrubbers on **overdrive** (masking **sweet** air deeper in — foreshadow atmo hooks).

## 5. Session 1 — practical run plan

**Length:** ~3–4 hours typical; flex at “lobby” depth.

| Block | Time | Focus |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Arrival & dock** | 15–25 min | Approach chatter; **one** clear ship-side problem (Cherish clears alert, Smolder calls shots). |
| **Beat A — failed PDS** | 20–40 min | Combat **light** or **skill** — evasion, repair, suppressing one hot turret, **not** a war of attrition. |
| **Crossing the bay** | 15–25 min | Crane bay / claymore strip; **perception** finds the “why broken” clue. |
| **Beat B — lobby** | 30–50 min | **Investigation** roleplay; **tension** without combat (unless you trigger a **drone** or **changed** peek — optional sting). |
| **Decision point** | 10–20 min | North corridor: toward **hab** vs east **maintenance** — **stop at a strong image** (door label, distant sound, sensor spike) for **session 2**. |

**Exit / cliffhanger options:**

- **North:** lights **die** in sequence — something **routing** power away from the party.  
- **East:** maintenance channel carries **wet** sound + **old** PA snippet (*“…trial period…”* / **Catalog** teaser from [[station-and-antagonist]]).  
- **Dataterm:** partial map — **four levels** named, **Heart** redacted.
