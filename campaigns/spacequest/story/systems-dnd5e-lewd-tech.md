## 1. Tension (lewd / psychic pressure)

Use a **Tension** score **per creature** (0–10) in relevant scenes: Amalgam broadcast, traps, certain spells, social escalation.


| Tension | Mechanical nudge (pick what fits; don’t stack all)                                                                       |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 0–2     | No effect                                                                                                                |
| 3–4     | First **Wisdom** save each **round** to avoid **distraction**: until start of next turn, no **Reaction** unless you pass |
| 5–6     | **Disadvantage** on **one** of: next Con save, next Wis save, or Perception (your choice which when the trigger fires)   |
| 7–8     | **Concentration** checks made with **+2 to the DC** while Tension ≥ 7                                                    |
| 9–10    | **Charmed**-lite toward a defined source (can’t attack source; GM may allow extra save each round)                       |


**Raising / lowering Tension**

- **+1** on failed Wis save vs broadcast / trap / contact effect.
- **+1** GM discretion for vivid stress (cap +2/round from this).
- **−1** after a **short rest** in a **clean** zone, or when **dispel / cold shower / slap** fiction (GM).
- **Clear to 0** on **long rest** away from hive influence (or **greater restoration**-tier cleanse).

**One roll default:** When an effect would “turn you on,” make **one** save:

- **Wisdom** (willpower) or **Charisma** (poise)—GM picks which fits the fiction.

**Integration with existing PCs (from characters channel):** psionics / telepathy ([[../characters/pcs/cherish]], [[../characters/pcs/geli-pelsin]]) can **probe** or **broadcast** feelings—resolve as **contested Insight vs Deception** or **Wis save** with DC = 8 + proficiency + relevant mod, or use class features when applicable.

### 1.1 Conditions — **Exposed** & **Wired** (Tension + body / ship tech)

These are **campaign conditions** for hive boarding, ship corridors, and **Endo** scenes. They stack with Tension thresholds but are **declared** in fiction (GM or player).

#### Exposed (psychic / social vulnerability)

You are **Exposed** when **Tension ≥ 5** **and** the scene has you **physically or socially laid open**—wardrobe failure, broadcast lock-on, intimate contact, or you **voluntarily** drop cover for a short-term gain (GM agrees).

| While Exposed | Effect |
| ------------- | ------ |
| **Read** | Hostile creatures have **advantage** on **Wisdom (Insight)** checks against you. |
| **Stealth** | You have **disadvantage** on **Dexterity (Stealth)** when the fiction is “you can’t hide what you’re broadcasting.” |
| **Hive pressure** | The **first** time you would gain **+1 Tension** from a **hive / broadcast** source in a round, it becomes **+2** instead. |

**Ends:** Tension drops to **4 or below**, or you **re-seal** (armor, environmental suit, leaving broadcast zone) / **short rest** in a **clean** zone—GM picks what fits.

#### Wired (ship tech / Endo / HUD)

You are **Wired** when you are **jacked in** to **ship systems**, **AR overlay**, or **Endo** as a **relay**—not casual comms, but **full-sensory** or **data-rich** link.

| While Wired | Effect |
| ----------- | ------ |
| **Systems** | **Advantage** on **Intelligence (Technology)** checks to operate **ship** hardware, doors, and **artificer** gadgets **through** that link. |
| **Bleed** | If **Tension ≥ 7**, you have **disadvantage** on **Wisdom** saves vs **digital intrusion**, **psychic** broadcast carried on the wire, or **charm** through the feed. |
| **Red + Endo** | Once per **short rest**, while **Wired** and Endo is within **30 ft**, Red can grant **advantage** on **one** **Intelligence (Technology)** or **Arcana** check tied to **ship** or **Endo** (player picks the roll). |

**Ends:** **Disconnect** (action or bonus action), **EMP** / ion damage that breaks the link, or **unconscious**.

#### Exposed + Wired together

In **hive-thick** zones, if you cast a spell with a **verbal** component while **both** conditions apply, **+2 Tension** at end of that turn (instead of +1 from hive-thick alone)—the wire **carries** the hive’s voice.

## 2. Magic (5e + station rules)

### 2.1 Zones (simple)


| Zone type                      | Rule (pick one per zone; don’t over-layer)                                                                                               |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Normal**                     | Spells work as written                                                                                                                   |
| **Containment / dispel field** | **No** spells, **or** spells **≤3rd** only, **or** caster must pass **Arcana DC 15** as part of casting or the spell fizzles (slot lost) |
| **Hive-thick**                 | **+1 to Tension** at end of any turn you cast a spell with a **verbal** component (audible vulnerability)                                |


**Breaking circle anchors:** Treat anchor as **object**: AC 15–18, HP 25–50, immune psychic; destroying it ends the linked effect (GM sets exact numbers once per encounter).

### 2.2 Assimilation / “corruption” saves

When the fiction is **rewrite desire / self** (not just horny):

- **Charisma** or **Wisdom** save (DC 14–18), **proficiency** if you already resisted the same source today.

On **fail**, **+1 Tension** and a **story** cost (whispered urge, embarrassing truth, temporary fixation)—not necessarily incapacitation.

## 3. Gunplay & tech (5e-friendly)

**Ranged weapons** resolve like **normal 5e** ranged attacks—no special proficiency layer unless your table adds one.

### 3.0 Party tier — damage & DCs (**Lv ~5** vs **Lv ~11**)

Lock the table to the party’s **current** band. Numbers below scale **hazards**; PC damage uses their sheets.

| Hazard | **Tier A** (~party Lv 5) | **Tier B** (~party Lv 11) |
| ------ | ------------------------ | ------------------------- |
| **Turret** reaction attack | **+7** to hit, **2d6+2** kinetic, range 60/240 | **+10** to hit, **3d6+3** kinetic, same range |
| **Turret** AC / HP | **AC 16**, **HP 35** | **AC 18**, **HP 55** |
| **Turret** hack DC | **DC 16** (2 successes) | **DC 18** (2 successes) |
| **Suicide FPV** ram attack | **+6**, **3d6** fire, AC **13**, HP **8** | **+8**, **4d6** fire, AC **14**, HP **12** |
| **FPV** splash Dex save | **DC 14** half | **DC 16** half |
| **Kamikaze sprinter** detonation | **DC 14** Dex, **3d6** + prone | **DC 17** Dex, **5d6** + prone |
| **Landmine** trigger | **DC 14** Dex, **4d6** half | **DC 17** Dex, **6d6** half |
| **Spot / disarm** mines | Spot **DC 14–15**, disarm **DC 16** | Spot **DC 15–16**, disarm **DC 18** |
| **Grenade launcher** (§3.1.1) Dex / damage | Save DC same as **suppression** row above; **2d6** full / half | Save DC as Tier B suppression; **3d6** full / half |

**Suppression / spray / GL** save DC (§3.1): **Tier A** use **DC 8 + prof + Dex**; **Tier B** use **DC 10 + prof + Dex** for **burst** suppression, **automatic** spray, and **GL** unless the weapon says otherwise.

### 3.1 Firearms — weapon **profiles** & fire **modes**

**Profiles** = what the gun **is** (pistol, rifle, GL, …). **Modes** = how you **fire** this round (semi, burst, …). Pick **one profile** for the weapon on your sheet; layer **one mode** on an attack unless the weapon is built for more (GM).

#### Profiles (chassis — flavor + at most one nudge)

Use PHB-style **range** and **damage dice** from your weapon pick (or GM stat block). The nudge is **optional**; if it doesn’t fit the scene, ignore it and play the numbers straight.


| Profile | Examples | Optional nudge (GM / player agrees once) |
| ------- | -------- | --------------------------------------- |
| **Pistol / sidearm** | Autopistol, machine pistol (one-hand) | In **tight ship** corridors, ranged attacks vs targets **within 5 ft** don’t automatically use **cover** rules for “too close”—still use normal **ranged** attack (fiction: retention shooting). **Past normal range:** **disadvantage** if the weapon’s long range is short (e.g. 30/90). |
| **Revolver** | Single- or double-action wheelgun | **Fanning** (**bonus action**): make **two** attacks at **disadvantage** **or** **one** attack and add **+1 weapon die** on hit (recoil / wild aim). **Cylinder:** treat like **bolt/pump** only if you’re tracking **slow reload**—one **bonus action** to reload most of the cylinder. |
| **SMG / PDW** | Compact auto, room-clearing | **Burst** mode (below) is **on-theme**; attacks past **normal** range have **disadvantage** (light round + short barrel). |
| **Carbine / assault rifle** | Intermediate cartridge, selective fire | Default for **semi** or **burst** tags; **two-handed** unless a braced one-hand stunt (GM). |
| **Battle rifle / DMR** | Full-power semi-auto, designated marksman | **Within normal range only:** **+1 weapon die** on hit **or** **+2** to hit if you **did not move** on your previous turn (**aimed** shot). **In melee** (hostile within 5 ft): **disadvantage** unless you drop the long gun or use a **pistol** sidearm. |
| **Sniper / long rifle** | Scoped bolt or semi | Same as **DMR** aim rule; **bolt/pump** mode matches **manual** repeaters. **Disadvantage** if you **moved** more than half speed this turn (no stable brace). |
| **LMG / squad automatic** | Belt-fed, sustained fire | First **burst** each round does **not** cost a **bonus action** if you **did not move** this turn (**bipod / mount**). **Automatic** spray (below) is on-theme; **heat** rises fast—track **heat** carefully. |
| **Shotgun** | Breacher, combat shotgun | Use **Shotgun** mode (below); **past normal range** = **disadvantage** as written. |
| **Grenade launcher** (standalone) | 40mm tube, underslung M203-style | **Not** a single-target ranged weapon attack for the **explosion**—see **§3.1.1**. |
| **Underbarrel** | M203, smoke, secondary tube | **Bonus action** once per **short rest:** launch **smoke** ( **10-ft radius** lightly obscured until end of next round) **or** a **single-target** **Dex save** (DC 8 + prof + Dex) for **1d6** bludgeoning + **prone** (stun grenade fiction)—**one** resolution, no attack roll. |

#### Recoil & heat (use these)

**Heat** tracks per weapon from **0** (cool) upward. **Recoil** is a **condition** on *you* after certain modes—not optional flavor.

| Source | Heat |
| ------ | ---- |
| **Semi** | **+0** |
| **Burst** | **+1** |
| **Automatic** (spray) | **+2** |
| **Shotgun** spread mode | **+1** |
| **Bolt / pump** (manual) | **+0** (mechanical cycle bleeds energy differently—no heat spike) |

**Heat thresholds (per weapon, end of your turn unless you cool):**

| Heat | Effect |
| ---- | ------ |
| **0–2** | No penalty |
| **3–4** | **−1** to hit with this weapon until heat drops (**cool**) |
| **5+** | **−2** to hit with this weapon **and** on a natural **1** the round **jams** / **cooks**—weapon can’t fire until you spend an **action** to **clear** (drops heat to **1**) |

**Cool:** **Bonus action** → remove **2** heat (vent, swap hand, short let-off). **Full action** in cover → set heat to **0** (full barrel discipline / barrel change fiction).

**Recoil (after burst only):** After you resolve a **burst** (below), you have **recoil** until the **start** of your next turn: **−2** to hit on **any** further **ranged weapon attacks** with **that** weapon **unless** you spend a **bonus action** to **brace / reacquire** (clears **recoil** but does **not** cool heat). **Semi** and **automatic** do **not** apply this **recoil** line—only **burst**.

#### Fire modes (layer **one** on an attack; semi vs burst vs automatic)

| Mode | Mechanic |
| ---- | -------- |
| **Semi** | One normal ranged weapon attack. **Once per turn**, your **first** attack that is **semi** with this weapon gets **+1** to hit **or** **+1** to damage on a hit (choose when you declare)—**controlled** fire, **no** burst **recoil**, **+0** heat. |
| **Burst** (controlled short group) | **Bonus action** *or* **expend 2+ ammo** (if tracking): **one** attack roll vs **one** target; on hit, **+1 weapon die** **or** target rolls **Dex save** (suppression DC, **§3.0**) or can’t take **Reactions** until start of your next turn. **After burst:** apply **+1 heat** and **recoil** (above). |
| **Automatic** (mag dump / spray) | **Action**; **expend** most or all of a magazine (GM: **half** max ammo or **10+ rounds** if you track). **No attack roll.** Choose a **cone** (**15 ft**) or **5-ft-wide line** (**30 ft**) originating from you—**only** creatures in **normal range** can be affected. Each creature in the area makes a **Dexterity saving throw** vs suppression DC (**§3.0**). **Damage:** roll the weapon’s **damage dice once** and apply that total to each creature who **failed**; **half** as much on a **success** (round down each). **After automatic:** **+2 heat**; **no** burst-style **recoil**—until **start** of your next turn you **can’t** take **Reactions** (you’re **committed** to the spray). |
| **Shotgun** (spread) | **Point-blank (≤10 ft):** optional **+1 weapon die** on hit **or** **two** creatures within **5 ft** of each other: **one** attack vs higher AC; on hit, **split** damage. **Past normal range:** **disadvantage**. **Heat +1** if you use spread buckshot this way. |
| **Bolt / pump** (manual action) | After an attack, **next** attack with this weapon before end of next turn is **−1** to hit unless **bonus** to work the action. **No** burst **recoil** from this—different fiction. |

**Summary:** **Semi** = **accurate**, low heat. **Burst** = **punch + suppression**, pays **recoil + heat**. **Automatic** = **area Dex saves**, **ammo dump**, **no** single attack roll, **high heat** + **no Reactions** until next turn.

#### 3.1.1 Grenade launcher (area)

**Action:** Choose a point you can see within the weapon’s **range** (typically **60/120** or as statted). **One** resolution for the explosion (not **burst** fire mode—launcher has its own profile):

- **Primary:** Each creature in a **10-ft radius** makes a **Dexterity saving throw** (DC **8 + prof + Dex**, or **§3.0** suppression scaling if you use Tier B for DCs). On a **failed** save, **2d6** piercing or fire (GM); **half** on success.
- **Optional simple mode:** **One** attack roll vs **AC 10** (or target’s AC if GM wants cover to matter) against **one** creature in the blast **center**; on hit, **full** damage to that creature and **half** to others who fail Dex (faster but swingy).

**Shapes:** **Airburst / frag** = radius as above. **Smoke** = no damage; **lightly obscured** in radius until wind/end of round. **Concussion** = damage **only**; no shrapnel—creatures **save** or are **deafened** until end of next turn (GM).

**Reload:** **Bonus action** to chamber next round, or **action** if under pressure (GM).

#### 3.1.2 Cover & overpenetration

Use normal **5e cover** (**half** +2 AC, **three-quarters** +5, **full** blocks targeting) for **single-target** shots. The table below adds **station / firefight** defaults—**one** extra rule per fight so it doesn’t slow play.

**Cover quality (typical dock / corridor)**

| Kind | vs ranged attacks | Notes |
| ---- | ------------------- | ----- |
| **Half** | +2 AC | Pallets, crane stanchions, **turret alcove** lip, open mesh gantry rail |
| **Three-quarters** | +5 AC | Stacked crates **two high**, reactor pipe cluster, **reception desk** edge |
| **Full** | Can’t be targeted | **Bulkhead** slot closed, **ship hull** (berth), sealed kiosk shutter |

**Automatic spray** (§3.1): targets behind **half** cover **do not** add the **+2** to the **Dex save** (suppression chews the edge); targets behind **three-quarters** cover have **advantage** on the save. **Full** cover = **line of effect** blocked unless the **cone / line** clearly wraps the obstacle (GM).

**Grenades, mines, other explosives** (§3.1.1, §3.5): **Dex save** only—cover still helps: **half** cover → **+2** to the save; **three-quarters** → **+5**; **full** cover if **wholly** behind **thick** wall and **not** in radius = **auto success** on save for **shrapnel** (GM: **full** damage if **in** the **fire** / **pressure** radius anyway).

**Overpenetration** (single-target **semi** or **burst** only—not **automatic** spray)

When fiction matters (metal walls, stacked bodies, thin partitions):

1. **Light / thin** cover (office panel, cargo strap wall, **one** crate thickness): on a **hit** that would **only** strike cover, or when GM rules **same** bullet carries through—**one** creature **directly behind** in a **straight line** (first one along the attack trace) takes **half** the weapon’s **damage dice** (round down, no modifier)—**once** per attack.
2. **Heavy** plating, **ship hull** squares (`H` on maps), reinforced **airlock**: **no** overpen from **pistol / SMG / shotgun**; **battle rifle / DMR / sniper** with **AP** or **fated** shot: GM may allow **overpen** as (1) **or** **1d4** kinetic **spall** to a target **within 5 ft** of the impact side.
3. **Stacked targets:** If two creatures are **in a line** and the **front** is **hit**, GM may call for **overpen** (1) on the **rear** target **once** per round—usually only for **rifle-caliber** semi.

**Player telegraph:** declare **“AP / aimed through cover”** before rolling; GM sets whether overpen is on the table.

### 3.2 Automatic turret (deployed hazard)

Treat as **creature** (Tiny or Small) with:

- **AC**, **HP**, and **reaction** attack **per §3.0** (Tier A / B)—not tuned for a fair shootout.
- **Immunities:** charmed, frightened (machine)
- **Reaction** (1/round): when a hostile creature it can see **moves** or **attacks**, make **one** ranged attack using **§3.0** bonus and damage, range **60/240**.

**Intentionally unfair:** turrets are **zone denial**. They **punish** movement and split attention; winning is **cover, smoke, hack, or destroying the emplacement**—not trading shots evenly. **Minion** (1 HP) turrets are for **mook** lanes only.

**Hack / disable:** **Intelligence (Technology)** or **tools** vs **§3.0** hack DC (2 successes) or **attack** vs AC to **brake** the mount.

### 3.3 Suicide FPV drone

- **Move** 50 ft (hover); **AC**, **HP**, attack bonus, and **ram** damage from **§3.0**.
- **Action:** **Ram** one creature (melee spell attack per tier): on hit, fire damage from **§3.0** **and** drone dies.
- **If drone ends turn adjacent to hostile**, that creature can **use Reaction** to **make attack vs drone** before detonation (optional cinematic rule)

**Simple:** One attack roll; miss = drone **overshoots** and **explodes** in nearest **unoccupied** space within 10 ft (Dex save / half from **§3.0**).

### 3.4 Kamikaze sprinter (mechanical or biological)

**Idea:** a fast runner that **detonates** when it closes—either a **tech** payload (drone legs, rigged corpse, cyborg courier) or **bio** (infected, wolf/werewolf/shifter **kamikaze**, zombie **sprinter**). Use the same **one** trigger block; swap the stat block for flavor.

**Stat block (pick one per encounter):** **Ghoul**, **dire wolf**, **zombie** (+ **Dash** or extra **move** from hazard), or a **werewolf / shifter** stat block **stripped** to speed + HP appropriate to tier—GM sets AC/HP.

**Detonation:** When it **starts its turn** within **5 ft** of a creature, that creature makes **Dex save** and takes damage **per §3.0** (force, or necrotic for bio-bomb fiction) and is **knocked prone**; the sprinter **dies** (or is **destroyed** if mechanical).

### 3.5 Landmines / pressure plates


| Stage       | Roll |
| ----------- | ---- |
| **Spot**    | Passive Perception vs **§3.0** spot DC band, or active **Investigation** |
| **Disarm**  | **Dexterity (Thieves’ tools)** or **Int (Technology)** vs **§3.0** disarm DC |
| **Trigger** | **Dexterity save** per **§3.0**: **half** on success, full damage tier on fail |


**Minefield (zone):** Entering a **5-ft line** for the first time on a turn: **one** Dex save for that line (don’t roll per square).

### 3.6 Drones as mobs

Use **minion** rule: **1 HP** each; **hit** = dead; **miss** might hit another minion in swarm (GM). Keeps FPV swarms fast.

## 4. One-roll combat cadence (keep fights moving)

1. **Declare** intent (move + attack / cast / tech / help).
2. **One** primary d20 resolution (attack, save, or check).
3. **Tension** tick if fiction demands (usually **one** save at end of round for hive-heavy rooms).
4. **Environmental** hazard (turret, mine): **either** on **initiative count** or **start of round** GM triggers **one** effect.

Avoid: separate rolls for **spot + disarm + attack** unless the scene is **about** the heist—otherwise collapse **spot** into passive Perception.

## 8. Discord RP bot, dashboard & **AI** (tooling)

**Goal:** keep **Tension**, **zone**, **Exposed / Wired**, and hazard flags **live** in `#rp`—and pipe that state into your **actual AI** so the bot can **propose or execute** overlay-consistent rolls (saves, checks, attack rolls) instead of hand-rolling every line.

### State the bot must hold (per channel or thread)

- Party **tier** (A or B from §3.0)
- **Tension** band or numeric (0–10)
- **Zone:** Normal / dispel / hive-thick
- **Conditions:** who is **Exposed**, **Wired**, or both
- **Hazards:** turret count / lanes, minefield, sprinter wave, etc. (GM shorthand)

### AI integration (rolls + narration)

- **Backend** receives Discord commands or dashboard updates, builds a **compact system prompt** chunk: current §1 Tension rules, §1.1 conditions, §3.0 tier row, active zone.
- **AI** (your existing model / API—same stack you use for RP assistance) returns: **suggested d20 outcome** (or raw die + modifier if you prefer transparency), **which rule** applied, and **short** in-character or GM line optional.
- **Safety:** **GM-only** commands confirm **lethal** hazards (turret volley, mine) or **+Tension**—players can request rolls, but **state changes** that affect the whole scene stay **role-gated** (Discord role or dashboard login).
- **Dice:** implement either (a) **cryptographic / standard** RNG in bot code with AI only choosing **modifiers and DCs**, or (b) AI outputs **roll expression** and bot executes `/roll`—pick one pipeline and stick to it so results stay auditable.

### Surfaces

- **Bot:** slash commands (`/tension`, `/zone`, `/condition`, `/hazard`, `/overlay roll ...`) posting **pinned** embeds or thread headers.
- **Dashboard:** GM edits the same JSON/state; **webhook** pushes embed updates to Discord; optional export line for [[../discord-export/elastic-bulk/ingest-instructions]].

## 9. Adult gear, hazards & props (NSFW)

**Scope:** mechanics + **fiction prompts** for mature salvage, corporate “wellness,” and hive-adjacent chem. **§0** applies: lines/veils, **choices** over forced detail, **cut to black** on request. Nothing here requires graphic narration—use **implication**, **Investigation** results, or **“you know what this is.”**

### 9.1 Discarded props & scene dressing (no combat)

Use for **Investigation**, **Insight**, or **Urban Survival**-style checks when PCs search lockers, bunks, vents, or trash.

| Find | Typical DC | What it can signal (pick one) |
| ---- | ---------- | ------------------------------ |
| **Spent condom** / wrapper | 10–12 | Crew downtime; **safe-ish** habits; timeframe (fresh vs dessicated). |
| **Torn wrapper only** | 12–14 | Hurried exit; panic; “someone was interrupted.” |
| **Toy packaging** (empty) | 12–15 | Mail-order novelty; **corp** SKU → trace supplier. |
| **Lube bottle** / pump | 10–13 | Medbay vs personal; **contaminated** if plot needs. |
| **Broken vibe** / power cell | 13–16 | Improvised tool attempt; **EMP**-fried or **chewed** (something not human?). |

**Loot:** these are usually **clues**, not treasure. **Gold** value **0** unless blackmail or rare collector’s item (GM).

### 9.2 Personal gear (carry rules — light touch)

Treat as **normal objects** unless a player **builds** a scene around them.

| Item | Fiction | Rule of thumb |
| ---- | ------- | --------------- |
| **Vibrator** (handheld) | Tool / distraction / salvage | **Action** to activate (audible in quiet); **no** attack unless improvised (§9.4). |
| **Dildo** (silicone, etc.) | Club, gag, doorstop | Improvised weapon **§9.4**; **nonmetal** for scanners that care. |
| **Restraints** (cuffs, straps) | Capture, kink, prisoner transfer | Use **grapple** + **object** AC (AC 11–13, HP 5); **Dex** DC 12–16 to slip **one** try. |

### 9.3 Chem & “novelty” grenades (hazards)

Resolve like **small explosives** for **placement** (Dex save for splash) but **damage** is usually **0**—instead apply **Tension** / **conditions**. Use **§3.0** suppression DC band for **save DC** (Tier A **14–15**, Tier B **16–17**) unless weaker commercial gear (**DC 12–13**).

#### Aphrodisiac gas cartridge / “party bomb”

- **Trigger:** **Action** to throw to a point within **20/60**; **5-ft radius** burst (or **10-ft** military hive-grade—GM).
- **Effect:** each creature in area **Constitution** save (inhale / contact) **or** **Wisdom** save (psychic aerosol)—GM picks **one** per device label.
  - **Fail:** **+1 Tension** immediately; until end of **next** turn, **disadvantage** on **one** roll type (Insight, Perception, or **first** save vs **charm**-like effect—player picks worst fear).
  - **Success:** no Tension from **this** burst; **hive-thick** rooms may still add normal **§2.1** ticks.
- **Commercial** variants: **half radius**, **DC −2**, **no** disadvantage—only **+1 Tension** on fail.

#### Contact gel / spray (single-target)

- **Action** range **5 ft** or **15 ft** sprayer; **ranged spell attack** vs AC (touch AC if willing) **or** **Contested** Dex vs your **Tech/Sleight of Hand**.
- **Hit / win:** target **+1 Tension**; until end of your next turn, **hostile** Insight checks against them have **advantage** (overwhelming scent / flush—**Exposed**-adjacent without auto-applying **§1.1** unless Tension already **5+**).

#### Pheromone smoke (area denial)

- **10-ft radius**, **lightly obscured**, **1 minute** (commercial) or **Concentration**-like **3 rounds** (cheap).
- **End of turn** in cloud: **Wis save** or **+1 Tension** (max **+1/round** from this source). **Stacks** with **hive-thick** verbal-casting rule only if GM says the cloud **carries** broadcast spores.
