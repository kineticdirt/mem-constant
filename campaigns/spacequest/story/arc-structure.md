# Arc structure — loose scaffold

*Flexible notation only; reorder beats as play demands.*

## 1. Rising action (already in play)

Briefing → bar → ship → corpo mall / hallway. **Mission:** remote blacksite, salvage dataterms, **~one month** clock, **Captain Smolder** holds jump point. Payment sold as life-changing — which makes **compliance** and **risk appetite** both plausible.

## 2. Climax of **discovery**

The turn is **not** “monsters attack” first — it’s **what the station was for** and **why your employer lied or undersold**.

Possible discovery payloads (mix and match):

- The research wasn’t abandoned; it **graduated**.  
- “Data salvage” is cover for **retrieval of something alive or contagious** (memetic, biological, or social).  
- **Assimilation tech** is the product line — horniness as **interface** or **delivery vector**.

**Table goal:** one clear “oh no” that recontextualizes every flirty or gross beat from earlier sessions.

## 3. Falling action — **escape**

Short, brutal, logistical. After the reveal, the game is **egress under constraint**:

- Who do you save if you can’t save everyone?  
- What do you **not** take with you (data, person, habit)?  
- Does the ship still feel like “home,” or has the mission stained it?

Assimilation stakes: escape might mean **leaving part of yourself** — memory, desire, or trust — not just clearing a map.

## 4. Finale

Land the **emotional invoice**: payoffs for Smolder’s authority, Aurora’s control, Red’s intimacy, Cherish’s appetite, Daji’s delayed violence, Geli’s hidden signal. Corporate fallout optional — the **human** fallout is the close.
