# Blacksite station + main antagonist (design draft)

*Aligned to Discord beats: **salvage contractors**, corporate **deniability**, abandoned **research** installation in a **hollowed asteroid**, **labyrinth** navigation. **Mostly scientific** station with **some** installed magic systems (dispel fields, circle anchors, scheduled “top-ups”). Crew still use **D&D-style magic** while **technology encroaches** on practice and dependency. Iterates as you lock canon.*

---

## Canon premise (whole story)

The station is a **general bad-corporate** asset: **mining** (ore / ice / volatiles), **research & development**, and light **processing** — plausible cover for a big hollowed rock. **One R&D line** produced a **biological Flood**: a propagating **mass** that **cannibalized** the facility into **its own playground**, remaking corridors and crew into **spawn** and **monsters**. That threat is **the Amalgam** (same thing as “the Flood experiment” in pitch meetings — **the lab eaten by its own trial**). Everything else (salvage run, cults, employer lies) hangs off **containment failed; the experiment owns the map**.

---

## Station — working name

**Facility** (internal): abandoned **mining / R&D / processing** station — **public** face: stripped hulk, bad orbit, **why contractors** not a fleet.

**Form:** **Four main levels** (vertical metaphor below), **asteroid-carved** transit + prefab rings — **labyrinthine** maintenance spine, not a shopping mall map.

| Level | Metaphor | Primary function | Nav / feel |

## Main antagonist — **the Amalgam** (primary)

**The biological Flood** — **Flood**-style **biological mass** born here as **corporate science**, not an outside invasion: hive **broadcast** (esp. **sexualized** suggestion — pleasure as **weapon** but mostly **plot lubricant**), absorption and **redeployment** of people as **different** selves, spawn of **“monsters of yore”** and other **templates**, tentacle **traps** and breeding snares to **perpetuate** the network. Spread = **corruption** as *it* defines corruption; not classic reproduction. **It turned the station into its playground** — layout, power, and crew are **toys** and **feedstock**.

**Objectives on station:** restore **power**, break or **dispel** hostile **magical circles**, survive **changed** crew and **spawn**, uncover **cult** collaboration.
