# Asteroid station — systems bible (draft)

*Mostly scientific infrastructure; **magic** exists as crew capability plus a few **installed** systems (dispel fields, ritual “top-ups,” circle anchors). Design for a **derelict** state: broken, rerouted, or corrupted. **In-fiction cover:** mining + **R&D** + processing — until the **Flood** experiment repurposed systems for biomass and spawn (see [[station-and-antagonist]]).*

---

## 1. Life support & atmosphere

| Subsystem | Normal role | Derelict hook |
|-----------|-------------|---------------|
| **Atmo processors** | O₂/N₂ balance, CO₂ scrubbing, trace gas control | Patchwork zones: breathable / thin / “sweet” air (hive pheromone load) |
| **Pressure hulls** | Compartment isolation | Micro-breaches sealed by foam or biomass; audible leaks |
| **Thermal** | Station-wide climate | Cold spots near coolant; hot near reactor slag or growths |
| **Water reclamation** | Greywater → potable | Stagnant reservoirs; corruption tinting taste (plot, not mechanics unless you want) |

---

## 2. Food, water, growth

Assume **self-sufficiency** at full ops; salvage timeline = **scavenge what’s left**.

| Source | Flavor | Notes |
|--------|--------|--------|
| **Hydroponics / aeroponics** | Leafy greens, algae, flavor bases | Tiered racks under grow lights; **silos** of nutrient paste and mineral stock |
| **Protein** | **Lab-grown** vat meat, mycoprotein, insect farms (pick what fits tone) | Bioreactors overlap thematically with **enemy biomass** — visual rhyme |
| **Import stock** | Freeze-dried, MRE analogs | **Warehouses** near dock; loot / hazard |
| **Water** | Ice mining (asteroid), reclamation, bulk storage | **Chemical processing** for industrial use vs potable (different pipes) |

**Table question:** Is any food supply **actively hijacked** by the amalgam (spores in paste, euphoric additives), or only ambient corruption?

---

## 3. Power & industrial backbone

- **Reactor / fusion / radioisotope** — whatever your hard-SF level allows; **restoring power** is a campaign lever (lights, doors, fields).
- **Coolant & heat sinks** — ties to Level “bone” corridors; flood of hot coolant as set-piece.
- **Grid segments** — **manual bus transfer**: puzzles to route power around dead sectors.

---

## 4. Logistics: silos, warehouses, spare parts

- **Bulk storage** — vertical silos in the asteroid rock (ore, volatiles, **nutrient** powder, inert ballast).
- **Spare parts** — fab chips, actuator kits, suit seals; **salvage team** motivation to loot.
- **Internal rail or mag carts** — abandoned trains, jammed doors, ambush lines.

---

## 5. IT, comms, security

| Layer | Function | Derelict use |
|-------|----------|--------------|
| **Local mesh** | Door auth, sensor net, crew locators | Ghost signals; **hive** mimics announcements |
| **Server vaults / “dataterms”** | Mission MacGuffin | Employer’s real interest |
| **External comms** | Tight beam, relay | Why the month clock bites — backup calls fail |
| **Dispel / antimagic fields** | Corporate **containment** for lab floors | **Gameplay:** suppresses hostile circles; **may** stress PCs who rely on magic |

---

## 6. Habitat & “nice” civ spaces

Sell that people **lived** here before the horror:

- **Apartment stacks** — modular units, shared baths, soundproofing (failed → overheard trauma).
- **Parks / atriums** — LED “sky,” hydroponic trees, benches; now overgrown or **garden of wrong**.
- **Amenities** — gym, clinic, mess hall, **corporate mall** energy (ties to existing corpo beats).
- **Transit** — ring corridors, lifts, maintenance **crawl** networks (labyrinth spine).

---

## 7. Security & armory

- **Armory** — kinetic weapons, nonlethal, **power cells**; maybe corporate **no heavy weapons** policy (explains loadout limits).
- **Internal security** — barriers, nonlethal, drone bays (some drones **repurposed** by hive or cult).
- **Quarantine** — airlocks, decon, **old** protocols the party can re-enable.

---

## 8. Research / magitech interface (light touch)

- **Circle anchors** — physical rings, capacitors, **bloodless** corporate ritual hardware (until it wasn’t).
- **Top-ups** — scheduled **aether** injections for long experiments (jargon for “why magic didn’t fizzle”).
- **Fields** — dispel zones as **engineering**, not hand-wave: emitters, maintenance hatches, **destroy emitter = zone falls**.
