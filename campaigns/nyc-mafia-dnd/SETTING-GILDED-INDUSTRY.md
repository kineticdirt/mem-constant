## 1. What "Gilded" means here

The city wears **mid-century jazz-vice** on the surface and **late-1800s brass** in the walls — but the **money** that built both layers often smells like **Gilded Age robber-baron money**: ostentatious limestone fronts, tenement sprawl behind them, patronage politics, and fortunes made on **rail**, **foundry**, and **warded freight**.

This is not a separate era pasted on top. It is a **texture in the uneven stack** (`SETTING-CAPABILITY-ASYMMETRY.md` §2): the **industrialists** who own the ley-lines and commuter bonds still dress like Commission lawyers and country-club members. Their fathers (or grandfathers — nobody agrees) built the **ward-rail** when guild magic finally beat horse-haul on the Jersey flats.

**Play it as contrast:**
- Marble lobby, broken radiator in the stairwell.
- Philanthropist name on a hospital wing; parish priest says the wing only admits **guild-stamped** patients.
- A conductor in gold braid who cannot read the **schedule board** because the feast-day sigil is wrong again.

## 2. Magitech* (spell-rooted industrial craft)

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **magitech\*** | Industrial machines — trains, foundry loaders, speaking-tube exchanges, steam-assist subway grades — powered by **D&D spells, sigils, bound spirits, and temple patronage**. Iteration = guild charter + ritual QA, not lab notebooks. |
| **NOT magitech** | Void 1931 frame: chemistry-first, Prohibition gin, "science discovers magic." See `_trash/setting/SETTING-MAGITECH-DIVERGENCE.md`. |
| **Agent rule** | Before inventing a machine, run `SETTING-SPELL-TECH-TREE.md` §2 (Goal → Need → Spell). If the answer is "chemistry" or "electronics," stop. |

**Typical magitech\* stacks:**

| Machine | Spell / ritual layer | Industrial skin |
|---------|----------------------|-----------------|
| **Ley locomotive** | **Animate objects** on drive rods + **continual flame** firebox + bound **steam spirit** in brass boiler | Polish, coal smoke, crew in leather aprons |
| **Ward-rail grade** | **Glyph of warding** on ties; **alarm** on trespass; ley-survey stakes every mile | Looks like normal track until the sigils glow at dusk |
| **Foundry loader** | **Unseen servant** gallery + **animate objects** (crates only) | 8-foot iron frame; union calls it a scab |
| **Speaking-tube hub** | **Message** cantrip on sympathetic brass pairs | Midtown pneumatic mail; Ashford splices |

**Failure modes are ritual, not mechanical:** wrong patron on feast day → boiler **sings** the wrong hymn and loses pressure; ward-rail **misreads** a freight manifest and routes a crate to **Jamaica** instead of **Journal Square**; a loader **keeps stacking** after the foreman went home (automata lane — existential when the core slips).

## 3. Magical trains (ley-rail & ward-rail)

### 3.1 Ownership (who gets rich)

| Syndicate / holder | Base | Holds | Family / politics lean |
|--------------------|------|-------|------------------------|
| **Pemberton Ley-Rail Consolidated** | Jersey City foundry campus | **Northeast Corridor** trunk — Newark ↔ Manhattan ↔ New Haven commuter contracts | **Ashford** lawyers on retainer; **Green Desk** terror headlines before bond votes |
| **Harlan Brass & Steam** | Greenpoint / Passaic | **Freight** locomotives, **capsule**-fed switch engines, construct-loader **cores** (licensed and not) | **Valenti** night cargo; **Iron Guild** union; FSA watches **core** imports |
| **Vandermeer Terminal Co.** | Grand Central ward-hall (Midtown) | **Manhattan terminal** rights, **speaking-tube** trunk under concourse, **ritual conductor** guild charter | **Ashford** equity; **Chen-Okafor** numbers shops in terminal arcades |
| **Jamaica Yards Cooperative** | Queens | **Long Island** branch, **reagent** smuggling in **ice car** manifests | **Chen-Okafor** divination crews; OCU **cargo** search choke |
| **Morris & Essex Ward Line** | Newark Ironbound | **Jersey commuter** belts, **tank-farm** spurs, **cold-iron** cartridge runs | **Valenti** ↔ **Chen-Okafor** handoffs; druid **steam-main** sabotage stories |

**Ashford lean:** not a rail monopoly — **contracts, bonds, and terminal air rights**. Julian Ashford's people sit on **Pemberton** and **Vandermeer** boards the way other families sit on parish committees. They do not grease wheels; they **own the language** in the wheel contract.

**Thin names OK at table:** any robber-baron with a limestone lobby and a **guild charter number** works if they touch **rail, foundry, or ward freight**.

### 3.2 How a ley locomotive runs

1. **Ley survey** — dwarf **cartographers** stake the line; bad survey = train **hums** wrong and makes passengers nauseous.
2. **Ward-rail bed** — **glyph of warding** on ties; trespass triggers **alarm** to the **ritual conductor's** brass badge.
3. **Boiler** — bound **steam spirit** (licensed) or **elemental** shard (bootleg); **continual flame** maintenance crew on payroll.
4. **Drive** — **animate objects** on coupling rods; capped so it cannot become **autonomous** without a **core** swap (illegal).
5. **Conductor** — **ritual conductor** (guild-licensed) carries **schedule seal**, **patron rosary**, and **emergency word of recall** scroll (one use, temple-audited).

**Capsule-fed** switch engines use **mana pulse** capsules in a brass hopper — same supply chain as **Valenti-6** loads (`SETTING-ARMS.md`). Cheaper than a full spirit boiler; **drifts** on feast days (weak computation lane).

### 3.3 Routes (concrete)

| Line | Endpoints | Cargo / passengers | Choke / play |
|------|-----------|-------------------|--------------|
| **Pemberton Main** | Newark Penn ↔ Grand Central | Commuters, **guild-stamped** freight | **Schedule chaos** at rush; **OCU** ward-sniffers on platform 12 |
| **Harlan Shore** | Red Hook ↔ Passaic yards | **Construct cores**, cold-iron, smuggled **jade** | Night **loader** cars; Valenti **stamp** forgery |
| **Jamaica Reagent** | Jamaica Yards ↔ Flushing ↔ LI branches | **Ice cars** (alchemical reagents), numbers runners | Chen-Okafor **divination** room above platform kiosk |
| **Morris Commuter** | Hoboken ↔ Newark ↔ Manhattan ferry link | Jersey **Iron Guild** shifts, tank-farm crews | Druid **steam-main** scare stories; real sabotage **sometimes** |
| **Bronx Elevated** | South Bronx ↔ Manhattan els | **Not** ley-trunk — steam-assist **el**; smuggling on **roof** routes | Valenti vs Chen-Okafor **arterial** war (`borough-city-build.md`) |

**Grand Central** is the **cathedral of ward-rail**: vaulted brass, **continual flame** chandeliers, a **schedule board** that lies when automata cores pass within three blocks. Under the concourse: **Vandermeer** speaking-tube trunk — three families have **splices** (see `reports/2026-08-01-five-families.md`).

### 3.4 Schedules are human chaos (weak computation)

There is **no** reliable rail **algorithm**. Pemberton publishes a **printed timetable**; reality is:

- **Ritual conductors** argue with **station clerks** over feast-day patron conflicts.
- **Ley interference** from **Below** breaches adds **20–90 minutes** without explanation.
- **Divination** "predictions" on the **Jamaica** board are right **often enough** to keep gamblers betting, wrong **often enough** to keep bookies employed.
- **Ashford** lawyers bill by the hour when a **commuter bond** hearing needs a **human** witness because the **calculating engine** drifted to **666** again.

**Barry beat:** *"When's the train?" "Two-fifteen." "It's two-thirty." "Then it's late." "The board says two-fifteen." "The board's a liar. So's the conductor. Get a coffee."*

### 3.5 Failure modes (use at table)

| Failure | Cause | Street rumor |
|---------|-------|--------------|
| **Ghost schedule** | Ley stake knocked crooked | Train arrives **twice**; second one empty |
| **Feast-day stall** | Wrong patron in boiler | Passengers stuck **between stations**; salon vendors walk the cars |
| **Loader scab** | Bootleg **core** on freight | Car **unhooks itself** at Red Hook; union throws bricks |
| **Ward misroute** | Forged manifest sigil | Chen-Okafor **reagents** end up in **Moretti** funeral ice |
| **Conductor recall** | Emergency scroll used | Train stops **hard**; one passenger remembers a **past life** (side effect) |

## 4. Industrialists vs five families

The **Commission** runs **vice and turf**. **Industrialists** run **infrastructure** — and rent infrastructure to everyone else.

| Power type | Wants | How families touch them |
|------------|-------|-------------------------|
| **Pemberton board** | Bond votes, **Green Desk** quiet, low union wage | Ashford **contracts**; Valenti **freight** kickbacks |
| **Harlan foundry** | **Core** licenses, FSA looking elsewhere | Valenti **night loaders**; Kowalski **scrap** disposal |
| **Vandermeer terminal** | Speaking-tube **monopoly**, commuter **fares** | Ashford **splice**; Chen-Okafor **arcade** numbers |
| **Parish donors on rail bonds** | Terror headlines before votes | Moretti **spirit sweeps** as PR; druid cells as **convenient** enemy |

**Not every industrialist is Ashford.** Harlan is **dwarf-guild** old money; Pemberton is **human** robber-baron with a **philanthropist** wing. Families **bribe**, **marry**, and **threaten** — they rarely **own** the trunk line outright.

## 5. Agent invention gate

1. **Is it magitech\*?** → Spell/sigil/patronage first. See `SETTING-SPELL-TECH-TREE.md` §2.
2. **Is it rail?** → Name **owner syndicate**, **route**, **failure mode**, **schedule chaos** — not a perfect timetable.
3. **Is it computation?** → **Weak.** Clerks, divination, drift. `SETTING-CAPABILITY-ASYMMETRY.md`.
4. **Is it automata?** → Loader **scab** today, **existential** headline tomorrow. Distinguish **animate objects** from **autonomous core**.
5. **VOID check** — if it sounds like 1931 science-magitech or gunpowder industrialism, stop.
