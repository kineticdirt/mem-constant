# NYC Mafia × D&D (working title)

Urban fantasy TTRPG: **New York organized crime** (families, crews, rackets) where **D&D-style magic** is **spell-tech** — licensed, taxed, or bootleg. Culture reads **1950s–1960s** (jazz, vice, nightlife, Jersey adjacency); street technology is **late-1800s** brass/steam/guild craft. Automata are an existential threat. The mob runs the grey market for healing bands, ward keys, construct cores, and consultants who aren't quite clerics.

Crime drama + jazz-club noir + tabletop fantasy: speaking-tube wiretaps and warded safes; goblin Ledger couriers; a don who owns a legitimate front and an illegitimate salon. Serious stakes; violence possible; human neighborhood voice first; fantasy ancestries as diasporas. Fiction first — no real-world gang glorification.
