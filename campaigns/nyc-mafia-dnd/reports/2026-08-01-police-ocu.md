## OCU -- Arcane Division

Housed at the **1st Precinct Annex** (Broadway and Stone Street) -- converted bank, warded evidence vaults in the old safe-deposit basement. Task force under Organized Crime Control, not a standalone precinct.

### Chain of command

| Rank | Officer | Role |
|------|---------|------|
| **Captain** | Miriam Kostas | Division commander. 28-year veteran. Reads people and paper; doesn't cast. |
| **Lieutenant** | Marcus Diaz | Day-watch. Ex-narcotics. Informants and warrants. Cynical, effective. |
| **Sergeant** | Elena Voss | Forensics lead. Guild-certified forensic mage who went blue. Arcane Trace Unit (*detect magic* at-will). |
| **Detective** | Adaeze Chen-Okafor | Family grenade -- see `2026-08-01-npc-roster.md`. |
| **Detective** | Frankie O'Shea | Fifteen years. Ward-glyph wedding ring. Kowalski waste beat. Trusts gut over glamours. |
| **Detective** | Reynaldo "Ray" Santos | Ex-Vice. Ambitious. Midtown/Ashford cases. Speaks Infernal from dropped seminary. |
| **Forensic mage** | Dr. Lena Marchetti | Civilian contractor. Lab testimony that survives defense. Brother is a Moretti capo -- she doesn't discuss it over coffee. |
| **Forensic mage** | Kai Nakamura | Night shift. Fast, green. Father runs a Flushing divination kiosk. |

### Resources

- ~14 detectives, 3 forensic mages, 1 clerk on **guild speaking-tube** feeds (not modern networks), 2 uniforms on the vault.
- 4 unmarked sedans (same grey make), 1 evidence van with mobile ward / anti-scry lining.
- Vault: two warded doors; badge + guild cert + written order. Necromancy taint trips salt-line alarm.
- Budget: always short. Informant cash and overtime come from Kostas begging discretionary funds after the brass finishes their steaks.

**Street kit:** Fedoras, revolvers, ward-sniffer charms on chains, tube-splice warrants that leak.

## Federal magical crime desk (SDNY)

Housed Foley Square -- locked elevator, no exterior signage. Answers to the U.S. Attorney, not NYPD. **Audit vs seizure** structure may mirror older drafts; **do not** name OTR/BCC in play prose (B3).

| Role | Name | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| Lead AUSA | Andrea Pierce | Never lost an arcane conspiracy case. *Protection from evil* charm in the briefcase. |
| AUSA | Tom Nguyen | Grand jury paper; chain-of-custody for magical exhibits. |
| Contract diviner | Selene Ashworth | Reads scry residue; backlog measured in weeks. |
| FBI liaison | Derek Cross | Believes in evidence; treats infernal contracts as "mob spook stories" until they aren't. |

### City vs federal

| | OCU | Federal desk |
|--|-----|--------------|
| Trigger | Patrol, tip, guild referral | Multi-ward pattern, conspiracy paper, interstate |
| Tools | Surveillance, informants, tube warrants | Federal paper, contract diviners, forensic accountants |
| Weakness | Underfunded, compromised certifiers | Slow; one diviner for the office |

They are **not allies**. Case poaching, re-certification fights, informant jealousy. Kostas stopped sharing her board after the second lift.
