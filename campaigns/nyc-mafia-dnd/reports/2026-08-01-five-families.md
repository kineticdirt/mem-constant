# The Five Families — 1950s–1960s Underworld Roster

*Status: Canonical · Locked GM Decision · Supersedes all prior family reports*

---

## 1. Valenti Family (Brooklyn Docks)

- **Territory:** Red Hook, Sunset Park, and Gowanus.
- **HQ:** The Longshoremen's Union Hall (Pier 7, Red Hook). A brick building smelling of wet rope, coal smoke, and stale coffee. Paired brass speaking-tubes connect to every dock manager's office.
- **Rackets:** Forged ward stamps to clear imported artificer crates; smuggling unlicensed construct cores; operating illegal construct loaders on night shifts; manufacturing brass knuckles with shock runes.
- **Voice & Style:** Dock Irish and Italian longshoremen. Work coats over flannel shirts, St. Michael medals, and flat caps.

### Key Figures
- **Lena "Grease" Valenti (Boss):** A tough, fifty-year-old widow who runs the Red Hook piers with an iron fist. She wears a heavy wool work coat and a silver St. Michael medal. Her thumb glows with a faint blue light when she signs forged shipping invoices. Her kids attend St. Bridget's Parochial School; she still makes Communion every Sunday. Her current pressure is the Organized Crime Unit (OCU) sniffing around Pier 7, which threatens her family's legitimate front.
- **Lt. Rosa "The Crane" Lombardo (Capo):** Leads a crew of forty longshoremen, half of whom carry warded crowbars that can pry open magical crates without triggering alarms. She carries a heavy cargo hook that sheds a dim blue light. Her daughter is a student at St. Bridget's in Bay Ridge. Rosa knows exactly which Jersey artificer bound the hot ledger currently sitting in the cellar of The Blue Note.

## 2. Moretti Family (Little Italy)

- **Territory:** Mulberry Street, Greenwich Village, and the Lower East Side.
- **HQ:** The San Gennaro Social Club (Mulberry Street). Smells of floor wax, cigar smoke, and espresso. The basement houses the family's burial registry vault, containing bound ledgers of the dead.
- **Rackets:** Protection disguised as parish charity; Thursday "spirit sweeps" for local businesses; controlling the burial registry and funeral-home network to manipulate what the dead can say in court.
- **Voice & Style:** Sharp suits, Roman collars (often unordained), flat caps, and the scent of church incense mixed with cigarette smoke.

### Key Figures
- **Tommy "Two Prayers" Moretti (Capo):** An apprentice chaplain who drives a warded 1954 Buick sedan with a speaking-tube splice hidden under the dashboard. He manages the family's protection racket in Greenwich Village. Tommy wears a Roman collar but has never been ordained. He genuinely believes the parish rites, which makes him both a useful mediator and a dangerous fanatic.
- **Sal "The Sacristy" D'Ambrosio (Consigliere):** An elderly, soft-spoken advisor who smells of old parchment. He carries a silver rosary that warms in his hand when someone lies within ten feet of him. Sal forged his own ordination papers forty years ago to build the family's parish network. His hatred for the Kowalski family's crude corpse-disposal methods is deeply theological, not just business.

## 3. Chen-Okafor Family (Queens Numbers)

- **Territory:** Flushing, Jamaica, and numbers routes stretching into Harlem and East Harlem.
- **HQ:** The Golden Dragon Mahjong Club (Main Street, Flushing). A storefront that looks like a community lending office. The back room is filled with the clatter of mahjong tiles; the upstairs houses the family's divination rooms.
- **Rackets:** Illegal numbers rackets; probability manipulation on horse races and dice games; high-interest lending using jade-focus contracts; numbers runners using divination magic to predict winning combinations.
- **Voice & Style:** Flushing shop-front English, Harlem jazz slang, and Puerto Rican neighborhood banter. Sharp sharkskin suits, jade rings, and ivory mahjong tiles.

### Key Figures
- **David Chen-Okafor (Boss):** The respectable face of the family's community lending business. He always carries a heavy ivory die in his pocket that he never rolls. David is currently caught in a quiet succession fight with his traditionalist uncles. He needs to send an in-person message to a Valenti skipper—a message too hot for the speaking-tubes. His sister, Adaeze, wears an OCU badge; they have a strict agreement to never ask for favors or file reports on each other.
- **"Iron" Ling (Enforcer):** A dwarf enforcer with a steel prosthetic forearm that can block warded weapons. He is always seen reading the *Racing Form* on stakeouts. Ling has a legendary quarterly collection bet with the Kowalski family's enforcer, "Gutter," which they settle at a Long Island City tavern where the piano player never stops.

## 4. Kowalski Family (Staten Island Haul)

- **Territory:** Staten Island, Fresh Kills Landfill, and the harbor barge routes.
- **HQ:** The Bard Avenue Row House (Staten Island). A modest home with a basement door that is never opened. The backyard is a staging area for sanitation trucks; a speaking-tube hub connects to the Fresh Kills dispatch office.
- **Rackets:** Waste hauling; deep disposal of bodies, hot construct cores, and cursed artifacts; smuggling contraband across the harbor via garbage barges; maintaining the "Bone Reach" connection to the Below.
- **Voice & Style:** Sanitation uniforms, Polish parish rosaries, heavy work boots, and the smell of diesel fuel and river salt.

### Key Figures
- **Dominika "Docks" Kowalski (Boss):** A stern, middle-aged woman who carries a silver rosary and a scroll of *gentle repose*. She manages the Fresh Kills landfill. Dominika is terrified that unauthorized corpses in her landfill will trigger a federal audit, stripping her family of their lucrative municipal sanitation license. She refers to necromancy as "waste logistics" and hates the Moretti family's religious interference.
- **"Gutter" (Enforcer):** A half-orc night crew boss who carries a warded iron wrench and keeps a pigeon coop on the roof of the Staten Island waste station. He uses homing pigeons to carry schedules because the family's speaking-tubes are constantly spliced by rivals. Gutter is plagued by nightmares of the Bone Reach, making him a potential leak or a desperate recruit.

## 5. Ashford Family (Midtown Laundry)

- **Territory:** Midtown Manhattan, Madison Avenue, and the corporate offices of Wall Street.
- **HQ:** The Ashford Building (Madison Avenue). A limestone tower with a marble lobby. The family operates behind legitimate Delaware shell corporations and "consulting" firms.
- **Rackets:** Money laundering; warlock pacts disguised as corporate retainers; operating listening posts on the city's speaking-tube network; brokering high-level magical contracts for the Commission.
- **Voice & Style:** Bespoke wool suits, silk ties, squash-club etiquette, and polite denial.

### Key Figures
- **Julian Ashford (Boss):** A wealthy, elegant warlock who spends his afternoons at the Midtown Squash Club. He speaks with a soft, cultured accent and never raises his voice, even when ordering a hit. His private office features a portrait with eyes that follow visitors. Julian believes that every transaction is a contract, and every contract has a price paid in future favors.
- **"Quill" (Enforcer):** A fiend-pact enforcer with amber eyes and a shadow that seems to fidget even when he stands still. He wears a tailored suit over his scarred body. Quill fights to disable rather than kill, because "dead men cannot sign contracts." He has a fierce, professional rivalry with other independent ward-breakers in the city.

## 6. The Commission Heat Map

The quarterly sit-down of the five families is held in a private dining room at the Ashford-owned Waldorf Hotel. The current alliances and grudges shape the city's streets:

- **Valenti ↔ Chen-Okafor (Transactional Alliance):** Valenti clears Chen-Okafor's imported jade foci through the docks; Chen-Okafor finances Valenti's construct-loader foundries.
- **Moretti ↔ Chen-Okafor (Active Turf War):** A bloody conflict over the lucrative numbers routes in the Lower East Side.
- **Kowalski ↔ Moretti (Theological Grudge):** Moretti chaplains accuse the Kowalskis of desecrating graves; the Kowalskis accuse the Morettis of using ghosts to spy on their waste routes.
- **Valenti ↔ Ashford (Secret Rivalry):** Both families want control of the speaking-tube hub under The Blue Note, which contains splices to three different family networks.
- **Kowalski ↔ Valenti (Poaching Dispute):** A dispute over harbor smuggling routes and who has the right to dump construct scrap in the Jersey landfills.
