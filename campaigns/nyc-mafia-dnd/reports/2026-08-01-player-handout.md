## The Setup

**New York -- culture reads 1950s–1960s; street tech is late-1800s guild brass and steam.** Magic is real, licensed, taxed, or illegal. The Five Families run the grey market: bootleg healing bands, forged ward stamps, construct cores, chaplain rites, pact "consulting." The OCU investigates unlicensed magical crime. Audit and seizure desks press from above (names still settling). Between them sits the **Commission**.

You're **family-adjacent operators** -- fixers, muscle, club musicians, chaplain-runners -- not made men yet. The job comes through the **Ledger**.

Fit the role; class is flexible.

2. **Enforcer** -- breach, intimidate, shock knucks; no Midtown fireworks

4. **Chaplain-adjacent** -- wakes, binds, gentle repose  
5. **Driver / hauler** -- bridges, landfill gates, iron bells  
6. **Mage-for-hire** -- Ashford cover, scroll thief, scrap artificer  
7. **Numbers runner** -- Flushing offices, loaded ethics  
8. **Ward-breaker / Ghost** -- tenements, chalk circles, never here

## The Job: "The Blue Note Ledger"

**Employer:** The Ledger (neutral)  
**Pay:** Cash up front + a favor token (family TBD on delivery)  
**Shape:** Recover a **bound ledger** from the back room of **The Blue Note**, a licensed jazz club on West 52nd. The book records bootleg healing bands and construct shift payments -- not cash alone.

**Front:** Band, cigarette smoke, horn solos, polite denial.  
**Back:** Salon ward, tube splice, cellar with warded ice box.  
**Complications you may hear:** Owner blames Chen-Okafor muscle; Valenti says Moretti planted a ghost in a horn case; Kowalski truck idling in the service alley; OCU ward-sniffer on the beat.

**Twist seeds (GM reveals in play):** Parallel Ashford contract; ghost that hates being read; unlicensed clockwork bartender rumor in the cellar (automata foreshadow).
