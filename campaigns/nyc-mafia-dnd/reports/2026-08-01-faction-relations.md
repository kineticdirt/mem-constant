## How to read

Allies, grudges, debts. "X → Y" means X owes Y. "X ↔ Y" is mutual. The Commission is rules, not a sixth family. Heat travels by **ward cluster**, not borough slogans (G-1).

## Law

### OCU

Mandate: unlicensed magical crime. Every family is inventory. Needs **guild/audit certification** for evidence -- corrupt liaisons make cases soft. Det. Adaeze Chen-Okafor is a live grenade both sides pretend is a letter opener.

### Audit desk / Seizure desk (B3 draft names)

Audit taxes licenses and salon permits. Seizure raids cores and bootleg rituals. **Do not** use OTR/BCC in new prose. Ashford prefers audit capture; dock families fear seizure vans with salt lines.
