## Valenti

### Lt. Rosa "The Crane" Lombardo -- dock captain

Harbor enforcer. Reports to Lena. Longshore coat, close-cropped grey hair, missing finger joint from a '40s docking accident. Thermos of coffee laced with *enhance ability* -- shared on heavy lifts under steam-crane light. Fights with a warded cargo hook; never alone. Pressure: daughter's Bay Ridge scholarship. Knows the artificer who bound the Blue Note book.

### "Sully" Sullivan -- dock enforcer

Rosa's right hand. Broken face, stained jacket, *shillelagh*-taped lead pipe. Quiet; never learned another trade. Leads 3–4 dockhands in a cargo-yard shakedown; surrenders at half HP.

### Gina "Nails" Palladino -- cleaner / arson

Wiry, scarred hands, roll-your-owns, arcane lighter (*produce flame*). Cargo-only fires -- won't torch a civilian tenement. *Create bonfire* and alchemist's fire from range; lights her own fuse if cornered and runs through the smoke toward the river.

## Moretti

### Lt. Sal "The Sacristy" D'Ambrosio -- consigliere

Black suit, almost-collar, incense and ledger dust. Rosary glows on lies. Believes Kowalski disposal is blasphemy. Forged ordination papers = career kill-switch. Knows every burial site and which capo ordered a deliberate haunting as theater.

### "Two-Bit" Marconi -- street enforcer

Flat cap, scarred right hand, limp from an old revolver wound. Breaks fingers on a schedule. Mundane debt ledger in his coat. Street fight outside a Mulberry restaurant; pocket sand, civilian shields, dirty as a closing-time alley.

### Francesca Moretti -- ambitious niece

East Village crew of five: gambling, protection, diluted healing bands sold to college bars that still book live combos. Saint-medal earring, concealed revolver, *thaumaturgy* voice for boom commands -- **no flip phone**. Wants LES expansion into Chen-Okafor numbers. Recurring rival/ally: respects competence, surrenders rather than die for pride.

## Chen-Okafor

### Lt. David Chen-Okafor -- numbers manager

Adaeze's brother. Navy suits, jade ring (hostile-intent whisper within 30 feet). Portfolio, not a gun. Fragile silence with his sister. Knows loan lists, Canal Street Below access schedule (foreshadow), which Ashford warlock is sniffing real estate.

### Kaz -- Moretti defector

Nervous, track pants, brass knucks he won't swap (luck). Runs Chen-Okafor's LES push. Does not fight -- runs, then sings for protection. Liability with legs.

### "Iron" Ling -- dwarf muscle

Silver-touched beard braided with copper wire, steel prosthetic forearm, canvas coat over gambeson. Stoic; black tea, three sugars; Racing Form. Quarterly bet with Gutter. Brute fight; dwarven resilience; retreats only on order.

## Kowalski

### Lt. Marta Kowalski -- waste captain

Coveralls, high-vis vest (+1 AC work garment), clipboard that is a *speak with dead* focus (one question/day). Pragmatic: river bodies surface; Bone Reach bodies don't. Knows Staten schedules, Stapleton tunnel, Midtown skeleton leverage Ashford wants. Pressure: son in Sanitation -- audit burns him too.

### "Gutter" -- half-orc disposal

6'4", butcher apron, warded wrench (maul). Relentless endurance once/day. Doesn't chase retreats -- job is disposal. Pigeon coop for tube-proof messages. Recruitable if nightmares of the Reach get worse.

### Jimmy "The Leash" O'Donnell -- Below liaison

Gaunt, long coat in summer, black fingernails, jar of teeth on the desk. Speaks Infernal with an affected accent. *Toll the dead*, *chill touch*, *misty step*; flees into pre-wired Below routes. Only Kowalski who discusses the Below aloud at a wake.

## Ashford

### Lt. Julian Cross -- operations

"Cross Consulting" cards, rimless glasses, never raises his voice over lobby piano. Placed the parallel contract on the Blue Note ledger. Knows every active pact, three audit-desk clerks on retainer, stalled Midtown dig. Pressure: daughter who doesn't know; Murray Hill apartment under another name.

### "Quill" -- warlock enforcer

Black suit, no tie, amber vertical pupils, independent shadow. *Eldritch blast*, *hex*, *darkness*, *misty step*. Raven familiar with one glass eye (scry). Disables, doesn't kill. Retreats at 50% to report.

### Delia Park -- forger / laundry analyst

Jeans, cardigan, satchel -- looks like a Midtown clerk because she is one. Forged statements, shell companies, encyclopedic numbers memory. Gambling debt held through a Valenti front (bought from Two-Bit's shark book). Flip bait: offer an exit or a better cut. **No Venmo** -- cash envelopes and shell invoices only.

## Neutral / rivals

### "Mouth" -- goblin Ledger courier

Yellow courier vest three sizes too big, notched ear, talks through every set break. Sells the same tip twice. Find him at mahjong, all-night diners, Below mouths. Remembers prices, forgets faces.

### The Echo -- Below hazard

See `2026-08-01-the-below.md`. Swarm of residual dead; psychic scrapes; helps or hinders by mood.
