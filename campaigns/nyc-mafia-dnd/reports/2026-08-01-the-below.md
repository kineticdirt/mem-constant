# The Below — NYC's Magical Undercity

*Status: Canonical · Locked GM Decision · Mid-Campaign Foreshadowing Guide*

---

## 1. What is the Below?

The Below is not a literal hell, nor is it a simple network of subway tunnels. It is a **collapsed demiplane folded into the city's bedrock** during the massive water works and transit excavations of the nineteenth century. 

- **The Geometry:** Space inside the Below is non-Euclidean. A traveler can walk down a narrow brick corridor for four blocks in Brooklyn and exit through a storm drain in Canal Street, Manhattan.
- **The Atmosphere:** It is a dark, damp, echoing labyrinth of brick arches, rusted iron pipes, and abandoned steam chambers. The air smells of stagnant water, wet coal, and ozone.
- **The Law:** Surface laws do not exist in the Below. There are no OCU patrols or federal auditors. However, casting loud or flashy spells near the access grates will ring the guild seismographs on the surface, bringing immediate heat to the entrance.

## 2. Known Access Points

The Five Families guard and control the few stable entrances to the Below.

| Access Point | Location | Controlling Family | Operational Use |
|--------------|----------|--------------------|-----------------|
| **Court Street Station** | Brooklyn | **Valenti** | An abandoned subway platform. The Valentis use it to store smuggled cargo, hot construct cores, and warded crates that cannot see daylight. |
| **Canal Street Overflow** | Manhattan | **Chen-Okafor** | A massive storm junction. Chen-Okafor diviners maintain a checkpoint here to monitor Below traffic and spy on rival family movements. |
| **Stapleton Water Tunnel** | Staten Island | **Kowalski** | An 1870s water main. The Kowalskis use this tunnel for deep corpse disposal, dumping bodies into the deepest trenches of the **Bone Reach**. |
| **The Dead Stop** | Midtown (42nd St) | **Sealed (FSA)** | A massive iron vault door under Grand Central Terminal, sealed with seven federal wards. Marked: "NO ENTRY — HIGH VOLTAGE." Nobody opens it—yet. |

## 3. What Lives in the Bedrock?

The Below is home to strange, magical entities and hazards that have adapted to the dark.

### A. Drift-Rats
- **The Threat:** Six-legged, pale, cat-sized rodents that hunt in packs. They do not eat flesh; they are attracted to residual magic. They will swarm an operator to bite and drain active magical foci or bootleg elixirs.
- **The Repellent:** Operators carry **rowan-chalk** (a mixture of silver powder, rowan-wood ash, and stale bread). Drawing a chalk line on the floor keeps drift-rats at bay.

### B. The Echo
- **The Threat:** The collective, overlapping memories of dead transit workers, missing persons, and mob disposal victims. The Echo manifests as whispering voices in the pipes or shifting shadows on the brickwork.
- **The Interaction:** The Echo is not hostile, but it is disorienting. It speaks in scraps of 1920s slang and old transit schedules. It can guide travelers to safe exits or lead them into dead ends depending on its mood. It responds to minor offerings (a copper coin, a pinch of tobacco, or the mention of a dead person's name).

### C. The Sleeper in the Hollow
- **The Threat:** A massive, dormant entity embedded in the bedrock beneath the Brooklyn docks. It does not move or speak, but its presence causes a low, rhythmic vibration in the stone.
- **The Interaction:** The Valenti family maintains a quiet relationship with the Sleeper. They dump organic dock waste and warded scrap near its hollow; in return, the Sleeper occasionally returns warded objects wrapped in a protective, dried muscle-like membrane.

### D. The Chattering (Bone Reach Swarm)
- **The Threat:** A swarm of minor, toothy elementals that inhabit the Kowalski corpse-disposal pits in the Bone Reach. They speak in a constant, high-pitched clicking noise.
- **The Interaction:** The Kowalskis trade fresh bones and metal scrap to the Chattering in exchange for secrets and rumors whispered by the dead before they were dissolved.

## 4. Fringe Undead & Lycanthropes (Below Margins)

**LOCK (B18):** Vampires and werewolves are **not** mainstream citizens — but the Below is where **fringe** predators **actually** **live** when the surface gets **hot**.

| Presence | Where | Family / institution angle |
|----------|-------|----------------------------|
| **Platform crypts** | Sealed **IRT** side tunnels east of Court Street | Valentis **rent** **crypt** **shelves** — **$200**/month **cold** **storage** |
| **Bone Reach feeders** | Kowalski disposal pits | **Lycan** **scavenger** crews — **trade** **bones** for **Below** **passage** |
| **Night-market spillover** | Storm drains under Orchard St | **Blood** **brokers** **dump** **failed** **product** — **drift-rats** **compete** |
| **Sleeper hollow** | Brooklyn dock bedrock | **Ancient** **thing** — **not** a **vampire** — **attracts** **fringe** **cults** |

**Surface rule:** A **vampire** in Midtown is **polite denial**; a **vampire** in the Below is **a** **tenant** with **a** **key** and **a** **debt**.

**Repellents (street):** **Garlic** **braids** ($0.10); **silver** **chalk** ($0.25); **rowan** line — same as drift-rats (`§3.A`).

## 5. Non-Combat Encounter Table (d6)

When operators venture into the Below, use this table to generate atmospheric encounters:

| Die | Encounter | Details |
|-----|-----------|---------|
| **1** | **The Echo's Trade** | The voices in the pipes offer to guide the party to a safe exit or a forgotten Valenti stash, but they demand **the full name of a living person** in exchange. |
| **2** | **Drift-Rat Nest** | A pack of twelve drift-rats blocks the corridor ahead. They are sniffing the air, attracted to the PCs' magical foci or elixirs. They can be bribed with a minor scroll or fought. |
| **3** | **Chattering Scouts** | Three clicking, toothy elementals emerge from a drain pipe. They offer to trade a rumor about the Ashford family's listening posts in exchange for a warded bone or a silver coin. |
| **4** | **The Grate Above** | The PCs hear footsteps and muffled jazz music directly above them. A street grate opens, and someone drops a half-smoked cigarette and a crumpled betting slip through the bars. |
| **5** | **The Drift Shift** | The geometry of the tunnel shifts silently. The exit the PCs were heading toward is now two hundred yards to the left, blocked by a newly formed brick wall. Requires a DC 12 Perception check to find a detour. |
| **6** | **Sleeper's Curiosity** | The low vibration in the stone intensifies. Every PC must make a DC 10 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, they feel an overwhelming urge to leave their most valuable non-magical item on the floor as an offering. |
