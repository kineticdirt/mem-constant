# Law and Magic — Enforcement, Evidence, and Desks

*Status: Canonical · Locked GM Decision · Legal Framework Guide*

---

## 1. The Organized Crime Unit (OCU)

The OCU is a specialized division of the New York Police Department, established in 1952 to combat the rising tide of magical rackets and unlicensed construct smuggling.

- **The Detectives:** OCU detectives are hard-nosed, magic-aware cops. They wear traditional fedoras, wool trench coats, and carry standard Colt .38 revolvers. They do not cast spells, but they know how to spot them.
- **The Gear:** Every OCU patrol car carries a **ward-sniffer**—a brass pocket watch containing a tiny, captured elemental spirit that vibrates and glows faint red when within thirty feet of active magic or fresh ward residue.
- **The Wiretaps:** Wiretapping is done by physically splicing into the city's brass speaking-tube network. Detectives use wax cylinder recorders to capture conversations. Splicing requires a warrant signed by a Midtown judge, though corrupt cops frequently leak or forge these recordings.

## 3. Underworld Evidence Tiers

In metropolitan courts, magical evidence is admissible under strict federal guidelines. **Machine testimony is not** — brass calculating engines are folklore, not witnesses. The families have developed specific methods to beat or bypass each tier of evidence.

| Evidence Type | Legal Weight in Court | How Families Beat It |
|---------------|-----------------------|----------------------|
| **Ward Residue** | **Strong.** Scrapings of glowing dust left by a spell can be matched to a specific caster's magical signature. | **The Clean-Up:** Families hire independent ward-breakers to erase residue using silver chalk and rowan-wood vinegar before the OCU arrives. |
| **Tube Recording** | **Very Strong.** A wax cylinder recording of a speaking-tube conversation is damning if the federal seal is intact. | **The Splice-Out:** Ashford lawyers challenge the legality of the warrant, or pay a corrupt clerk to "accidentally" drop the wax cylinder on a radiator. |
| **Bound Ghost Witness** | **Messy.** A priest can summon a murder victim's ghost to answer one question. | **The Silence:** Moretti chaplains use *gentle repose* or cast *silence* over the corpse, claiming the deceased's soul has already been "consigned to the parish registry." |
| **Construct Core** | **Automatic Conviction.** Possession of an unlicensed, autonomous construct core is a federal crime. | **The Melt:** Kowalski disposal crews drop hot cores into acid baths or dump them into the deepest trenches of the Staten Island landfills. |
| **Bound-Light Plate / Scry Still** | **Very Strong** (original plate + guild seal). **Projection fetch** weaker in court — clerk chain matters. | **The Swap:** Wrong glyph in print; **shatter** plate; **living-ink** forgery; rare **image-deny** smear. Full tiers: `SETTING-SCRY-PRESS.md` §4. |

### Computation & Ledgers (Weak Lane)

Guild **calculating engines** and temple **augury engines** are expensive, unreliable, and **inadmissible** without a sworn operator. Families keep **human bookkeepers**; Chen-Okafor rooms use **divination** to cheat, not to close honest books. If a plot needs "the numbers," default to **clerical error, bribery, or divination burnout** — not a working supercomputer (`SETTING-CAPABILITY-ASYMMETRY.md` §4).

## 4. Underworld Rules & The Felony Stack

The Commission enforces a strict set of "street laws" to keep federal heat from burning the entire city:

- **No Civilian Magic Hits:** Using magic to assassinate a non-underworld civilian is strictly forbidden. It brings too many FSA agents into the wards.
- **OCU Heat Pooling:** If a family's reckless operation brings too much police pressure, the other families will vote to "pool" their resources to bribe the precinct captain or force the offending family to shut down the operation.
- **The Felony Stack:** If an independent operator is arrested by the OCU, they face a cumulative stack of charges:
  - Possession of an unregistered focus: **Class C Misdemeanor** ($50 fine).
  - Possession of unlicensed magical tools (silver chalk, warded lockpicks): **Class B Felony** (2–5 years).
  - Possession of speaking-tube splicing tools: **Class A Felony** (5–10 years, no parole).
  - Operating an unlicensed construct: **Class A Felony** (10–20 years).

## 5. Druid Designation & Green Desk (B18)

**LOCK:** Active **druid** and **land-cult** networks are **framed** as **communist terrorists** in federal and city propaganda. The frame is **useful** — and **corrupt**.

### Who says "terrorist"

| Voice | What they gain |
|-------|----------------|
| **FSA Green Desk** | Budget, **construct-site** **jurisdiction**, **seizure** **warrants** on **rural** **depots** |
| **Guild coal & timber donors** | **Clear-cut** **leases**; **fewer** **glyph** **fires** on **survey** **stakes** |
| **Commission families** | FSA **busy** in **Jersey** → **less** **heat** on **Manhattan** **cores** |
| **Respectable PCL donors** | **Tax** **write-offs**; **distance** from **Green Cord** **cells** |

### Who gets hurt

- **Tenant farmers** on **commuter** **sprawl** **lines** — **evicted** when **"terror"** **designation** **expands** a **buffer** **zone**.
- **Human** **union** **sympathizers** who **shared** **water** **rights** **intel** — **OCU Rural Liaison** (three detectives for all counties north of the Bronx) **closes** **cases** as **"outside** **jurisdiction."**

### Enforcement play

- **Green Cord sabotage** (steam main, glyph fire) → **Green Desk** + **Seizure** **joint** raid — **same** **evidence** tiers as **§3** (ward residue, tube recordings).
- **OCU** does **not** lead **Palisades** **raids** unless a **body** **surfaces** in **the** **Five** **Boroughs**.
- **Corruption hook:** A **newspaper** **terror** **headline** the **week** **before** a **foundry** **bond** **vote** — **Ashford** **lawyer** **files** **FAB** **objections**; **nobody** **mentions** **timber**.
