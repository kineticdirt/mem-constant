## The Blue Note (West 52nd Street)

The Blue Note is a premier jazz club in Midtown Manhattan operating under a **Licensed Visibility Tier** (B2). The front is a respectable establishment; the back and cellar house an active, illicit spell-tech smuggling hub.


### Key Figures & Operations
- **Artie Shaw (Club Owner):** A nervous human who operates the front but owes favors to the Chen-Okafor family. His speaking-tubes splice directly to family capos, bypassing OCU wiretap scans.
- **"Big Mike" (Doorman):** A half-orc enforcer who guards the stage door. He represents the typical cross-ancestry labor hired for front-of-house security in Midtown (B1c/C3).
- **The Clockwork Bartender:** In the cellar, an unlicensed, unregistered clockwork construct (CR 2) washes glasses. It runs on a crude spirit core.

### The Ledger Contraband
The club's safe holds records of **unlicensed construct core deliveries** and **bootleg healing band transactions** involving the Valenti, Moretti, and Chen-Okafor families. It is secured with a **Locksmiths' Guild Ward**, requiring silver chalk and rowan-wood wedges to bypass without triggering the Ashford warlock listening posts.
