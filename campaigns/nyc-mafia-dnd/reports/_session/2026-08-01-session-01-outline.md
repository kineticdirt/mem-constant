## Underworld Operations

This document preserves the concrete transit routes and heist mechanics used by family-adjacent operators (B8) when moving contraband across the borough choke points.

### 1. The Hoboken-Midtown Transit Corridor
Contraband (especially bootleg healing bands and unlicensed cores) is routinely smuggled from the New Jersey rail yards into Manhattan via the Hoboken-Midtown corridor.
- **The Briefing Hub:** **The Iron Rail Diner** (Hoboken, NJ). A mundane, working-class commuter diner thick with coal soot and coffee steam. Mickey "The Pen" Vance (a Ledger representative) runs operations from here.
- **The Harbor Checkpoints:** Federal Seizure Agency (FSA) and OCU detectives patrol the Hudson ferry lines with ward-sniffers. Operators must use lead-lined instrument cases or warded carriages to mask the magical signatures of active foci.

### 2. Physical & Magical Complications
- **The OCU Patrols:** Organized Crime Unit cruisers are equipped with ward-sniffers (brass pocket-watch frames that vibrate near active magic).
- **Valenti Longshore Crews:** Led by figures like Rosa "The Crane" Lombardo, these crews use warded crowbars to breach rival safes and seize cargo before the feds arrive.
- **The Alarm Backlash:** Black-market ledger safes are frequently bound with *alarm* and *illusory script* wards. Bypassing them requires specialized ward-breakers using silver chalk and rowan wood.
- **Construct Glitches:** Unlicensed dock loaders and clockwork workers (like the glass-washer at The Blue Note) operate on raw spirit cores. When exposed to active spellcasting, they can perceive the magical signatures as a hostile threat and attack.
