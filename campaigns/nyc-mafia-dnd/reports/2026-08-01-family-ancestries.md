## The question

Are the Five Families human-only, or do dwarven capos, goblin runners, and tiefling fixers work the grey market?

## Answer: Mixed crews, human Commission face

**Dons present human** to politicians, parish boards, and licensing desks. Beneath that, every family runs mixed crews. Ancestries are **diasporas and skill**, not 1:1 stand-ins for Italian/Irish/Jewish/Black/Puerto Rican/Chinese neighborhood voice. Those human stereotypes are the **playable speech layer** (B1c). Fantasy folk are additional communities under the same ward rules.

### Why mixed

1. **The life is mercilessly practical.** A goblin who fits a tube chase beats a louder man who doesn't. A dwarf who smells forged stamps is payroll.
2. **Spell-tech tools differ by body.** Dragonborn heat resistance on a foundry job; elven darkvision in the Below; tiefling presence for pact rooms that look like consulting.
3. **Licenses track operators, not races.** Bureaucracy protects mixed crews until someone makes it political at a Commission sit-down.

### Table meaning

- PC ancestries wide open; old-school human capo muttering is **tension**, not a wall.
- Human-only crews look suspicious (what are they hiding?).
- Sit-downs keep the human mask because it is useful -- everyone knows Capo's best collector is a goblin and Ashford's "Continental" is horned.

### Hooks

- Human-only Moretti crew making noise -- shakedown fuel.
- Goblin runner caught with a Valenti message in Flushing -- accident or double?
- Commission float of a "majority-human sit-down" rule -- three families lose voting weight overnight; jazz clubs still open either way.
