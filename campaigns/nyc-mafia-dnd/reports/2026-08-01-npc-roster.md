## The Ledger -- independent fixer network

Couriers, cleaners, information brokers across all five families. No loyalty -- contracts only. Jobs go in a physical black book; payment is anonymous cash drop behind a club or pier.

**Contact method:** A **speaking-tube** in a licensed salon rings twice, then silence. The crew answers from a different tube pair. A voice gives a time and a place. No names. (Payphones and burner flip-phones are **void**.)

---

## Mickey "The Needle" Fusco -- Sea Wolf Tattoo, Red Hook

**Role:** Information broker / tattoo artist (ward-glyph specialist)  
**Age:** 54  
**Look:** Wiry, bald, left arm ends at the elbow in a **brass-and-spring prosthetic** that ticks in quiet rooms. Stained white apron over a sleeveless work vest. Silver reading glasses. Smokes like the city owes him air.

**Personality:** Nervous, talks to himself over the needle buzz. His ward-glyph tattoos hold six months if the client stays out of Midtown polite-denial wards that scrub ink.

**Crew hook:** Ledger dead-drop. First friendly face when the Blue Note job goes sideways and someone needs glyphs or courier faces.  
**Pressure:** The prosthetic was a Valenti "gift." He can't remove it. It hums at night. It tracks him. He doesn't know.  
**Wants:** A hand that doesn't hum -- preferably one that still lets him hold a horn case steady when a client wants a focus tattoo under the club lights.

## Danny -- Ledger courier, AWOL

**Role:** Junior courier who ran with a hot book  
**Age:** 22  
**Look:** Lean, army-surplus jacket even in summer heat, messenger bag with a ward glyph stitched in the lining. Hair in his face like a sax man between sets.

**Personality:** Scared, not stupid. Knew the package was hot; didn't know it would dream at him -- a grey-suited man counting construct shifts.

**Crew hook:** Hiding at a girlfriend's walk-up in Sunset Park. Knows drop protocol and the storage key for whatever the Blue Note ledger trail becomes.  
**Wants:** Disappear. Cousin in Buffalo. Needs cash and a ride to the Port Authority tube exchange before the next set ends on 52nd.

---

## Eddie Trillo -- freelance courier

**Role:** Ledger's designated mover for high-value books  
**Age:** 31  
**Look:** Stocky, green coat, gold tooth, winged-coin tattoo on the forearm. Moves like someone who's been in alley fights behind jazz clubs.

**Personality:** Loyal to the contract, not the family. Cursed cargo doesn't scare him; Valenti dock boys do.

**Crew hook:** Holding a storage key (or tube-locker token) when the crew catches up. Knows the Valenti contact code ("The Bookkeeper") and that Ashford has a parallel contract on the same book.  
**Wants:** Finish the job, get paid, hear one clean trumpet solo without looking over his shoulder.

---

## Auntie Mei -- Golden Dragon Mahjong, Flushing

**Role:** Diviner / broker / neutral ground  
**Age:** 67  
**Look:** Silver bun, dark silk, jade bracelet. Hands shuffle tiles like a drummer's brushes.

**Personality:** Warm, maternal, ruthless on unpaid markers. Legitimate parlor; Chen-Okafor affiliate; independent exchange.

**Crew hook:** Knows Eddie's real name, mother's Queens address, which Valenti capo hired the Ledger, and that Ashford is scrying the book's movement through a third party.  
**Wants:** A favor unnamed. She collects when the piano stops and the crew thinks they're safe.

---

## Detective Adaeze Chen-Okafor -- NYPD OCU

**Role:** Law / family grenade  
**Age:** 30  
**Look:** Tall, braid, plainclothes blazer, revolver, **ward-sniffer** on a chain, warded evidence satchel. No jewelry that catches Midtown light.

**Personality:** Sharp, professional. Brother David runs the Flushing lending office. She doesn't take family money; she doesn't file the reports that would bury him.

**Crew hook:** Notices operators moving around The Blue Note and Red Hook. Not a threat yet -- watching.  
**Knows:** Family patterns, Valenti-Ashford ledger heat, OCU priority currently on a Kowalski dump case (crew breathing room).  
**Wants:** One conviction that isn't a plea -- clean paper, not Commission theater.

---

## Harlow "Cipher" Duvall -- curse specialist

**Role:** Unbinds what shouldn't have been bound  
**Age:** 41  
**Look:** Black turtleneck, grey slacks, copper grounding bracelets, clove smoke. Converted loft near the docks with salt lines on every threshold. The neighborhood still smells of coal and river.

**Personality:** Cynical, cash up front, tiered prices (Class-A through Class-C). Ashford wants her on retainer; she refuses ownership.

**Crew hook:** Can unbind an infernal ledger curse in 24 hours in a warded room -- then **must** report the unbinding to Ashford under non-compete. Clock starts when the last chord of the job fades.  
**Wants:** Independence. A crew that pays without attaching a collar.

---

## Manny "The Bookkeeper" Reyes -- Valenti capo

**Role:** Accountant who hired the Ledger  
**Age:** 48  
**Look:** Soft, diner-booth invisible. Plain suit, anvil tie clip, black coffee, *Daily News*.

**Personality:** Precise, terrified. Answers to Lena if the Blue Note book doesn't come home.

**Crew hook:** Unseen hand behind dock enforcers. Paper trail of every envelope -- leverage if found.  
**Wants:** Book recovered, curse quiet, grand-jury clock stopped. Authorized cash for return; authorized disappearance for failure.
