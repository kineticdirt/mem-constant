# Underworld Operator Archetypes

*Status: Session-Optional / Worldbuilding Substance*

---

## Underworld Operators

In mid-century New York (uneven temporal stack; no exact year), the gray market is run by specialized professionals, fixers, and muscle known as **operators**. These archetypes define how illicit magic and muscle interface with the Five Families.

### 1. The Fixer / Stringer
- **Magic Lane:** Minor illusion and message cantrips. Carry warded fountain pens or pocket watches that detect active scrying.
- **Racket Role:** Fast-talking brokers who manage unlisted speaking-tube connections, write ledger sheets, and negotiate terms between families. They hang out at Midtown diners and social clubs.

### 2. The Enforcer / Muscle
- **Magic Lane:** No flashy spells (to avoid FSA heat). They rely on **capsule arms** (revolvers loaded with pulse or sling caps like **blue kiss**), shock-rune brass knuckles, or silence choke caps.
- **Racket Role:** Muscle for the Valenti docks or Moretti protection crews. They enforce the family's physical will and defend their turf.

### 3. The Club Musician
- **Magic Lane:** Emotion-altering enchantment spells and message cantrips channeled through their instruments (trumpets, saxophones, double basses) as foci.
- **Racket Role:** Stage performers on West 52nd Street or Harlem who run bootleg healing salons in the back rooms during set breaks, using their music to mask the magic.

### 4. The Chaplain-Adjacent
- **Magic Lane:** Divination and necromancy. Use *gentle repose* to preserve evidence, *speak with dead* for one-question testimony, and *zone of truth* during social club sit-downs.
- **Racket Role:** Bound to the Moretti family's funeral-home racket or parish protection network. They manage the secrets of the dead.

### 5. The Driver / Hauler
- **Magic Lane:** Abjuration and transmutation. Carry warded iron bells that repel minor curses and use *expeditious retreat* on steam-carriages or sanitation trucks.
- **Racket Role:** Drivers for the Kowalski garbage routes or Valenti smuggling barges. They know every landfill gate and Jersey handoff point.

### 6. The Mage-for-Hire
- **Magic Lane:** Academic wizardry, *identify*, *detect magic*, and *alarm* sigils. Repair unlicensed construct cores using artificer scrap.
- **Racket Role:** Midtown consultants who masquerade as respectable clerks while laundering money and verifying the quality of magical contraband.

### 7. The Numbers Runner
- **Magic Lane:** Divination and luck manipulation. Carry warded ivory dice or jade coins to nudge probability.
- **Racket Role:** Runners for the Chen-Okafor numbers racket. They run routes through Harlem and Flushing, speaking in rapid-fire numbers code.

### 8. The Ward-Breaker
- **Magic Lane:** Abjuration and illusion. Erase ward residue, bypass guild security seals, and cast temporary *silence* bubbles.
- **Racket Role:** Independent thieves hired by the Ledger or family capos to breach rival safes, splice speaking-tubes, or infiltrate warded warehouses.
