# Location Bible — Five Keyed Underworld Hubs

*Status: Canonical · Locked GM Decision · Campaign Setting Guide*

---

## 1. The Blue Note (Midtown Manhattan)

- **Visibility Tier:** **Licensed / Polite Denial**. Magic is strictly hidden behind mahogany panels and service stairs.
- **The Front:** A high-end jazz club on West 52nd Street. The air is thick with Turkish tobacco smoke and the scent of expensive bourbon. A four-piece bebop band plays on a stage lit by soft pink neon. The patrons are wealthy Midtown executives, politicians, and socialites.
- **The Underbelly:**
  - **The Back Office:** Behind a velvet curtain lies Artie Shaw's private office. It contains a brass speaking-tube exchange with direct splices to the Valenti, Moretti, and Chen-Okafor families. A heavy oak ice box, warded with silver runes, keeps bootleg healing elixirs cold and stable.
  - **The Cellar:** A damp brick basement where an unlicensed clockwork bartender washes glasses with mechanical precision. Tucked behind the coal boiler is a warded iron safe containing the hot ledger.
- **The Price of a Drink:** $2.50 for a licensed cocktail; $15.00 for a back-room shot of "holy water" (minor healing elixir).

## 2. Red Hook Pier 7 (Brooklyn Docks)

- **Visibility Tier:** **Open**. Magic is used as raw industrial labor.
- **The Front:** A sprawling cargo pier dominated by massive steam-powered cranes and warded iron warehouses. The air smells of coal smoke, salt water, and wet hemp rope.
- **The Underbelly:**
  - **The Union Hall:** A two-story brick building that serves as the Valenti family's local headquarters. Longshoremen gather here to receive shift assignments and buy forged guild stamps to clear smuggled cargo.
  - **The Night Shift:** During the 2 a.m. shift, when city inspectors are asleep, the Valentis deploy **construct loaders**—heavy, iron-plated golems that can move ten tons of cargo an hour without breaking a sweat.
- **The Price of Labor:** $1.25 a day for a human longshoreman; $25.00 a night to rent an unlicensed construct loader.

## 3. Mulberry Street Social Club (Little Italy, Manhattan)

- **Visibility Tier:** **Licensed**. Magic is packaged as parish charity and funeral tradition.
- **The Front:** A modest storefront with a green awning. Elderly men sit at card tables out front, drinking espresso and playing scopa. Inside, a back room is permanently set up for wakes, smelling of funeral incense, lilies, and candle wax.
- **The Underbelly:**
  - **The Chaplain's Office:** Tommy "Two Prayers" Moretti's office is tucked behind the kitchen. It contains warded cabinets filled with burial registries, holy water vials, and scrolls of *gentle repose*.
  - **The Burial Registry Vault:** A locked cellar containing paper ledgers of every death in the borough since 1910. The Morettis use these records to summon ghosts as witnesses or to silence them before a trial.
- **The Price of Protection:** $10.00 a month for a "parish sweep" (spirit binding and protection ward for a local business).

## 4. Main Street Lending Office (Flushing, Queens)

- **Visibility Tier:** **Licensed**. Magic is used to calculate risk and manipulate probability.
- **The Front:** A respectable storefront operating as "The Queens Community Lending Corporation." Clerks in white shirts work behind brass grilles, typing on mechanical calculators.
- **The Underbelly:**
  - **The Mahjong Parlor:** Behind a double-locked door is a smoke-filled room where high-stakes games are played. The clatter of ivory tiles is constant.
  - **The Divination Room:** Located upstairs, where Auntie Mei and her apprentices burn rare incense and use warded jade coins to predict numbers outcomes, horse races, and federal audits.
- **The Price of Luck:** $5.00 for a "lucky charm" (a minor jade focus that grants a one-time reroll on a dice game).

---

## 5. Bard Avenue Row House (Staten Island)

- **Visibility Tier:** **Open / Hidden**. Magic is used for deep disposal and logistics.
- **The Front:** A modest, three-story brick row house in a quiet residential neighborhood. A fleet of green sanitation trucks is parked in the gravel yard behind the house.
- **The Underbelly:**
  - **The Dispatch Office:** The living room has been converted into a dispatch center. A massive speaking-tube hub connects directly to the Fresh Kills Landfill gates and the Staten Island ferry terminal.
  - **The Basement:** The door to the basement is secured with three heavy iron locks. Inside is the entrance to a sealed 1870s water tunnel that leads directly down into **The Below** (the Bone Reach).
- **The Price of Disposal:** $50.00 to make a "heavy load" (a body or a hot construct core) vanish forever in the Fresh Kills landfill.

## 6. The Jersey Touch (The Commuter Belt)

- **Bayonne Tank Farm:** A desolate landscape of massive oil tanks and steam pipes. At night, headlights from commuter sedans cut through the fog. This is where the Valentis hand off smuggled New Jersey artificer crates to Manhattan runners, far from the eyes of the OCU.
- **The Iron Rail Diner (Hoboken):** A 24-hour diner located next to the Hoboken train terminal. It serves as a neutral meeting ground for Ledger fixers. The air smells of grease, coffee, and coal soot from the nearby rail yards.
- **Palisades Fringe (Druid / Lycan edge):** Cliff caves and commuter-lot shacks above the Hudson. **Green Cord** druid cells meet under **PCL** hiking cover; **lycan** crews run **night** **garbage** routes for Kowalski subcontractors. FSA **Green Desk** raids are **rare** — **political** — unless a **steam main** blows.
