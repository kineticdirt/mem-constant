## 1. NPC quick cards (everyone who can speak tonight)

### Rosa "The Crane" Lombardo — Valenti capo (bookends the session)
Grey stubble, longshore coat, missing finger joint, cargo hook she does not lean on. Talks like a work order. **Secret she will not say:** she knows exactly which Jersey artificer bound that ledger, and she suspects what's written under the visible ink. If a player asks her a perceptive question at the handoff, let them catch a half-beat of hesitation — that's the whole tell. **Lever:** the crew's debt. **Off-ramp:** she pays bonuses for discretion and remembers it.

### Artie Shaw — club owner (probably never on stage)
Nervous human, damp collar, smiles like he's apologizing for a bill. Owes Chen-Okafor, reports to Flushing, keeps Midtown hours. He does not know what the ledger's deeper layer is; he knows Ashford pays him to keep the third-floor "consultancy" quiet. **Lever:** his debt, his liquor license. **If pressed:** he folds fast and lies badly — he will swear the safe is "just payroll" while his eyes do arithmetic.

### Big Mike — doorman
Half-orc, tailored tux, hands like a ferry gate. Thirty-minute door rotation; a younger human in a cheap suit covers his smoke breaks. Professional, not cruel: he throws people *out*, not *down*. **Lever:** he is paid by the club, not a family — a good enough story plus a decent tip genuinely works on him. **Clock:** any commotion at the front ticks **Club Notice**.

### The clockwork bartender — cellar hazard (CR 2-ish)
Unlicensed, unregistered, dead steady. Wash → pause → wash; the pause is ~8 seconds. It reads **active spellcasting within 15 ft as a threat**: it stops, turns, and walks toward the caster. It does not run. That's worse. **Off-ramp:** stop casting, hold still, let it finish a cycle — it loses interest. **Do not** let the crew fight it to the death in a room full of glass; the noise is the real attack (ticks **Club Notice** twice).

### Second doorman / waitresses / quartet
Texture, not obstacles. The waitresses know the back-office interval better than anyone; a charming Face can learn the rotation in one conversation. The quartet keeps playing no matter what they see — union rules.

### Off-screen but present
**Eddie Trillo** (Ashford parallel contract — packet §5), **Det. Adaeze Chen-Okafor** (OCU, watching the block some nights), **Julian Cross** (Ashford ops; the third-floor consultant answers to him), **Manny "The Bookkeeper" Reyes** (Valenti accountant whose neck is actually on the block — Rosa's errand is his life).

## 3. Clocks

Tick on the listed trigger; when a clock fills, fire its consequence. Do not announce clock names — announce the world.

| Clock | Ticks when | Full means |
|-------|-----------|------------|
| **Club Notice** (4) | Commotion at the front; drill heard; bartender walks; iron door left open | Big Mike + second doorman converge on the cellar stairs. Not combat-first: Mike blocks the stairs and asks, conversationally, who signs their checks. |
| **OCU Beat** (4) | Overt casting on the street; capsule discharged; a patron complains about "static" | A ward-sniffer walks in the front door at the worst available moment (packet complication 3). |
| **Ashford Ears** (4) | Speaking-tube splice used; drill near the third-floor riser; crew says "ledger" indoors | The consultant calls Julian Cross's house crew. They arrive ~20 minutes later — usually after the crew is gone, which is how you get an Ambush Later instead of a TPK Now. |

**Bone Index** has no clock. It has a *weather*. Note privately every time the crew handles, opens, or discusses the book. It is patient.

## 4. Complications — wired, not random

The packet's d6 table is the menu. Fire them on triggers, not vibes:

1. **Big Mike recognizes a PC** → fire when the Face fails a social roll, or when a PC with a prior record walks the front room undisguised. He calls the back office instead of acting. (+2 Club Notice)
2. **Bartender glitches** → any spell cast in the cellar. (+2 Club Notice if it walks into glassware)
3. **OCU ward-sniffer enters** → OCU Beat full, or the Face is having too easy a time and the front needs a pulse.
4. **Iron door padlocked from inside** → use if the crew's alley approach is going *too* smoothly and Muscle has had nothing to do. One-handed quiet work while Mike is fifteen feet away.
5. **Safe key in the warded ice box** → use if the crew brought drilling but no social plan — now someone must get past the bar into the back office.
6. **Consultant working late** → Ashford Ears full. The speaking-tube descends like a snake hearing a mouse.

Pick at most two. One is usually right.

## 5. If they open the ledger

They will at least want to. The packet says don't; players hear a dare.

- **Skimming the visible layer:** free. They learn the bootleg-bands and construct-core traffic (packet §5) — names they already half-know. This is the *reward* tier.
- **Reading the deeper layer:** the reactive script is visible only under a ward or to pact-signatories. A PC with the right sight (or a detect magic + arcana push) sees that a second text exists. Reading it **aloud** is the Bone Index fulfillment condition — nobody at the table should know that yet. If someone starts reading aloud, the lights do not flicker. The room gets *quiet*, like a held breath. Mark it. Collect in session 2 or 3, at the worst poetically available time.
- **Copying pages:** works, and is the smart coward's move. Rosa pays the same; the Commission question becomes who else has a copy.

## 6. Endings (pick by table state)

- **Clean handoff (clocks low):** Rosa counts nothing, nods once, and says the debt is square. Then — the packet's session fuel: Eddie Trillo finds them within the hour with "you have something of mine," politely, at the pier or the diner. End on his smile.
- **Loud exit (a clock filled):** handoff happens under a wagon circling two blocks out. Rosa's people wave the crew off the pier fast. End on the radio... no — the *speaking-tube* at their flop, ticking with a coded OCU bulletin that mentions West 52nd.
- **They kept the ledger / read it aloud:** Rosa knows immediately that something is off (she knows the binder, remember). She does not pay. She does not threaten. She *leaves*. End on the manhole cover outside the diner ticking like cooling bones. (Packet §5 — Bone Index weather begins.)

**Track for session 2:** final clock positions; who saw the crew's faces; whether the ledger was copied; Rosa's discretion bonus (yes/no); which escape leg ran.

## 7. G-5 contingency box (steer not yet picked)

The packet is draft-dependent; the run-sheet doesn't care. Whatever the GM picks for the archetype steer (`worldbuilding/strokes/` — New Ark / war-magus / "protected" backfired):

- **New Ark steer** → the Below route in escape leg C gets named streets and a toll-keeper; Bone Index weather thickens faster.
- **War-magus steer** → OCU response gains a federal veneer; Adaeze's courtesy call arrives with a formal ward-seal.
- **"Protected" backfire steer** → the Valenti leash itself becomes the story: the counting-room job that started all this was *arranged* to recruit the crew. Rosa neither confirms nor denies.

Until the pick lands, run the packet exactly as written. Nothing in Session 1 contradicts any of the three.
