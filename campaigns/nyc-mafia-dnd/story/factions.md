## The Five (working names)

| Family | Territory | Specialty | Supernatural angle |
|--------|-----------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Valenti** | Brooklyn / docks | Smuggling, unions | **Artificer** forgery rings; cursed cargo |
| **Moretti** | Little Italy / Lower Manhattan | Protection, restaurants | **Cleric** "chaplains"; burial rites that bind ghosts |
| **Chen-Okafor** | Queens / Flushing corridor | Gambling, numbers | **Divination** fixers; luck as product |
| **Kowalski** | Staten Island / bridges | Waste, logistics, "disappearing" | **Necromancy** taboo — rivals say they talk to the river |
| **Ashford** | Midtown towers (white-collar) | Money laundering, politicians | **Warlock** pacts framed as "consulting"; cleanest suits |

**Commission:** quarterly sit-downs; **no magic hits** on civilians without vote (broken often).
