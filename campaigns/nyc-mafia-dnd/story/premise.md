# Campaign Premise — NYC Mafia × D&D

*Status: Canonical · Locked GM Decision · Campaign Overview*

---

## 1. The Elevator Pitch

The calendar will not pin to one year — **uneven temporal stack** (LOCKS B14). The setting is a gritty, atmospheric New York metropolitan area where the culture reads **mid-century jazz-vice** (postwar boom, cool jazz, cigarette smoke, sharp sharkskin suits) but the street technology is **late-1800s industrial** (steam-powered elevated trains, brass speaking-tubes, warded horse-carriages, and coal-fired foundries). 

In this world, magic is not an academic pursuit or a superhero power—it is **spell-tech**. It is licensed, taxed, or bootlegged. The Five Families of the Commission run the grey market for unregistered construct cores, bootleg healing bands, and spirit-binding foci.

The players are **operators**—fixers, muscle, club musicians, and defrocked priests—caught between the corrupt Organized Crime Unit (OCU), the relentless Federal Seizure Agency (FSA), and the blood-bound obligations of the families.

---

## 2. The Layers of the City

The campaign operates across three distinct layers of reality:

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                               THE STREET                                |
|  - The mundane reality of mid-century New York (jazz-vice calendar).   |
|  - Debt ledgers, six-shot revolvers, manual typewriters, and wax        |
|    cylinder wiretaps on brass speaking-tubes.                           |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                THE GREY                                 |
|  - The illicit underworld of bootleg spell-tech.                        |
|  - Back-room healing salons, stolen guild stamps, and saxophones        |
|    used as foci to cast emotion-altering charms during jazz sets.       |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                THE DEEP                                 |
|  - The terrifying reality of the Below and the foundries.              |
|  - Existential automata threats, elemental spirit-binding, and the      |
|    collapsed, non-Euclidean demiplane folded into the city's bedrock.   |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

---

## 3. The Core Conflict

The campaign is built on the tension between **licensed craft** and **bootleg ritual**:

- **The straight game:** The Federal Audit Bureau (FAB) taxes and licenses every spell cast in Midtown. The Lamplighters' Union maintains the continual flame streetlights. Temple clinics offer expensive, taxed healing to the wealthy.
- **The crooked game:** The families sell illegal, untaxed alternatives. They run back-room salons for bullet wounds, splice speaking-tubes to intercept business secrets, and smuggle New Jersey elemental cores to power sweatshops.
- **The existential threat:** The industrial foundries have gone too far. They have bred **true automata**—constructs that operate without human operators. Some have escaped into the Below, forming a rogue collective that threatens to trigger a citywide military occupation.

---

## 4. Default Campaign Setup

- **The City:** NYC's five boroughs + the adjacent New Jersey commuter belt (Hoboken, Bayonne, Newark).
- **The Hook:** The Session 1 heist to recover Artie Shaw's hot ledger from the cellar safe of **The Blue Note** jazz club on West 52nd Street.
- **The Tone:** Gritty crime drama meets tabletop fantasy — dry, awkward, darkly funny until it isn't (*Barry*-adjacent voice; see LOCKS B13). No world-ending apocalypses—just a bad night on the wet cobblestones that binds the party to the Commission's web of debt and favors.
