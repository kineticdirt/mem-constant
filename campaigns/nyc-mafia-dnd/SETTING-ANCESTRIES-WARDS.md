## Design principle (non-negotiable)

**Human New York stays human and multicultural.** Italian, Irish, Jewish, Chinese, Black Harlem, Puerto Rican, Polish, and the rest of the period’s real communities are **not** stand-ins for elves, orcs, or dwarves. Fantasy ancestries are **additional** populations — older enclaves, separate immigrant waves, guild diasporas, or covenant refugees — who settled **alongside** humans in the same wards.

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Dwarves run a **stonecutters’ mutual aid hall** next to an Irish parish | “Dwarves = Italians” or any 1:1 ancestry ↔ ethnicity allegory |
| Orc dock crews **share** Red Hook piers with human longshoremen | Replace Harlem with “the elf quarter” |
| Tieflings face **horn stigma** unrelated to skin color politics | Use fantasy prejudice as a cheap metaphor for real racism |
| A Moretti capo is **human**; their best ward-smith is a **dwarf** on payroll | Make whole families ancestry-coded to a real-world group |

**At table:** prejudice tracks **ancestry visibility** (horns, green skin, size) and **magical licensing** — not real-world ethnicity. Human-on-human bigotry of the period still exists in fiction; it is **parallel**, not “explained away” by fantasy.

## How humans and non-humans coexist

Gods and miracles are **known facts** — churches, temples, and guild shrines operate openly enough that denial is polite fiction. **Licensed** divine work (healing, witness oaths, feast-day wards) sits next to **bootleg** salons the way gin sits next to communion wine. The **Bureau of Consecrated Commerce (BCC)** and **Office of Thaumaturgical Revenue (OTR)** tax registered charms; **Volstead** and **magical prohibition** run on the same rails (raids, bribes, seized stills *and* seized ritual kits).

Non-human ancestries usually **pass** in crowds if they can hide horns, height, or green skin — many don’t bother in their own blocks. **Patronage** binds the city: a dwarf guild funds a councilman’s church renovation; an elf salon sponsors a Harlem rent party; a family **chaplain** (Moretti) buries everyone who pays, regardless of blood. **Prejudice** is sharpest at hiring halls, “respectable” Midtown doors, and federal desks — and softest where **labor, racket, and temple** need each other to function.

## Five Families & ancestry (recruitment, not coding)

Families are **human-led** institutions shaped by **territory and racket**, not by fantasy bloodlines. Capos and soldati are **mostly human** because the Commission, parishes, and political clubs are human-majority — but **crew-level** recruitment follows **skill and ward**:

| Family | Territory | Who they hire (ancestry-agnostic) | Typical non-human roles |
|--------|-----------|-----------------------------------|-------------------------|
| **Valenti** | Brooklyn / docks | Smugglers, forgers, union muscle | **Dwarf** vault-smiths; **orc** pier crews; **artificer** cursed-cargo handlers |
| **Moretti** | Little Italy / Lower Manhattan | Restaurateurs, enforcers, chaplains | **Halfling** runners; **gnome** scribes for truth-bind books; **cleric** “chaplains” (any ancestry) |
| **Chen-Okafor** | Queens / Flushing | Numbers runners, diviners, bridge tolls | **Dragonborn** negotiators; **elf** appointment scryers; **kenku** couriers |
| **Kowalski** | Staten Island / bridges | Disposal, ferrymen, “river” specialists | **Tiefling** pact-breakers; **dwarf** ironworkers on bridge wards; taboo **necromancers** (any ancestry, denied publicly) |
| **Ashford** | Midtown towers | Lawyers, lobbyists, warlock consultants | **Tiefling** & **elf** “culture” fronts; **human** faces on every contract |

**Cross-ancestry crews** are normal at the **soldier** level; **blood-oath** inner circles may stay human for **political** access (clubs, marriage, witnesses) — a tension point for half-blood PCs. **No family** “owns” an ancestry; at most they **dominate a racket** that employs many ancestries (e.g. dwarf smiths work for Valenti *and* independents).

## Visibility & law (quick refs)

| Topic | Default (adjust at table) |
|-------|---------------------------|
| **Openly non-human** | Common in **dock, tenement, and guild** wards; **Midtown** expects glamour or passing |
| **Half-blood** | Stigma varies: **half-elf** often “artistic”; **half-orc** often “muscle”; **tiefling** always watched |
| **Permits** | ALB / BCC treats ancestry-neutral **on paper**; inspectors harass **visible** non-humans more |
| **Temples** | Human parishes **and** ancestry guild-shrines (dwarf forge-saints, elf moon salons, orc labor oaths) |

## Open questions for the GM

1. **Ancestry list:** Lock core six (dwarf, elf, orc/half-orc, tiefling, halfling, gnome) + dragonborn + goblinoids — or add **aasimar**, **goliath**, **kenku**, **tabaxi**?
2. **Visibility default:** Is non-human life **unremarkable in Harlem and the docks** but **denied in Midtown**, or citywide **polite fiction** (“they’re foreign”)?
3. **Half-blood stigma:** Is **human-passing** half-elf/half-orc a **social ladder**, or does **any** known non-human blood bar ALB permits and “respectable” jobs?
4. **Family inner circles:** Are **human-only** made-men a **hard** rule, **soft** tradition, or **already broken** by one family?
5. **The Below:** Do tunnel ancestries (**deep gnomes**, **mutants**, **aberrant** bleed) appear above ground often, or stay **mid-campaign** reveal?
6. **Harlem & elves:** Keep elves as **patron-artist** minority (above), or shrink elf presence so Harlem stays **overwhelmingly human** Renaissance?
7. **Puerto Rican wave:** Human-majority **East Harlem / South Bronx** only for now, or a **small** separate ancestry enclave (e.g. **lizardfolk** river-workers) that must **not** stand in for Latino identity?
