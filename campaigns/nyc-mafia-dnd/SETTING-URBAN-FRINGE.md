# Setting Urban Fringe — Cities, Transit, and What Lives at the Edge

*GM lock · **2026-08-02**. Urban political order, fringe humanoids, surface transit, skyline shape, druid designation. After `LOCKS.md` (B18); with `SETTING-CAPABILITY-ASYMMETRY.md`.*

**One line:** Humans run the money and the law. Vampires rent at the margins. Druids make the front page as terrorists. The subway works until it doesn't. Nobody's built a steel tower because nobody needs one yet — brick's cheaper and the guilds like it that way.

---

## 1. Urban political order (cities dominate)

**LOCK:** The **city** — ward boards, guild charters, municipal contracts, Commission turf — is where power actually lives. Albany and Washington send paper. Jersey sends tank farms and guys who commute in on the 7:14. But rackets, licenses, vice, and who gets raided on Tuesday? That's negotiated **block by block**.

| Layer | Who holds it | What players notice |
|-------|--------------|---------------------|
| **Street** | Families, unions, parish networks | Rackets, salons, dock loaders, numbers rooms |
| **Ward** | Aldermen, guild inspectors, OCU precinct captains | Licenses, visibility tiers, who gets raided |
| **Metro** | Commission sit-downs, FAB desks, rail monopolies | Cross-borough smuggling, speaking-tube warrants |
| **Fringe** | County sheriffs, Palisades druid cells, Below toll-keepers | Heat when the city **exports** its problems |

**Not locked:** a single mayor's name. **Locked:** if it isn't **urban**, it isn't **central**. Farms feed the city. Forests harass it. That's the relationship.

---

## 2. Fringe humanoids (vampires, lycanthropes, and kin)

**LOCK:** Vampires, werewolves, and the rest are **real** and **known**. Human society pushed them to the edges. They don't hold ward office. They don't get FAB salon licenses without a **human front** and a **bribe schedule** that would make a comptroller weep.

### Where they actually are

| Zone | Examples | How it plays |
|------|----------|--------------|
| **Sprawl edge** | Pelham Bay marsh shacks, Jamaica Bay stilt houses, outer Queens truck farms | Night markets, blood brokers, lycan crews on **garbage routes** |
| **The Below** | Court Street overflow, Bone Reach side passages, sealed platform crypts | Smuggling, disposal, **feeds** that don't appear on surface maps — see `reports/2026-08-01-the-below.md` §5 |
| **Jersey wilds** | Palisades cliff caves, Passaic rust belt, Pine Barrens **"commune"** headlines | Druid-adjacent cover; Valenti **handoff** lots at tank farms |
| **Night markets** | Orchard St after 1 a.m., Brighton Beach back alleys, Hunts Point before the fish auction | **Licensed** stalls sell garlic wards; **unlicensed** stalls sell **names** |

### Social reality

Midtown does **polite denial** — vampire in a sharkskin suit reads as rich eccentric until someone checks the parish registry. Harlem and the docks are more honest: you pay the club, the union, or the family. Same money, different envelope.

OCU default when fringe humanoids show up near a murder: **special desk** referral. Not "random monster." They've seen this before.

**Barry beat:** *"He's not a person." "He pays rent." "So does the ice machine."*

## 3. Druid cells and the "terrorist" label

**LOCK:** Druids — and allied **land-cult** networks — hate urban domination. Federal and city propaganda calls active cells **communist terrorists**. Some cells **are** violent. Many aren't. The useful question is **who benefits** when the label sticks.

### Concrete institutions

| Actor | Role |
|-------|------|
| **Palisades Conservation League (PCL)** | Respectable **donor** face — hikes, bird counts, **warded** trail markers |
| **Green Cord cells** | **Underground** — sabotage on **steam mains**, glyph fires on **clear-cut** lots, **harassment** of guild surveyors |
| **FSA "Green Desk"** | Federal **anti-sabotage** unit — shares **files** with Seizure on **construct** sites |
| **OCU "Rural Liaison"** | Underfunded — **three** detectives for **all** counties north of the Bronx |
| **Ashford & parish donors** | **Timber**, **foundry coal**, **commuter rail** bonds — **lobby** for **expanded** terror designation |

### Propaganda vs. fact (play both)

Newspaper: *"Red-root saboteurs threaten the steam grid."* Street: *"They blew up a valve because the guild **stole** the spring."* Commission, quietly: *"If FSA chases druids in Jersey, they aren't chasing **our** cores this week."*

Don't make them cartoon eco-villains. Cells have internal politics, informants, human allies in tenant unions. OCU heat on a Green Cord safe house uses the **same** warrant stack as a numbers room — splice, ward residue, ghost witness.

**Pointer:** legal frame in `reports/2026-08-01-law-and-magic.md` §5.

## 4. Subways (brass, steam-assist, ward-lit)

**LOCK:** The city has a **subway** — not a modern metro, but a **late-1800s** **brass-and-brick** network with **steam-assist** grades, **continual-flame** platform lamps, and **guild ward** strips along the rails.

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Lines** | **IRT**-style elevated legs in the Bronx; **IND** deep tubes in Manhattan/Brooklyn; **shuttle** stubs to yards |
| **Ride feel** | **Lurching** — steam pistons help **inclines**; cars **rock**; windows **fog** |
| **Ward lighting** | Blue-white **platform** glow; fails to **gas mantle** backup in **outer** stations |
| **Automata glitch** | Near **active cores** or **Below** breaches, **signals lie**, **doors stick**, **schedules** repeat a **stop** that isn't on the map |
| **Below overlap** | Some **abandoned** platforms open into **The Below** — families **tax** access — `reports/2026-08-01-the-below.md` |

**Fare band (surface legal):** **$0.15** token (working stiff); **$0.25** late-night **ward-car** (brighter lamps, OCU spot-checks); **$2.00** **family** bribe to skip **cargo** search at Court Street.

## 5. Cabs — motorized carriages (slow)

**LOCK:** **Cabs** are **motorized carriages** in the **literal** sense — **early chassis** + **steam or weak electric** assist + **horse backup** when the engine **sulks**. They are **not** a modern taxi fleet.

| Type | Speed / feel | Fare band (midtown ↔ docks) |
|------|--------------|-----------------------------|
| **Hack license (warded carriage)** | **Slow** — 12–18 mph on clear streets | **$1.50–$2.50** metered; **$5.00** if you want **speed** (illegal ward-boost) |
| **Unlicensed "jitney"** | Faster, **no** insurance | **$0.75** flat — **may** stop for **other** fares |
| **Guild courier cab** | **Speaking-tube** to dispatch; **reliable** | **$3.00** base + **$0.25**/minute waiting |
| **Family "black car"** | **Armored** brass; **driver** doesn't talk | **Favor**, not cash |

**Barry beat:** *"How long?" "Twenty minutes." "It's six blocks." "Then walk."*

## 6. Fire response — engines and magic brigades

**LOCK:** Fire response is **dual-track**: municipal **steam pump companies** (brass engines, hose wagons) and **contracted magic brigades** (guild or temple).

| Service | Who | When they win the call |
|---------|-----|------------------------|
| **Engine Company** | City **steam pump** + volunteer ladder | **Ordinary** brick fires, tenement coal stoves |
| **Iron Ladder Guild** | **Warded** ladders, **break-glass** hydrant keys | **High** row-houses, factory **belt** fires |
| **Temple Hose (Moretti contract)** | Clerics with **create water** / **control flames** bands | **Licensed** parish blocks — **fast** on **spirit** fires |
| **Foundry Brigade (Valenti contract)** | **Anti-construct** foam — **cold iron** dust | **Core** fires, **automata** **runaway** in warehouses |

**Response time (play):** **Engine** 8–12 minutes; **magic brigade** 5–8 if **pre-paid** ward on the building; **both** 15+ in **sprawl edge** where **hydrants** are **politics**.

**Barry beat:** *"The building's on fire." "Call the church." "We did. They're billing us."*

## 7. Skyline and sprawl (no steel towers, no interstates)

**LOCK:** The metro has **no large steel-frame towers**. Skyline is **brick**, **stone**, **guild spires**, and **factory stacks** — mostly **4–8 stories**, occasional **12-story** **limestone** **cliff** on Midtown **avenues**. **Suburban sprawl** rings the boroughs; **main arterial roads** connect hubs; **no big interstates** yet.

| Feature | Locked shape |
|---------|--------------|
| **Manhattan spine** | **Avenues** + **numbered streets**; **elevated** els cast **shadow** on **arterials** |
| **Arterials** | **Grand Concourse**, **Queens Blvd**, **Sunset Park** **shore** road, **Jersey** **Commuter** **Causeway** (toll) |
| **Sprawl** | **Levitt-style** **not** yet — instead **lot-by-lot** **wood** **bungalows** and **brick** **duplexes** on **old** **farm** **lines** |
| **Highways** | **Parkways** (cars **allowed**, **trucks** **policed**); **no** **multi-lane** **interstate** **belt** — **smuggling** uses **arterials** + **rail** |
| **VOID** | Glass **curtain-wall** **skyscrapers**, **interstate** **cloverleafs**, **Helicopter** **commute** |

**Pointer:** borough grain `worldbuilding/strokes/borough-city-build.md` §7–8.

## 10. Metro surrounds — NY State, New Jersey, CT & LI

**LOCK for shape:** The **metro** is **not** five boroughs alone. **Commission** rackets, **OCU** warrants, and **fringe** humanoids **export** across **rivers** and **parkways** — still **no interstates**, still **low brick** skyline everywhere.

### New York State

| Zone | Locked play |
|------|-------------|
| **Hudson Valley** | Commuter rail **smuggling**; **PCL** donor respectability over **Green Cord** cells; **covenant** deed fights tied to Harlem property lines |
| **Albany adjunct** | **Ward** license **appeals**; dwarf **clerks**; federal **Green Desk** copies — **paper** power, not street sit-downs |
| **Upstate fringe** | **Adirondack** lycan headlines; druid **terror** propaganda; **timber/foundry** donors **benefit** from the label |

### New Jersey

| Zone | Locked play |
|------|-------------|
| **Hoboken / Jersey City** | **Iron Guild** pipe-fitters; waterfront **union** halls; Dutch **sluice** maintenance invoices |
| **Newark** | **Ironbound** Portuguese fish cold-chain; Central Ward **jazz** circuit; **sparse** South Asian **India Square** |
| **Palisades** | Welsh firbolg **lumber**; druid **cave** cover; Valenti **tank-farm** handoffs |
| **Passaic / Pine Barrens** | Russian **cold-iron** smuggle; **"commune"** front pages; FSA **raid** politics |

### Connecticut & Long Island

- **CT commute belt:** Ashford **country-club** sit-downs — **polite denial** visibility; **human** political face, **mixed** back-room crews.
- **Long Island:** **Sprawl** arterials; Scandinavian **boat** yards; Greek **offshore** lines; **county** sheriff **frays** where **city** families **vacation**.

## 11. Agent rules (quick)

1. **Fringe humanoids** — **edge**, **Below**, **Jersey** — not **city hall**.
2. **Druids** — **political** **enemy** **label**; show **who** **profits**.
3. **Transit** — **slow** **cabs**, **glitchy** **subway** near **automata**.
4. **Skyline** — **low** **brick**; **sprawl** **arterials**; **no** **towers** **or** **interstates**.
5. **Metro surrounds** — **NJ** handoffs, **Hudson** deeds, **upstate** terror headlines — **county** lines **fray** jurisdiction.
6. **No exact year** — mid-century **culture**, late-1800s **hardware** — `SETTING-CAPABILITY-ASYMMETRY.md` §2.
