## OTR stamp forgery — mini-flow

### §C grammar (forged permit stamp)

| Step | Answer |
|------|--------|
| **Need** | Salon looks licensed without paying pair-tax / miracle registration |
| **Medieval-magical?** | Partial — **wax + dye craft** mundane; **truth-bind ink** is guild secret (Valenti forgery rings) |
| **Cost** | Rare **notary-grade ink**; stolen blank forms; engraver hours |
| **Failure / law** | Stamp **smudges** under BCC listening seal; ink **lies** when clerk reads aloud; felony = tax fraud **plus** unregistered miracle |
| **Invention** | Brass **OTR die** + violet **truth-bind ink**; good forges pass a **clipboard glance**; fail under **spoke audit** (clerk forces verbal read of stamp date) |

### Play steps (one scene)

1. **Acquire blank** — steal from clerk bag, bribe print-shop, or Valenti “sample kit.”
2. **Strike stamp** — engraver needs quiet hour; **silence ward** on the room is itself evidence.
3. **Ink test** — dab on scrap; if ink **humms** wrong, BCC residue detector will catch it later.
4. **Plant on door / ledger** — Midtown denial wants the **sign** visible; open wards use stamp only when federal walks in.
5. **Stress test** — OTR **spoke audit** (read date aloud) or BCC **seal** → forged stamp **bleeds violet** / wrong feast-day code.

**Hooks:** Fence wants a **real** die after a bad forge; Ashford front needs a clean stamp by Thursday; Moretti refuses forgeries that endanger chaplain franchise.
