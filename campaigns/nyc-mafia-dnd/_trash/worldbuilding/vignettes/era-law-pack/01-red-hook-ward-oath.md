# The Forge-Shrine Oath

## Scene

Four a.m. on the Red Hook pier, the air tasted like coal and river rot. Men human and otherwise leaned on crates while the foreman — human, Irish, missing two fingers — read the hiring list off a slate that wasn't slate at all but a **truth-bind** board Valenti's people "donated" to the union hall. When he called **Kazimir**, a dwarf three feet shorter than the stevedores around him didn't duck his head. He walked to the front, stamped his iron-shod boot on the wet planks, and the board recorded his name with a sound like a nail pulled from green wood.

Nobody stared. On the pier, a dwarf was a **smith**, not a spectacle.

Kazimir's crew was half orc, half human, all hungover. They'd spent the night drinking at a **healing hole** two blocks inland — not because anyone was hurt, but because the barber's back room sold **laying-on** cheaper than a hospital and faster than prayer. The tincture left a copper taste and a faint glow behind the eyes that the foreman pretended not to see when he assigned the grain lift.

"Ward on the north shed failed again," Kazimir said, not asking permission. He unrolled a charcoal rubbing from a **seal** on the warehouse door — dwarven script mixed with harbor numbers. "Goliath sling won't hold past noon unless someone resets the anchor."

The foreman spat. "Talk to Valenti's ward-smith."

"I *am* Valenti's ward-smith until Tuesday. Then Moretti's funeral house buys my contract." Kazimir said it like weather. On the docks, guild oaths and family payroll were as ordinary as the tide.

A human kid — fourteen, maybe, scarf thin as conscience — watched from behind a coil of rope. He'd come down from Bushwick because his mother burned her hand on a **still** and the licensed salon on Delancey wanted eight dollars they didn't have. Kazimir glanced at the kid, jerked his chin toward the shed.

"Hold the lantern. Don't touch the seal."

The kid held the lantern. Kazimir sang under his breath — not English, not Italian, the forge-shrine verse his mutual-aid hall taught before anyone cared about **BCC** registration forms. The seal warmed; the goliath sling groaned and held. When they finished, Kazimir pressed two fingers to the kid's palm, checked the burn, clicked his tongue.

"Barber on Richards. Say Kazimir sent you. Tell them the dock paid already."

The kid ran. The foreman wrote nothing down. Up on the street, a **Tribune** boy shouted headlines about Hoover and breadlines; he didn't shout about dwarves, because on Red Hook there was nothing to sell in that story.
