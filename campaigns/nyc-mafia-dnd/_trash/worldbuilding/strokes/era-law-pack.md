## Dialectic record (why these beats the forks)

### Year — 1931 vs 1928 vs 1933

**Thesis (1931):** Depression makes every racket hungrier; federal desks understaffed but vicious; players feel moral exhaustion without Repeal dominating every scene.

**Antithesis (1933):** Repeal parallel gives magical-legalization drama; stronger thematic rhyme for salon licensing debates.

**Synthesis:** **1931** — grit default; Repeal becomes **drip foreshadow** (newspaper editorials, BCC draft bills) without dating the whole campaign to repeal season.

**Residual:** If table wants Repeal party, run a **prologue stroke** later — do not retcon 1931 start.

### Visibility — tiered vs citywide open vs polite denial everywhere

**Thesis (tiered):** Same city, two social grammars — noir double life in Midtown; open wonder in Red Hook. Geography becomes play.

**Antithesis (citywide open):** Less exposition friction; easier for new players.

**Synthesis:** **Tiered** — pairs with ward table (C2 lock). Midtown denial is **performance**, not ignorance; cops and families exploit the gap.

### Federal — OTR+BCC vs BCC monopoly vs +Occult Squad

**Thesis (split):** OTR audit → BCC raid chains mirror IRS → Treasury on gin; NYPD occult desk gives Valenti/Moretti bribe lanes.

**Antithesis (BCC monopoly):** Simpler for players; one warrant one agency.

**Synthesis:** **OTR + BCC + local inspectors** — more moving parts but richer raid fiction (wrong agency day = comedy + danger).

### Prohibition target — salons vs relics vs broad miracles

**Thesis (salon focus):** Blind-tiger **healing/luck** parallels speakeasies cleanly; intimate craft (bootleg **links**) natural bootleg economy.

**Antithesis (relic focus):** Chen-Okafor relic runs from Canada — strong import ward play.

**Synthesis:** **Salons primary, relics secondary** — raids hit salons weekly; relic runs are **season arcs**.

## Implications for play

- **Jobs:** Smuggle gin **and** unregistered charm batches; disable a ward before a raid; bribe the ward-inspector **or** the OTR clerk — different failure modes.
- **Social:** PC with visible non-human blood works Red Hook; Midtown job needs glamour, paperwork, or a human face.
- **Heat:** Evidence is **truth-bind ledgers**, seized speaking-tube wax, and ritual residue — not bodycams or cloud logs.
- **Intimate economy:** Bootleg **paired-stitch links** (portal-lace) are contraband parallel to gin — kinky when relevant, not every scene (`explicit: measured`).
