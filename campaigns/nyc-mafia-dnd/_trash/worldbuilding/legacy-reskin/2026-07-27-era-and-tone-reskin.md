## Era lock: 1931 Prohibition New York

**Year:** 1931 — breadlines, Hoovervilles, Volstead still law; Repeal rumor, not yet statute.

### Tech snapshot

- **Rotary phones** and **speaking-tubes** — long-distance is scheduled mirror-scry or ward-tube splice; no pocket device.
- **Cash and truth-bind ledgers** — families move bags of cash; big scores use warded safes and notary-clerics.
- **Radio infant, print king** — *Tribune* and *Daily Mirror* set mood; gramophone needles on shared copper; no television in homes.
- **Brick skyline** — 4–6 story bulk; Ashford ward-tower exception, not glass curtain walls.

### NYC texture

- Docks still working-class; LES not yet glass-towered; Harlem **human-majority** with patron-artist elf minority (C6).
- Breadlines beside **blind-tiger salons**; ward-inspectors take bribes at barber back rooms.
- Packards and Model As; Tommy guns; etched rounds rare street garnish (D5 muted).
- Depression pressure — every racket hungrier; federal desks understaffed but vicious.

### Why 1931, not 1999 (original) or today

| Factor | What it gives |
|--------|---------------|
| **Prohibition parallel** | Bootleg gin **and** bootleg **healing/luck/links** — same raid grammar, same family money |
| **No surveillance state** | Evidence is wax seals, compelled testimony circles, seized stitch-pairs — not Ring, not ALPR |
| **Low skyline** | Chase fiction on fire escapes and piers, not elevator lobbies |
| **Tiered visibility** | Midtown **polite denial** vs Red Hook open wonder — geography teaches the setting |
| **Distinct identity** | *1931 NYC but guild-magic* is a choice — depression noir, not generic urban fantasy |

### What stays the same

The Five Families, the Commission, **OTR + BCC**, ward-inspectors, **The Below** (mid-campaign) — portable after reskin. Premise resolves: **1931, locked.**

## Tone: Mob noir, infrastructure magic, high heat

### Core feeling

*The city is wet. The gin is bad. The loyalty is real but the loyalty is also a leash.*

### Three pillars

| Pillar | What it means at the table |
|--------|---------------------------|
| **Grounded procedural** | Every job is logistics: route, driver, ward-smith, bribe, backup. Tension from planning, not spell slots. |
| **City as character** | Boroughs differ: Brooklyn = docks/salons, Midtown = denial + Ashford contracts, Queens = numbers + divination |
| **Magic is costly infrastructure** | Most people never cast. A **flare ward** in a Midtown club draws BCC in an hour. Healing is salon appointment or bootleg hole. |

### What is NOT in this tone

- **No heroic fantasy** — you're trying to get paid, not save the world.
- **No cyberpunk / no enchanted smartphone** — speaking-tubes and paired lace, not apps.
- **No parody** — serious stakes, dark humor, not camp.

### The magic rule

> If a spell would be visible in a public place **without** the ward's social grammar, it's a problem.

Subtle magic rules: enchanted objects, ritual circles, basement bargains. Open **flare** magic in a Midtown dining room brings the Commission, **BCC**, and whatever is Below — none friendly.

### The 1931 texture rule

> Everything that existed in our 1931 exists here, unless the GM says otherwise — plus guild infrastructure where §C grammar allows.

Breadlines, Hoover speeches, ward-inspectors, jazz clubs, **Repeal editorials**, NYPD shakedowns. Use what works; ignore anachronism.
