## Valenti — Brooklyn / docks & union smuggling

Valenti controls waterfront Brooklyn — Red Hook to Sunset Park — and every crate, **relic run**, and **forged BCC seal** that moves through it. Real business: **artificer forgery rings** that clear customs as "industrial hardware." Enforcers carry **alchemical stunners** disguised as mechanic's tools; safehouses are warehouse lofts with dwarf-forged wards over roll-up doors. When a shipment of pact-bound goods vanishes, Valenti sends the **dock boss** — who knows every crane operator and which **ward-inspector** takes cash. Volume, not prestige; books messy on purpose.

**NPC seed — Lena "The Grease" Valenti:** 38, Red Hook terminal dock boss. Steel-toed boots, St. Michael pendant (consecrated at licensed shrine). Signs invoices on **truth-bind** paper that glows if the amount lies. PCs know her as smuggle-anything — for favor, rarely cash. Two kids in parochial school; ex-husband owes guild child-support. Ambition: **Holland Tunnel** route that bypasses Moretti inspection fee.

## Moretti — Little Italy / protection & clerical services

Historic heart — Mulberry social clubs, linen restaurants, **cleric chaplains** who bless restaurants, bind ghosts to burial sites, and whisper last rites that cost a fortune. Protection racket oldest in the city: every restaurant, bakery, funeral home below Houston pays; Chaplain sweeps spirits every Thursday. Don's eldest serves year as Chaplain's apprentice before rank. Real power: **burial rite registry** — who died with debts, which souls bound, which graves hold more than bones. Chaplains answer temple **Arcane Affairs**, not **BCC** — jurisdictional loophole.

**NPC seed — Tommy "Two Prayers" Moretti:** 29, underboss-in-waiting, Chaplain's apprentice. Drives a **'31 Packard** with ward glyph in glovebox. Handles delicate blessings — haunting kitchen, funeral where body is evidence. Wants to **modernize**: duplicate registry via **speaking-tube copies** to Staten archive; uncle the Don thinks tubes are for degenerates. Filial loyalty vs gilded cage.

## Chen-Okafor — Queens / gambling & divination fixing

Youngest Commission family — Chinese and Nigerian networks consolidated late '20s — Flushing-to-Jamaica corridor, **gambling and divination-fixed numbers**. Every mahjong parlor, backroom poker, OTB satellite runs on their terms because they own the **luck**. Diviners Class-C licensed on paper; beyond limits they read odds before they happen. Don't break legs — break probability. Money cleanest in the organization; older families resent a house that sees the future.

**NPC seed — David Chen-Okafor:** 34, public face — "community lending" office Main Street, Flushing. Tailored suits; pays with **truth-bind IOUs** and cashier's checks, not street cash. Loaded divination die in breast pocket — always rolls well; never used. "Knowing the outcome is a kind of poverty." Father's dementia; fighting cousins for succession. Needs PCs to deliver message to Valenti crew skimming joint route — **in person, no scry** (diviners warned of intercept).

## Kowalski — Staten Island / waste, logistics & disappearing

Commission's trash men. Waste-hauling contracts, Fresh Kills "special disposal," trucks moving what Sanitation doesn't know exists. **Necromancy taboo** open secret — river talk, bodies that don't surface, patriarch's basement. Deny everything; drivers carry iron bells, never alone at night. Move evidence, witnesses, cursed artifacts, summoned entities, people who must **not exist** six months. Nobody likes them; everybody uses them. Garbage trucks cross checkpoints.

**NPC seed — Dominika "Docks" Kowalski:** 45, Bayonne–Staten logistics coordinator. Reflective vest; rosary one pocket, **gentle repose** scroll the other. Macabre reputation = business asset: "Fear sets a fair price." Bard Avenue row house; three cats; basement door never opened. Needs PCs to find who's dumping **unauthorized corpses** in her landfill — OCU-analog **federal ward-men** trace it, hauling license revoked, cover blown.

## Ashford — Midtown / money laundering & pact consultancy

Don't look like a crime family. Fourteenth-floor Madison office — receptionist, conference room, espresso from a **spirit-wind pulley** (rare automaton garnish). Commission's **white-collar arm**: laundering, political connections, real estate, **warlock pacts as corporate retainers**. Consultant doesn't cast — **negotiates** with entities that do; payment as deferred equity; liability in Delaware shell. Cleanest suits, most dangerous. Don hosts quarterly sit-downs, serves scotch, never votes. Neutrality works because nobody sees the ledger in his head.

**NPC seed — Julian Ashford:** 55, managing partner, Don. Never held weapon, never ordered hit. Bespoke suits; University Club squash; wife thinks he's a lawyer. Single painting — Renaissance portrait, eyes follow you — "investment." Knows **full map** of every family's magical debts. PCs meet him when something must vanish from Commission memory — price is future favor, never cash now.

## Commission dynamics (1931)

Quarterly sit-downs at Ashford's office:

- **No miracle hits on civilians without vote.** Broken often; never acknowledged.
- **Territorial disputes → Commission**, not street. Ashford bills both sides.
- **The Ledger** (independent fixers) — neutral delivery; no family may kill them.
- **Federal heat** — when **BCC/OTR** push hard, families pool bribes, discredit inspectors, reassign prosecutors.
