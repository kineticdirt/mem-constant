## The hook — "The Sea Wolf handoff" (reskinned)

Valenti dock boss **Lena "The Grease"** contacts PCs through a cutout: package missing en route Red Hook → Kowalski disposal. Package: **leather-bound ledger** — thirty years Valenti customs manifests (ships, dates, **cursed cargo**, bribe recipients, **ward-inspector** initials). **Federal truth-bind push** building; ledger cornerstone of indictment. Lena can't send family muscle — off-books handoff ties Valenti directly. Needs **unattached operators** before **Commission of Charms** convenes grand examination in **ten days**.

**Twist:** Ledger is **cursed** — holder >24 hours sees faces of dead from cargo listed inside. Last courier catatonic in Bellevue charity ward. Chain broke once; ledger absorbed psychic residue each handoff.

**Crew angle:** **$500 cash up front** (1931 money — significant) + **Valenti favor** (dock access, clean car, pallet untraceable elixir). Kowalskis offer **$300 finder's fee** — close the loop. Work one side, both, or neither.

## Locations (period)

### 1. Sea Wolf Tattoo — Red Hook

Valenti-affiliated tattoo parlor on Van Brunt — biker front, sideline **enchanted ink** (ward-glyphs, memory tattoos, pact brands). Courier missed Kowalski pickup.

**Now:** **Mickey "The Needle" Fusco** (60s, ex-enforcer). Swears ledger never arrived; Valenti crew already leaned on him. Hiding: apprentice **Danny** stashed it, went to ground.

**There:** Alchemical back room; ward-glyph flash book / coded address book; floor safe — Danny's Sunset Park address; **Kowalski dispatcher** card Mickey "doesn't remember."

### 2. Golden Dragon Mahjong — Flushing

Chen-Okafor territory. Ledger in Kowalski driver's trunk **two hours** — stolen during mahjong stop.

**Now:** **Auntie Mei** (70s, elder). Clean game; divination for gamblers. Shares thief name if PC sits for reading — free; cards decide what she sees.

**There:** Eight tables; Buddha filing cabinet with day-book. Thief: **Eddie Trillo**, freelance courier for **the Ledger** (fixers' guild). Took trunk with **knock** cantrip; panicking.

### 3. BQE rest stop — Exit 29

Eddie left ledger in **coin locker** at gas station under BQE. Plans morning call to Ledger drop — policy avoids known-cursed items; Eddie unaware of curse.

**Now:** **Rafi** (night attendant, sees everything). Locker **#14**. Valenti tail car across street (two young crew followed from Red Hook).

**There:** Coin lockers, 24-hour counter, **speaking-tube booth**, broken stamp machine, cats. Clean approach: sandwich, rent locker, blind spot.

## Complications (1931)

### C1 — Ward on the ledger

**Wizard-locked** — Valenti hired ward-specialist through dead cutout. Only bloodline opens without **glyph of warding** (force + audible pop → safehouse signal). Lena unaware. Specialist in Mickey's book: **Harlow "Cipher" Duvall**, Ashford-owned Murray Hill flat.

**Table:** coerce Cipher; *dispel magic* + alert; Kowalski burn unread; Chen-Okafor diviner loophole.

### C2 — Valenti tail

Told ledger lists **federal cooperators** — Lena lied. **Vinnie Palermo** has clipped brother ('28); if he opens ledger, ward kills him, **BCC** buries everyone.

**Table:** deal with Vinnie; misdirection; non-lethal takedown; let fallout happen.

### C3 — Kowalski roadblock

Driver lost ledger at mahjong; should have incinerated at Fresh Kills. **Dominika "Docks"** + two disposal specialists hunting. Knows Eddie's route. **$100 tip** for intelligence.

**Table:** cooperate = favor; cross = truck follows three blocks; double-agent = necromancy-adjacent specialists notice.

### C4 — Ashford frame bait

Julian's office buying Valenti customs debt six months. Paid **Ledger operative** to plant **fake pact document** in locker **#15**. OCU-analog finds both → frame implicates Chen-Okafor accountant; exonerates Ashford. PCs framed as Ashford assets either way.

**Table:** Rafi saw 1 a.m. drop; take both = only ones who know Ashford's play.
