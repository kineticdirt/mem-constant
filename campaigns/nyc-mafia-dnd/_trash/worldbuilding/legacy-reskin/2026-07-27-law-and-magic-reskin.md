## Magic evidence — admissibility (1931)

Thin case law; SDNY drafting **miracle evidence rules**.

| Evidence type | Admissible? | Notes |
|---------------|-------------|-------|
| **Spell residue** (certified reader affidavit) | Yes | Within 24 hours; defense challenges certification |
| **Compelled testimony circle** | Corroboration only | *People v. Marchetti* (1928): arcane compulsion ≠ sole evidence |
| **Scry vision** | No — hearsay | *People v. Kowalski* (1924): warrants ok, not trial testimony |
| **Cursed object** | Yes — blessing + dispel first | Chain: two officers witness blessing; cursed ledger nightmare — ink vs examiner |
| **Memory stitch exposure** | Mistrial if discovered | Federal **stitch-victim** registry bars witness |
| **Pact contract (infernal)** | As written agreement | Ashford uses UCC framing; raw Abyssal needs certified translator |

**Ward-lock problem:** Enchanted objects need licensed reader before lockup. Broken chain (unlicensed handler, undocumented dispel, non-anti-scry bag) = tainted. Valenti crew claims ward broken at arrest → "unattributed magical material."
