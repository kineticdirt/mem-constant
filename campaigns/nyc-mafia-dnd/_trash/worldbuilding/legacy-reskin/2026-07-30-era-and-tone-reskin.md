## Era: 1931 Prohibition New York

- **Rotary phones and speaking-tubes** — long-distance talk is scheduled mirror-scry or ward-tube splice; nothing is always-on in your pocket.
- **Cash and truth-bind ledgers** — families still move bags of cash; big scores use **warded safes** and **notary-clerics**; crypto does not exist.
- **Federal heat** — **OTR** audits charm licenses; **BCC** seizes unregistered miracles; **NYPD ward-inspectors** take bribes at salon doors. Evidence is wax seals, compelled testimony circles, and seized stitch-pairs — not Ring doorbells.
- **Old grudges, old tools** — The Five run unions, gambling, and **blind-tiger salons** beside gin. Tommy guns plus **etched rounds** (rare); healing as patent elixir or bootleg salon.
