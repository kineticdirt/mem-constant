## Core rule: three tiers (1931 grammar)

Not "does magic exist?" — **who sees what, on which block, and what does federal law cost them?**

### Tier 1 — Open street magic (ward-open)

Magic so mundane it's **trade**, not scandal — **Red Hook, LES, Harlem street, Chinatown basements, Bushwick halls.**

| What | Who sees it | Who provides it |
|------|-------------|-----------------|
| Healing lay / tincture at a **healing hole** | Anyone with cash or barter | Barber, midwife, dock mutual-aid |
| Luck tray / dice blessing | The gambler | Street cleric, numbers runner |
| Forge-shrine oath on a pier | The crew | Dwarf mutual-aid, union hall |
| Speaking-tube splice tip | The client | Guild operator, bootleg back room |

**Rule:** In **open wards**, Tier 1 doesn't draw **federal** heat — ward-inspectors want **shakedown**, not martyrdom. Unregistered **still** behind the curtain is still bootleg.

### Tier 2 — Hidden underworld magic (family layer)

Magic that scares civilians, tips **BCC**, or breaks Commission rules — **primary PC operating layer.**

| What | Who knows | Why it's hidden |
|------|-----------|-----------------|
| Combat charms / etched rounds | Shooter and target | Burns get questions; bodies get river |
| Illegal second question / ghost-bind | Kowalski network | Taboo; witness tampering |
| Scry-proof back room (unlicensed) | Renter and ward-smith | Looks like a normal office until raid |
| Forged **OTR stamps** / fake BCC seals | Valenti supply chain | Counterfeit miracle — seizure + truth-bind perjury |
| Planar contact / binding without notary | Caster and the thing | Unregistered; Below attention |

**Rule:** Tier 2 exposure gets the party **made** or **burned**. A **flare ward** in a tenement courtyard means someone telegraphed **BCC** — and the family that owns the block asks who embarrassed them.

### Tier 3 — Licensed face (OTR + BCC)

Legal magic with paperwork, inspectors, mob kickbacks.

| What | License needed | How families profit |
|------|----------------|---------------------|
| Permanent wards / vault seals | **BCC** Class-2 + **OTR** stamp | Valenti sells forged permits at 60% of guild rate |
| Commercial divination parlors | BCC Seer License | Chen-Okafor owns inspection routes |
| Clerical rites / one-question necromancy | Temple endorsement + BCC log | Moretti controls burial permits |
| Binding consultancy / pact framing | BCC Binding Agent + notary-cleric | Ashford writes contracts as "advisory retainers" |
| Registered **link-pair** fitting | OTR intimacy tax + witness | Bootleg **unregistered link** parallel gin (D4 high) |

**Rule:** Licensed magic is the **front**. Every legitimate permit has a family cut. Clean paperwork lets Midtown clients pretend Tier 1 doesn't exist upstairs.

## Party cheat sheet

- **Open ward** = name salons, tubes, luck; horns unremarkable on the pier
- **Denial ward** = glamour is lighting; service entrance; **rudeness** punished
- **Hidden** = conceal components, burn guest logs, cover story
- **Licensed** = keep OTR stamp current; know which ward-inspector owns which family
- **BCC** chases Tier 2 miracles and bootleg links; **OTR** audits Tier 3 books
- **NYPD ward-inspectors** don't care about theology — they care about **cuts**
