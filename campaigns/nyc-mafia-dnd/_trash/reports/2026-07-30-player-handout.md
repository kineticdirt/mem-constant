## The Setup

**New York City, 1999.** Magic is real — and illegal. The Five Families control the grey market: stolen spell scrolls, forged healing licenses, warded counting rooms, warlock accountants who never sleep. The ALB (Arcane Licensing Board) issues permits for "legitimate" magic use. The OCU (Organized Crime Unit, Arcane Division) investigates unlicensed magical crime. Between them sits the Commission — the Five Families' governing body, which quietly regulates who gets to sell what.

You're a crew of operators caught in the middle. Fixers, muscle, mages-for-hire, family-adjacent operators. The job comes from Valenti — a dock boss named Lena "The Grease." She needs a package recovered before a federal grand jury convenes in 10 days.

## The Job: "The Sea Wolf Handoff"

**Employer:** Lena "The Grease" (Valenti dock boss, Red Hook)
**Pay:** $5,000 up front + a Valenti favor (dock access, clean car, alchemical goods)
**Deadline:** 10 days (federal grand jury)

**What happened:** A leather-bound ledger containing 30 years of Valenti customs manifests (dates, cargo, bribes, ALB inspector initials) was being moved from Red Hook to a Kowalski disposal site. The courier never made it. The ledger is now in the hands of a freelance courier named Eddie Trillo, who doesn't know what he's holding — or that it's cursed.

**The ledger's curse:** Anyone who holds it for more than 24 hours starts seeing the faces of people who died because of cargo listed inside. The last courier went catatonic.

**The twist:** The ledger is wizard-locked. Only a specific bloodline can open it without triggering a *glyph of warding*. The ward specialist — Harlow "Cipher" Duvall — is in Ashford territory.

## Quick Reference — Law & Consequences

| Threat | What It Means |
|--------|---------------|
| **OCU** | NYPD Arcane Division (~12 detectives, 2 forensic mages). Response time: 15 min (Manhattan) to 45 min (Brooklyn off-shift). |
| **RICO** | Federal indictment = frozen assets, tapped phones, cooperating witnesses. Two magical predicate acts within 10 years = RICO charge. |
| **ALB** | Arcane Licensing Board. Issues permits. Corrupt liaisons on OCU payroll. Can certify or block magical evidence. |
| **OCU Heat Scale** | 1–5. Every overt magical incident, unlicensed cast in public, or family-connected assault raises heat. At 5: OCU stakeout, warrants, grand jury subpoenas. |
