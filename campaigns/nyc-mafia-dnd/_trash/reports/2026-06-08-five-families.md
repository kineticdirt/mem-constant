## The Five Families — expanded

### Valenti (Brooklyn / docks)

The Valentis run the waterfront the way their grandparents ran the longshoreman unions — through a mix of legitimate logistics companies, a shell game of LLCs, and the quiet understanding that every container coming through Red Hook gets inspected by someone on their payroll. Their supernatural edge is **artificer forgery**: enchanted cargo manifests that pass both mundane customs scans and ALB ward-checks, plus a sideline in cursed antiques that "fall off the truck" and end up in estate sales across the tri-state area. The family is old-blood Italian-American but has intermarried with Haitian and Dominican crews over the decades; their magic tradition is part Catholic folk saint devotion, part Caribbean brujería, filtered through a very Brooklyn pragmatism. They're the family most likely to have a problem that requires the PCs' specific skill set — a cursed shipment that's killing dockworkers, a forged manifest that's about to be audited, a union vote that needs supernatural persuasion.

**NPC seed — Lucia Valenti ("The Appraiser"):** Mid-40s, runs the family's "antiques and appraisal" front in DUMBO. She's the one who identifies what's magical in a seized shipment and decides whether to sell it, destroy it, or use it. Speaks four languages, has a Seeing Eye dog that's actually a re-skinned familiar. Hates the Kowalski family with a personal intensity that predates any business dispute.

---

### Moretti (Little Italy / Lower Manhattan)

The Morettis are the family that still does things "the old way" — and in this city, "the old way" means clerical magic dressed up as Catholic tradition. Their "chaplains" are actual clerics who perform burial rites that bind ghosts to family service, run underground healing clinics for soldiers who can't go to a hospital, and maintain a network of safe houses warded against scoping and divination. The family controls a string of restaurants and social clubs in Little Italy and the Lower East Side that serve as fronts, meeting places, and — in the case of their flagship social club on Mulberry Street — a consecrated ground where their bound dead can manifest. They're the most overtly religious of the five families, which makes them both the most principled and the most dangerous: a Moretti hit comes with last rites.

**NPC seed — Father Tommaso Moretti:** Late 50s, a genuine priest (defrocked, technically, but he kept the collar) who serves as the family's chief chaplain and spiritual advisor. He believes the family's ghost-binding tradition is a form of mercy — giving the dead purpose instead of letting them fade. He's wrong, or at least incomplete, and the ghosts know it. He has a crisis of faith arc waiting to happen if the PCs ever get close enough to trigger it.

---

### Chen-Okafor (Queens / Flushing corridor)

The Chen-Okafor family is the newest of the five, having earned their Commission seat in the early 2000s through a combination of aggressive expansion, strategic marriages, and a supernatural specialty nobody else could match: **divination as a service industry**. They run the numbers rackets, the sports betting, and the underground poker games — but their real product is *information*. Chen-Okafor fixers sell curated divination readings to stock traders, politicians, and other families. Their Flushing corridor territory is a mix of Chinese, Korean, and West African businesses, and the family itself reflects that diversity: the Chen and Okafor bloodlines merged two decades ago, and their magical tradition blends I Ching, Ifá, and a proprietary form of probability magic that lets them nudge odds in closed systems. They're the family most comfortable with technology — enchanted smartphones, ward-protected server farms, algorithmic divination.

**NPC seed — David Chen-Okafor ("The Bookie"):** Early 30s, the family's public face for their "market research consultancy" in Flushing. He's a statistics PhD dropout who realized he could make more money applying Bayesian inference to divination than to academia. Runs a tight operation, keeps digital records (encrypted, warded, and backed up to a cloud server he's 90% sure isn't compromised). Has a younger sister in law enforcement who doesn't know what the family really does — a ticking time bomb.

---

### Kowalski (Staten Island / bridges)

The Kowalskis are the family everyone else whispers about. They control waste management, logistics, and the art of making things — and people — disappear. Their territory on Staten Island and their stranglehold on the bridges and tunnels give them a monopoly on *movement* in and out of the city. The supernatural rumor that follows them is **necromancy**: rivals say they talk to the river, that the harbor dead do their bidding, that their waste processing plants are processing more than garbage. The truth is more nuanced — the Kowalskis practice a form of ancestor veneration that borders on necromancy, drawing on the spirits of the dead who died in the waterways around Staten Island. They're Polish-American, insular, and deeply suspicious of outsiders. They don't come to Commission meetings; the Commission comes to them.

**NPC seed — Vera Kowalski ("The Ferryman"):** Late 60s, the family matriarch and the one who actually communicates with the river dead. She's a retired sanitation worker who discovered her gift after a near-drowning in the Kill van Kull. She doesn't leave Staten Island anymore — not because she can't, but because the dead are louder there, and she's needed. She speaks to the PCs only if they come to her, and only if they bring an offering from the water.

---

### Ashford (Midtown towers)

The Ashfords are the white-collar family, the ones who wear suits to Commission meetings and file taxes on their supernatural income. They operate out of Midtown high-rises, laundering money through real estate, hedge funds, and a "security consulting" firm that's actually a warlock pact brokerage. Their supernatural angle is **warlock pacts framed as consulting contracts**: they connect aspiring magic-users with extraplanar entities, take a percentage, and provide legal (ALB-compliant) paperwork that makes the whole thing look like a B2B transaction. They're the cleanest family — no bodies, no curses, no ghosts — which makes them the most dangerous in a different way. They own judges, ALB clerks, and at least two city council members. They're also the most likely to have a PC on retainer as a "consultant."

**NPC seed — Marcus Ashford ("The Consultant"):** Mid-40s, the family's chief warlock and managing partner of Ashford Security Consulting. He's a former corporate lawyer who made his first pact at 29 and never looked back. He's charming, impeccably dressed, and genuinely believes that regulated, contractual magic is better for everyone than the alternative. He's not wrong, which makes him a more interesting antagonist (or ally) than a simple villain. He has a patron he never speaks about, and a clause in his contract he's never told anyone.
