# A-5 — PC Role Menu

**Date:** 2026-07-07
**Campaign:** NYC Mafia × D&D
**Item:** A-5 — PC role menu (6–8 archetypes for the table)
**Status:** Draft — propose 1–2 per player, table picks and modifies freely

---

## 1. The Fixer — "I know a guy who knows a guy"

You're the one with the address book. Contacts, safe houses, black market rates, and which ALB clerk takes a bribe on the third Tuesday of the month. You don't need to be the best shot — you need to know who to call.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **D&D class** | Bard (College of Eloquence), Mastermind Rogue, or Knowledge Cleric |
| **Magic** | Licensed Tier 1 permitted (message, guidance, identify); grey-market divination if you have the right ALB front |
| **Party role** | Intel, negotiation, job sourcing, "the guy who walks into a sit-down alone because the families know they can't touch you" |
| **Family fit** | Chen-Okafor (divination pipeline), Valenti (cargo intel), or independent (no family, works referrals) |
| **Hook** | You owe a marker to Lucia Valenti from a job where the intel was bad. She didn't call it in — yet. |

---

## 2. The Enforcer — "Make them understand"

You handle the part of the job that requires physical persuasion. Muscle, intimidation, breach-and-clear, or just standing in the right doorway at the right time so the other guy reconsiders.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **D&D class** | Fighter (Battle Master), Barbarian, or Paladin (Oath of Conquest / Vengeance) |
| **Magic** | None or minimal (Tier 2 grey combat magic if you have a warlock on retainer) |
| **Party role** | Combat anchor, intimidation, asset protection, "the one who makes the crew's threats credible" |
| **Family fit** | Kowalski (river muscle), Moretti (chaplain-backed), or hired gun independent |
| **Hook** | Your last crew got made — OCU has a file on you with a grainy photo and a note: "known associate, Kowalski circuit." You can't afford another sheet. |

---

## 3. The Mage — "I handle the weird"

You're the one the crew calls when the job has a component they can't crack with lockpicks or leverage. Cursed cargo, warded doors, a ghost that won't stay put — magic is your trade, and in this city, trade requires a license or a family connection.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **D&D class** | Wizard, Sorcerer, or Warlock |
| **Magic** | Licensed Tier 1 (day job front) + grey-market Tier 2 combat/illusion/enchantment for crew work |
| **Party role** | Supernatural problem-solving, counterspelling rival family magic, arcane research, "the one who actually reads the ALB permit fine print" |
| **Family fit** | Ashford (warlock consulting contract), Moretti (clerical tradition), or rogue unlicensed (dangerous — OCU priority target) |
| **Hook** | Your ALB license was suspended six months ago and you haven't reapplied. Every job you cast on is a felony. The families know. They're just not saying anything — yet. |

---

## 4. The Driver — "I'll get you there. The question is whether you'll like where we end up."

Cars, boats, trucks, the occasional enchanted cargo van that runs on something other than gasoline. You move product, people, and bodies. In a city of bridges and tunnels, the person behind the wheel controls the timeline.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **D&D class** | Rogue (Thief or Scout), Artificer (Armorer — for the tricked-out vehicle), or Fighter (Cavalier) |
| **Magic** | None needed; some drivers pick up a Tier 1 utility cantrip (prestidigitation for plates, mage hand for quick cargo swaps) |
| **Party role** | Exfil, pursuit, logistics, route planning across family-controlled territory, "the one who knows which tunnel the Kowalskis let you use at 3 AM" |
| **Family fit** | Valenti (dock trucks), Kowalski (bridge logistics), or freelance across all five |
| **Hook** | You ran a Valenti shipment through a Kowalski checkpoint without the customary offering. The Kowalskis let it pass — which means they want something, and they'll find you when they're ready to collect. |

---

## 5. The Face — "Let me do the talking"

The one who walks into a Commission sit-down, a club back room, or a mayoral fundraiser and gets what the crew needs without drawing a weapon. You're a negotiator, a liar, and occasionally a spy.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **D&D class** | Bard (College of Glamour or Whispers), Swashbuckler Rogue, or Sorcerer (Aberrant Mind for subtle enchantment) |
| **Magic** | Tier 2 grey-market enchantment/illusion — carefully, because OCU watches for unauthorized mind-affecting magic on civilians |
| **Party role** | Social infiltration, information gathering, deal-making, "the one who can walk into an Ashford midtown office without a warrant" |
| **Family fit** | Ashford (corporate front), Chen-Okafor (information trades), or Valenti (union negotiations) |
| **Hook** | You burned an Ashford consultant on a deal six months ago. Marcus Ashford doesn't hold grudges — he holds promissory notes. Yours is accruing interest. |

---

## 6. The Handler — "I keep us in one piece"

Medic, therapist, ritual cleanser, and crew morale. You patch bullet wounds, dispel lingering curses, and talk down the enforcer after a job that went sideways. In a world of haunted cargo and tempers, you're the one who keeps the crew functional.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **D&D class** | Cleric (Life, Grave, or Twilight), Circle of Dreams Druid, or Divine Soul Sorcerer |
| **Magic** | Licensed Tier 1 (registered clergy or ALB medical permit) + deep knowledge of Tier 3 cleansing rites (unlicensed, but the families don't ask) |
| **Party role** | Healing, curse removal, magical hygiene, moral compass, "the one who gets called when a Moretti ghost-binding goes septic" |
| **Family fit** | Moretti (chaplain tradition), or independent / ex-Church (defrocked, working the grey economy because the ALB won't re-license you) |
| **Hook** | You were assigned to a Moretti burial that went wrong — the ghost didn't bind, and instead of fixing it, you walked. Father Tommaso hasn't forgotten. He hasn't reported you either. He's waiting. |

---

## 7. The Infiltrator — "They won't see me until I want them to"

Second-story work, safe cracking, data exfiltration, and the art of being somewhere you're not supposed to be. In a world of warded vaults, the infiltrator is the counterweight to magical defenses.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **D&D class** | Arcane Trickster Rogue, Shadow Monk, or Gloom Stalker Ranger |
| **Magic** | Minimal — a few Tier 1 utility spells (knock, invisibility if you can get a scroll) plus mundane expertise. The best infiltrators don't need magic; they just need to be faster than the ward response. |
| **Party role** | B&E, surveillance countermeasure, intel retrieval, "the one who gets the manifest photo before the job starts" |
| **Family fit** | Independent / freelancer (all families hire outside talent for deniable work) |
| **Hook** | The last safe you cracked belonged to a Chen-Okafor accountant. You left the cash and took the ledger. The Bookie knows you're missing something and has put out feelers for a "data recovery specialist." That specialist is probably you. |

---

## 8. The Wild Card — "You'll know my job when you see me do it"

An archetype that defies easy categorization — a genuine occult investigator, a forensic accountant who reads aura spreads into spreadsheet columns, a Kowalski river-witch who crossed the family, or a former OCU turned unlicensed operative. This slot is open for the table to invent.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **D&D class** | Any class reflavored — the chassis matters less than the backstory |
| **Magic** | Whatever fits the concept; the "Wild Card" is defined by having an angle nobody at the table has planned for |
| **Party role** | Surprise utility, plot hook delivery system, "the one whose past is going to catch up with them mid-season" |
| **Family fit** | Any — or none. A Wild Card with no family ties is the biggest liability and the biggest asset. |
| **Hook** | You have information that two families want suppressed and one family wants published. You haven't decided which side to sell it to yet. The crew is your insurance policy — or your exit plan. |

---

## Table notes

- **Mix & match:** players can combine archetypes (the Driver who also knows a bit of magic, the Face who handles the crew's intel, etc.)
- **Class is flexible:** the archetype describes *what you do for the crew*; the class and subclass are negotiation items between player and DM
- **Family ties are optional:** a crew of independents is viable and has its own dynamics — every family is a potential employer or enemy
- **First session hook:** each archetype has a seeded hook that can be developed into a session-1 tie to the Five Families or the world at large
