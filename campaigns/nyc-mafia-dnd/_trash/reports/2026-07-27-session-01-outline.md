## Scene 1 — "The Grease's Welcome" (Investigation / Social)

**Location:** Sea Wolf Tattoo, Red Hook, Brooklyn (A-4 cold open)
**Tone:** Noir introductions. The crew meets each other through action, not a round-table.

**Beat:** The crew arrives at Sea Wolf Tattoo — separately or together — in the wake of a Valenti enforcer visit. Mickey "The Needle" Fusco is behind the counter, one-clockwork-handed, sweating under the fluorescent lights. He's just told the enforcers the ledger never arrived. He'll tell the crew the same thing — unless someone notices the apprentice's jacket draped over a chair in the back, or the safe under the floorboards isn't flush with the concrete.

**Pressure points:**
- **Mickey's tell:** He keeps touching his prosthetic hand (nervous habit). A successful Insight (DC 13) reveals he's hiding something specific, not just general fear.
- **The back room:** Half-finished tattoo commissions include a ward-glyph flash book that's actually a coded address book (cursive marginalia visible under *detect magic* or a DC 15 Investigation check). The safe contains Danny's Sunset Park address.
- **Pacing:** The Valenti enforcers are still parked outside. The crew has maybe 10–15 minutes inside before one comes back to "check the back room." A Parley roll or bribe (Fixer role) can buy more time.

**Exit trigger:** The crew finds Danny's address (Sunset Park, girlfriend's apartment) — or follows Mickey's glance to the Gowanus Canal direction when he says "the kid probably went underground."

**Scene 1 complication (choose or roll):**

| 1 | The Valenti tail follows the crew from the shop | Car chase or misdirection scene |
| 2 | Danny left a *sending* stone — one message stored: "Don't look for me" in a woman's voice | Introduces the girlfriend as a lead |

## Scene 2 — "Three Threads" (Investigation / Chase)

**Locations:** Sunset Park apartment (Brooklyn) + Golden Dragon Mahjong (Flushing, Queens) via BQE
**Tone:** Multiple leads converge. The crew splits or prioritizes.

**Beat:** Danny's girlfriend's apartment in Sunset Park — a walk-up above a bodega. The door is unlocked. Inside: a half-packed duffel bag, a burner flip phone on the nightstand with one outgoing call to a number with a 718 prefix, and a mahjong tile (east wind) on the kitchen table — a calling card from Eddie Trillo. Danny is gone. The girlfriend is at work (night shift at a laundromat). The burner phone's last call is to **Golden Dragon Mahjong** in Flushing.

**Pressure points:**
- **The mahjong tile:** A Chen-Okafor signal — someone at the Golden Dragon knows about the ledger. A Lore (underworld) or History (NYC underworld, DC 14) check identifies the tile as a "marker" — the bearer is asking for a sit-down.
- **The girlfriend's schedule:** She works at **Happy Suds Laundromat** on 5th Ave, 10 PM–6 AM. If the crew waits for her, they lose time. If they search the apartment thoroughly (Investigation DC 12), they find a photo of Danny and Eddie Trillo together at Coney Island — they're cousins.
- **The BQE route:** Driving from Sunset Park to Flushing takes ~30 mins in 1999 traffic (no Waze, no smartphones). The Valenti tail from Scene 1 may still be following. A Driver role can lose them via the Gowanus Expressway on-ramp (Driving contest).

**Golden Dragon Mahjong (arrival):** A bustling parlor — eight tables, incense smoke, low chatter. Auntie Mei presides from a center table, shuffling tiles with a practiced rhythm. She won't give up Eddie's name for free. She demands a reading for one of the PCs. The reading (cowrie shells or tarot, depending on how the crew describes the PC) reveals a fragment of the next 24 hours: *"A man in a green coat will open a metal door under an overpass. After that: fog."*

**Exit trigger:** Auntie Mei gives up Eddie Trillo's name — freelance courier, runs jobs for the Ledger, currently panicking and lying low. Last seen heading toward the BQE corridor with a storage locker key.

**Scene 2 complication (choose or roll):**

## Scene 3 — "The BQE Handoff" (Recovery / Climax)

**Location:** BQE Rest Stop, Exit 29, Brooklyn-Queens border (A-4)
**Tone:** Multiple factions converge on the same spot. Timed pressure.

**Beat:** The crew arrives at the rest stop — a 24-hour gas station / deli with coin-operated storage lockers under the BQE overpass. The Valenti tail is parked across the street (two crew members, watching). Kowalski disposal specialists are pulling in from the other direction. Eddie Trillo is already here — green coat, nervous, holding a storage locker key in his hand, standing at the locker bank. He doesn't know the ledger is cursed. He hasn't opened it.

**The standoff — three approaches:**

**A — Quiet recovery (social/deception):** The crew approaches Eddie as "the people the Ledger sent to take possession." A Deception (DC 13) or Intimidation (DC 12) check convinces him to hand over the key. He leaves immediately. The crew opens Locker #14 — the ledger is inside, wrapped in a cloth. The Ashford plant document is in Locker #15 (A-4 complication). The crew has 2–3 minutes before the Valenti tail realizes Eddie is leaving empty-handed.

**B — Leverage (bargain):** The crew explains the ledger is cursed and offers Eddie a cut of the finder's fee ($1,000) or Kowalski protection. He's scared — Persuasion (DC 12) or a good argument about the curse works. He hands over the key and a warning: *"Don't hold it more than a day. That thing — it talks to you."*

**C — Force (combat):** A Valenti tail spots the crew reaching Eddie. Combat breaks out — 2 Valenti thugs (low-level, pistols, no magic) vs the crew. Kowalski disposal specialists watch from a distance, not intervening. Eddie drops the key in the scuffle. The crew has 3 rounds to secure the key and the ledger before:
- Gunfire draws a 911 call → OCU response in 6 rounds (B-2 response time)
- Kowalski team decides to claim the ledger for themselves
- Eddie runs (Athletics DC 10 to catch him)

**After recovery — the ledger check:** Whoever picks up the ledger must make a Wisdom saving throw (DC 12) or feel the first wave of the curse — a fleeting vision of a dead face (the ledger's last courier, who went catatonic). The Ashford plant document (Locker #15) is visible if anyone checks adjacent lockers. Taking both items means the crew knows about Ashford's frame — but carrying two cursed-adjacent documents increases the curse pressure.

**Session 1 end state:**
| Outcome | What happens next |
|---------|------------------|
| Ledger recovered, crew clean | Session 2: find Harlow "Cipher" Duvall to dispel the ward (or deliver to Kowalski for destruction) |
| Ledger recovered, OCU on scene | Session 2: the crew is swept into an OCU interview — they need a cover story and a lawyer |
| Ledger lost (Valenti or Kowalski got it) | Session 2: the crew must steal it back from whichever family has it — now with a grudge |
| Ledger destroyed | Session 2: Valenti needs a new strategy; the crew owes Lena $5k or a replacement |
