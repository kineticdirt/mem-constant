# Era + tone note — NYC Mafia × D&D
**Date:** 2026-07-24
**Status:** Draft — table canon may override

## Era: Present-day, 2026

**Why today:** The table wants a mafia D&D. Modern NYC has immediate visual texture (smartphones, surveillance, traffic, social media, mixed immigration). Law has real modern stakes: OCU/special narcotics, RICO, wiretaps, Broken Windows. Magic can sit in the shadows of a city that already ignores half what it sees.

**Flashback exception:** PCs can carry prequels only. A character might have been recruited in the 1990s (age 55+), but their *play* happens now. The campaign clock is current-day unless the session explicitly cuts to Bombay/Calabria backstory.

**Tech level:**
- Smartphones ubiquitous (Signal/WhatsApp for capos; burner culture still alive).
- Transit: subway + rideshare + personal car; no hovercars, no cybernetics publicly visible.
- Surveillance: NYPD real-time cameras + dmv + toll readers; magic that leaves no digital trace is the real power.

## Tone: Crime drama, not gangland cartoon

- **Serious stakes, realistic scale.** Five Families still exist, but they are leaner, legal-entangled, and more paranoid. A made guy is likely a mid-level manager in a family construction/union shell — not a loud suit on the docks.
- **Urban fantasy is quiet, not neon.** Magic is mostly invisible to civilians. A public hex is a news story; a private curse is a body in an alley. The tone should feel like *The Sopranos* × * Dresden Files* (closed-circle investigation, personal loyalties, supernatural leaks).
- **Violence is cheap until it isn't.** Knife-and-fist in the bathroom; guns in the car trunk; explosives only when someone wants federal attention. Magic inflates stakes — a Sleep hex on a rival captain is worth more than two bullets because bullets leave ballistics.
- **Morality is gray.** Cops who look the other way, capos who donate to church, witches who rent lofts. No one is purely clean. PCs should feel the pull between Oath (family/sponsor) and personal code.
- **No glorification.** No romanticized "wise guy" voice. Play is immersive, not performative.

## Palette cues for the table

- **Visual:** Fluorescent bodega signs, diesel-fume alleyways, brownstone stoops, ferry horn + sirens, basement social clubs with smoked glass.
- **Sound:** Hip-hop / Italo-disco in the ride; static walkie in the warehouse; silence in the back room.
- **Magic hint:** A single candle left burning for Strega; tarot cards in the lockbox; salt line taped under the threshold.

## One-line elevator pitch

> 2026 NYC. Five Families run the city's honest and dishonest dollars. Magic runs the part between.
