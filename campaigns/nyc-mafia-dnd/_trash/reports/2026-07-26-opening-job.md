# A-4 — Opening Job One-Pager

**Date:** 2026-07-26
**Campaign:** NYC Mafia × D&D (1999, Brooklyn Gothic)
**Status:** DRAFT — table canon pending
**Premise hook:** Recover a **cursed evidence ledger** before a federal grand jury convenes

---

## The Hook — "The Sea Wolf Handoff"

A Valenti dock boss (Lena "The Grease") contacts the PCs through a cutout: a package went missing en route from Red Hook to a Kowalski disposal site. The package is a **leather-bound ledger** containing thirty years of Valenti customs manifests — ships, dates, cursed cargo, bribe recipients, ALB inspector initials. A **federal RICO push** is building, and that ledger is the cornerstone of the indictment. Lena can't send family muscle because the handoff was off-the-books — involving it would tie the ledger to Valenti directly. She needs **unattached operators** who can retrieve it before the grand jury convenes in 10 days.

**The twist:** The ledger isn't just evidence — it's **cursed**. Anyone who holds it for more than 24 hours begins seeing the faces of people who died because of cargo listed inside. The last courier went catatonic and was admitted to Bellevue's restricted ward. This matters because the handoff chain was already broken once, and the ledger has been absorbing psychic residue each time it changes hands.

**The crew's angle:** Lena offers **$5,000 cash up front** and **a favor from Valenti** (which in the Brooklyn Gothic underworld means dock access, a clean car, or a pallet of untraceable alchemical goods). The Kowalskis are offering a **$3,000 finder's fee** — the ledger was supposed to reach their disposal site, and they want to close the loop. The PCs can work one side, both, or neither.

## Locations

### 1. Sea Wolf Tattoo — Red Hook, Brooklyn

The ledger's last known position. A Valenti-affiliated tattoo parlor on Van Brunt Street that fronts as a biker shop but runs a sideline in **enchanted ink** (ward-glyphs, memory tattoos, pact-sealing brands). The courier was supposed to drop the ledger here for a Kowalski driver to collect. The drop was missed.

**Who's here now:** The shop's owner, **Mickey "The Needle" Fusco** (60s, ex-Valenti enforcer, one hand replaced with a clockwork prosthetic). Mickey swears the ledger never arrived. He's nervous — a Valenti crew already leaned on him. He's hiding something: the ledger *did* arrive, but Mickey's apprentice, **Danny**, stashed it when he saw Valenti muscle pull up, and now Danny has gone to ground.

**What's there:** An alchemical back room with half-finished tattoo commissions, a ward-glyph flash book that doubles as a coded address book, and a safe under the floorboards that Mickey will open for a bribe or a threat. Inside: Danny's last known address (a girlfriend's apartment in Sunset Park) and a business card for a **Kowalski waste-dispatcher** that Mickey insists he doesn't remember picking up.

### 2. Golden Dragon Mahjong — Flushing, Queens

Chen-Okafor territory. The ledger surfaced here for two hours before vanishing again — the Kowalski driver stopped for a game and the ledger was stolen from his trunk. 

**Who's here now:** The parlor is run by **Auntie Mei** (70s, Chen-Okafor elder, doesn't acknowledge any family beyond her mahjong tables). Auntie Mei runs a clean game and a side business in **divination readings for gamblers** — she knows who was at the tables when the ledger was taken, and she'll share that information if one of the PCs sits for a reading. The reading is free. What she sees in the cards is up to the dice.

**What's there:** A bustling parlor with eight active tables, a back room with tea and incense, a locked filing cabinet beneath a Buddha statue that contains the parlor's day-book (customer names, times, debts). The thief's name is in there — **Eddie Trillo**, a freelance courier who runs jobs for the **Ledger** (the independent fixers' guild). Eddie took the trunk lock with a simple *knock* cantrip and didn't realize the package was a Valenti item until an hour later. He's now panicking.

### 3. BQE Rest Stop — Exit 29, Brooklyn-Queens Border

The current location of the ledger. Eddie Trillo, realizing he's holding a Valenti+Chen-Okafor hot potato, left the ledger in a **coin-operated storage locker** at a gas station / rest stop under the BQE overpass. He plans to call the Ledger's neutral drop service in the morning — but the Ledger has a policy of not handling known-cursed items, and Eddie doesn't know about the curse.

**Who's here now:** The rest stop is staffed by **Rafi** (late-night attendant, tired, paid minimum wage, sees everything and says nothing). The storage lockers are old — coin-operated, rusted, some jammed. Locker #14 holds the ledger. A Valenti tail car is parked across the street (two young crew members who followed Eddie from Red Hook but lost him at the parlor — they're watching the rest stop on instinct).

**What's there:** Twelve storage lockers, a 24-hour deli counter, a payphone, a broken ATM, and a feral cat colony. The Valenti tail will recognize anyone who looks like a Kowalski operative or a Chen-Okafor player. A clean approach — buy a sandwich, rent a locker, wait for the attendant's blind spot — avoids detection.

## Complications

### C1 — The Ward on the Ledger

The ledger isn't just evidence; it's **wizard-locked**. The Valenti family contracted a freelance ward-specialist to bind the book so that only a specific blood-line can open it without triggering a *glyph of warding* (force damage, audible pop, signals the Valenti safehouse). Lena doesn't know about the ward — she hired the wiz through a cutout and the cutout is dead. The ward specialist's name is inked in the back of Mickey's address book: **Harlow "Cipher" Duvall**, last seen in an Ashford-owned co-op in Murray Hill.

**At the table:** The PCs can (a) find and coerce/bribe Cipher for the dispel, (b) use *dispel magic* and risk the alert, (c) bring the ledger to a Kowalski disposal specialist who will burn it unread (destroying the evidence), or (d) convince a Chen-Okafor diviner to find a loophole in the ward's terms.

### C2 — The Valenti Tail

The Valenti crew watching the BQE rest stop isn't just muscle — they were told the ledger contains a list of Valenti family members who cooperated with the feds. This **isn't true** (Lena lied to motivate them). One of the tail crew, **Vincent "Vinnie" Palermo**, has a brother who was clipped for cooperating in '95. If Vinnie gets his hands on the ledger first, he'll open it, trigger the ward, and die — and the ensuing OCU investigation will bury everyone.

**At the table:** The PCs can (a) make a deal with Vinnie (convince him the ledger is clean), (b) remove the tail through misdirection (fake call, decoy car), (c) take them out non-lethally (attracting cops), or (d) let Vinnie take the ledger and deal with the fallout.

### C3 — Kowalski Roadblock

The Kowalski driver who lost the ledger was supposed to incinerate it at Fresh Kills. Instead, he lost it at a mahjong parlor. The Kowalski family has now deployed **Dominika "Docks" Kowalski** (logistics coordinator) and two disposal specialists to find and retrieve it. They have the route — they know Eddie stopped at Golden Dragon and the BQE rest stop. Dominika is professional but time-pressured: she wants the ledger before Valenti's tail, and she'll pay $1,000 for a tip.

**At the table:** If the PCs cooperate with Dominika, they get a Kowalski favor and a clean exit. If they cross her, they gain a Kowalski enemy and a truck that follows them for three blocks before turning off. If they sell the ledger to both Valenti and Kowalski, they play double agent — but Dominika's disposal specialists are *necromancy-adjacent* and will notice if they're being played.

### C4 — Ashford Frame Bait

Julian Ashford's office knows about the ledger. Ashford has been quietly buying Valenti customs debt for six months, and the ledger's destruction would consolidate Valenti's financial dependence on Ashford capital. To ensure the ledger never makes it to court, Ashford has paid a **Ledger-affiliated operative** to slip an Ashford-owned *pact document* into the storage locker next to the ledger. If OCU finds both together, the evidence trail points to Ashford — which is exactly what Ashford wants, because the document is a **fake** that exonerates Ashford and implicates a mid-level Chen-Okafor accountant. The PCs will be framed as Ashford assets whether they know it or not.

**At the table:** The PCs might notice the plant (Rafi saw someone drop it off at 1 AM; the Valenti tail didn't — they were watching the wrong angle). If they take both documents, they become the only people who know Ashford is playing the Commission. If they leave the pact document, the Chen-Okafor accountant takes the fall and Ashford's plan proceeds.

## Recommended Entry Point

Start the PCs at **Sea Wolf Tattoo** — Mickey has just finished telling the Valenti enforcers the ledger never arrived, and the PCs walk in as "the people Lena sent." Mickey sizes them up. The clock starts ticking: Eddie has the ledger, Valenti is watching the BQE, Kowalski is hunting, Ashford is planting evidence, and the grand jury convenes in 10 days. Each faction adds pressure, and the ledger's curse makes every hour it stays in the open a risk.
