# B-3 — Session 1 Beat Outline (3 Scenes)

**Date:** 2026-06-08
**Campaign:** NYC Mafia × D&D
**Item:** B-3 — Session 1 beat outline (3 scenes)

---

## Design goals

Session 1 has three jobs:
1. **Introduce the crew** — who they are, how they work together, what they want.
2. **Introduce the world** — magic is real but regulated, the families are everywhere, the law is a weather system.
3. **Create a hook that outlasts the session** — the crew should end session 1 with a problem they didn't expect and a contact they didn't plan on.

The outline below follows the opening job from A-4 ("The Red Hook Manifest") and assumes a 3–4 hour session with 4–5 players. Each scene has a **purpose**, a **beat-by-beat breakdown**, and **GM pivot notes** for when players inevitably go off-script.

---

## Scene 1: "The Appraiser's Office" — Hiring & Setup

**Purpose:** Establish tone, introduce Lucia Valenti, let the crew negotiate, set up the job.

**Location:** The Appraiser's Workshop, DUMBO (see B-1). Late afternoon. Golden light through warehouse windows. Glass display cases full of "antiques." The back room hums faintly — *Detect Magic* field is active.

**Estimated time:** 30–45 minutes.

### Beats

1. **Arrival.** The crew arrives separately or together (let the players decide — it tells you how they know each other). Lucia's familiar "Cisco" (German Shepherd, actually a Chain Pact imp) greets them at the door. If anyone has *Detect Magic* or a passive Perception high enough (16+), they notice the dog's aura is "off" — not hostile, but not a normal animal.

2. **The pitch.** Lucia is professional, direct, and already knows more about the crew than they'd like. She lays out the job:
   - A sunken barge ("St. Brigit") in Red Hook contains twelve reliquaries bound with Moretti funeral magic.
   - The barge is salvageable. The reliquaries are valuable. The manifest is *evidence*.
   - She wants the reliquaries and the manifest recovered before the other families find out.
   - Pay is fair. She also offers a **favor from the Appraiser** — one free appraisal or identification of a magical item, redeemable anytime.

3. **The negotiation.** Lucia expects the crew to negotiate. She has a ceiling (don't go above 150% of her opening offer) but respects competence. If the crew pushes hard, she doesn't budge on price but adds a small perk (access to her fence network, a contact at the ALB, a warning about OCU patrol patterns near Red Hook). If the crew accepts immediately, she's slightly suspicious — are they desperate, or do they know something she doesn't?

4. **The warning.** Before they leave, Lucia mentions — casually, as an afterthought — that the barge "has been down there three weeks and the river hasn't disturbed it." She doesn't explain what she means. (This is the Kowalski river-dead hook. The barge is in their territory, and they've been *letting* it sit.)

5. **Crew prep.** Lucia gives them:
   - A hand-drawn map of the Red Hook Container Terminal, with the barge's approximate location marked.
   - A photocopy of the ALB permit (fraudulent, Ashford-issued — see complication #4 from A-4).
   - A burner phone with one number pre-loaded: hers.

**GM pivot notes:**
- If the crew tries to investigate Lucia's background during the meeting, a DC 14 Persuasion or History check reveals she's known in certain circles as someone who "knows what things are worth" — including things people would rather keep hidden.
- If the crew asks about the Morettis, Lucia's expression tightens. "The Morettis don't forget, and they don't forgive. But they also don't leave cargo sitting in the harbor for three weeks unless something's wrong with it."
- If the crew refuses the job, Lucia says: "That's fine. I'll find another crew. But the barge won't stay quiet forever, and when it doesn't, I hope you're not in the way." This is not a threat — it's a weather report.

## Scene 2: "The St. Brigit" — The Job

**Purpose:** Deliver the heist/salvage, introduce the supernatural element, trigger the first major complication.

**Location:** Red Hook Container Terminal (night) → the sunken barge "St. Brigit." Estimated time: 60–90 minutes.

### Beats

1. **The approach.** The crew arrives at Red Hook at night. The container terminal is a maze of stacked metal boxes, floodlights, and chain-link. One bored Valenti security guard is on patrol — he can be avoided (Stealth DC 12), bribed (Persuasion DC 10, he's on Lucia's payroll and will look away for the right price), or bypassed entirely if the crew finds another route.

2. **The OCU drive-by.** Complication #1 from A-4 triggers here. An OCU patrol car (Sgt. Adrienne Voss and her partner) does a routine drive-by. If the crew is visible — on the dock, near the water, carrying equipment — Voss slows down. She doesn't stop (she's on patrol, not investigation), but she **sees** them. If the crew is hidden, they watch the patrol car pass. Either way, Voss makes a note on her report pad. This is the first dot in the OCU paper trail (see B-2).

3. **The water entry.** The barge is half-submerged in 20 feet of water. Getting to it requires:
   - Swimming (Athletics DC 13 in cold water, or DC 10 if they have gear).
   - Or finding a way to access it from the dock (a crane that hasn't moved in two weeks — can be activated with a DC 14 Sleight of Hand or Tools check, or the crew can climb down).
   - The water is **unnaturally cold**. A DC 13 Arcana check reveals faint necromantic resonance — something in the water is *aware*.

4. **The barge interior.** The "St. Brigit" is a decommissioned cargo lighter. The captain's cabin is accessible but partially flooded. Inside:
   - The twelve reliquaries are in a locked chest (Thieves' Tools DC 14, or brute force DC 18).
   - The manifest is in a waterproof tube strapped to the chest.
   - The burial magic on the reliquaries is **active but dormant** — a DC 15 Arcana check reveals they're keyed to Moretti funeral sigils. Disturbing them without the proper counter-rite will trigger a **ghost-binding tether** (see A-4, complication #5).

5. **The supernatural bite.** When the chest is opened, one of two things happens:
   - **If the crew is careful** (Arcana DC 15 to identify the sigils, or Religion DC 14 to recognize the burial rite): They can suppress the binding temporarily (10 minutes of in-game time, one caster maintaining concentration). The reliquaries are safe to transport. But the suppression will wear off, and when it does, the ghost will manifest at the worst possible moment.
   - **If the crew is not careful** (or fails the check): The ghost-binding tether triggers. One random crew member feels a **cold hand on their shoulder** — a ghost has latched onto them. The ghost is **Enzo Parra**, a Moretti foot soldier killed in 1998. He is not hostile. He is *grief-mad* and will speak through the tethered crew member at unexpected moments, revealing fragments of information: where the Morettis keep their backup ghost-binding records, who killed him, and — critically — that the reliquaries were **supposed to be destroyed**, not stored. Someone in the Moretti family is hiding them.

6. **The river-dead.** As the crew exits the barge, the water around the pier begins to move. A shape rises — not fully, just enough to be seen. **The Thing in the Water** (Kowalski river-dead, see A-4). It doesn't attack. It *watches*. If the crew has the reliquaries, it reaches toward them — not grabbing, just... curious. A DC 14 Arcana or Religion check reveals it's drawn to the burial magic. If the crew offers it something (a reliquary, a gesture of respect, or simply speaks to it), it sinks back down and doesn't interfere. If they ignore it or threaten it, it follows them to the shore and watches from the water — a silent witness that will report back to the Kowalskis.

**GM pivot notes:**
- If the crew triggers every complication at once, **pace it**: OCU on the approach, ghost-binding on the barge, river-dead on the exit. One per beat. Don't stack them.
- The ghost of Enzo Parra is the most important narrative element in this scene. He is a **source of information** that will drive the next several sessions. Even if the crew suppresses the binding, he should manifest later — in a quiet moment, during another job, at the worst possible time.
- If a player comes up with a creative solution to the ghost-binding (e.g., *Dispel Magic*, *Remove Curse*, talking to the ghost directly), **let it work** with a reasonable DC (15+). The goal is drama, not punishment.

## Scene 3: "The Handoff" — Exfil & Escalation

**Purpose:** Deliver the cargo, introduce the second major complication, end the session with a hook.

**Location:** Red Hook → exfil route → handoff point (Lucia's workshop or a neutral location). Estimated time: 30–45 minutes.

### Beats

1. **The exfil.** The crew needs to get the reliquaries out of Red Hook. The pre-positioned van is on Otsego Street (see A-4). The route passes through Valenti-adjacent but not Valenti-controlled territory — which means other eyes.

2. **The Ledger fixer.** Complication #3 from A-4 triggers here. **"Gus"** — a warlock-for-hire working for an unnamed client — steps out of a shadowed doorway on the exfil route. He has two associates (use **Thug** stat blocks, but one has *Hex* and *Eldritch Blast* — Gus's bodyguard, a low-level warlock). Gus is not here to fight. He's here to **negotiate**.
   - He knows about the barge. He knows about the reliquaries. He knows who hired the crew.
   - He offers to buy the reliquaries at **double Lucia's price**. His client (unnamed) wants them.
   - If the crew refuses, Gus doesn't escalate — he nods, says "Your call," and leaves. But now **The Ledger** knows the crew has the cargo, and they know the crew's faces.
   - If the crew accepts, they get double the money but lose Lucia's favor and her future patronage. Lucia will find out. She always finds out.

3. **The handoff.** The crew delivers the reliquaries to Lucia (or to a neutral location if they're being cautious). Lucia:
   - Pays the agreed amount.
   - Examines the reliquaries briefly. Her expression changes — just for a moment — when she sees the Moretti sigils. She knows what these are. She doesn't explain.
   - If the crew mentions the ghost-binding or Enzo Parra, Lucia goes very still. "Say that again." She wants to know everything the ghost said. This is worth **more than the job** to her — the Moretti ghost-binding records are leverage the Valentis have wanted for years.
   - If the crew mentions the river-dead, Lucia's expression darkens. "The Kowalskis are watching the barge. That means they knew about it before I did. That means someone told them." This is the seed of the next job.

4. **The hook.** As the crew is leaving (or immediately after the handoff), one of two things happens:
   - **If the crew still has the ghost-binding active:** Enzo Parra manifests fully — not through the tethered crew member, but as a **spectral figure** visible to everyone. He speaks a single sentence: "The records are in the basement on Mulberry. Father Tommaso knows. He's the one who buried me." Then he fades. The crew now has a **target** (the Mulberry Social Club archive — see B-1) and a **moral question** (do they tell Lucia, who will use it as leverage, or do they tell the Morettis, who might pay more — or kill them for knowing?).
   - **If the crew suppressed or removed the ghost-binding:** A **burner phone** (not one of theirs) rings in the vicinity. If answered, a calm, cultured voice says: "You have something that belongs to my family. I'd like to discuss a return. No hard feelings — business is business." This is **Father Tommaso Moretti** (or someone calling on his behalf). The Morettis know about the reliquaries. They want them back. And they're willing to negotiate — for now.

5. **Denouement.** The session ends with the crew having been paid, having made at least one enemy (The Ledger, the Kowalskis, or the Morettis — depending on their choices), and having a clear next step. Lucia offers one final piece of advice: "You did good work. But in this city, good work gets noticed, and noticed gets complicated. Watch your backs."

**GM pivot notes:**
- The session should end on a **question**, not an answer. The crew should be asking: "What do we do with this information?" "Who do we trust?" "What's in the Moretti archive?" "Why do the Kowalskis care about a sunken barge?"
- If the crew double-crossed Lucia (sold to Gus), the next session opens with Lucia's familiar "Cisco" waiting outside their safe house. Lucia wants to talk. She's not angry — she's **disappointed**, which is worse. She offers them one more job, but the terms are different now: no favor, no trust, cash only.
- If the crew was honest with Lucia, she becomes a **recurring contact** — the crew's most reliable source of work, information, and magical appraisal. But she will ask for the Mulbury archive information eventually, and the crew will have to decide whether to give it to her.

## Session 1 — Quick reference

| Scene | Location | Purpose | Key NPCs | Complications |
|-------|----------|---------|----------|---------------|
| 1. The Appraiser's Office | DUMBO workshop | Hiring, negotiation, setup | Lucia Valenti, Cisco (familiar) | Lucia's knowledge, the "river hasn't disturbed it" warning |
| 2. The St. Brigit | Red Hook / sunken barge | Heist, salvage, supernatural | Sgt. Adrienne Voss (OCU), Enzo Parra (ghost), The Thing in the Water | OCU patrol, ghost-binding tether, river-dead encounter |
| 3. The Handoff | Exfil route → handoff | Delivery, escalation, hook | Gus (Ledger fixer), Father Tommaso Moretti (phone) | Ledger negotiation, Moretti contact, ghost manifestation |

**Tone targets:**
- Scene 1: Noir-professional. Scorsese dockwork meets corporate negotiation.
- Scene 2: Horror-tinged salvage. The water is wrong. The dead are curious.
- Scene 3: Noir-escalation. Every choice has a price. The city is watching.

**Estimated total time:** 3–4 hours (flexible; Scene 2 can expand or contract based on player creativity).
