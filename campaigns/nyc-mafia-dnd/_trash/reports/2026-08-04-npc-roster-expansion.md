**Relation:** `2026-07-30-npc-roster.md` (C-1) covers neutral contacts and fixers. This report covers family-aligned **enforcers, lieutenants, and named opponents** — the people the crew fights, negotiates with, or runs from.

## Valenti (Brooklyn — docks, smuggling, artificers)

### Lt. Rosa "The Crane" Lombardo — Valenti Dock Captain

**Role:** Harbor-side enforcer. Rosa runs the Red Hook docks crew — forty longshoremen, half of whom carry warded crowbars. She reports directly to Lena Valenti (family boss, never named in prior reports) and handles all cargo disputes at the piers.

**Age:** 38
**Appearance:** Stocky, powerful build, close-cropped grey hair. Wears a longshoreman's coat with a Valenti insignia stitched inside the collar. Missing the top joint of her left index finger (docking accident, age 19). Keeps a steel thermos of coffee laced with *enhance ability* potion — shared with her crew during heavy lifts.
**Combat style:** Fights with a warded cargo hook (treat as a war pick that sheds dim blue light). Coordinated — she never fights alone, always with 2–3 dock enforcers.
**Pressure point:** Her daughter attends St. Bridget's in Bay Ridge on a scholarship Rosa can't afford to lose. A word to the school board would get her attention.
**What she knows:** Cargo schedules, Valenti safehouse locations in Brooklyn, the identity of the Valenti artificer who made Manny's cursed ledger.

### "Sully" Sullivan — Dock Enforcer

**Role:** Rosa's right hand. Muscle and intimidation. Been with Valenti since his teens.
**Age:** 29
**Appearance:** Tall, broad-shouldered, a face that's been broken at least twice. Wears a stained green jacket and work boots. Carries a *shillelagh*-enchanted lead pipe wrapped in electrical tape.
**Personality:** Quiet, does what he's told, doesn't ask questions. He's not cruel — he's just never learned another trade.
**Combat encounter:** Sully leads 3–4 unnamed dockhands in a cargo-yard shakedown. He fights until half HP, then surrenders.

### Gina "Nails" Palladino — Valenti Arsonist / Cleaner

**Role:** Fire specialist. When a job needs to *disappear* — a boat, a warehouse, a body — Gina handles the accelerant.
**Age:** 33
**Appearance:** Wiry, hair in a tight ponytail, coveralls under a leather jacket. Her hands are scarred from chemical burns. Smokes roll-your-own cigarettes. Carries a custom arcane lighter that produces *produce flame* on command.
**Personality:** Professional, a little unhinged, loves her work. She doesn't take jobs that target civilians — cargo only.
**Combat encounter:** She uses *create bonfire* and alchemist's fire from range. If cornered, she lights a fuse on her own position and tries to escape in the chaos.

## Moretti (Little Italy / Lower Manhattan — protection, clerical rites)

### Lt. Sal "The Sacristy" D'Ambrosio — Moretti Consigliere

**Role:** Moretti's fixer and canon lawyer. Sal handles disputes between family crews and manages the Cleric "chaplains" who perform the binding burial rites. He's a middle-aged man with a minor clerical ordination from an unaccredited seminary — enough to perform rites, not enough to be a real priest.
**Age:** 56
**Appearance:** Soft, balding, always in a black suit with a Roman collar (not a priest, but he likes the look). Smells of incense and old paper. Keeps a rosary that glows faintly when someone lies within ten feet.
**Personality:** Patient, calculating, soft-spoken. He believes in the old ways — the Commission, the rites, the chain of command. He sees the Kowalski necromancy not as a competitor but as blasphemy. His hatred is theological, not territorial.
**Pressure point:** His ordination papers are forged — if exposed, he loses credibility with the Cleric network and his position in the family.
**What he knows:** The location of every Moretti burial site, the Cleric rotation schedule, and the identity of the Moretti capo who authorized Fusco's deliberate haunting (rumor #6).

### "Two-Bit" Marconi — Moretti Street Enforcer

**Role:** Debt collector. Works the Mulberry Street protection racket. Known for breaking fingers — one per week of missed payment, escalating.
**Age:** 44
**Appearance:** Short, barrel-chested, wears a flat cap and a long coat. His right hand is heavily scarred — he's broken too many bones without gloves. Walks with a slight limp (gunshot wound, '96).
**Personality:** Grouchy, loves his job, has a system. He keeps a ledger (mundane, not cursed) of every debt and every payment. Hates the Valenti family for no particular reason — "their guys always talk too much."
**Combat encounter:** Street-level fight outside a Little Italy restaurant. Two-Bit has 2–3 unnamed enforcers. He fights dirty — pocket sand, low blows, using civilians as shields.

### Francesca Moretti — Family Nephew, Ambitious Upstart

**Role:** Young blood pushing for a seat at the table. Francesca is the niece of Don Moretti and runs a small crew of five in the East Village — gambling, protection, a side hustle selling diluted healing potions to college bars.
**Age:** 24
**Appearance:** Sharp, athletic, wears designer streetwear with a concealed holster. Short hair, a gold earring shaped like a saint medal. Carries a *thaumaturgy*-enhanced flip phone that can produce booming voice commands.
**Personality:** Ambitious, reckless, thinks the old guard moves too slow. She wants to expand into the Lower East Side — directly into Chen-Okafor territory. Her uncle hasn't approved the move but she's testing borders.
**Combat encounter:** Francesca's crew is cocky and under-equipped. She leads from the front, using *command* (one target, one word) and a concealed pistol. If the fight goes bad, she surrenders fast — she's too ambitious to die in a street brawl.
**Rival angle:** Francesca would make a good recurring antagonist / reluctant ally — she respects competence and will work with anyone who can help her climb.

## Chen-Okafor (Queens — gambling, numbers, divination)

### Lt. David Chen-Okafor — Numbers Rackets Manager

**Role:** David is Adaeze's older brother (see C-1 NPC roster, Det. Chen-Okafor). He runs the family's numbers operations in Flushing and Jackson Heights. He's the public face of the lending offices — approachable, well-dressed, no visible weapons.
**Age:** 36
**Appearance:** Lean, clean-shaven, tailored suits in navy and charcoal. Wears a jade ring on his right hand (a divination focus — it warns him of hostile intent within 30 feet). Carries a leather portfolio, not a gun.
**Personality:** Smooth, professional, deeply protective of his sister's career. He knows she's a cop and he knows she knows what he does. They have a standing agreement: he doesn't ask her for favors, she doesn't investigate his operations. This balance is fragile.
**What he knows:** The full customer list for Chen-Okafor's loan sharking operation, the schedule for the Below access point at Canal Street, and the name of the Ashford warlock who's been asking about family real estate purchases.
**Pressure point:** His sister. If the crew threatens Adaeze's career or safety, David drops everything to negotiate. If the crew protects her, he owes them a serious favor.

### Kaz — Defector from Moretti

**Role:** Moretti numbers runner who flipped to Chen-Okafor (previously referenced in rumor #10 and faction relations). Now runs Chen-Okafor's Lower East Side expansion.
**Age:** 27
**Appearance:** Nervous energy, thin, wears hoodies and track pants. Has a habit of checking his watch every thirty seconds. Carries the same brass knuckles he used in the Moretti crew — he's superstitious about changing luck.
**Personality:** Anxious, talkative, eager to prove his loyalty. He knows the Moretti system inside out — routes, schedules, safehouses. He's a huge liability if captured: he'll sing in exchange for protection.
**Combat encounter:** Kaz doesn't fight. If cornered, he runs. If caught, he spills everything about Chen-Okafor's East Village push within minutes.

### "Iron" Ling — Chen-Okafor Enforcer (Dwarf)

**Role:** Muscle and heavy protection for high-value collections. Ling is a dwarf with Valenti artificer connections who now works exclusively for Chen-Okafor.
**Age:** 67 (dwarf years — looks 40)
**Appearance:** Broad, heavily scarred, silver-touched beard braided with copper wire. Wears a reinforced leather gambeson under a canvas work coat. His left forearm is a polished steel prosthetic (old war injury) that doubles as a +1 improvised weapon.
**Personality:** Stoic, reliable, never talks about his past. Reads the Racing Form during stakeouts. Drinks black tea with three sugars. Has a standing bet with a Kowalski half-orc enforcer (see below) about who can collect more debts in a month — they settle up quarterly at a neutral bar.
**Combat encounter:** Ling is a brute force encounter. He fights with the prosthetic arm and a warded sap. He has *dwarven resilience* — poison resistance, advantage on saves against fear. He does not retreat unless his employer tells him to.

## Kowalski (Staten Island — waste, disposal, necromancy)

### Lt. Marta Kowalski — Waste Disposal Captain

**Role:** Don Kowalski's niece. Runs the family's Staten Island waste transfer station and the corpse-disposal pipeline that feeds into the Bone Reach (Below connection).
**Age:** 41
**Appearance:** Heavy-set, strong arms, wears coveralls and a high-vis vest that doubles as a +1 AC work garment. Carries a clipboard that's actually a *speak with dead* focus — she can ask a corpse one question per day by touching the clipboard to its forehead.
**Personality:** Pragmatic, grim, runs a tight operation. She doesn't believe in the necromancy rumors the other families spread — she just believes in efficiency. A body in the river can be found. A body in the Bone Reach is gone forever. That's logistics, not sorcery.
**What she knows:** The full waste disposal schedule for Staten Island, the Kowalski Below access route (Stapleton Water Tunnel), the location of the giant skeleton (rumor #11), and the name of the Ashford contractor trying to buy it.
**Pressure point:** Her son works in the Sanitation Department. If the Kowalski disposal operation is ever audited, he goes down with her.

### "Gutter" — Kowalski Night Crew Half-Orc Enforcer

**Role:** Kowalski's primary disposal specialist. Gutter (real name abandoned) handles the messy part of "deep disposal" — bodies that need to reach the Bone Reach. He's the half-orc who has a standing bet with Iron Ling (see Chen-Okafor above).
**Age:** 33
**Appearance:** Massive — 6'4", scarred, tusks filed to points. Wears a leather butcher's apron over a stained tank top. Carries a two-handed wrench wrapped in warding tape. Speaks in short sentences.
**Personality:** Quiet, methodical, disturbingly calm. He doesn't enjoy his work but he doesn't mind it either. His only hobby is pigeons — he keeps a coop on the roof of the Kowalski waste station.
**Combat encounter:** Gutter is a serious threat. He fights with the warded wrench (treat as a maul) and has *relentless endurance* (drop to 1 HP instead of 0, once per day). He does not chase retreating enemies — the job is disposal, not pursuit. If the crew defeats him, the Kowalski family will send a lieutenant to "discuss" the loss of their best disposal man.

### Jimmy "The Leash" O'Donnell — Kowalski Necromancy Liaison

**Role:** The Kowalski family member who actually does practice necromancy. Jimmy manages the Below contact — he's the one who talks to the Chattering (C-3 report) and arranges the fresh-teeth-for-surface-news trade.
**Age:** 52
**Appearance:** Thin, gaunt, prematurely grey. Wears a long coat even in summer. His fingernails are permanently black (residual necromantic stain). Keeps a jar of teeth on his desk as a conversation piece.
**Personality:** Eccentric, literate, speaks Infernal with an affected accent. He fancies himself a scholar, not a gangster. He's the only Kowalski who will openly discuss the Below.
**Combat encounter:** Jimmy doesn't fight directly — he uses *toll the dead*, *chill touch*, and *misty step* from range. If pressed, he retreats into the Below through a pre-wired escape route. The crew will have to follow him into the tunnels — which is exactly what the Chattering wants.

## Ashford (Midtown — finance, politics, warlock pacts)

### Lt. Julian Cross — Ashford Operations Manager

**Role:** Julian runs the Ashford family's day-to-day operations — contract management, fixer hiring, political payments. He is the person who placed the parallel contract on the cursed ledger (see faction relations). He is not a warlock himself — he's a suit who manages warlocks.
**Age:** 45
**Appearance:** Expensive suits, precise grooming, rimless glasses. No visible tattoos, no jewelry, no affectation. He looks like a mid-level corporate executive because that's exactly what he is — his business cards say "Cross Consulting LLC."
**Personality:** Cold, precise, never raises his voice. He treats the underworld as a supply chain. He's the kind of man who will ruin someone's life over a missed deadline and then send a condolence card.
**What he knows:** Every active Ashford contract, the identity of the Ashford warlock who maintains the family's pact network, the bribery schedule for three ALB clerks, and the real estate valuation of the stalled 50th and 8th development (rumor #11).
**Pressure point:** His daughter is a student at NYU. She doesn't know what he does. He has a separate apartment in Murray Hill under a different name that he uses for family business. If the crew discovers his family life, they have real leverage.

### "Quill" — Ashford Warlock Enforcer

**Role:** Julian's supernatural enforcement arm. Quill is a warlock (fiend pact) who handles Ashford problems that require magical response — curse retrieval, witness intimidation, contract enforcement with arcane teeth.
**Age:** Unknown (appears mid-30s, likely older)
**Appearance:** Sharp-featured, immaculate, wears a black suit with no tie. His eyes are amber — pupils contract vertically in low light. Speaks in measured tones. His shadow sometimes moves independently.
**Personality:** Professional, eerie, completely loyal to the contract. He doesn't care about family loyalty — he cares about the terms of his pact with Ashford (they feed him souls via marked contracts). He cannot break the pact, and he cannot be bought off.
**Combat encounter:** Quill is a high-threat encounter. Uses *eldritch blast*, *hex*, *darkness*, and *misty step*. His familiar is a raven with one glass eye that serves as a scrying sensor. He fights to disable, not kill — dead people can't sign contracts. He retreats when he's taken 50% HP, reporting back to Julian.

### Delia Park — Ashford Financial Analyst / Forger

**Role:** Ashford's records specialist. Delia handles the "clean" side — forged financial statements, laundered ledgers, fictional shell companies. She's not a field operative; she works out of a WeWork in Midtown with noise-cancelling headphones.
**Age:** 29
**Appearance:** Unremarkable — jeans, cardigan, laptop bag. Could be anyone in any coffee shop. That's the point.
**Personality:** Quiet, precise, deeply anxious. She knows she's committing felonies but tells herself it's "financial analysis" because the money goes through a Cayman shell. She has insomnia and an encyclopedic memory for numbers.
**Pressure point:** She has a gambling problem. Ashford knows and uses it. If the crew offers her a way out — or a better percentage — she'll flip. She has the entire Ashford financial architecture in her head.

## Other Rivals & Neutral Threats

### "Mouth" — Goblin Ledger Courier / Gossip

**Role:** A Ledger goblin courier who knows everyone and tells everything. Not loyal to any family. A source and a risk in equal measure.
**Age:** Unknown (goblin, probably mid-20s in human terms)
**Appearance:** Fast, small, wears a stained yellow courier vest three sizes too big. His left ear is notched (lost in a bet). Talks constantly — hence the name.
**Personality:** Friendly, chatty, utterly untrustworthy. He'll sell information to anyone, including the person he's selling information about. He forgets faces but remembers prices.
**Utility:** The crew can find Mouth at any neutral gathering spot (mahjong parlor, Below access point, all-night diner). He always has a lead, a rumor, or a job offer — but whatever he gives you, he's also giving to someone else.

### The Echo — Below Hazard (from C-3)

Not expanded here; see `2026-08-01-the-below.md` for full description. In combat terms: treat as a swarm of Medium undead (psychic damage) that can speak in fragmented sentences and may help or hinder the crew depending on narrative context.

## How to Use This at the Table

- **Lieutenants** (8 named) are faction-level threats — negotiating with them or defeating them earns faction reputation changes.
- **Goons** (7 named) are encounter-fillers. Use them singly or in groups of 2–3 to give a fight texture.
- **Rivals** (Francesca, Quill) are recurring characters. They have goals beyond "kill the party" — they can be allied with, betrayed, or competed against.
- **Pressure points** are listed for every NPC. A clever crew can resolve encounters without combat by targeting what the NPC cares about.
