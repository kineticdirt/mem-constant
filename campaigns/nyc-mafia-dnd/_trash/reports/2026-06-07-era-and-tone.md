# A-1 — Era & Tone Lock

**Date:** 2026-06-07
**Campaign:** NYC Mafia × D&D
**Item:** A-1 — Lock era + one-page tone note

---

## Recommended era: **2015–2025 (the "decade that was")**

Not literally today — give the table a **five-year-ago buffer** so real-world politics and current events aren't directly in play, but all the technology readers actually use (smartphones, Wire/encrypted apps, dashcams,Ring doorbells) works as expected.

**Why not 1990s?**  Great aesthetic, but the campaign already has enough moving parts without requiring the table to analog how they'd manage burner phones and no GPS.  The factions use enchanted comms and divination gambling — those land better when everyone has a supercomputer in their pocket.

**Why not near-future?**  The premise already asks a lot of the table (faction geometry, magic rules, crime worldbuilding).  Adding speculative tech on top doesn't earn its slot.

## Working year: **Monday, 2021**

Yes, really.  The COVID-era shuttered blocks, absent landlords, and ghost-streets of 2020-2021 are **built-in adventure terrain**: entire borough blocks the families quietly occupied, bribed out, or enclosed wards around.  It's recent enough to feel immediate, distant enough to be "the old city."

The campaign **present day** is simply "a few years later" — vague on purpose.

## Tone note (one-pager)

| Axis | Setting | Dial |
|------|---------|------|
| **Grounded ↔ Fantastical** | Grounded-crime first, magic as *infrastructure* | Supernatural escalates slowly; first sessions feel like a Scorsese film; By session 6 the table accepts a warlock accountant |
| **Gritty ↔ Stylized** | Gritty consequences, stylized *presentation* | Wounds matter, witnesses talk, money is tracked — but characters have larger-than-family names, cool crews, lines they actually want to say |
| **Dark ↔ Humor** | Dark backdrop, dark-humor relief | Capital-B Bad Things happen off-screen (or on-screen with content warnings); table banter, crew-colorful insults, and the comic contrast of magic meeting bureaucracy are always welcome |
| **Player-driven ↔ GM-narrated** | Player-driven in the fallout | Jobs come from the world; *how* the crew handles them is fully player-authored. Fail forward. |

### Three reference touchstones (the table table-setting homework)

1. **The Wire** (S1–S3) — institutional pressure, everyone thinks they're the hero
2. **A Brilliant Young Mind / Ex Machina** — NYC is full of people who are smarter than you and one mistake from breaking
3. **Inception** (the office-building dream heist) — magic is *bureaucracy until it isn't*

### Content boundaries

- **Organized crime is the antagonist structure** — no real-world ethnic stereotypes; family names are fictional and mixed-heritage by design (see `factions.md`)
- **Violence**: consequence-first; describe impact, skip anatomy
- **Magic abuse / exploitation**: allowed as *plot*, never glorified; table safety tools apply
