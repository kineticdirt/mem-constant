# A-5 — PC Role Menu

**Date:** 2026-07-26
**Campaign:** NYC Mafia × D&D (1999, Brooklyn Gothic)
**Status:** DRAFT — table canon pending
**Premise:** Seven archetypes for crew composition, each rooted in the Five Families' underworld

---

## 1. The Fixer — "I know who to call"

You're the one with the Rolodex. Contacts, black-market rates, ALB clerks on the payroll, and safehouses in every borough. You don't carry a gun — you carry a burner phone and a stack of favors. The job starts because you heard about it first.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **D&D class** | Mastermind Rogue, Bard (Eloquence), Knowledge Cleric |
| **Magic** | Licensed Tier 1 (message, identify, illusory script for forgeries) |
| **Party role** | Intel, negotiation, job sourcing, neutral ground at sit-downs |
| **Family fit** | Chen-Okafor (divination intel pipeline), Valenti (cargo manifests), or independent Ledger operator |
| **Hook** | You owe a Valenti dock boss a favor from a job where your intel was wrong. She hasn't called it in. You're starting to think that's worse. |

---

## 2. The Enforcer — "They'll listen eventually"

You handle the part of the job where talking stops working. Breach, clear, intimidate, protect. You're not a killer for hire — you're a problem that can be pointed at a worse problem.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **D&D class** | Battle Master Fighter, Barbarian (Zealot), Vengeance Paladin |
| **Magic** | None or minimal — grey-market combat scrolls if the job pays well |
| **Party role** | Combat anchor, intimidation, asset protection, credible threat |
| **Family fit** | Kowalski (river muscle), Moretti (chaplain-backed), or hired gun independent |
| **Hook** | Your last crew got made — OCU has a file with your photo and the note "Kowalski circuit." One more sheet and you're looking at RICO time. |

---

## 3. The Mage — "I handle the weird"

Cursed cargo, warded doors, a ghost that won't stay put. Magic is your trade, and in this city trade requires a license — or a family connection. You're the one the crew calls when lockpicks and leverage aren't enough.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **D&D class** | Wizard, Sorcerer, Warlock |
| **Magic** | Licensed Tier 1 (day-job front) + grey-market Tier 2 for crew work |
| **Party role** | Supernatural problem-solving, counterspelling, arcane research, ALB fine-print reader |
| **Family fit** | Ashford (warlock consulting contract), Moretti (clerical tradition), or rogue unlicensed |
| **Hook** | Your ALB license was suspended six months ago. Every spell you cast on a job is a felony. The families know. They're just not saying anything — yet. |

---

## 4. The Driver — "I'll get you there. Question is whether you'll like where we end up."

Cars, boats, trucks, the occasional enchanted cargo van that runs on something other than gasoline. You move product, people, and bodies. In a city of bridges and tunnels, the person behind the wheel controls the timeline.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **D&D class** | Thief Rogue, Armorer Artificer (tricked-out vehicle), Cavalier Fighter |
| **Magic** | None needed; a Tier 1 utility cantrip (prestidigitation for plates, mage hand for cargo swaps) |
| **Party role** | Exfil, pursuit, logistics, route planning across family-controlled territory |
| **Family fit** | Valenti (dock trucks), Kowalski (bridge logistics), or freelance across all five |
| **Hook** | You ran a Valenti shipment through a Kowalski checkpoint without the customary offering. They let it pass — which means they want something, and they'll find you when they're ready. |

---

## 5. The Ghost — "They won't see me until I want them to"

Second-story work, safe cracking, data exfiltration, and the art of being somewhere you're not supposed to be. Warded vaults are just a challenge — you're the counterweight to magical defenses, and you move faster than the ward response.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **D&D class** | Arcane Trickster Rogue, Shadow Monk, Gloom Stalker Ranger |
| **Magic** | Minimal — a few Tier 1 utility spells (knock, invisibility scrolls) plus mundane expertise |
| **Party role** | B&E, surveillance countermeasure, intel retrieval, the one who gets the manifest photo before the job |
| **Family fit** | Independent / freelancer — every family needs deniable outside talent |
| **Hook** | The last safe you cracked belonged to a Chen-Okafor accountant. You left the cash and took a ledger. They know something's missing, and they've put out feelers for a "data recovery specialist." That specialist is probably you. |

---

## 6. The Chaplain — "I keep the dead in line and the living in one piece"

Medic, ritual cleanser, spiritual fixer. You patch bullet wounds, dispel lingering curses, and bind restless souls — whether they want binding or not. You're defrocked Moretti, unlicensed ALB, or someone who learned the rites from a source that isn't the Church.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **D&D class** | Grave/Life/Twilight Cleric, Divine Soul Sorcerer, Dreams Druid |
| **Magic** | Licensed Tier 1 (registered clergy or medical permit) + deep Tier 3 cleansing rites the families don't ask about |
| **Party role** | Healing, curse removal, ghost-binding, magical hygiene, the crew's uneasy conscience |
| **Family fit** | Moretti (chaplain tradition — defrocked), or independent (working the grey economy because the ALB won't re-license you) |
| **Hook** | You were assigned to a Moretti burial that went wrong. The ghost didn't bind. Instead of fixing it, you walked. Father Tommaso hasn't reported you — he's waiting. Waiting for what, you don't want to find out. |

---

## 7. The Alchemist — "I built the thing that saves your life. You're welcome."

You make things. Enchanted things. Illegal things, usually. The Valenti forgery ring needs enchanted manifests; the Ashford pact brokers need warded contracts; every counting room needs wards that keep divination out. You're the one who keeps the family's supernatural infrastructure running.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **D&D class** | Alchemist Artificer, Armorer Artificer, Transmutation Wizard |
| **Magic** | Tier 2 infused items (your trade), grey-market alchemical goods, ward schematics |
| **Party role** | Gear, wards, forgery, item repair, "the one who knows why the counting-room glyph failed last Tuesday" |
| **Family fit** | Valenti (artificer forgery ring), Ashford (ward-system contractor), or Kowalski (custom disposal equipment) |
| **Hook** | A ward you designed went into a Valenti safehouse — and last week it failed during a hit. Three Valenti soldiers are dead. The family knows the ward was yours. Your next conversation with them will determine whether you're fixing the problem or joining the dead. |

---

## Crew composition notes

- **Minimum crew:** Fixer + Enforcer + one magic-user (Mage or Chaplain). The fixer gets the work, the muscle makes it stick, the magic-user handles the impossible.
- **Recommended:** 4–5 players — one from each quadrant: social (Fixer), violence (Enforcer/Ghost), magic (Mage/Chaplain), technical (Driver/Alchemist).
- **Conflict hooks:** Mix family ties for built-in drama. A Moretti defector and an Ashford contractor in the same crew have history they haven't discussed. A Kowalski driver and a Valenti dock kid have crossed paths before the campaign started.
