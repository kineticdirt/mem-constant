## How to use this table

- **C-5 hooks layer on C-3 and C-4 content**: The Below (Bone Reach, Chattering, kuo-toa) and expanded NPCs (Rosa, Sal, Gutter, Ling, Quill, Delia) appear across multiple rumors. Each hook rewards players who have read the earlier deep lore reports.
- **Cross-table consequences**: Rumors #1 and #6 both involve the Bone Reach's expanding boundary. Rumor #7 (Gutter's defection) is a direct cascade from #4 (Gutter's pigeon operation). Run both and the crew faces a Below that is actively recruiting surface assets.
- **Every family appears**: Valenti (1, 2, 4, 8), Moretti (3), Chen-Okafor (2, 7), Kowalski (2, 4, 7), Ashford (5, 8). The Ledger and Below appear as neutral/enemy factions.
- **Three escalation tiers**: Rumor #5 (diplomatic/political hook), rumors #2, 3, 8 (intrigue/blackmail), rumors #1, 4, 6, 7 (combat-capable field ops).
- **No re-use of 2026-07-30 rumors**: All eight hooks are new and reference worldbuilding added after the initial rumors table.

**Reminder:** Proposed hooks for the GM. Not canon until the table picks.
