## The Ledger — Independent Fixer Network

A loose network of couriers, cleaners, and information brokers who operate across all five families. The Ledger has no loyalty — it fulfills contracts. Its members know each other by handle, not real name. A job goes on a physical black book (the actual "ledger"), and payment is anonymous cash drop.

**Contact method:** A payphone rings twice, then silence. The crew calls back from a different phone. A voice gives a time and a place. No names.

---

## Mickey "The Needle" Fusco — Sea Wolf Tattoo, Red Hook

**Role:** Information broker / tattoo artist (ward-glyph specialist)
**Age:** 54
**Appearance:** Wiry, bald, left arm ends at the elbow in a brass-and-spring clockwork prosthetic (tarnished, ticks audibly in quiet rooms). Wears a stained white apron over a sleeveless denim jacket. Deep-set eyes, silver-framed reading glasses perched on his nose.
**Personality:** Nervous, chain-smokes, talks to himself. Genuinely good at his craft — his ward-glyph tattoos are clean and hold for six months. He's not a fighter and never has been.
**Connection to the crew:** The Ledger uses Sea Wolf as a dead-drop location. Mickey knows the couriers by face, not name. He's the first person the crew meets in Session 1 (B-3, Scene 1).
**Pressure point:** His prosthetic hand — a gift from a Valenti dock boss. He can't remove it. It tracks his location. He doesn't know this.
**What he knows:** Courier schedules, drop protocols, the Ledger's current contract list (he reads the incoming mail when no one is watching). He doesn't know the *contents* of any contract.
**What he wants:** A new hand — one that doesn't hum at night.

## Danny — Ledger Courier, AWOL

**Role:** Ledger junior courier (the one who ran with the cursed ledger)
**Age:** 22
**Appearance:** Lean, nervous energy, dark hair that falls in his face. Wears a green army surplus jacket, even in summer. Carries a messenger bag with a ward glyph stitched into the lining.
**Personality:** Scared, resourceful, not stupid. He knew the ledger was hot but didn't know it was *cursed* until he started dreaming about a man in a grey suit. Now he's running.
**Connection to the crew:** The crew chases him through Sessions 1–2. He's hiding at his girlfriend's apartment in Sunset Park.
**What he knows:** The Ledger's drop protocol for high-value items, the employer code for the Valenti contract (someone inside the family), and the location of the storage locker key (B-3, Scene 3).
**What he wants:** To disappear. He has a cousin in Buffalo who can get him a new ID. He needs $2,000 and a ride to Port Authority.

## Eddie Trillo — Freelance Courier, Ledger Contract

**Role:** The Ledger's designated courier for the cursed ledger. He picked it up from the Valenti contact and was supposed to deliver it to the BQE storage locker.
**Age:** 31
**Appearance:** Stocky, broad-shouldered, wears a green coat (B-3, Scene 3). Has a gold tooth and a faded tattoo of a winged coin on his forearm. Moves like someone who's been in fights.
**Personality:** Pragmatic, loyal to the contract, not to any family. He's done this before — cursed items, hot documents, things that need to sit in a locker for a week before anyone touches them. He's not scared of the curse; he's scared of the Valenti enforcers who are following him.
**Connection to the crew:** The crew meets him at the BQE Rest Stop (Scene 3). He's holding the storage locker key and doesn't know what to do.
**What he knows:** The identity of the Ledger's Valenti contact (a mid-level capo who calls himself "The Bookkeeper"), the drop schedule for the next three days, and the fact that the Ashfords have a parallel contract on the same ledger.
**What he wants:** To complete the contract and get paid ($5,000). If the crew can guarantee his safety, he'll hand over the key. If not, he'll try to negotiate with the Valenti enforcers.

## Auntie Mei — Golden Dragon Mahjong, Flushing

**Role:** Diviner / information broker / neutral ground
**Age:** 67
**Appearance:** Small, precise, silver hair in a tight bun. Wears traditional cheongsam in dark silk, always with a jade bracelet on her right wrist. Her hands are fast — shuffling mahjong tiles is a performance.
**Personality:** Warm, maternal, utterly ruthless when crossed. She runs a legitimate mahjong parlor that is also a Chen-Okafor affiliate and an independent information exchange. She doesn't take sides, but she remembers who paid and who didn't.
**Connection to the crew:** The crew visits her parlor in Session 1, Scene 2, looking for Eddie Trillo's location.
**What she knows:** Eddie Trillo's real name, his mother's address in Queens, the name of the Valenti capo who contracted the Ledger, and the fact that the Ashfords are watching the ledger's movement through a third-party scryer.
**What she wants:** A favor. She doesn't name it yet. She'll collect when the crew least expects it. (Table: roll this favor when the crew is in a tight spot — Auntie Mei calls in a marker.)

## Detective Adaeze Chen-Okafor — NYPD OCU, Arcane Division

**Role:** Law enforcement / family connection
**Age:** 30
**Appearance:** Tall, athletic, wears her hair in a tight braid. Plainclothes — blazer, jeans, comfortable shoes. Carries a service weapon and a warded evidence bag in her shoulder bag. No visible jewelry.
**Personality:** Sharp, professional, caught between two worlds. She's a good cop and she knows her brother David runs a Chen-Okafor lending office. She doesn't take money from the family, but she doesn't report them either. This silence is a live grenade.
**Connection to the crew:** Introduced in B-1 (Jamaica Market arcane kiosk) and B-3 (Scene 1 complication). She's the one who notices the crew's movements. She's not a threat — yet.
**What she knows:** The Five Families' operational patterns, the Valenti-Ashford ledger dispute, the OCU's current investigation priorities (currently focused on a Kowalski body-dumping case, which gives the crew breathing room).
**What she wants:** To solve a case that matters — a real conviction, not a plea deal. She's looking for a way to take down a family without involving her own. The ledger could be that case, if she can get clean evidence.

## Harlow "Cipher" Duvall — Curse Specialist, Freelance

**Role:** The person you call when a cursed object needs unbinding
**Age:** 41
**Appearance:** Thin, pale, wears black turtlenecks and grey slacks. Keeps a copper bracelet on each wrist (grounding wards). Smokes clove cigarettes. Her apartment is a converted storage unit in Dumbo with salt lines on every threshold.
**Personality:** Cynical, direct, competent. She's seen too many cursed objects to be impressed by any of them. She charges by the curse tier: Class-A ($1,000), Class-B ($2,500), Class-C ($5,000+). Payment upfront, cash, no receipts.
**Connection to the crew:** Mentioned in B-3 (Session 2 hook) as the person who can dispel the ledger's curse. The crew needs to find her through the Ledger or through an underworld contact.
**What she knows:** The curse on the Valenti ledger is infernal in origin — not a standard ward. She can identify the entity that wrote it (a minor debt-collector demon) and can unbind it, but the process takes 24 hours and requires the ledger to be in a warded room. She also knows that the Ashfords have a standing contract with her for "first refusal" on any infernal unbinding in the five boroughs.
**What she wants:** To stay independent. The Ashfords keep trying to put her on retainer. She refuses because she doesn't want to be owned. If the crew brings her the ledger, she'll do the work — but she'll also report the unbinding to Ashford, as required by her non-compete. This creates a time pressure: the crew has 24 hours after Cipher unbinds the ledger before Ashford knows.

## Manny "The Bookkeeper" Reyes — Valenti Capo, Ledger Contact

**Role:** The Valenti family's accountant and the person who contracted the Ledger to move the cursed ledger
**Age:** 48
**Appearance:** Soft, unremarkable, the kind of man who blends into a diner booth. Wears plain suits, always a tie clip with a tiny anvil on it. Reads the Daily News. Drinks black coffee.
**Personality:** Quiet, precise, deeply afraid. He's the one who signed the contract with the Ledger, and he's the one who will answer to Lena Valenti if the ledger isn't recovered. He's not a fighter — he's an accountant who made a mistake.
**Connection to the crew:** The crew never meets him directly in Session 1. He's the unseen hand behind the Valenti enforcers. If the crew traces the Ledger contract, they find his name on the paperwork.
**Weakness:** He keeps a paper trail. Every transaction, every contract, every envelope of cash. He can't help it — he's an accountant. If the crew gets access to his records, they have leverage over the entire Valenti operation.
**What he wants:** The ledger recovered and the curse dispelled before the grand jury deadline. He's authorized to pay up to $10,000 for its safe return. He's also authorized to make the crew disappear if they fail.
