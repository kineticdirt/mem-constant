## The Job: "The Lucky Rabbit's Foot"

### Elevator Pitch

A Chen-Okafor runner is late with a drop — an enchanted satchel of numbered-draw receipts that traces who in the Queens corridor has been **borrowing luck on credit**. The family's diviners say the runner is alive but *hiding*, somewhere in Midtown. They need a crew who can find him, recover the satchel, and deliver it to Kian Chen-Okafor's tax-prep office in Flushing before the Ashfords figure out what's in it. The runner won't come out for anyone he knows; the crew are unknowns, which makes them the only option.

### The Hook

The contact is **Kian Chen-Okafor** himself (see A-3: Five Families) — a soft-voiced numbers boss with a diviner's tic. He meets the crew at his Flushing tax-prep office, surrounded by filing cabinets full of lottery tickets. He doesn't say the job out loud — he writes it on a Post-it, then burns it. The runner, **Jin Ha**, has a satchel with thirty-three numbered receipts representing borrowed luck — each one a favor that a Queens gambler or hustler hasn't repaid yet. If the satchel falls into Ashford hands, they can call in those debts *through* the Chen-Okafor paper trail, which means the family's entire Queens operation becomes collateral.

Kian offers **$15,000 cash plus a "free reading"** — one divination question answered truthfully, no strings. For a crew starting out, that's leverage worth more than the money.

### Locations

1. **Kian's Tax Prep (Flushing, 8:30 p.m.)** — The setup. A fluorescent-lit office with a mynah bird that repeats winning numbers. Kian is polite, direct, and never uses a name twice in the same sentence. He gives the crew a burner phone, a photo of Jin Ha, and a last-known location: **a Midtown after-hours club called The Rabbit's Foot**.

2. **The Rabbit's Foot (Midtown East, 11 p.m.)** — A speakeasy-style poker room in the basement of a failing hotel. No sign, no Yelp listing, no bouncer at the street door — entry is through a service alley on 47th Street where a woman with a deck of cards asks you to cut before you descend. Inside it's velvet, cigar smoke, and seven tables running games with stakes from $500 to $50,000. The Rabbit's Foot is *unaffiliated* — neutral ground — which is why Jin Ha chose it. But Ashford has eyes here too.

3. **The Hotel's Basement Boiler Room** — Jin Ha is hiding not in the club itself but in the abandoned boiler room beneath it, accessible through a locked maintenance hatch in the men's restroom. He's been there for three days, living off vending-machine crackers and tap water. The satchel is stashed in a dead boiler pipe. He won't leave: he saw an Ashford Warlock consultant at the club's front door last night.

### DM Notes

- **Session flow:**
  1. **Cold open** — Meeting Kian at the tax-prep office. Tone: quiet, bureaucratic, ominous. Kian is unsettlingly polite. (15 min)
  2. **Arrival at The Rabbit's Foot** — The card-cut, navigating the club, spotting Vane. Information-gathering phase. (20 min)
  3. **Finding Jin Ha** — The basement / boiler room negotiation. Trust challenge. (20 min)
  4. **The Extraction** — Leaving the club with Jin and the satchel. Complications converge. One is Ashford, one is Voss, one is the ward. (15 min)
  5. **Delivery** — Back to Flushing. Kian pays, gives the reading. The crew has a favor and a target. (10 min)

- **Fail forward:** If the crew rolls badly on the social checks with Jin Ha, he doesn't refuse to give up the satchel — he insists on *coming with them* to deliver it personally. Now the crew is babysitting a terrified civilian through a club with Ashford eyes.

- **Tone:** Chinatown-by-way-of-Daniel-Craige-era-Bond. The underworld here runs on paperwork, luck-as-currency, and politeness as a weapon. Violence is the last resort, not the first.

### Three In-Opening NPCs

1. **Kian Chen-Okafor** — The boss who hired them. Never says a number aloud (diviner's tic). Keeps a mynah bird that repeats winning lottery numbers. Offers a free reading as a bonus.
2. **Mira, the Card-Cutter** — Untrained seer at the club's door. She cuts cards to let people in. She knows when someone's lying. She doesn't care — she's just here to work her shift and collect tips.
3. **Jin Ha (the runner)** — 24, scared, resourceful. Hid in a boiler room for three days. He's not a fighter — he's an accounting major who took a job as a Chen-Okafor numbers runner and got in over his head.
