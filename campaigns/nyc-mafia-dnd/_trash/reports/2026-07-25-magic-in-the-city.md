# Magic visibility in the city — three tiers

*Draft for table discussion. 2026-07-25.*

---

## Overview

Magic in New York isn't hidden — but it's not on the evening news either. The city runs on a **three-tier visibility system** enforced by the Commission and the Arcane Licensing Board (ALB). Breaking tier boundaries is the fastest way to draw heat from both the mob *and* the law.

---

## Tier 1: Public (licensed, legal, boring)

What the public sees and accepts as normal commerce:

| What | Example | Who regulates |
|------|---------|--------------|
| **Healing** | Walk-in clinics (licensed cleric); ambulance enchantment patches | ALB + NYPD EMS |
| **Warding** | Banks, jewellery district, some bodegas — discreet rune work | ALB (building code) |
| **Augury** | "Feng shui consultants", "business analysts" — all divination | ALB, loosely |
| **Minor cantrips** | Mending, prestidigitation — restaurant kitchens, tailors | No license needed |

Public magic must be **boring, commercial, and invisible to the average person** unless they're looking for it. A glowing sigil is fine — it's a security sticker. A fireball in Midtown is not public magic; it's a crime scene.

**Penalty for Tier 1 abuse:** ALB revokes license. Commission fines the family that sponsored it. Repeat → "accident" or OCU booking.

---

## Tier 2: Hidden (unlicensed, tolerated, grey)

The engine of the underground economy. Everyone in the life knows it exists; nobody talks about it to outsiders.

| What | Who needs it | Key players |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| **Forged scrolls** | Valenti artificer rings; freelance mages without ALB papers | Valenti, The Ledger |
| **Cursed objects** | Intimidation, debt collection, "insurance" | Kowalski (rumoured) |
| **Enchanted burner phones** | Every operator who doesn't want a wiretap to work | The Ledger, crew fixers |
| **Pact brokerage** | Warlock "consultants" — Ashford territory | Ashford, independent warlocks |
| **Luck manipulation** | Rigged games, fixed races | Chen-Okafor divination units |
| **Ghost-binding** | Burial rites that make witnesses impossible | Moretti chaplains (off the books) |

Hidden magic is **tolerated** as long as it stays:
- **Indoor** — never on the subway, never in a public park
- **Deniable** — no civilian witnesses who aren't already "in the life"
- **Small-scale** — a ward is fine; a summoned demon is a Commission problem

**Penalty for Tier 2 exposure:** If caught by OCU → RICO enhancement (magic as organised crime). If caught by the Commission → the family that leaked pays. If caught by **The Below** → nobody finds the body.

---

## Tier 3: Licensed (controlled, rare, white-collar)

The Commission and ALB coexist in a corrupt equilibrium. Certain magic is so powerful that only **licensed operators** may use it — and those licenses are family-owned assets.

| Licensed good | Who holds the license | What it actually does |
|--------------|----------------------|-----------------------|
| **Necromancy** | No legal license exists (Kowalski denied; they do it anyway) | Body disposal, witness silencing |
| **Planar calling** | Ashford (3 licensed summoners in all NYC) | "Corporate consultants" — actual devils |
| **Large-scale illusion** | Moretti (the Feast of San Gennaro every year — real enchantments mixed with fireworks) | Crowd control, product launch "theatre" |
| **Divination on government targets** | Chen-Okafor front company | Political intelligence; ALB turns a blind eye |
| **Artificer mass-production** | Valenti (union-recognised "specialty manufacturing") | Cursed cargo containers, tamper-proof vaults |

Licenses are **heirlooms**, not permits. You don't apply — you inherit or buy. The ALB issues them on paper; the Commission decides who actually gets them. A license is worth more than a crew's annual take.

---

## What civilians know

- **Nothing definitive.** They've heard rumours. Their cousin's roommate had a healing charm. That bodega's security rune is "just a sticker."
- **Deniability is the system's genius.** The public half-believes and half-doesn't — exactly the sweet spot for the families.
- **OCU keeps it quiet.** Arrests are filed as "organised crime" with no mention of magic. Witnesses who push too hard on the supernatural angle get discredited first, silenced second.
