## How to read this

Each entry lists a faction, its allies, its grudges, and its debts. Arrows show direction: "X → Y" means X owes Y. "X ↔ Y" is mutual. The Commission is the Five Families' governing body — not a faction, but a set of rules all five agree to.

## The Five Families

### Valenti (Brooklyn — docks, smuggling, artificers)

| Type | Faction | Detail |
|------|---------|--------|
| **Grudge** | Kowalski | Escalating border friction over Staten Island waste routes through Brooklyn shipping lanes. Kowalski night crews keep poaching Valenti's cargo manifests. |
| **Grudge** | Ashford | Standing dispute over the cursed ledger (see C-1). Valenti contracted the Ledger to move it; Ashford placed a parallel contract. Neither family trusts the other's intent. |
| **Debt →** | The Ledger | Active contract for the cursed ledger transport. Manny "The Bookkeeper" Reyes signed the contract. $10,000 authorized for recovery. |
| **Ally** | Chen-Okafor | Cold alliance — Valenti moves gambling shipments through Brooklyn docks for Chen-Okafor's Queens operations. Neither family likes the other, but the trade route is too profitable to break. |
| **Grudge** | Brighton Beach Union | The orc steamfitter union (rumor #8) left the Kowalski umbrella and Valenti is interested in picking them up. Kowalski considers this a betrayal. |

### Moretti (Little Italy / Lower Manhattan — protection, clerical rites)

| Type | Faction | Detail |
|------|---------|--------|
| **Grudge** | Chen-Okafor | Active turf war over Lower East Side numbers operations. Chen-Okafor recruited a Moretti numbers runner (Kaz, rumor #10) and took two weeks of Moretti's take. |
| **Grudge** | Kowalski | Theological tension — Moretti's Cleric chaplains perform proper burial rites; Kowalski's necromancy-inclined disposal practices are an affront to the Church. Kept quiet by the Commission. |
| **Debt ←** | Fusco ghost | Deliberate haunting (rumor #6). The consigliere ordered the botched rite to send a message to the crew. The ghost is not an accident — it's a performance. |
| **Ally** | Ledger (informal) | Moretti uses the Ledger for courier work when they need deniable transport. No formal contract — they prefer the "ask Father Paulo" arrangement. |

### Chen-Okafor (Queens — gambling, numbers, divination)

| Type | Faction | Detail |
|------|---------|--------|
| **Grudge** | Moretti | Active squeeze on Moretti's Lower East Side numbers. The Kaz defection (rumor #10) is part of a broader territorial expansion. |
| **Ally** | Valenti | Trade alliance — Chen-Okafor gambling goods move through Valenti docks. Purely transactional, but stable. |
| **Interest** | FurCab (Beastkin co-op) | Chen-Okafor has a fixer negotiating with the striking Beastkin cab co-op (rumor #7). They see a unified cab-courier network as a gambling and courier pipeline. |
| **Grudge** | Kowalski | Kowalski's unlicensed cabs (rumor #7) undercut the Beastkin co-op Chen-Okafor wants to absorb. |

### Kowalski (Staten Island — waste, disposal, necromancy)

| Type | Faction | Detail |
|------|---------|--------|
| **Grudge** | Valenti | Border friction over Staten Island waste routes through Brooklyn. Kowalski night crews have been testing Valenti's cargo security. |
| **Grudge** | Moretti | Theological tension — Kowalski's necromantic practices are a direct challenge to Moretti's clerical authority over the dead. |
| **Interest** | The Below | Active Below contact through kuo-toa intermediaries (rumors #4, #11). The giant skeleton at 50th and 8th is a key bargaining chip. Kowalski wants it intact. |
| **Grudge** | Brighton Beach Union | The orc union (rumor #8) voted to leave the Kowalski umbrella. This is a direct loss of territory and labor. |
| **Debt ←** | Ashford | Ashford's Midtown development is stalled by the giant skeleton excavation (rumor #11). Kowalski bribed the foreman to report the find. The skeleton is now a Kowalski asset, which costs Ashford money. |

### Ashford (Midtown — finance, politics, warlock pacts)

| Type | Faction | Detail |
|------|---------|--------|
| **Grudge** | Valenti | Parallel contract dispute over the cursed ledger. Ashford placed a secondary contract through the Ledger without informing Valenti. |
| **Debt →** | Kaelthon Collective | Ashford owns the dragonborn charter company's bond debt (rumor #12). The Collective is gathering evidence on Ashford's rivals under duress. |
| **Interest** | Harlow "Cipher" Duvall | Ashford has a standing non-compete contract with Cipher for first-refusal on infernal unbindings in the five boroughs. She resists but is bound by the terms. |
| **Grudge** | Kowalski | Kowalski's seizure of the giant skeleton (rumor #11) has stalled Ashford's 50th and 8th development tower. A direct financial loss. |
| **Ally** | Elven data haven | Serevyn Aelaris (rumor #9) is running an unlicensed teleportation circle in the West Village for Ashford's benefit. The ALB is paid to stay out. |

## Law Enforcement

### NYPD Occult Crimes Unit (OCU)

| Type | Faction | Detail |
|------|---------|--------|
| **Grudge** | All Five Families | Institutional mandate. The OCU exists to investigate unlicensed magical crime. Every family is a target. |
| **Debt ←** | The ALB | OCU needs ALB liaison officers to certify magical evidence. The ALB is corrupt — several liaisons are mob-owned. The OCU can't function without them. |
| **Interest** | Det. Adaeze Chen-Okafor | An OCU detective with a family connection to Chen-Okafor. She doesn't take mob money but doesn't report her family either. A live grenade for both sides. |

### Arcane Licensing Board (ALB)

| Type | Faction | Detail |
|------|---------|--------|
| **Debt ←** | Ashford | Ashford-backed ALB policies keep magical evidence inadmissible in court. The ALB's position benefits the families. |
| **Debt ←** | Multiple families | Several ALB liaisons are on family payrolls. The ledger of amnesty (rumor #3) would expose every unlicensed practitioner who bought their way clean. |
| **Grudge** | OCU | The ALB resents OCU's enforcement authority. They cooperate when forced; they obstruct when paid. |
