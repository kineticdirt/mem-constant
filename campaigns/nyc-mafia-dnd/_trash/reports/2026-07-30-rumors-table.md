**Date:** 2026-07-30
**Campaign:** NYC Mafia × D&D

*10+ hooks the crew can pick up between jobs. Each rumor has a source (who's talking), a seed (what they heard), and a complication (what's actually going on — for GM eyes only).*

## Using this table

- **Drop one rumor** as a scene opener (the crew hears it at a social club, a bar, a dockside meet).
- **Let the crew chase 2–3** in a single session if the opening job goes cold.
- **Consequences cross-table:** rumor #4 (tusks) and #11 (skeleton) both connect to the Below. If the crew investigates both, they'll run into Kowalski operatives either way.
- **Each family is named in at least two rumors** — no family sits this tick out.

**Reminder:** These are proposed hooks for the GM to accept, reject, or modify. They are not story canon until the table picks.
