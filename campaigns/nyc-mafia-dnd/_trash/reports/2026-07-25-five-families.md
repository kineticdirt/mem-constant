# Five Families — expanded profiles

*Draft for table · 2026-07-25*

One-paragraph description of each family and a hook-ready NPC seed. Built from the [[factions]] skeleton.

---

## Valenti (Brooklyn — docks, smuggling, unions)

The Valentis control the Brooklyn waterfront from Red Hook to Sunset Park — legitimate shipping fronts, longshoreman locals, and a network of freight-forwarding warehouses that move anything from untaxed cigarettes to cursed antiquities. Their **Artificer** rings operate out of converted container workshops where stolen schematics become working enchanted hardware. They're the family most likely to own a working spelljammer engine, or at least know someone who *can* build one. Territory is their religion; they don't tolerate crews operating in Brooklyn without a Valenti license.

**NPC seed:** *Domenica "Domino" Valenti* — the don's niece, mid-20s, runs a container-yard forge that reverse-engineers captured enchanted gear. Sharp dresser, sharper eye for cursed items. She'd rather buy you a drink than break your kneecaps, but won't hesitate if you waste her time. Has a standing bounty on anyone selling counterfeit Valenti-brand wands.

---

## Moretti (Little Italy / Lower Manhattan — protection, restaurants, burial rites)

The Morettis are the oldest of the Five and the most public-facing — they run a dozen famous Italian restaurants in Little Italy, but those kitchens launder money for extortion, loan-sharking, and a lucrative **clerical protection** racket. Every Moretti soldier knows basic divine rites; their "chaplains" are actual Clerics who bless shops, bind restless spirits after hits, and perform burials that keep the dead in the ground. The family charges weekly tithes for this blessing, and businesses that pay up never suffer "accidental" fires or poltergeists. Their influence in the Church (the old kind, not the ALB) gives them leverage no other family can match.

**NPC seed:** *Father Paulo Moretti-Torres* — a soft-spoken man in his 40s who wears a collar over a bulletproof vest. Runs St. Rocco's Social Services as a front for Cleric dispatch. He doesn't carry a weapon; he carries a reliquary that can Turn Undead across two city blocks. Every OCU detective knows him by face and *chooses* not to see him.

## Chen-Okafor (Queens — gambling, numbers, luck as product)

The Chen-Okafor family runs the Queens gambling corridor — Flushing poker rooms, Corona cockfights with minor divination tweaks, and a city-wide numbers game where the winning digits are *genuinely* random because a Diviner on payroll checks each draw. They deal in luck as a commodity: you can buy a "lucky night" at the tables or rent a probability fix for a heist. Their diviners are the real product — trained from childhood in a Flushing basement academy that treats foresight like a trade skill. The family is the most ethnically hybrid of the Five (Chinese-Nigerian alliance marriage two generations back) and the most willing to work with outsiders.

**NPC seed:** *Kian Chen-Okafor* — the family's 30-something numbers boss, a soft-voiced man with an abacus that never gives a wrong total. Runs a legitimate tax-prep office by day. His diviner's tic: he never says a number aloud unless he's seen it in a reading first. Keeps a pet mynah bird that repeats winning lottery numbers.

---

## Kowalski (Staten Island — waste, logistics, "disappearing")

The Kowalskis handle the city's garbage — literal sanitation contracts, toxic-waste hauling, and the less-legal business of making people and evidence vanish. Their Staten Island operations center around the Fresh Kills landfill (closed to the public, not to them) and a network of barges that run at night. They talk to the river; whether that's **Necromancy** or just years of working in silence around drowned things is a question other families ask quietly. The Kowalskis don't answer. Their soldiers are quiet, patient, and rarely seen outside Staten Island unless a body needs to cross a bridge.

**NPC seed:** *Vera Kowalski* — the family undertaker, a gaunt woman in her 50s who runs a funeral home that processes the dead for both burial *and* disposal. She speaks to corpses before cremation. Sometimes they answer. Her ledger of "unclaimed remains" is a map of everyone the family has ever made disappear.

## Ashford (Midtown towers — money laundering, politics, warlock pacts)

The Ashfords don't *look* like a crime family — they wear tailored suits, run shell companies out of Midtown glass towers, and sit on the boards of three major banks. They are the Five's white-collar arm: money laundering at institutional scale, political bribery routed through LLCs, and a network of **Warlock** "consultants" who draft contracts with infernal clauses buried in fine print. An Ashford deal never breaks cleanly. Their power is in paper: deeds, shell-company charters, arcane agreements that bind signatories even after death. They have fewer soldiers than the other families and more lawyers.

**NPC seed:** *Marcus Ashford-Pierce* — the family's contract specialist, a man in his 30s who never raises his voice. Every document he produces has at least one loophole that benefits an Ashford interest. Known for saying "I don't threaten people; I draft terms they agree to." Keeps an owl familiar that perches on his office door. Owns a brownstone in Brooklyn Heights but sleeps in a warded panic room.

## The Commission norm — "No magic hits on civilians"

The Commission's oldest rule: magical violence in public view must have a Commission vote first. The rule is broken often, and the enforcement escalates: first infraction is a fine paid to the victim's family; second is loss of territory; third is a *binding geas* administered by all five family heads together. The rule exists not for human decency but for *plausible deniability* — as long as the public doesn't see fireballs in the street, the ALB and NYPD OCU have limited jurisdiction. Break the rule too loudly and the city brings down heat on all five.
