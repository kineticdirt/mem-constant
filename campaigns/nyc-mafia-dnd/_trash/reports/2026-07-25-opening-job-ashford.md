## The Job: "The Gilded Cage"

### Elevator Pitch

An Ashford warlock-consultant has gone off-grid with a **pact ledger** that names every Midtown politician, judge, and precinct captain who owes the family a favor in ink. The Ashfords want him back — alive, with the book intact — but they can't send their own people without tipping off the Commission that they lost control of a consultant. They need a disposable crew to retrieve both the man and his ledger before the next Commission sit-down.

### The Hook

The contact is **Gideon Ashford III**, the family's fixer and public face (see A-3). He meets the crew in a private dining room at a Midtown steakhouse that the family owns — white tablecloths, waiters who don't make eye contact, a pianist playing jazz standards. Gideon doesn't shake hands. He slides an envelope across the table: inside is a photo of **Caspian Vane**, a 38-year-old warlock whose contract with the Ashfords is technically unbreakable — yet he's been missing for six days. His apartment is undisturbed. His phone is still at home. The only sign he left is a credit-card charge at a **Bronx salvage yard** three days ago.

Gideon wants the crew to find Caspian and bring him and his ledger to a flat in Murray Hill by **Friday midnight** — 72 hours from now. He offers **$20,000** upfront and a clean introduction to **Arcane Licensing Board** fast-track permits, which gives the crew a legal magic license without the usual paperwork.

Gideon does not say what happens if Caspian doesn't come back.

### Locations

1. **The Ashford Room (Midtown steakhouse, Tuesday 8 p.m.)** — The setup. Gideon Ashford III explains the job over a meal the crew didn't order. The pianist plays "Take Five" on loop. Gideon writes Caspian's home address on a napkin and burns it in his coffee cup. He is polite, precise, and never raises his voice. The room has an invisible **silence ward** — no sound escapes. The crew may notice this.

2. **Caspian Vane's Apartment (Murray Hill, Tuesday 9:30 p.m.)** — A surprisingly modest one-bedroom in a prewar building with a working elevator. Caspian left his phone on the kitchen counter, his warlock's focus on the nightstand, and his pact ledger — the *official* one — in a locked safe in the bedroom closet. The official ledger is a decoy: contracts for minor favors (parking tickets, zoning variances, bar fights). The real ledger — the one with the names that matter — is missing. A faint **divination residue** lingers on the apartment: someone has searched it before the crew, and they were professional — nothing disturbed, nothing wiped.

3. **Voss Salvage & Demolition (South Bronx, Wednesday afternoon)** — A fenced wrecking yard behind a highway overpass, surrounded by rusting car crushers and a trailer office with a TV playing static. The owner, **Lena Voss**, is Caspian's sister-in-law and the last person who saw him. She says Caspian stopped by to "borrow a crowbar and a bottle of Jameson" and left in a hurry. She noticed an Ashford-branded sedan parked on the street corner that morning — same model, different plates — and hasn't seen it since. The yard has a **mild necromantic tang**: something dead was buried here recently, and it wasn't human.

Either way, the crew has learned that accepting Ashford hospitality leaves a mark. |

### DM Notes

- **Session flow:**
  1. **Cold open** — Dinner with Gideon Ashford III. Tone: corporate-polished menace. The crew is being assessed for usefulness. (15 min)
  2. **The search** — Caspian's apartment (investigation) → the salvage yard (lead) → deciding to enter the Below. (25 min)
  3. **The Below** — Navigating an abandoned IND station with a thing that speaks in PA announcements. Social negotiation with a terrified, blind warlock. (25 min)
  4. **Extraction + complication spike** — Returning to surface with Caspian. Kowalski or Ashford tail converges. (15 min)
  5. **Delivery** — The Murray Hill flat. Gideon pays. The ledger is returned. The crew leaves with an ALB contact and a recorded favor. (10 min)

- **Fail forward:** If the crew botches the negotiation with Caspian, he doesn't refuse to come — he insists on bringing the Below-thing with him (it can't leave the tunnels, but it can *reach* through him if he calls). Now they have a warlock who can contact an eldritch subway voice at will.

- **Tone:** *Michael Clayton* meets *The Wicker Man*. The Ashfords operate in the quiet space between legal and binding. Everything is polite until it isn't. The Below is the first time the crew encounters something that doesn't care about family names.

### Three In-Opening NPCs

1. **Gideon Ashford III** — The fixer. Family man, never raises his voice, knows your name before you tell him. He's the friend who offers you a cigarette before the firing squad. His silence ward makes steakhouse dinners feel like confessionals.

2. **Caspian Vane** — The AWOL warlock. Six days on the run, now blind, hiding in a subway tunnel. He's scared, articulate, and desperate. He wants to go home but knows the Ashfords will kill him slowly for the breach of contract. He has the real ledger.

3. **Lena Voss** — Salvage-yard owner. Caspian's sister-in-law by marriage. She hates the Ashfords but won't say why. She'll help the crew with a lead if they earn it — she wants someone to know what happened to her sister.
