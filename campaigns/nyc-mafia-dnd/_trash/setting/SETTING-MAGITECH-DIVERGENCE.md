## A. Principles

### Divergent evolution, not “1930s + Wi‑Fi”

This New York never walked the **scientific-industrial ladder** (calculus → steel frame → electrification → mass electronics). Instead, guilds, temples, and street alchemists iterate on **medieval magical craft and sympathetic pseudo-science** until it *loosely* solves familiar human needs — with different materials, patrons, failure modes, and law.

**Mundane period gear is real:** Tommy guns, Packards, rotary phones, telegraph, radio, brick tenements, assembly lines for **non-magical** goods. **Magical infrastructure** reads as ritual, contract, bound spirit, or artificer guild work — never firmware, apps, or “enchanted smartphone.”

### Skyline and engineering

**High-rises are rare.** Advanced structural mathematics and steel-frame skyscraper modernity are **uncommon or in infancy** — a few prestige projects, not the default city shape.

| Skyline element | Typical role |
|-----------------|--------------|
| **Brick & timber mid-rise** | Tenements, warehouses, parish halls — default bulk |
| **Temple spires & ward-towers** | Visible consecration; ley-anchors; BCC inspection beacons |
| **Automaton stacks** | Clockwork + bound spirit housing — freight lifts, message relays, factory galleries |
| **Low prestige magical spires** | Ashford Midtown, guild halls — glamour skin over masonry, not glass curtain walls |
| **Steel-frame exceptions** | Rare; often **ward-reinforced** (iron + runes), expensive, politically visible |

**What steel/calculus would have solved** often went to **golem labor, spirit contracts, or sympathetic craft** instead — path-dependent, not inevitable.

### Automatons and bound spirits as labor inflection

The city’s **second industrial revolution** is magical, not electrical-at-scale.

| Our-world role | In-world replacement (typical) |
|----------------|--------------------------------|
| **Elevator** | **Winding-spirit cage** (bound air elemental, licensed); **golem hoist** in warehouses; wealthy buildings use **weight-ward pulleys** — stairs remain normal in tenements |
| **Messenger / courier** | **Speaking-tube** boys; **kenku** / imp return-charms; **automaton porters** on fixed routes |
| **Factory hands (repetitive)** | **Clockwork galleries** + one bound **task-spirit** per line; human union fights over “spirit scabbing” |
| **Switchboard operator** | Human operator **plus** **whisper-splice** ritual (magic carries voice, human names the connection) |
| **Night watch / patrol** | **Scry-tag** chalk; **gramophone needle** on shared copper; automaton **knockers** that report threshold crossings |
| **Accountant / notary** | **Truth-bind** ledgers; **notary-cleric** witness — still human-heavy |

**Bound spirits** are **employees with contracts** (sometimes literally): patron saint, hours, feed, revocation clause. Unions and churches argue about personhood. Bootleg binding is a racket.

### Domain stance (summary)

| Stance | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Infant** | Known in theory; rare, bespoke, breaks often |
| **Divergent-rich** | Mature **magical** solutions unlike our tech tree; high variety, guild secrets, bootleg markets |
| **Similar** | Looks like our 1920s–30s — guns, cars, print, some mass production |

Intimate craft, communications, and vertical transport skew **divergent-rich**. Ballistics, automobiles, and garment sweatshops skew **similar** (with magical garnish).

**Intimate craft (explicit texture, not joke):** Commerce and consent are **regulated like liquor** — licensed salons, registered **link-pairs**, notary witness for some contracts; **bootleg links** (unregistered spatial sympathy) fund families the way gin stills do. Devices are **crafted objects** (garments, rings, paired glass), not gadgets with batteries. Failure modes: **wrong partner** on the link, **echo bleed** (neighbor feels a trace), **oath-break** nausea. Table explicitness is opt-in; **setting** treats the trade as real industry.

## C. Simulation grammar for AI

**Use this procedure** whenever think, Docs Beta, or a report invents a device, service, or practice. **Do not skip steps.** If step 2 is **No** and step 3 is not **mass-producible charm**, default to **mundane period solution** instead of magic.

### Procedure

1. **Name the need** — What human problem? (e.g. “lover away on a run,” “boss wants proof of loyalty,” “factory needs night shift.”)

2. **Medieval-magical plausibility** — Could **medieval-magical iteration** solve it **before** steel-frame engineering and calculus became dominant?
   - **Yes** → magical craft path allowed.
   - **No** → use mundane 1930s tech or declare **infant / bespoke only**.

3. **Cost class** — Pick one (sets rarity and who owns it):
   - **Rare reagents** (batch fails, supply raids)
   - **Priest / temple patronage** (feast-day limits)
   - **Guild secret** (trade war, stolen formulae)
   - **Mass-producible charm** (cheap bootleg risk — BCC registration)

4. **Failure mode + legal status** — How does it break **weird**? What is **Volstead / BCC / OTR** analog?
   - Bootleg, licensed, taboo, one-question-only, etc.

5. **Invent** — Describe materials, hours, patron, sensory detail. **Never** “enchanted iPhone,” “magic app,” “Bluetooth,” “Wi‑Fi,” “CCTV cloud,” or always-on pocket scry.

### Worked example 1 — Speaking-tube network

| Step | Answer |
|------|--------|
| **Need** | Fast voice message across boroughs without running new copper for every pair |
| **Medieval-magical?** | **Yes** — sympathetic resonance + brass craft predates calculus |
| **Cost** | **Guild secret** (Tube-Layer’s Union) + **mass charm** on exchange hubs (bootleg splices common) |
| **Failure / law** | Wrong voice, wax seal leaks gossip; **BCC** licenses exchanges; blind-tiger **splice** rooms unregistered |
| **Invention** | Paired **brass horns**, wax-sealed at rest; hub **whisper-splice** operator connects lines; sounds like distant tinny voice, not cellphone clarity |

### Worked example 2 — Portal-underwear / spatial intimacy craft

| Step | Answer |
|------|--------|
| **Need** | Erotic connection when partners are separated (tour, prison, smuggling run) |
| **Medieval-magical?** | **Yes** — sympathy + spatial **pin** craft; textiles + oath binding |
| **Cost** | **Guild secret** (lace-house) for quality; **bootleg** single-thread links without notary |
| **Failure / law** | **Echo bleed** to nearby sympathetic; **wrong pairing**; **OTR** taxes registered **link-pairs**; unregistered = bootleg intimacy (raid parallel to speakeasy) |
| **Invention** | **Portal-lace** brassard or **spatial-link** chemise — sensation maps across a **paired stitch** or **oath-lock** ring; requires fitting, renewal on new moon; consent **witness** at licensed salon; not a remote “vibrator app” — a **garment** with a **counterpart** kept in a warded box |

### Worked example 3 — Trench war magitech

| Step | Answer |
|------|--------|
| **Need** | Break stalemate, protect squad, demoralize enemy |
| **Medieval-magical?** | **Partial** — artillery and gas are **similar**; **divergent** garnish on top |
| **Cost** | **Rare reagents** for etched rounds; **priest patronage** for trench blessings; mass **morale totems** cheap but superstitious |
| **Failure / law** | Etched round **misfire** (spirit backlash); **gas** as alchemical cloud — Geneva-analog debates; **necromancy** beyond one question = war crime |
| **Invention** | Tommy guns **plus** one-shot **etched** shells; **silence ward** on a dugout (not a drone); **trench totems** (carved saint + blood) — fear and luck, not satellite intel |

## D. Randomness / path-dependence

**Problem:** Two think ticks invent contradictory skylines or duplicate incompatible comms networks.

**Ponytail rule:** Small **seeds**, not a simulation engine. Consult (or roll **d6**) once per report when inventing **new** infrastructure — then stay consistent for that thread.

### Divergence seeds (pick one axis per invention batch)

| d6 | Seed axis | If low (1–2) | If high (5–6) |
|----|-----------|--------------|---------------|
| 1 | **Vertical city** | Mostly 4–6 story brick; elevators exotic | Several ward-towers + one infamous spire |
| 2 | **Automaton labor** | Spirit/scab fights rare; mostly human | Dock lines run clockwork galleries; union war hot |
| 3 | **Comms monopoly** | Fragmented ward tubes | Tube-Layer’s Union near monopoly; splice raids |
| 4 | **Intimate regulation** | Strict salon licensing; bootleg rare | Bootleg link epidemic; OTR raids parallel gin |
| 5 | **War bleed** | WWI garnish muted; veterans use mundane trauma | Etched rounds common in street violence; gas nightmares |
| 6 | **Divine vs arcane** | Temple-led healing/notary | Guild alchemy dominates; priests lobby Congress |

**Consistency hook:** Note chosen seed in report header: `Divergence seed: comms=high, intimate=low`. Docs/scenes in same campaign **reuse** noted seeds unless GM retcons.

### Quick “what exists here?” (d6)

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 1–2 | **Mundane period** only — magic is rumor or backstage |
| 3–4 | **Licensed** infrastructure visible (tubes, salons, wards) |
| 5 | **Bootleg boom** — unregistered charms common; raid risk |
| 6 | **Weird failure week** — systems glitch; omens in press |
