# 03 — Peoples & Ancestries

Long file. Use it when you build a character. Skip around.

## Anti-allegory (read this)

Races are diasporas, not stand-ins. Humans live in every group. The Morettis aren't "the hobgoblin family" — they're an Italian-American syndicate that hires hobgoblin capos, human fronts, and halfling runners because those kids know which stairs squeak on Mulberry.

Prejudice at this table tracks **ancestry visibility** (horns, height, tusks) and **magical licensing**. An orc longshoreman gets stopped at a Midtown bank because he's unlicensed, not because of his block. Human-on-human ugliness still exists — play it concrete.

**WardID** = what a street guard checks on a nonhuman. **Passport-tier** = what a Midtown hotel desk wants. Families buy forgeries. The Iron Guild sells stamped originals.

## The visibility ladder (who gets stopped where)

| Tier | Who | How they're treated |
|------|-----|--------------------|
| **Baseline** | Humans | Everywhere. No questions. |
| **Soft-visible** | Halflings, gnomes, dwarves | Polite. Condescending. Nobody's scared. |
| **Visible** | Elves, half-elves, genasi, firbolgs, goliaths | Noticed. Priced differently in nice rooms. |
| **Hard-visible** | Tieflings, half-orcs, dragonborn, goblinoids | Stopped. Charged. Asked for papers. |
| **Fringe** | Vampires, lycanthropes, the Below-born | Not citizens at all. See `06`. |

A WardID shrinks the tier for the holder. A silver-tier license gets a tiefling past the door. Gold gets a goblinoid into a Midtown club. Nobody asks what a human's tier is.

## The peoples, group by group

*The format: who they are, where they live, the street texture (playable, dry), their family lean, and their relation to constructs/arms/healing. Percentages are neighborhood mix — humans are in every group.*

### Germans — Elves
**Mix:** Elf 70% · Human 30%. **Where:** Yorkville (UES), Hoboken waterfront gardens.

Quiet, formal, perpetually annoyed by dirt. Stiff wool suits in July. They don't shout; they stare until you leave. Pocket-brushes, perfectly combed hair, clipped flat delivery. They dominate the high-end optical glass and licensed telescope/scry-glass trade — neutral independent suppliers. They hate automata as a vulgar insult to craft, manufacture elegant long-barreled brass pulse rifles (heavily licensed), and only use expensive cathedral healing.

### French — Half-Elves / Genasi / Tieflings
**Mix:** Half-Elf 50% · Genasi 30% (fire/air) · Tiefling 20%. **Where:** West Chelsea, Greenwich Village basement salons.

High-fashion cynicism. Underground art clubs, perfume imports, poetry salons. Silk scarves over horn-stubs or elemental skin-fractures, lavender or sulfur on the collar, laughing at things that aren't funny. Independent brokers and Ashford consultants; they run glamour-laced speakeasies and act as neutral intermediaries for Midtown transactions. Concealed pocket capsule-derringers with sleep or glamour loads; perfume-scented alchemical restorations.

### Spanish — Humans / Dragonborn / Tieflings
**Mix:** Human 40% · Dragonborn 40% · Tiefling 20%. **Where:** East Harlem (El Barrio), Lower East Side tenements.

Intense, transactional, deeply family-oriented. Import-export for tobacco, citrus, raw copper. Burnt orange peel, heavy silver rings on multiple fingers, an absolute refusal to do business without a shared meal first. Chen-Okafor and Valenti associates — shipping corridors and raw-ingredient smuggling, especially sulfur and copper ore for capsule loads. Dragonborn fire-resistance in boiler-room foundries; heavy high-temp forge-pulse pistols; bootleg *cure wounds* tinctures cut with cheap rum.

### Welsh — Firbolgs
**Mix:** Firbolg 80% · Human 20%. **Where:** Jersey Palisades, Staten Island lumber yards, isolated canal-lock houses.

Massive, soft-spoken, deeply suspicious of concrete. Oversized tweed caps, boots caked in river mud. They talk to horses and steam-boilers like cousins. Duck entering doors; chew dry clover. Kowalski and Valenti associates — timber-haulers, canal-boat pilots, heavy draymen. Refuse to ride automata-driven carriages; carry heavy kinetic sling-staves (lead weight, no capsules); herbal compresses that double natural healing over 48 hours.

### Scottish — Goliaths
**Mix:** Goliath 70% · Human 30%. **Where:** Greenpoint docks, South Brooklyn grain terminals, quarry yards.

Loud, wind-burned, completely literal. Don't understand sarcasm; find human secrets hilarious. Ash-grey mottled skin, collarless linen, their own iron wrench in the back pocket. Valenti dock crews and heavy enforcers — cranes, boiler installations, dockside protection. Treat automata as "glorified shovels" and break them by overloading; massive kinetic sling-blasters loaded with scrap; heal on toughness and salt-water rubs.

### Jews — Erklings & Goddarts (campaign goblinoids)
**Mix:** Erkling 40% · Goddart 30% · Human 30%. **Where:** Lower East Side, Williamsburg, Brownsville.

The campaign defines two goblinoid lineages. **Erklings:** wire-thin, pale-sallow, long fingers, needle-sharp ears — master watchmakers, precision alchemists, printers; oversized flat caps or yarmulkes, quiet tongue-clicking when thinking, ink-stained fingertips. **Goddarts:** stockier, heavy-jawed, leathery grey skin — bookkeepers, ledger-keepers, legal scribes who can smell a double-entry forgery; thick brass spectacles, tap a heavy iron ring on tables, refuse to speak while a clock is ticking.

Street texture: fast, hyper-intellectual, cynical of any law written after the Bronze Age. They run the printing shops, legal consultancies, precision alchemy labs. Moretti bookkeepers and independent alchemical wholesalers — truth-bind ledgers, licensed filings, high-end refineries. Tiny clockwork calculating gears; fountain-pen capsule darts (sleep or tracking ink); the city's premier bootleg *lesser restoration* bands at $45 for a copper-wire wrist.

### Irish — Humans / Half-Orcs / Orcs
**Mix:** Human 50% · Half-Orc 35% · Orc 15%. **Where:** Hell's Kitchen, Woodlawn (Bronx), Bay Ridge, Red Hook docks, Jersey City waterfront bars.

Damp wool, corned beef, cabbage water, cheap rye. Rosary in the coat pocket. Half the longshoremen are half-orcs who'll shake your hand, buy you a porter, then break a chair over your head if your cousin works the municipal audit desk. Thick trailing accents full of parish guilt. Valenti dock muscle; Moretti parish protection; independent Iron Guild pipe-fitters in Jersey. Orc longshoremen treat construct loaders as scab labor — riots on Pier 7 are common. Kinetic sling-hooks, warded shillelagh staves, parish lay-on-hands bands, and a cousin who failed seminary doing bootleg *lesser restoration* behind a coal yard.

### English / British — Humans / Half-Elves / Dwarves
**Mix:** Human 70% · Half-Elf 20% · Dwarf 10%. **Where:** Murray Hill, Gramercy club row, Tudor City service corridors.

Polite frost, club memberships, the belief that queueing is a moral virtue. Import houses, shipping insurers, Ashford junior desks. Rolled umbrellas indoors, tea at inappropriate hours, apologizing before bad news. Ashford consultants, neutral shipping witnesses, licensed scry-glass appraisers. Dwarf fitters keep punctual automata maintenance schedules; half-elf juniors favor sleep-load capsule pens for "diplomatic" disputes.

### Dutch — Humans / Gnomes / Halflings
**Mix:** Human 50% · Gnome 30% · Halfling 20%. **Where:** Old Brooklyn (Breuckelen), Jersey City canal streets, Hoboken accounting row.

Salt-herring, pipe tobacco, river mud. Iron-smooth wooden shoes from scrubbing stoops. They talk about "riparian boundaries" like sacred text. Cops call them sluice-devils — if Queens floods, a Dutch clerk is holding the ward-gate invoice. Municipal contract brokers; Kowalski sanitation engineering; Chen-Okafor flood-ward installers. Gnome pump crews service the steam-assist subway grades; water-pulse capsule loads for sluice sabotage; quiet 24-hour herbal healing.

### Italians — Hobgoblins / Humans / Halflings
**Mix:** Hobgoblin 50% · Human 30% · Halfling 20%. **Where:** Little Italy (Mulberry St), Williamsburg parish blocks, East New York, Arthur Ave, Newark Third Ward.

Funeral gravity, Sunday gravy, a social club on every block. Black mourning band on the arm. Garlic and incense — you smell them before the handshake. They kiss cheeks, shake hands too long, run protection and burial-registry fraud out of restaurants that pass inspection up front. This is **Moretti core turf**, plus Valenti dock marriages and parish ghost-binding contracts. Hobgoblin capos run crews on clockwork schedules; halfling runners fit basement stills; capsule loads get blessed at parish fonts; funeral-home chapels sell spirit-touched healing bands.

### Portuguese — Humans / Tritons / Sea Elves
**Mix:** Human 55% · Triton 25% · Sea Elf 20%. **Where:** Newark Ironbound, South Brooklyn fish markets, Red Hook salt sheds.

Garlic, cod brine, olive oil. Heavy wool sweaters in July heat. A permanent feud with the Greek harbor crews older than anyone's grandfather. Fish-glue hands, azulejo tile in basement kitchens, fado that sounds like a dry sob after the third port. Valenti cold-chain handoffs; independent ferry ice contracts; Kowalski disposal kill-floor shifts. Tritons refuse Hudson fish but run offshore lines; frost capsule loads in hold disputes; salt-water rubs and dockside *cure wounds*.

### Belgians — Humans / Gnomes
**Mix:** Human 70% · Gnome 30%. **Where:** Midtown chocolate counters, Garment District margins, Hell's Kitchen bakeries.

Pristine linen aprons, roasted cacao and almond paste, a trailing French-Flemish drift lost in subway noise. They argue over the exact millimeters of a warded praline mold. Independent — warded confection labs and high-end Midtown import counters. Pocket-watch sugar trackers, warded lace-needles, sweet restorative syrups over raw clerical healing. *"He says he is neutral. He just charges a dollar more to say it in two languages."*

### Swiss — Humans / Dwarves
**Mix:** Human 60% · Dwarf 40%. **Where:** Lower Manhattan banking row, Wall Street service tunnels, Yorkville clock shops.

Stiff collars, gold watch-chains, a voice like dry gravel. Private vaults and escrow tables. Pocket magnifiers, three keys on a steel ring, refuse to speak while a pendulum swings. *"They don't take sides. They take a seven percent fee."* Neutral escrow witnesses, Ashford bond-vault auditors, licensed scry-glass appraisers. Dwarven clockmakers; pocket capsule-derringers (sleep or truth); expensive private restoration seals.

### Polish — Dwarves / Gnomes / Humans
**Mix:** Dwarf 50% · Gnome 30% · Human 20%. **Where:** Greenpoint, South Bronx industrial belt, Maspeth rail yards.

Foundries, coal soot, cabbage rolls, hard rye bread. Clear potato spirit that smells like paint thinner. Soot-caked eyebrows, welding burns on the knuckles, humming hymns while adjusting steam valves. Kowalski/Valenti — rail yards, boiler repair, warded vault-smithing. They build the cylinder chambers for Valenti-6 revolvers, distrust automata but repair them for cash, and heal on dwarven toughness and cheap bootleg bands.

### Russian — Shifters / Orcs / Humans
**Mix:** Shifter 40% · Orc 40% · Human 20%. **Where:** Brighton Beach boardwalk, Staten Island dock sheds, Sheepshead Bay fur warehouses.

Wet wolf-fur, sour cream, hot samovar tea. Thick accents, laughing too loud at unfunny jokes, obsessed with cold-iron amulets against hexes. Kowalski smuggling corridors; independent bathhouse information brokers. Shifters work night salvage and stock cold-iron capsule boxes; they heal via steam and vodka, both applied with a birch switch. (If your character is a shifter from Brighton Beach, read `06` — the street has opinions about what you are.)

### Ukrainian — Dwarves / Humans / Gnomes
**Mix:** Dwarf 40% · Human 40% · Gnome 20%. **Where:** East Village, Brighton Beach inland, Jersey City church halls.

Borsch, garlic, basement choir practice that flickers the street lamps. White embroidered shirts under grease-stained jackets, hand-painted eggs for luck, arguing beet-soup recipes like municipal law. Parish mutual-aid halls; Kowalski rail subcontractors; bakery fronts for numbers drops. Grain-silo crews, sewing-machine repair for garment smuggling, borsch stew that cures exhaustion over 48 hours.

### Greek — Humans / Tritons / Sea Elves
**Mix:** Human 40% · Triton 30% · Sea Elf 30%. **Where:** Astoria, Jersey waterfront diners, Hunts Point fish-auction fringe.

Coffee that strips copper pipes, retsina, spit-roasted lamb. Amber worry beads, a pocket knife to scale fish on the spot, refusal to eat Hudson fish — "the water is too soft for a clean fish." Ferry contracts, harbor salvage, sponge import — neutral between Valenti and Chen-Okafor on water routes. Triton smuggle cavities under hulls; water-pulse specialty loads; temple-blessed healing bands on namedays.

### Scandinavian — Goliaths / Humans
**Mix:** Goliath 50% · Human 50%. **Where:** Bay Ridge, Staten Island North Shore, Sunset Park waterfront fringe.

Pine sawdust, diesel, pickled herring. Blond beard oil on jaw-ridges; they speak once every three hours. *"If a Scandinavian is talking, he is either lying or trying to sell you rope."* Valenti barge haul; independent rope merchants; Kowalski bridge maintenance subs. They overload construct loaders on purpose because "it builds character"; kinetic sling scrap loads; salt and sauna healing.

### Balkan peoples — thin pockets
Serbian/Croatian (human/half-orc — Astoria margins, kolo dance halls, mutual-aid fights that become union votes); Albanian (human/shifter — Belmont coffee back rooms, construction crews with no paperwork); Hungarian (human/gnome/tiefling — Yorkville overlap, paprika import, accordion bars, clock repair).

### Puerto Rican / Caribbean — Humans / Lizardfolk / Genasi
**Mix:** Human 60% · Lizardfolk 25% · Genasi 15% (air/fire). **Where:** East Harlem, South Bronx, Loisaida, Bushwick.

Sofrito, fried plantains, leaded fuel. Plena off the fire escapes loud enough that OCU can't hear their wire splices. Lizardfolk on stoops in guayaberas drinking rum-shrub, telling swamp stories. *"El Barrio don't run on clockwork. It runs on lightning and loud relatives."* Valenti/Chen-Okafor runner corridors; tenement repair co-ops; independent music clubs. Lizardfolk roof crews; genasi boiler talent; root-rum tinctures; lightning capsule loads in rail disputes.

### Central American — Humans / Lizardfolk / Genasi
**Mix:** Human 70% · Lizardfolk 20% · Genasi 10% (earth). **Where:** Corona, Garment District back stairs, Newark shift hotels.

Garment piecework, sewing machines, cilantro and hot masa. Quiet hardworking families with bus tickets taped to bedroom mirrors. *"Everyone's aunt is in the kitchen and everyone's cousin is carrying a spade. No drama, just masonry."* Chen-Okafor labor brokers; kitchen fronts; Valenti warehouse nights. Earth-genasi masonry crews; *abuela* herbal stocks; rarely armed — tools are enough.

### South American — Humans / Dragonborn / Tieflings / Lizardfolk
**Mix:** Human 60% · Dragonborn 20% · Tiefling 15% · Lizardfolk 5%. **Where:** Jackson Heights, Corona, Long Island City factory rooms.

Arepa windows, soccer on warded TV tubes, clerks who calculate three-currency exchange on wooden beads. Dragonborn roasting meat on rooftop grills into the Midtown air. Chen-Okafor import desks; money wire fronts; Valenti cold storage. Dragonborn forge side jobs; tiefling nightclub glamour; capsule loads traded for import favors.

### Black New York / Harlem — Humans / Elves / Half-Orcs / Aarakocra
**Mix:** Human 55% · Elf 28% · Half-Orc 12% · Aarakocra 5%. **Where:** Harlem (Striver's Row, Sugar Hill, the 125th corridor), Bed-Stuy, Brownsville, Newark Central Ward.

Sweet-potato pie, collard greens, hair pomade, jazz clubs with warded basement doors. Elves in sharp zoot suits on Striver's Row; half-orc bouncers reading paperback poetry outside the Velvet Horn; Lenox Avenue humans who know every OCU precinct rotation by the squeak of the police-carriage wheels. *"Don't go to Harlem looking for a wire splice. The birds on the roof see you before the sergeant does."* Independent numbers kings; Chen-Okafor club partnerships; Ashford talent scouts who lose paperwork.

**Aarakocra** are individuals, not a flock — pigeon handlers, antenna riggers, lookouts who don't use stairs, feathers under newsboy caps, whistle codes between blocks. Red Hook grain-elevator nest crews; Bed-Stuy chimney repair and dawn gospel that hums the ward-stones wrong; Iron Guild pays per mile flown over the Passaic tank farms.

**The slavery legacy** shows up as artifacts, not essays: Harlem deeds with void covenant clauses Ashford clerks redact for fees; pre-Emancipation bound-labor wards still buried in the Below's disposal routes (OCU calls it "heritage necromancy"); a grandmother's trunk and train tickets and the family story about why north; dock unions and numbers routes built on excluded guild lists that still matter at sit-downs.

### Chinatown — Humans / Dragonborn / Kenku
**Mix:** Human 60% · Dragonborn 20% · Kenku 20%. **Where:** Manhattan Chinatown, Flushing, Sunset Park fringe.

Abacus clicks behind iron grilles, herbal shops with warded mahogany drawers, basement restaurants doubling as numbers banks. Kenku runners who repeat your lunch order in the exact voice of the capo who placed it. Dragonborn wok-cooks sending green fire up roof vents. Chen-Okafor numbers, gambling, scry-tag forgery. Jade ward coins; sleep darts in fan handles; bootleg restoration bands from herbal-counter back rooms.

### South Asian — Humans / Tieflings / Aasimar (sparse)
**Mix:** Human 80% · Tiefling 10% · Aasimar 10%. **Where:** Jersey City India Square (Newark Ave), one temple block in Jackson Heights.

Sandalwood, Gujarati weeklies, taxi medallion debts that take three generations to pay. Tiefling clerks with brass caps on their horn-stubs to pass Midtown inspection. Independent wire rooms; Chen-Okafor import clerks; licensed textile counters. Aasimar lay healers at temple clinics; rarely armed.

### Southeast Asian — Humans / Kenku / Tabaxi (sparse)
**Mix:** Human 70% · Kenku 20% · Tabaxi 10%. **Where:** Elmhurst/Woodside slivers, Bronx service hotels.

Fish sauce in break-room microwaves, nail salons with lavender vent-wards, English-class flyers on laundromat walls. Tabaxi stock boys who hear a shoplifter three aisles over. Chen-Okafor kitchen supplies; restaurant co-ops; garment sub-contractors. No family default to rackets — poverty is the plot enough.

## Beyond the boroughs

**Hudson Valley:** Dutch gnome sluice families, German elf weekend estates, druid "conservation" donors over the sabotage cells. Commuter-rail smuggling; deed disputes.

**New Jersey:** Hoboken/Jersey City is Iron Guild pipe-fitters and union beer halls. Newark is the Portuguese Ironbound cold-chain, the Central Ward jazz circuit, the India Square wire rooms. The Palisades are Welsh firbolg lumber, druid caves, and Valenti tank-farm handoffs. Passaic and the Pine Barrens are Russian shifters, cold-iron runs, and "commune" headlines the FSA raids.

**Connecticut / Long Island:** Ashford country-club suburbs, polite denial, weekend sit-downs. Scandinavian goliath boat yards, Greek triton offshore lines, sprawl bungalows on old potato fields — not city power, but city money.

## The families by blood (who's actually in the room)

Ancestry runs in the families, not over them — mixed ancestry, marriage, and adoption are common. But the old neighborhoods hold:

| Family | Founding stock | The neighborhoods it still draws from |
|--------|---------------|--------------------------------------|
| **Valenti** | Sicilian hobgoblin line, softened by generations of human and halfling marriage; goliath and dwarf dock stock | Little Italy, Red Hook, Greenpoint |
| **Moretti** | Hobgoblin capos, human clergy, halfling parish networks, goddart lawyers | Little Italy, Lower East Side, Arthur Ave |
| **Chen-Okafor** | Chinese dragonborn and human bankers; Nigerian human and aasimar financiers; kenku couriers | Flushing, Harlem, Jamaica |
| **Kowalski** | Polish dwarf and gnome sanitation clans, human and erkling bookkeepers | Greenpoint, Staten Island, South Bronx |
| **Ashford** | Anglo-Dutch human and high-elf old money; genasi and tiefling specialists as consultants | Midtown, Gramercy, Tudor City |
