**Three Centuries of Spell-Industry**

Locked frame: ~**300 years** from Renaissance guild/temple craft to late-1800s street brass — while culture later wears mid-century clothes.

**Invention grammar** (how anything lasting gets built)
1) Name a need (light, lift, kill, haul, prove, heal)
2) Fill it with a D&D-plausible spell / school / ritual
3) Price it (street craft / guild secret / temple patronage / reagents / construct core)
4) Learn the failure mode (**weird**, not mechanical)
5) License it — or bootleg it
6) Iterate one generation back and one forward (path dependence)

**Forbidden path:** chemistry-first industry, gunpowder, “science discovers mana,” reliable symbolic computers.

**Stages**
• **Guild** — brick workshops; *mending* / *light* / *message* / hand *fabricate*; sail & horse
• **Early industrial** — smokestacks, clock towers, yards; continual-flame forges, *unseen servant* lines, *animate objects* galleries; brass, coal, **capsule** frames
• **Rising steampunk** — ward-towers, foundry spires; bound-air cages, batch *fabricate*, shield-guardian prototypes; no Tommy guns; no radio as default
• **Threat tier (present)** — empty factories that still run; **true automata** (purpose without operator); FSA shoot-to-scrap

**Computation stall**
Money went into **bodies, bindings, and brass** — not abstract state machines. “Calculating engines” = prestige follies (feast-day drift; spirits that hate arithmetic). Numbers rackets prefer **clerks + divination**. Rail schedules are human chaos. Not a temporary lag — a civilizational choice.

**Capsule arms replace powder**
Foundries sealed spell-craft into brass cylinders. Base families: **mana pulse** and **kinetic sling**. Specialty loads (silence, healing-deny, ward-pierce…) followed an underworld arms race: medicine was strong, so killers competed on **how** a body stayed broken. Reload is a ritual — slow, humiliating, fatal under fire. Mass powder automatics were never the path.

**Automata — domestic hubris**
**Iron Guild of New Jersey** (+ allies) bind elemental spirits into clockwork cores. Operator-required loaders = ordinary industrial tension (unions call them scabs). **Autonomous cores** — purpose that persists without an operator — are the existential inflection. Runaways vanish into the digs. The city fears what it bred. FSA: shoot-to-scrap.
