**Demographics of the Metro**

Percentages are **neighborhood mixes**, not destiny. Humans in every group. Ancestries do not equal ethnic allegories.

**Visibility ladder (who gets stopped)**
• Baseline — Humans — no questions
• Soft-visible — Halflings, gnomes, dwarves — polite, condescending
• Visible — Elves, half-elves, genasi, firbolgs, goliaths — noticed; priced
• Hard-visible — Tieflings, half-orcs, dragonborn, goblinoids — papers, **WardID**
• Fringe — Vampires, lycans, Below-born — not citizens

**WardID** = street check. **Passport-tier** = Midtown door. Silver/gold licenses shrink tiers. Families forge; Iron Guild stamps originals.
