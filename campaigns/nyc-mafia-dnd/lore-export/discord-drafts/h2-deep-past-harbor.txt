**Deep Past & the Harbor**

**Magic before machines**
Before brass and charter stamps, peoples on this coast practiced **patron craft**: oaths, bindings, light that stayed lit because a saint or spirit was paid in attention. Tech started in temple kitchens, guild attics, and funeral houses that needed the dead to answer **one** question — and no more.

Oldest industrial bias in the stack: **guild magic learned what a body was long before it learned what a number was.** Healing, death rites, and oath-binding outran abstract calculation — and never surrendered that lead.

**Peoples, not allegories**
Fantasy ancestries arrived like human diasporas: ships, wars, famines, ambition, rumor. They do **not** map 1:1 onto ethnic labels. Humans live in every people-group. Elves among German settlers, hobgoblins among Italian parish networks, Erklings/Goddarts in Jewish LES trades, firbolgs on Welsh lumber routes — **neighborhood mixes**, not racial destinies. (Full tables → Demographics thread.)

Fringe beings (vampires, lycans, older things) were known early and pushed early — night markets, wilds, sealed digs. Midtown polite denial is a late fashion on an old habit.

**Pre-Charter dusk**
Long stretch when casting was common and industry was not. Travel slow. Truth local. A *continual flame* on a hook was wealth. A speaking-tube pair across two courtyards was a marvel. Automata were children’s stories or overseas siege nightmares — not dock loaders. It ends when charters tax and stamp spell-craft like flour.

**Why this coast**
Five boroughs + Jersey belt dominate because **cities dominate**. Harbor geography: deep water, river mouths, island choke points. Whoever taxed the chokes taxed the eastern appetite.

**Early municipal craft**
Light → *continual flame* / Lamplighters · Message → *message* / brass tubes · Repair → *mending* · Truth → notary-cleric circles (later bootleg “truth rooms”) · Lift → stairs first, then wind-spirit cages.

Speaking-tube craft matures over a long eighteenth century: sympathetic brass pairs → hub exchanges → Midtown pneumatic mail. Wrong pairings leaked then. They still leak.

**Death as soft power**
Funeral houses and parishes learned early: **the dead are evidence**. One-question *speak with dead*, *gentle repose*, paper ledgers. Moretti-linked registry tradition claims continuous borough death records **since 1910**. “Consigned to the parish registry” ends trials.

**Emancipation scars / Great Migration**
Slavery’s end did not end its wards. Old bound-labor residue still surfaces in deeds and dig-adjacent routes (OCU slang: “heritage necromancy”). Harlem void covenants get Ashford-clerk redaction for fees. Migration memory lives in trunks, tickets, kitchen wall-contracts — artifacts, not essays.
