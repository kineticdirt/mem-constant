**A History of the Metropolitan Stack**
*Player lore · textbook series · replaces the old “Stack” post*

There is **no single calendar year**. Historians argue in eras, feast-days, and guild charter numbers. When a date appears — **1910**, **1952**, **1870s** — it is a **local institutional stamp** (registry plaque, founding plaque), not a claim that the whole world is “1958.”

**How the city feels “now”**
• Nightlife / dress / voice → mid-century NYC/Jersey
• Street hardware → late-1800s brass (tubes, steam subway, capsule arms, coal)
• Medicine / death → ahead of the street
• Automata → modern threat
• Computation → behind everyone (clerks + divination beat engines)

**Relative timeline (no one year)**
Pre-Charter patron craft → parish/guild licensing → early industrial smokestacks & capsule frames → **1870s** dig plaques (Below folklore) → gilded ward-rail fortunes → **1910** burial-registry stamp → loaders common / autonomous cores terrifying → mid-century culture veneer → **1952** OCU founded → present play.
