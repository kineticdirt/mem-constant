**What the City Manufactures**

Street price bands from the economics SoT. Iron Guild stamps = legal ceiling; bootleg molds mean wax seals, patron marks, and dud rates.

**Heavy industry & rail**
Brass foundries (Greenpoint, Passaic) — chambers, frames, boilers
Ley-rail / ward-rail (Jersey flats → Grand Central) — locos, ties, conductor gear (Pemberton, Vandermeer)
Capsule switch engines (Harlan yards) — mana-pulse hoppers
Construct loaders (Jersey + Red Hook night) — operator-required iron (Iron Guild; Valenti nights)
Autonomous cores — licensed & not; black market ~$150; federal heat
Cold-iron ammo (Brighton / Passaic) — shield-shred
Tank-farm logistics (Bayonne) — Valenti ↔ Chen-Okafor handoffs

**Arms** (detail also in Spell-Tech post)

**Healing / alchemy / vice**
Bathtub *cure wounds* $15 (Mulberry bakery bags) · lesser restoration copper wrist $45 · greater restoration funeral back room $250 · neurological salon $500–$1,200 (West Chelsea)
Glow-Gin $0.75 / $2 bottle · Mending-Rye $0.50 · Blues/Vitamins $1 · river-spirit incense $5

**Optics / press (“sight industry”)**
Scry-glass (Yorkville) · bound-light plates $8–$25 · living ink $40+ · glyph-index mass print · verity-lantern $0.05/view · shadow-prints $0.02
Industrial truth: the city can *see* across wards (licensed). It cannot *search* a million plates by face. **Clerks are the database.**

**Soft manufacture / services**
Speaking-tube hubs (Tube-Layer’s Union; tapping clips ~$35) · warded locks ($50/yr safe license; forged stamps ~$20) · continual-flame lamps · steam-lifts · ward-glyph tattoos (~6 mo outside Midtown scrub) · jade-focus 6-for-5 lending · numbers slips (600-to-1; ~40% house) · ice-car reagent slots ~$120 · disposal “heavy load” ~$50 (Kowalski cover)

**Wage bands**
Unskilled $4–$6/day · union longshore / dwarf boiler-smith $10–$15 · OCU detective 1st grade ~$18 · successful ledger job $50–$100
A bootleg restoration band ≈ a week of skilled wages. A neurological salon is a small house’s nightmare. Resurrection is politics, not a price list.

**Not manufactured here**
Gunpowder arms · reliable citywide radio · television · glass curtain-wall towers · interstate cloverleafs · stable spreadsheet engines · lab-coat biotech · casual undead labor · vampire citizenship papers.
