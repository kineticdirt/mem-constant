# 00 — Start Here

Hand this folder to anyone sitting down. Read `09` if you want the city as a history textbook (demographics + manufacturing included). Read `01` and `04` before you build a character if you care how the city *works*. Read `03` when you pick an ancestry. Read `06` so you don't walk into Midtown assuming vampires are fairy tales.

---

## Pitch

Fantasy New York. Culture sounds mid-century — bebop, sharkskin, Hoboken commuters, cold-war dread in the papers. Hardware under your hands is late-1800s — speaking-tubes, steam-assist subway, capsule revolvers, ley-rail that lies about its schedule. Magic is licensed, taxed, or bootleg. The Five Families tax the grey market. The full story of how the stack got here is in `09`.

## Rules of the pack

- True as far as your characters know. GM secrets are not in here.
- No exact year.
- No gunpowder. Capsules only.
- Vampires and werewolves are real. Most people pretend otherwise in Midtown.
- Lines marked **〔proposal〕** in `09` are research-like guesses — GM may cut or promote.
