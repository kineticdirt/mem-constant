## Anti-Allegory Note (Required Table Protocol)

Races are **diasporas**, not 1:1 ethnic allegories. Humans live in every people group. The Morettis aren't "the halfling family" — they're an Italian-American syndicate that hires halfling tenement runners and gnome bookkeepers because those kids grew up on the same blocks and know which stairs squeak.

Prejudice at the table targets **ancestry visibility** (horns, height, tusks) and **magical licensing** (B2/C2), not skin color as a stand-in for real-world bigotry. A visible orc longshoreman gets stopped at a Midtown bank because he's an unlicensed orc, not because he's Irish. Human-on-human ugliness still exists — play it diegetic, concrete, dry.

## Core People Group Mapping

### 1. Germans (LOCKED)
- **Primary Race(s):** Elves / Elf Subraces (Wood Elves, High Elves) — 70% Elf, 30% Human mix.
- **Neighborhoods:** Yorkville (Manhattan Upper East Side), Hoboken waterfront gardens.
- **Stereotype Texture (Barry-dry):** Quiet, overly formal, and perpetually annoyed by dirt. They wear stiff wool suits even in July. They don't shout; they stare until you leave. Playable tells: perfectly combed hair, carrying small brass pocket-brushes, and speaking with a clipped, flat delivery.
- **Family/Racket Lean:** Neutral/Independent suppliers. They dominate the high-end optical glass, precision lens-grinding, and licensed telescope/scry-glass trade.
- **Relation to Automata/Arms/Healing:** They hate automata (seeing clockwork as a vulgar insult to craft); they manufacture elegant, long-barreled brass **pulse rifles** (highly licensed); they only use high-grade, expensive cathedral healing.

### 2. French (LOCKED)
- **Primary Race(s):** Half-Elves or Genasi + Tiefling mix — 50% Half-Elf, 30% Genasi (Fire/Air), 20% Tiefling.
- **Neighborhoods:** West Chelsea, Greenwich Village basement salons.
- **Stereotype Texture (Barry-dry):** High-fashion cynicism. They run the underground art clubs, perfume imports, and poetry salons. Playable tells: constant smelling of lavender or sulfur, silk scarves used to cover horn-stubs or elemental skin-fractures, and a habit of laughing at things that aren't funny.
- **Family/Racket Lean:** Independent brokers and Ashford consultants. They manage high-end glamour-laced speakeasies and act as neutral intermediaries for Midtown transactions.
- **Relation to Automata/Arms/Healing:** They use small, decorative clockwork lighters and music boxes; they carry concealed pocket **capsule-derringers** (specialty glamour or sleep loads); they prefer expensive, perfume-scented alchemical restorations.

### 3. Spanish (LOCKED)
- **Primary Race(s):** Darker-complexion Humans + Dragonborn + some Tieflings — 40% Human, 40% Dragonborn, 20% Tiefling.
- **Neighborhoods:** East Harlem (El Barrio), Lower East Side tenements.
- **Stereotype Texture (Barry-dry):** Intense, transactional, and deeply family-oriented. They run the import-export houses for tobacco, citrus, and raw copper. Playable tells: a smell of burnt orange peel, heavy silver rings on multiple fingers, and an absolute refusal to conduct business without a shared meal first.
- **Family/Racket Lean:** Chen-Okafor and Valenti associates. They run the shipping corridors and manage raw ingredient smuggling (especially sulfur and copper ore for capsule loads).
- **Relation to Automata/Arms/Healing:** They deploy Dragonborn fire-resistance in boiler-room foundries; they favor heavy, high-temp **forge-pulse pistols**; they run bootleg *cure wounds* tinctures cut with cheap rum.

### 4. Welsh (LOCKED)
- **Primary Race(s):** Firbolgs — 80% Firbolg, 20% Human.
- **Neighborhoods:** Jersey Palisades, Staten Island lumber yards, isolated canal-lock houses.
- **Stereotype Texture (Barry-dry):** Massive, soft-spoken, and deeply suspicious of concrete. They wear oversized tweed caps and heavy boots caked in river mud. They talk to horses and steam-boilers as if they were cousins. Playable tells: slow, deliberate movements, chewing on dry clover, and a habit of ducking when entering normal-sized doors.
- **Family/Racket Lean:** Kowalski and Valenti associates. They work as timber-haulers, canal-boat pilots, and heavy draymen.
- **Relation to Automata/Arms/Healing:** They refuse to ride in or operate automata-driven carriages; they carry heavy kinetic **sling-staves** (no capsules, just lead weight); they use natural herbal compresses that double natural healing rates over 48 hours.

### 5. Scottish (LOCKED)
- **Primary Race(s):** Goliaths — 70% Goliath, 30% Human.
- **Neighborhoods:** Greenpoint docks, South Brooklyn grain terminals, quarry yards.
- **Stereotype Texture (Barry-dry):** Loud, wind-burned, and completely literal. They don't understand sarcasm and find human secrets hilarious. Playable tells: ash-grey skin-mottling, heavy linen shirts with no collars, and a habit of carrying their own heavy iron wrenches in their back pockets.
- **Family/Racket Lean:** Valenti dock crews and heavy enforcers. They run the heavy cranes, boiler installations, and dockside protection.
- **Relation to Automata/Arms/Healing:** They treat automata as "glorified shovels" (often breaking them by overloading them); they carry massive **kinetic sling-blasters** loaded with scrap; they heal via raw physical toughness and salt-water rubs.

### 6. Jews (LOCKED)
- **Primary Race(s):** Goblin mix of **Erklings** and **Goddarts**, mixed with Humans — 40% Erkling, 30% Goddart, 30% Human.
- **Neighborhoods:** Lower East Side, Williamsburg (Brooklyn), Brownsville.
- **Campaign Goblinoid Lineages Defined:**
  - **Erklings:** Wire-thin, pale-sallow skin, with long fingers and needle-sharp ears. Exceptionally skilled in high-end watchmaking, precision alchemy, and printing/copying. Playable tells: wear oversized flat caps or yarmulkes, constant quiet whispering or clicking their tongues when thinking, and ink-stained fingertips.
  - **Goddarts:** Stockier, heavy-jawed, broad-shouldered and quiet. They have tough, leathery grey-skin and are excellent bookkeepers, ledger-keepers, and legal scribes who can detect double-entry forgery by smell. Playable tells: thick brass spectacles, a habit of tapping a heavy iron ring on tables, and an absolute refusal to speak while a clock is ticking.
- **Stereotype Texture (Barry-dry):** Fast, hyper-intellectual, and deeply cynical of any law written after the Bronze Age. They run the printing shops, legal consultancies, and precision alchemy labs. Playable tells: constant debating of minor technicalities, carrying multiple pairs of glasses, and a smell of vinegar and fresh ink.
- **Family/Racket Lean:** Moretti bookkeepers and independent alchemical wholesalers. They manage the truth-bind ledgers, licensed legal filings, and high-end alchemical refineries.
- **Relation to Automata/Arms/Healing:** They use tiny, precise clockwork calculating gears; they carry **fountain-pen capsule darts** (sleep or tracking-ink loads); they are the city's premier manufacturers of bootleg **lesser restoration bands** ($45.00 copper wire wrists).

## Western Europe — Dense Coverage (LOCKED)

### 7. Irish (LOCKED)
- **Primary Race(s):** Humans + Half-Orcs + Orcs — 50% Human, 35% Half-Orc, 15% Orc.
- **Neighborhoods:** Hell's Kitchen (Manhattan), Woodlawn (Bronx), Bay Ridge (Brooklyn), Red Hook docks, Jersey City waterfront bars.
- **Stereotype Texture (Barry-dry):** Damp wool, corned beef, cabbage water, and cheap rye. Rosary in the coat pocket. Half the longshoremen are half-orcs who will shake your hand, buy you a pint of porter, and then break a chair over your head if they find out your cousin works the municipal audit desk. Accents are thick, trailing, and full of parish guilt. Cops say: "Don't search an Irish trunk without a backup sergeant, or you'll end up with a bootleg mass card in your hand." Flat joke: You ask an Irish sergeant for directions, and he points to three bars that went dark in the '40s and a parish hall that burned down.
- **Family/Racket Lean:** Valenti dock muscle; Moretti parish protection; independent **Iron Guild** pipe-fitters on the Jersey side.
- **Relation to Automata/Arms/Healing:** Orc longshoremen treat construct loaders as scab labor — riots on Pier 7 are common; kinetic sling-hooks and warded shillelagh staves; parish lay-on-hands bands; they have a cousin who failed seminary who does bootleg *lesser restoration* in the back of a coal yard.

### 8. English / British (LOCKED)
- **Primary Race(s):** Humans + Half-Elves + Dwarves — 70% Human, 20% Half-Elf, 10% Dwarf.
- **Neighborhoods:** Murray Hill, Gramercy club row, Tudor City service corridors, Greenwich Village boarding houses (Anglo expat clerks).
- **Stereotype Texture (Barry-dry):** Polite frost, club memberships, and a belief that **queueing** is a moral virtue. They staff import houses, shipping insurers, and **Ashford** junior desks. Playable tells: rolled umbrellas even indoors, tea at inappropriate hours, and a habit of apologizing before delivering bad news.
- **Family/Racket Lean:** Ashford consultants; neutral shipping witnesses; **licensed** scry-glass appraisers who never quite leave the office.
- **Relation to Automata/Arms/Healing:** Dwarf fitters keep **punctual** automata maintenance schedules (ironically); half-elf juniors favor **sleep-load** capsule pens for "diplomatic" disputes; humans use **cathedral-grade** healing only when the club doctor insists.

### 9. Dutch (LOCKED)
- **Primary Race(s):** Humans + Gnomes + Halflings — 50% Human, 30% Gnome, 20% Halfling.
- **Neighborhoods:** Old Brooklyn (Breuckelen founding lines), Jersey City canal streets, Hoboken accounting row, Flushing Meadow drainage yards.
- **Stereotype Texture (Barry-dry):** Smells of salt-herring, pipe tobacco, and river mud. Gnomes and humans with wooden shoes worn iron-smooth from scrubbing kitchen stoops. They talk in flat, nasal vowels about "riparian boundaries" like it's a sacred text. Cops call them "sluice-devils" because if there's a flood in Queens, some Dutch clerk is probably holding the billing invoice for the ward gate. Flat joke: A Dutchman will spend three days calculating the exact cubic foot of water in a puddle before he steps over it.
- **Family/Racket Lean:** Municipal contract brokers; Kowalski sanitation engineering; Chen-Okafor **flood-ward** installers.
- **Relation to Automata/Arms/Healing:** Gnome pump crews service **steam-assist** subway grades; they carry **water-pulse** capsule loads for sluice sabotage; they prefer **herbal compress** healing that works over 24 hours without spectacle.

### 10. Italians (LOCKED)
- **Primary Race(s):** Hobgoblins + Humans + Halflings — 50% Hobgoblin, 30% Human, 20% Halfling.
- **Neighborhoods:** Little Italy (Mulberry St), Williamsburg parish blocks, East New York tenements, Arthur Ave (Bronx), Newark Third Ward.
- **Stereotype Texture (Barry-dry):** Funeral gravity, Sunday gravy, social club on every block. Black mourning band on the arm. Garlic and incense — you smell them before you see the handshake. They kiss cheeks, shake hands too long, run protection and burial registry fraud out of restaurants that pass inspection up front.
- **Family/Racket Lean:** **Moretti** core turf; Valenti dock marriages; parish **ghost-binding** contracts.
- **Relation to Automata/Arms/Healing:** Hobgoblin capos keep crews on **clockwork** schedules; halfling runners fit basement stills; human fronts hold the **licensed** restaurant signs; **capsule** loads blessed at parish fonts; **spirit-touched** healing bands from funeral-home chapels.

### 11. Portuguese (LOCKED)
- **Primary Race(s):** Humans + Tritons + Sea Elves — 55% Human, 25% Triton, 20% Sea Elf.
- **Neighborhoods:** Newark **Ironbound**, South Brooklyn fish markets, Red Hook salt sheds (distinct from Spanish East Harlem).
- **Stereotype Texture (Barry-dry):** Smell of garlic, cod brine, and olive oil. Men wear heavy wool sweaters even when the July heat is melting the asphalt. They have a permanent feud with the Greek harbor crews that started back before anyone's grandfather got off the boat. Playable tells: fish-glue hands, azulejo tile backsplashes in basement kitchens, and fado singing that sounds like a dry sob after the third glass of port. Cops say: "If a Triton starts singing, the pier is about to freeze." Flat joke: A Portuguese skipper will tell you the Hudson has too much mud, then buy a bucket of it to grow cabbage.
- **Family/Racket Lean:** Valenti cold-chain handoffs; independent ferry **ice** contracts; Kowalski disposal crews on the kill-floor shift.
- **Relation to Automata/Arms/Healing:** Tritons refuse Hudson fish but run **offshore** lines; they use **frost** capsule loads in hold disputes; they heal with **salt-water** rubs and dockside **cure wounds** tinctures.

### 12. Belgians (LOCKED)
- **Primary Race(s):** Humans + Gnomes — 70% Human, 30% Gnome.
- **Neighborhoods:** Midtown chocolate counters, Garment District margins, Hell's Kitchen bakeries.
- **Stereotype Texture (Barry-dry):** Pristine linen aprons over dark cardigans, smells of roasted cacao and almond paste. They speak with a quiet, trailing French-Flemish drift that gets lost in subway noise. They argue about the exact millimeters of a warded praline mold. Cops say: "The chocolate is licensed. The filling will put you to sleep for a week." Flat joke: "He says he is neutral. He just charges a dollar more to say it in two languages."
- **Family/Racket Lean:** Independent. They manage warded confection labs and high-end Midtown import counters.
- **Relation to Automata/Arms/Healing:** They use pocket watch-style sugar trackers; carry warded lace-needles; prefer sweet, restorative syrups over raw clerical healing.

### 12b. Swiss (LOCKED)
- **Primary Race(s):** Humans + Dwarves — 60% Human, 40% Dwarf.
- **Neighborhoods:** Lower Manhattan banking row, Wall Street service tunnels, Yorkville clock shops.
- **Stereotype Texture (Barry-dry):** Stiff collars, gold watch-chains, and a voice like dry gravel. They run the private vaults and escrow tables. Playable tells: pocket magnifying lenses, carrying three different keys on a steel ring, and absolute refusal to speak while a pendulum is swinging. Cops say: "They don't take sides. They take a seven percent fee." Flat joke: A Swiss banker won't tell you the time of day unless you sign a three-copy non-disclosure agreement first.
- **Family/Racket Lean:** Neutral escrow witnesses; Ashford bond-vault auditors; licensed scry-glass appraisers.
- **Relation to Automata/Arms/Healing:** Dwarven clockmakers service high-end chronometers; carry pocket capsule-derringers (sleep or truth loads); prefer expensive, highly private restoration seals.
