# Setting Peoples & Races — Prohibition New York

*GM vision lock · 2026-08-01. Extends `SETTING-ANCESTRIES-WARDS.md` and `LOCKS.md` (B17).*

---

## Anti-Allegory Note (Required Table Protocol)

**Races are diasporas, not 1:1 ethnic allegories.** Humans exist within every single people group in the city. The Morettis are not "the halfling family"; they are an Italian-American crime syndicate whose crews hire halfling tenement runners and gnome bookkeepers because they live on the same streets and have the right skills. 

Prejudice in the city does not target human skin color or real-world nationality—it targets **ancestry visibility** (horns, height, tusks) and **magical licensing** (B2/C2 visibility rules). A visible orc longshoreman faces suspicion at a Midtown bank because he's an unlicensed orc, not because he's Irish. Human-on-human bigotry still exists parallel to this, but at the table, we play the fantasy friction. Keep it diegetic, concrete, and dry.

## Core People Group Mapping

### 1. Germans (LOCKED)
- **Primary Race(s):** Elves / Elf Subraces (Wood Elves, High Elves) — 70% Elf, 30% Human mix.
- **Neighborhoods:** Yorkville (Manhattan Upper East Side), Hoboken waterfront gardens.
- **Stereotype Texture (Barry-dry):** Quiet, overly formal, and perpetually annoyed by dirt. They wear stiff wool suits even in July. They don't shout; they stare until you leave. Playable tells: perfectly combed hair, carrying small brass pocket-brushes, and speaking with a clipped, flat delivery.
- **Family/Racket Lean:** Neutral/Independent suppliers. They dominate the high-end optical glass, precision lens-grinding, and licensed telescope/scry-glass trade.
- **Relation to Automata/Arms/Healing:** They hate automata (seeing clockwork as a vulgar insult to craft); they manufacture elegant, long-barreled brass **pulse rifles** (highly licensed); they only use high-grade, expensive cathedral healing.

### 2. French (LOCKED)
- **Primary Race(s):** Half-Elves or Genasi + Tiefling mix — 50% Half-Elf, 30% Genasi (Fire/Air), 20% Tiefling.
- **Neighborhoods:** West Chelsea, Greenwich Village basement salons.
- **Stereotype Texture (Barry-dry):** High-fashion cynicism. They run the underground art clubs, perfume imports, and poetry salons. Playable tells: constant smelling of lavender or sulfur, silk scarves used to cover horn-stubs or elemental skin-fractures, and a habit of laughing at things that aren't funny.
- **Family/Racket Lean:** Independent brokers and Ashford consultants. They manage high-end glamour-laced speakeasies and act as neutral intermediaries for Midtown transactions.
- **Relation to Automata/Arms/Healing:** They use small, decorative clockwork lighters and music boxes; they carry concealed pocket **capsule-derringers** (specialty glamour or sleep loads); they prefer expensive, perfume-scented alchemical restorations.

### 3. Spanish (LOCKED)
- **Primary Race(s):** Darker-complexion Humans + Dragonborn + some Tieflings — 40% Human, 40% Dragonborn, 20% Tiefling.
- **Neighborhoods:** East Harlem (El Barrio), Lower East Side tenements.
- **Stereotype Texture (Barry-dry):** Intense, transactional, and deeply family-oriented. They run the import-export houses for tobacco, citrus, and raw copper. Playable tells: a smell of burnt orange peel, heavy silver rings on multiple fingers, and an absolute refusal to conduct business without a shared meal first.
- **Family/Racket Lean:** Chen-Okafor and Valenti associates. They run the shipping corridors and manage raw ingredient smuggling (especially sulfur and copper ore for capsule loads).
- **Relation to Automata/Arms/Healing:** They deploy Dragonborn fire-resistance in boiler-room foundries; they favor heavy, high-temp **forge-pulse pistols**; they run bootleg *cure wounds* tinctures cut with cheap rum.

### 4. Welsh (LOCKED)
- **Primary Race(s):** Firbolgs — 80% Firbolg, 20% Human.
- **Neighborhoods:** Jersey Palisades, Staten Island lumber yards, isolated canal-lock houses.
- **Stereotype Texture (Barry-dry):** Massive, soft-spoken, and deeply suspicious of concrete. They wear oversized tweed caps and heavy boots caked in river mud. They talk to horses and steam-boilers as if they were cousins. Playable tells: slow, deliberate movements, chewing on dry clover, and a habit of ducking when entering normal-sized doors.
- **Family/Racket Lean:** Kowalski and Valenti associates. They work as timber-haulers, canal-boat pilots, and heavy draymen.
- **Relation to Automata/Arms/Healing:** They refuse to ride in or operate automata-driven carriages; they carry heavy kinetic **sling-staves** (no capsules, just lead weight); they use natural herbal compresses that double natural healing rates over 48 hours.

### 5. Scottish (LOCKED)
- **Primary Race(s):** Goliaths — 70% Goliath, 30% Human.
- **Neighborhoods:** Greenpoint docks, South Brooklyn grain terminals, quarry yards.
- **Stereotype Texture (Barry-dry):** Loud, wind-burned, and completely literal. They don't understand sarcasm and find human secrets hilarious. Playable tells: ash-grey skin-mottling, heavy linen shirts with no collars, and a habit of carrying their own heavy iron wrenches in their back pockets.
- **Family/Racket Lean:** Valenti dock crews and heavy enforcers. They run the heavy cranes, boiler installations, and dockside protection.
- **Relation to Automata/Arms/Healing:** They treat automata as "glorified shovels" (often breaking them by overloading them); they carry massive **kinetic sling-blasters** loaded with scrap; they heal via raw physical toughness and salt-water rubs.

### 6. Jews (LOCKED)
- **Primary Race(s):** Goblin mix of **Erklings** and **Goddarts**, mixed with Humans — 40% Erkling, 30% Goddart, 30% Human.
- **Neighborhoods:** Lower East Side, Williamsburg (Brooklyn), Brownsville.
- **Campaign Goblinoid Lineages Defined:**
  - **Erklings:** Wire-thin, pale-sallow skin, with long fingers and needle-sharp ears. Exceptionally skilled in high-end watchmaking, precision alchemy, and printing/copying. Playable tells: wear oversized flat caps or yarmulkes, constant quiet whispering or clicking their tongues when thinking, and ink-stained fingertips.
  - **Goddarts:** Stockier, heavy-jawed, broad-shouldered and quiet. They have tough, leathery grey-skin and are excellent bookkeepers, ledger-keepers, and legal scribes who can detect double-entry forgery by smell. Playable tells: thick brass spectacles, a habit of tapping a heavy iron ring on tables, and an absolute refusal to speak while a clock is ticking.
- **Stereotype Texture (Barry-dry):** Fast, hyper-intellectual, and deeply cynical of any law written after the Bronze Age. They run the printing shops, legal consultancies, and precision alchemy labs. Playable tells: constant debating of minor technicalities, carrying multiple pairs of glasses, and a smell of vinegar and fresh ink.
- **Family/Racket Lean:** Moretti bookkeepers and independent alchemical wholesalers. They manage the truth-bind ledgers, licensed legal filings, and high-end alchemical refineries.
- **Relation to Automata/Arms/Healing:** They use tiny, precise clockwork calculating gears; they carry **fountain-pen capsule darts** (sleep or tracking-ink loads); they are the city's premier manufacturers of bootleg **lesser restoration bands** ($45.00 copper wire wrists).

## Proposed Peoples & Races (Pending GM Feedback)

### 7. Chinatown / Chinese Diaspora (PROPOSED)
- **Primary Race(s):** Dragonborn (small, old merchant houses) + Kenku (messengers, forgers) + Human majority — 60% Human, 20% Dragonborn, 20% Kenku.
- **Neighborhoods:** Chinatown (Manhattan), Flushing (Queens) corridor.
- **Stereotype Texture (Barry-dry):** Quiet, high-pressure, and deeply formal. Tongs run the public face; elders sit in basement mahjong rooms calculating margins while the steam-pipes hiss. Playable tells: smell of dry tea leaves and coin copper, carrying brass speaking-tube couplers, and a habit of repeating your last three words back to you (Kenku style).
- **Family/Racket Lean:** Chen-Okafor Family. They run the numbers game, high-stakes gambling, and cross-borough scry-tag routes.
- **Relation to Automata/Arms/Healing:** They use copper-plated automata as silent guards in back rooms; they use compact, high-velocity **kinetic needle-slings**; they sell smuggled river-spirit incense that grants temporary hit points but leaves the user smelling of marsh water.

### 8. Greeks (PROPOSED)
- **Primary Race(s):** Tritons + Sea Elves + Humans — 40% Human, 30% Triton, 30% Sea Elf.
- **Neighborhoods:** Astoria (Queens), Jersey City waterfront.
- **Stereotype Texture (Barry-dry):** Loud, sun-starved, and deeply superstitious about water. They wear wool sweaters year-round and refuse to eat anything caught in the Hudson. Playable tells: faint green-blue sheen on skin under streetlights, pockets full of salt-cured olives, and a habit of spitting whenever an automaton passes.
- **Family/Racket Lean:** Independent shipping and smuggling. They run the ferry contracts, sponge imports, and harbor salvage.
- **Relation to Automata/Arms/Healing:** They despise steam-automata on water (claiming they anger the river-spirits); they carry **heavy brass harpoon-slings** (kinetic); they heal via saltwater-steeped seaweed compresses.

### 9. Scandinavians (PROPOSED)
- **Primary Race(s):** Goliaths + Cold-Adapted Humans (Winter-touched) — 50% Goliath, 50% Human.
- **Neighborhoods:** Bay Ridge (Brooklyn), Staten Island North Shore.
- **Stereotype Texture (Barry-dry):** Grim, hard-drinking, and intensely competitive about physical labor. They work the heavy iron bridges and shipyards. Playable tells: pale, ice-blue eyes, woolen suspenders, and an absolute refusal to use umbrellas during snowstorms.
- **Family/Racket Lean:** Kowalski and Valenti associates. They work the bridge ironwork, heavy timber yards, and barge hauling.
- **Relation to Automata/Arms/Healing:** They treat automata as weak metal toys; they carry heavy iron **kinetic rail-slugs** (massive kinetic damage); they use pine-tar ointments that numb pain but leave skin smelling like a forest fire.

### 10. Italians (PROPOSED)
- **Primary Race(s):** Humans + Halflings (tenement clans, food trade) + Gnomes (precision watchmakers, lock-ward specialists) — 50% Human, 30% Halfling, 20% Gnome.
- **Neighborhoods:** Little Italy (Manhattan), Williamsburg, East New York.
- **Stereotype Texture (Barry-dry):** Rapid-fire speech, heavy emphasis on neighborhood parishes, and a dark, dry humor about mortality. Playable tells: constant gesturing with espresso spoons, gold-leaf parish medals worn openly, and a habit of whispering names of the dead before drinking.
- **Family/Racket Lean:** Moretti and Valenti Families. They run the protection rackets, funeral-home ghost-binding, and bootleg alchemical stills.
- **Relation to Automata/Arms/Healing:** They use small clockwork kitchen-timers and coffee-grinders; they are the premier importers of Italian-style **capsule revolvers** (the "Valenti-6"); they run the bootleg **mending-rye stills** ($0.50 a shot).

### 11. Irish (PROPOSED)
- **Primary Race(s):** Orcs + Half-Orcs + Humans — 50% Human, 35% Half-Orc, 15% Orc.
- **Neighborhoods:** Hell's Kitchen (West Side Manhattan), Red Hook, South Boston adjacency.
- **Stereotype Texture (Barry-dry):** Pugnacious, sentimental, and deeply loyal to the precinct house. They run the longshoremen's unions and dominate the rank-and-file police force. Playable tells: heavy wool flat-caps, thick knuckles, and a habit of singing mournful songs after three beers.
- **Family/Racket Lean:** Valenti and Moretti associates. They run the dockside labor unions and the street-level muscle.
- **Relation to Automata/Arms/Healing:** They use automata as "scab labor" (frequently sabotaging them with sugar in the boilers); they carry heavy, brutal **kinetic pipes** and **heavy pulse-revolvers**; they heal via parish-clinic holy water and heavy rye-tinctures.

### 12. Polish (PROPOSED)
- **Primary Race(s):** Dwarves + Rock Gnomes — 50% Dwarf, 30% Gnome, 20% Human.
- **Neighborhoods:** Greenpoint (Brooklyn), South Bronx industrial pockets.
- **Stereotype Texture (Barry-dry):** Methodical, suspicious of banks, and obsessed with metallurgy. They run the heavy brass foundries, boiler-repair shops, and locomotive yards. Playable tells: thick calluses, leather work-aprons worn outside the shop, and a habit of checking the silver content of coins with their teeth.
- **Family/Racket Lean:** Kowalski and Valenti associates. They work the rail yards, boiler repair rackets, and vault-smithing.
- **Relation to Automata/Arms/Healing:** They are the city's main mechanics for unlicensed **construct loaders**; they carry precise, self-made **pneumatic kinetic slings**; they heal via copper-wire grounding bands that draw "sick humors" out of the blood.

### 13. Russians (PROPOSED)
- **Primary Race(s):** Shifters (Swiftstride/Wildhunt) + Orcs — 40% Shifter, 40% Orc, 20% Human.
- **Neighborhoods:** Brighton Beach (Brooklyn), Staten Island docks.
- **Stereotype Texture (Barry-dry):** Cold, fatalistic, and completely quiet. They wear heavy, fur-lined long-coats regardless of the weather. Playable tells: sharp, canine teeth visible when speaking, smelling of grease and sea-ice, and a habit of staring at your throat while you talk.
- **Family/Racket Lean:** Kowalski associates and independent smugglers. They run cold-iron magic smuggling and offshore rum-running.
- **Relation to Automata/Arms/Healing:** They use automata for heavy offshore hauling in winter; they carry heavy, winter-adapted **kinetic ice-rifles** (cold-pulse specialty); they heal via raw, frozen meat packs and hot alchemical steam-baths.

### 14. African American / Harlem Communities (PROPOSED)
- **Primary Race(s):** Humans + Elves (arts enclave — high-magic bebop, salons) + Half-Orcs — 60% Human, 30% Elf, 15% Half-Orc.
- **Neighborhoods:** Black Harlem, Striver's Row, Sugar Hill.
- **Stereotype Texture (Barry-dry):** High-tempo, intellectually sharp, and deeply weary of federal licensing clerks. They run the premier jazz clubs, theatrical troupes, and independent newspapers. Playable tells: pristine silk ties, carrying high-quality leather music cases (often lead-lined), and speaking in complex, rhythmic street codes.
- **Family/Racket Lean:** Independent and Chen-Okafor associates. They run the speakeasy glamour networks and local numbers banks.
- **Relation to Automata/Arms/Healing:** They use small, quiet clockwork metronomes; they carry elegant, concealed **brass pocket-slings**; they heal via high-end, independent hoodoo-style herbal amulets and licensed clinic drafts.

### 15. Puerto Rican / Caribbean (PROPOSED)
- **Primary Race(s):** Humans + Lizardfolk + Water/Earth Genasi — 60% Human, 25% Lizardfolk, 15% Genasi.
- **Neighborhoods:** East Harlem (El Barrio), South Bronx tenements.
- **Stereotype Texture (Barry-dry):** Warm, quick-tempered, and highly survival-oriented. They run the tenement markets, transit lines, and basement repair shops. Playable tells: colorful linen shirts, carrying small pocket-knives etched with soil runes, and a habit of clicking their tongues in disagreement.
- **Family/Racket Lean:** Valenti and Chen-Okafor runners. They manage the elevated rail smuggling routes and neighborhood numbers routes.
- **Relation to Automata/Arms/Healing:** They splice steam-pipes to heat tenements; they carry quick-draw **kinetic pocket-slings** with sulfur caps; they heal via tropical plant extracts and clay packs that draw out curse-residue.
