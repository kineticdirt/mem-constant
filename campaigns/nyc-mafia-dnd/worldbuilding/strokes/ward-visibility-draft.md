# Ward-tiered visibility — dialectic draft

*2026-08-01 · Agent draft — scene rules for play; pairs locked B2/C2 until GM promotes*

## Thesis (tiered by ward)

**Magic and non-human life are geography, not secret.**

In **Red Hook, Hell's Kitchen, Harlem club blocks, Chinatown basements, and Bushwick stairwells**, a dwarf vault-smith walks the pier in daylight; an orc foreman barks orders in the hiring hall; a salon sign for **luck** or **stitch** hangs beside the butcher. Healing chalk on a tenement door is ordinary. Speaking-tube splices in a café back room are discussed like shared laundry lines. Federal law still applies — but **social grammar** treats wonder as **labor, parish, and racket**, not scandal.

In **Midtown, Ashford lobbies, and Broadway “legitimate” clubs**, the same facts exist under **polite denial**: glamour is “lighting”; a tiefling consultant is “Continental”; horned patrons use the service entrance. Cops, inspectors, and maître d's enforce **performance** — not ignorance. Everyone in the room knows; they punish **rudeness** (naming the ward aloud) more than the ward itself.

**Play win:** Two social rules in one city → jobs need **route planning** (who walks in the front door), **heat** differs by block, and **exposition** comes from contrast (“last week on the pier they didn't blink”).

## Antithesis (citywide open)

**One rule everywhere: gods and guilds are public infrastructure.**

Non-human stevedores and open salon signage on Fifth Avenue. No glamour tax; no “service entrance” plot tax. New players never ask “can I show horns here?” — the answer is always yes, with bigotry as personal NPC flaw, not zoning.

**Play cost:** Loses noir **double life**; Midtown becomes flavorless; **bribe lanes** flatten (ward-inspectors lose leverage); **tiered raid fiction** weakens (why smuggle charms through Midtown if docks are same as towers?). **Exposition** gets cheaper but **geography** stops teaching the setting.

## Antithesis (polite denial citywide)

**Nobody says it aloud, anywhere — even the docks.**

Magic is whispers, basement only, “foreign superstition.” Non-humans exist but every block performs ignorance. Harlem and Red Hook **also** pretend.

**Play cost:** Fights **SoT** (“gods are real,” guild halls on the map). Forces constant **cloak-and-dagger** for scenes that should breathe. **Ancestry table** becomes decorative — if everyone hides everywhere, ward rows don't matter. **Dock / foundry labor fiction** breaks: you cannot run a dwarf mutual-aid forge-shrine if the block pretends dwarves aren't there.

## Synthesis (recommendation — aligns with LOCKS)

**Keep tiered visibility (locked).** Add **scene rules** so agents and players don't improvise incompatible grammar block to block.

### Visibility tiers (play shorthand)

| Tier | Wards (see `SETTING-ANCESTRIES-WARDS.md`) | Magic in street dialogue | Non-human in public | Inspector default |
|------|-------------------------------------------|--------------------------|---------------------|-------------------|
| **Open** | Red Hook, Hell's Kitchen, LES, Bushwick/South Bronx warrens, Chinatown back streets, Harlem **street** (not club VIP) | Named: salons, tubes, luck, **links** (euphemism ok) | Common; horns/size unremarkable | Bribe **local**; federal raid needs warrant + wrong-day comedy |
| **Licensed veil** | Queens corridor parlors, Staten ferry shrines | “Registered” vs “back room” distinction | Present but **trade-coded** (smith, boxer, scryer) | OTR paperwork spot-checks |
| **Polite denial** | Midtown towers, Broadway clubs, Ashford-clean blocks | “Stage effects,” “European lighting,” “atmosphere” | **Passing** or **service entrance**; visible non-human = scandal fodder | **Harassment** up; glamour **expected** on floor |
| **Federal face** | Courthouse steps, Grand Central concourse (not back halls) | Magic discussed only in **legalese** | Visible non-human **invites** document check | OTR/BCC **show** enforcement |

**Key:** Midtown denial is **theater**, not amnesia. A captain who “doesn't see” horns at a club still **counts** them in his ledger.

### Harassment & heat (light table)

Roll or pick when a **visible** non-human or **named-magic** PC enters **denial** tier without glamour:

| d6 | Result |
|----|--------|
| 1 | **Ignored** — staff trained; money talks |
| 2 | **Surcharge** — cover, “insurance,” wardrobe fee |
| 3 | **Side door** — seated, but not seen from main room |
| 4 | **Ward-inspector** shakedown (NYPD local) |
| 5 | **Federal** spot-check (OTR clerk “auditing” club receipts) |
| 6 | **Scandal hook** — photographer, rival family, **Tribune** stringer |

Repeat visits without fixing cover → escalate 1→6 over sessions.

### Intimate visibility (intimate=high, measured)

**Open tier:** Bootleg **link** fitting discussed like gin — “Tuesday stitch,” “paired appointment.” **Denial tier:** **link** commerce is **double blind** — garment in, box out; salon front is **hair/massage** licensed; OTR raids target **unregistered pairs** same night as gin. **Echo bleed** in tenements is **neighborhood gossip**, not federal crime until someone complains.
