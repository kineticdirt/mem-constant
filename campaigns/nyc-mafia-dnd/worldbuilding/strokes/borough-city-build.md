Manhattan is a vertical labyrinth of brick, coal smoke, and neon. It is the political and financial center of the Commission, where the highest-level rackets are brokered and hidden behind limestone fronts.

## 2. Brooklyn: The Industrial Engine

Brooklyn is a sprawling expanse of brick factories, iron foundries, and crowded tenements. It is the industrial muscle of the city, dominated by heavy labor and raw material smuggling.

* **Red Hook (Valenti Territory - Irish/Italian Orc and Goliath mix):**
  * **The Docks (Conover St):** Pier 7 is a forest of steam-powered cranes and warded warehouses. Smuggled cargo is cleared using forged Locksmiths' Guild stamps. Frequented by Irish Orc stevedores and Scottish Goliath dockers.
  * **The Night Shift:** Unlicensed **construct loaders** (heavy, 8-foot iron golems) are deployed at 2 a.m. to move cargo, bypassing union labor and city inspectors.
  * **The Local Hub:** **The Anchor & Anvil** (Conover St), a rough longshoreman tavern serving heavy, warded stout ($0.20 a pint).

* **Greenpoint (Polish-Dwarf & Gnome Foundry District):**
  * **The Foundries:** A dense belt of brass foundries and locomotive repair depots. Mountain Dwarves (50%) and Rock Gnomes (30%) work in heavy leather aprons, smelting copper and machining warded cylinder chambers for **Valenti-6 revolvers**.
  * **The Racket:** Boiler-room repairs and unlicensed construct assembly. Gnomes build pneumatic kinetic slings ($35.00) in basement tool shops.

* **Brighton Beach (Russian Shifter & Orc Corridor):**
  * **The Shoreline:** A frozen, desolate coast lined with fur warehouses and cold-iron depots. Shifters (Swiftstride/Wildhunt, 40%) and Orcs (40%) wear heavy, fur-lined long-coats.
  * **The Smuggle:** Brighton Beach longshoremen run offshore rum-running boats and smuggle raw **cold-iron ammunition** ($10.00 a box) to bypass magical protections.

## 3. Queens: The Probability Table

Queens is a quiet, residential borough of shopfronts, lending offices, and sprawling rail yards. It is the financial ledger of the numbers racket and a hub for water-transit smuggling.

* **Flushing (Chen-Okafor Territory - Cantonese Dragonborn & Kenku mix):**
  * **The Lending Face (Main St):** Storefronts operate as respectable lending corporations. Behind the grilles, Dragonborn clerks calculate loan-shark interest rates.
  * **The Back Rooms:** Mahjong parlors where high-stakes games are played. Upstairs divination rooms house mages who use warded jade coins to predict numbers outcomes.
  * **The Street Couriers:** Kenku runners carry betting slips in code, utilizing their mimicry to repeat instructions perfectly without leaving written evidence.

* **Astoria (Greek Triton & Sea Elf Waterfront):**
  * **The Docks:** Astoria's waterfront is run by Greek Tritons (30%) and Sea Elves (30%). They wear heavy wool sweaters and refuse to eat Hudson River fish.
  * **The Racket:** Sea-spirit shipping contracts, sponge imports, and harbor salvage. Tritons run ferry smuggling routes, moving contraband under the hulls of boats using warded water-baskets.

* **Jamaica:** A massive rail and transit hub where steam locomotives arrive from Long Island and New Jersey. A critical choke point for smuggling alchemical reagents.

## 4. Staten Island: The Deep Disposal

Staten Island is a desolate, marshy borough of transfer stations, landfills, and quiet residential streets. It is the graveyard of the city's secrets, run by the Kowalski family.

* **Fresh Kills (Kowalski Territory):**
  * **The Landfill:** A massive, smoking expanse of waste. Kowalski's human-majority crews use their municipal sanitation license to dispose of bodies, hot construct cores, and cursed artifacts.
  * **The Haul:** Sanitation trucks equipped with warded iron bells (for *gentle repose*) move through the city at night, collecting "heavy loads" from Midtown clubs and Brooklyn docks.
* **Stapleton:** A quiet neighborhood of row houses. The basement of the Kowalski coordinator's home contains the entrance to a sealed 1870s water tunnel leading down into the **Bone Reach** (The Below).

## 5. The Bronx: The Smuggling Corridor

The Bronx is a rugged, hilly borough of brick tenements, elevated rail lines, and contested smuggling routes.

* **The South Bronx (Puerto Rican Lizardfolk & Genasi Wave):**
  * **The Corridor:** A rapidly growing, vibrant neighborhood of Puerto Rican families, featuring a prominent Lizardfolk (25%) and Genasi (15%) population.
  * **The Turf:** A contested smuggling corridor. Valenti longshoremen and Chen-Okafor runners compete for control of the elevated rail lines and highway routes that bring New Jersey alchemical reagents into Manhattan.
  * **The Local Hub:** **El Coquí Club** (East 138th St), a basement hub for lizardfolk transit crews drinking root-tincture rum ($0.35 a cup).

## 6. Travel Choke Points & Transit

Moving people and contraband between the boroughs is a highly regulated, dangerous business.

* **The Bridges & Tunnels (The Toll Racket):**
  * **The Choke:** The Brooklyn Bridge, Manhattan Bridge, and Holland Tunnel are heavily guarded by OCU checkpoints equipped with ward-sniffers.
  * **The Smuggle:** Runners must use lead-lined compartments or warded carriages to transport bootleg elixirs or unlicensed cores across the river.
* **The Staten Island Ferry:**
  * **The Rites:** The ferry is operated under a municipal license. Dock priests perform "water walk" and "calm wave" rites to keep river spirits from capsizing the boats. The Kowalskis use garbage barges to move heavy contraband under the guise of municipal waste transport.
