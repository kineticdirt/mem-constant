## What vice is here

This city does not run on mainland cocaine mythology. It runs on **spell-tech intoxicants**: bathtub spirits cut with cantrips, sulfur-copper "vitamins" for burned-out diviners, incense that smells like the harbor, and healing liquor that closes a cut and holes your Friday.

Alcohol is the public face. Reagents are the money. Capsule loads are hardware, not habit — but the same dock crews move both.

Capability asymmetry bites both ways. A Moretti chaplain can put you back on your feet for $15. A calculating engine still drifts after feast days. So the street buys **clarity pills** and **ward-dull gin**, not better ledgers.

## History — what influenced what

No exact year. Era stack only.

### Real-world rhymes (texture, not calendar)

1. **US Prohibition → speakeasy architecture** — the city already lived through a dry decade in mortal memory. Spell-tech just changed what the bathtub held. Glow-Gin is bathtub gin with a cantrip.
2. **1950s–60s jazz / vice corridors** — West 52nd and Harlem as meeting rooms for money, federal auditors, and family rackets. Culture layer, not the hardware layer.
3. **Opium dens / Chinatown press myths** — the Jade Dragon incense basement is the rhyme without the racist cartoon. Hot tea up front. River-spirit in the back.
4. **Numbers + stimulants** — runners burned out before Blues. The city answered with chemistry because **computation magic is bad**. You cannot spreadsheet your way out of divination fatigue.

### In-world cause chain

| Stroke | Belief | Pressure | Choice | Result |
|--------|--------|----------|--------|--------|
| Late dry years (memory) | Ban makes a market | Temple temperance + FAB stamps | Families take the stills underground | Basement architecture becomes inheritance |
| Guild medical monopoly | Licensed healing = order | Underclass injuries + heist scrapes | Moretti chaplains + LES bands undercut temples | $15 vials; blue-glow skin tell |
| Numbers boom | Runners need longer shifts | Future-blur from overwork | Chen-Okafor compresses sulfur/copper into Blues | Clarity racket; long-term prophecy rot |
| Mid-century club boom | Ward-sense ruins deals | Enchantment crews need soft marks | Glow-Gin recipe standardized | Velvet Horn as dull-ward salon |
| Commission peace | Open street dealing invites OCU cameras | Bond-vote weeks + verity-lantern press | Vice stays **indoors + labeled as nightlife** | Public drunk is a misdemeanor; unlicensed still is a sit-down |

## Architecture — how the building does the crime

### Tenement still (Moretti / LES)

- Street: stoop, gravy smell, mourning band on the door if someone died useful.
- Parlor: social club chairs, espresso, anise.
- Cellar: copper coil, prestidigitation-salt jar, ward-damp sandbags under the joists so the glow does not leak into the sidewalk grate.
- Air shaft: vent that smells like juniper and hot metal. Neighbors call it "the laundry."

### Jazz club (52nd / Harlem)

- Street door: Entertainment License in a frame. FAB likes frames.
- Bandstand: instruments as foci — the real magic license loophole.
- Booth: curtain + bass *silence* bubble for sit-downs.
- Basement: icebox stamped for produce. Healing bands under the lettuce. Clockwork glass-washer off every ledger (Blue Note).

### Dock warehouse (Valenti / Red Hook)

- Street: guild stencil, honest crates.
- False crate: pulse capsules and sulfur tins share the same false bottom geometry — spring latch, not gunpowder smell.
- Night office: speaking-tube to the yard clerk. Timetable is a joke. The kickback is not.

### Funeral home (Moretti)

- Street: black wreath, respectful hours.
- Chapel: incense, garlic, parish faces.
- Sub-basement: restoration chair bolted to the slab; ozone under the frankincense; ledgers in a warded drawer.

### Numbers bank grille (Chen-Okafor)

- Street: Main Street metal grille, jade abacus rumor.
- Back room: slip printers, ink from Erkling shops, Blues tin for runners who shake.
- No calculating engine on site. Engines drift. People do not — until the Blues stop working.

## Street texture

- Midtown: cigarette, perfume, green gin glow under a booth curtain.
- Mulberry: espresso, anise, butcher paper that crinkles like a $15 secret.
- LES: ink, copper, steam, a still that ticks like a bad clock.
- Red Hook: river mud, stout, cold iron in a long coat.
- Bronx basement: sofrito, rum-shrub, the elevated shaking dust into your cup.
- Brighton steam: wolf-wet wool, vodka, birch.

A guy at the end of the bar will tell you Blues make you sharp. A guy who used to run numbers will tell you what day it is twice and mean both.
