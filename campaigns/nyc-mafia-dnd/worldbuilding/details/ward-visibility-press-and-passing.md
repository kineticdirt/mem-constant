# Detail — press & polite denial (Tribune + doors + open grammar)

Promotes draft residual **Press** + **Passing & paperwork**. Scene rules for play — **not** a newspaper bible. Harassment d6 stays in the stroke draft.

---

## Need (from locked stroke)

Players and agents need one rule for **what the Tribune prints**, one rule for **who walks Midtown front doors**, and a short contrast for **how open wards talk** — so Midtown jobs don't invent incompatible grammar.

---

## 1) Tribune naming policy

**House rule:** Print **names the raid, not the miracle**. Magic appears as **euphemism, seizure inventory, or scandal innuendo**. Open-ward papers (Harlem Defender–style neighborhood sheets, Yiddish sheets) break this rule; the **Tribune** is Midtown-facing and does not.

| Layer | What they print | What they don't |
|-------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **Front page** | RAID, seizure, “unlicensed clinic,” Volstead + “contraband charms” as **goods** | Spell names, ancestry as headline identity, “witch” as news fact |
| **Society / Broadway** | “Continental lighting,” “European atmosphere,” guest lists | Horns, glamour as magic, who used the service door |
| **Rumor column** | “Sympathy vice,” “miracle traffic,” unnamed consultants | Addresses that tip a live salon (stringers sell those privately) |

### Three headline examples (max — improvise variants)

1. **FEDERAL MEN SEIZE WEST SIDE CLINIC — “CHARM” STOCK LISTED AS CONTRABAND**
2. **BROADWAY CLUB DENIES STAGE TRICKERY AFTER OTR RECEIPT AUDIT**
3. **DOCK MUTUAL-AID HALL HOSTS FORGE MASS — TRIBUNE NOTES “FOREIGN RITE,” NO ARREST**

**Play use:** PCs reading papers get **direction + heat**, not a spell list. A stringer on harassment d6 **6** is scooping **scandal**, not teaching the setting.

## 2) Passing vs service entrance (denial-tier Midtown)

Midtown / Ashford lobbies / Broadway “legitimate” floors. Denial is **theater** — staff and cops **count** what they pretend not to see.

| Who | Front door (floor) | Service / alley | Without cover |
|-----|--------------------|-----------------|---------------|
| **Human-passing** half-elf / light stigma, good clothes | Default **yes** | Optional | ALB interview may still **out** blood later |
| **Visible horns / green / size**, glamoured or veiled | **Yes** if veil holds and grammar stays “consultant / lighting / Continental” | Preferred if veil thin | Escort out, surcharge, or inspector stop |
| **Visible**, no glamour | **No** — doorman refuses or “helps” toward Harlem/docks | **Yes** — kitchen, freight, back booth | Harassment d6 (stroke draft) |
| **Bootleg salon client** | Human face + password; ask for “massage / fittings / atmosphere” | Charm tray / **link** room never faces the street | OTR sting via false premise waiver |
| **Orc / goliath labor** | Almost never | Default — dishwashing, freight, boiler | Scandal if seen from main room |

**Ashford-specific:** Receptionist sells **appointment fiction**. Warlock retainers enter as **cultural brokerage**. Horned consultants who insist on the revolving door **twice** become **scandal fodder** (photographer / rival / stringer) even if money is good.

**Key:** Service entrance is not shame in open wards; in Midtown it is **the deal that keeps the lease**.

---

## 3) How open wards talk (short)

Red Hook, Hell's Kitchen, LES, Chinatown back streets, Harlem **street** (not club VIP):

- Name the work: **luck, stitch, seal, tube, forge-oath** — like naming the butcher.
- Non-human bodies are **unremarkable labor**; prejudice still exists but isn't **zoning**.
- Federal law is real; social grammar treats wonder as **parish, payroll, and racket**.
- A Tribune boy shouting Hoover on the pier **doesn't** sell “dwarf vault-smith” — no story there.

**Contrast line for GMs:** Open ward = *say it*. Denial tier = *translate it*. Same city; jobs fail when PCs use the wrong grammar on the wrong block.
