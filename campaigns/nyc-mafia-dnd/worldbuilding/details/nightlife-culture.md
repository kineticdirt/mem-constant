## 1. The Jazz Club Scene

In mid-century New York (uneven temporal stack, no single pin year), the night belongs to the jazz clubs. Concentrated along West 52nd Street ("The Swing Street") and throughout Harlem, these clubs are the primary interfaces between wealthy Midtown clients, federal auditors, and the family rackets.

### Primary Vice Corridors & Key Venues

* **West 52nd Street (Midtown Manhattan):**
  * **The Blue Note:** (West 52nd St). A premier bebop club operating under a *Licensed Visibility Tier*. Front-of-house is clean, human-dominated jazz; the basement cellar contains a warded icebox for bootleg healing bands and an unlicensed clockwork glass-washer. Serviced by a Moretti/Chen-Okafor split.
  * **The Velvet Horn:** (West 52nd St). Operated by a West Chelsea French/Half-Elf glamour ring (C4). The walls are layered with *silent ward* sigils, letting patrons speak in total secrecy. Serves imported French liqueurs and **Glow-Gin** ($0.75 a glass).
  * **The Onyx Foundry:** (West 52nd St). Owned by German-Elf optics dealers (Yorkville diaspora). High-end brass fixtures, displaying licensed telescope lenses. Serves imported German pilsner ($1.50 a stein) in chilled heavy-glass mugs. No automata allowed inside the door.

* **Harlem Corridor (Upper Manhattan):**
  * **The Savoy Salon:** (Lenox Ave). A massive, human-majority ballroom with a prominent High-Elf artist minority. The stage utilizes illusion and glamour wards to project swirling auroras above the dancers. Fully protected by independent Harlem operators; federal inspectors are routinely bribed with high-end optical focuses.
  * **Minton's Parish:** (West 118th St). A Moretti-protected bebop venue. Features late-night jam sessions where musicians use brass saxophones as enchantment focuses. A Moretti "chaplain" conducts a weekly spirit sweep ($5.00/week protection fee) to keep the cellar free of lingering clerk-ghosts.

* **Lower Manhattan & Outer Boroughs:**
  * **Cafe Palermo:** (Mulberry St, Little Italy). A Moretti turf social club smelling of dark espresso and anise. Tommy Moretti's enforcers operate from the back tables, drinking **Mending-Rye** ($0.50 a shot) and selling bootleg *cure wounds* elixirs ($15.00 a vial) wrapped in butcher paper.
  * **The Jade Dragon:** (Pell St, Chinatown). A Pell Street mahjong basement under a Dragonborn merchant house front. Serves hot tea and sells smuggled river-spirit incense ($5.00 a bundle) that grants temporary hit points but leaves the user smelling of brackish harbor water.
  * **The Anchor & Anvil:** (Conover St, Red Hook). A rough, low-ceilinged tavern frequented by Scottish Goliath and Irish Orc longshoremen. Serves heavy, warded stout ($0.20 a pint) that numbs pain and keeps the harbor chill out of the joints.
  * **El Coquí Club:** (East 138th St, South Bronx). A crowded basement venue run by the Puerto Rican lizardfolk-majority transit crews. Serves rum infused with earth-genasi root-tinctures ($0.35 a cup) to soothe sore muscles after shifts on the elevated rail lines.

## 2. Instruments as Status & Magic Foci

Musical instruments are the primary focus tools for enchantment, illusion, and abjuration magic in the nightlife corridors. Musician-operators are essentially highly skilled, unlicensed mages who hide their craft in bebop solos.

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                         INSTRUMENTS AS FOCI                             |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  The Saxophone (Enchantment Focus)                                      |
|  - Channeled primarily by German/Harlem Elves and French Half-Elves.    |
|  - Used to cast emotion-altering spells (calm emotions, suggestion).    |
|  - A smooth bebop solo can make a rival capo forget a turf dispute      |
|    or compel a warded witness to sign a contract.                       |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  The Trumpet (Evocation/Illusion Focus)                                 |
|  - Preferred by Italian and Irish Humans.                               |
|  - Used to cast light, minor illusion, or sound-manipulation spells.    |
|  - A sharp, high-register note can create a blinding flash of light or  |
|    a deafening blast to distract OCU detectives during a raid.          |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  The Double Bass (Abjuration Focus)                                     |
|  - Preferred by Scottish Goliaths and Irish Orcs.                      |
|  - Used to cast protective wards or silence bubbles.                    |
|  - The deep, low vibrations reinforce doorframes or create a ten-foot   |
|    zone where no sound can escape, perfect for back-room business.      |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### The Instrument License
Carrying an instrument case in Midtown Manhattan requires an **Entertainment License** stamped by the Federal Audit Bureau (FAB). OCU detectives frequently stop street-level operators to inspect brass valves and strings for hidden runes. Carrying an unregistered focus is a **Class C Misdemeanor** ($50 fine and confiscation).

## 3. Underworld Vice: Alcohol & Reagents

### Licensed Spirits vs. Tenement Alchemy
* **FAB-Audited Spirits:** Legitimate alcohol, taxed and stamped. Safe but highly expensive ($1.00 for a glass of beer).
* **Bathtub Elixirs (Moretti & Valenti Stills):**
  * **"Glow-Gin" ($0.75 a glass / $2.00 a bottle):** Infused with *prestidigitation* salts. It glows a sallow green in the dark and dulls the consumer's ward-sense, making them highly susceptible to enchantment spells. Manufactured in tenement basements by German-Elf and Jewish-Goblin alchemists.
  * **"Mending-Rye" ($0.50 a shot / $1.50 a flask):** Infused with trace healing herbs. It accelerates natural healing of minor cuts and bruises but causes severe hangovers, dry mouth, and temporary memory loss.

### Divination Burnout & "Vitamins"
Numbers runners (Chen-Okafor) and street-level diviners face severe mental exhaustion, insomnia, and paranoia from tracing multiple future timelines.
* **The "Blue" Racket:** The Chen-Okafor family imports raw sulfur and copper reagents from New Jersey foundries. Their Jewish-Goblin (Erkling) alchemists in the Lower East Side compress these into small, blue pills sold on the street as **"Vitamins"** or **"Blues"** ($1.00 per pill).
* **The Effect:** Suppresses burnout symptoms and restores mental clarity for 12 hours. Long-term use causes permanent "future-blur"—where the user can no longer distinguish between memory and prediction.
