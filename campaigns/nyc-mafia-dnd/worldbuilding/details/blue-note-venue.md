# Detail — The Blue Note (West 52nd Street)

## 1. Front stage

Soft pink neon on a small raised bandstand. Legit bebop — three-piece quartet on a slow night (casing count; opening layout calls it a legit band, same room). Heavy cigarette / Turkish tobacco smoke. Midtown money in the seats: attorneys, club owners, Ashford-adjacent faces. No family colors on a quiet night.

**Door:** Two-door entry — main street door + service alley side (see §9). **Doorman: "Big Mike"** — half-orc, tailored tuxedo, stage-door enforcer. Works a ~30-minute rotation; a younger human in a cheap suit covers smoke breaks. Represents cross-ancestry Midtown front labor (B1c/C3). He does not chat. He measures.

**Binder note:** If Mike knows a face from another job, he does not stop them on the floor. He calls Artie's tube while they are still drinking.

---

## 2. Mahogany bar

Full-length mahogany rail, brass foot rail, mirror behind bottles. Licensed cocktails **$2.50**. Back-room "holy water" shot **$15** — minor healing elixir, not on the menu; face can ask; bartender looks nervous and names a booth.

**Public bartender:** Human, thin, jumpy. Not the cellar construct. Tip him; he remembers tippers and forgets questions that sound like casing.

**Face cover window:** Napkin on the bar = crew-clear signal (heist plan). Spilled drink / watered-bourbon complaint pulls Big Mike off the door for Plan B.

---

## 3. Back office

Behind the velvet curtain off the bar floor. Owner's desk (Artie Shaw — nervous human; front man who **owes Chen-Okafor**, not Valenti). Brass speaking-tube hub on the wall (splice map below). Ledgers for the licensed books; the hot book is not here — it is in the cellar safe.

**Access:** No good face cover past the curtain. Muscle / second-story / wires use alley, fire escape, or stairs — not the velvet ask.

---

## 4. Warded ice box

Heavy oak box in the back office, lined with **lead** and **silver runes**. Keeps bootleg healing bands / "holy water" stock cold and stable. Complication seed: Artie's safe **key** can sit inside a dead bottle of rye in this box (heist d6 #5) — then the crew needs the office without crossing the bar.

**Ward class:** Containment / cold-stability (silver). Not the Locksmiths' Guild floor ward on the vault. Touching runes bare-handed risks a sting and a smell OCU ward-sniffers know.

---

## 5. Cellar

Damp brick. Coal boiler. Empty bottles. **Unlicensed, unregistered clockwork bartender** (CR ~2) washes glasses — dead steady cycle: wash → pause → wash; ~**8-second** silence between loads. That silence is the floor-crossing window.

**Threat read:** Crude spirit core. **Spellcasting nearby registers as threat.** Cantrip near the safe can stop the wash cycle; construct turns toward the cast source. Do not fight it as the main fight — it is a distraction; the stairwell is the real problem.

Service stairs from the third-floor east "PRIVATE — EMPLOYEES ONLY" door drop here. Cellar door from alley iron door / internal stair.

---

## 6. Vault / safe

Brick room / niche behind the coal boiler. **Warded iron door** (opening layout) / **warded iron safe** (locations + heist) — treat as one vault package for play: iron body + Guild ward on approach.

**Locksmiths' Guild Ward:**
- Silver-chalk boundary lines on the floor
- Rowan-wood wedges in the door frame
- Trigger-glyph on the **safe handle** that reads **living touch**

**Bypass (heist kit):** Rowan wedge across the chalk line (breaks circuit). Gloved hand on handle (cloth ≠ skin — partial bypass). Body resists *knock* / force — pick Guild pin-tumbler or drill keyplate (loud; ~15 s before upstairs notices). Contents: Spirit-Ledger / hot rackets book — bag closed; do not read.

**Listening risk:** Ashford warlock posts listen for ward breaks that skip silver chalk + rowan. Clean bypass is quieter than drill.

---

## 7. Coal chute

Exterior cover in the service alley → drops into the **coal bin in the cellar**, same room as the safe. Tight fit. Secondary egress / Leg C re-entry path. Loud if the cover scrapes; soot marks clothes (binder: track who climbed).

---

## 8. Fire escape

East face of the brownstone. Drops to a **locked gate** in the service alley. Climb route for second-story: alley/gate → up → third-floor landing.

**Third-floor gate lock:** Standard Locksmiths' Guild **pin tumbler**, **not warded** — Ashford staff do not want their own office alarms firing on every entry. From the hallway: east-end service stairs down to cellar.

---

## 9. Service alley (north side)

Iron door — no visible lock (magnetic seal or push-bar; complication: padlocked from inside on some nights). Single bare bulb. Trash cans. Coal chute cover. **Speaking-tube junction box** with an old scratched **Valenti guild stamp**.

Connects to 52nd midblock. Foot traffic dies after 1 a.m. Wheel parks at alley mouth; muscle holds the iron door cracked at go-time.

---

## 10. Third-floor Ashford consultancy

Rented-office floor dressing: plaques, quiet carpet, after-hours lamp. Front for Ashford listening / cutout work. Pin-tumbler gate (see §8). Hallway → east "PRIVATE — EMPLOYEES ONLY" → service stairs → cellar.

**GM pocket:** Speaking-tube splice runs **down to the cellar** (not a coincidence). Late consultant hears drill through the tube and can call Ashford house crew before the ledger leaves the building (heist d6 #6).
