## 1. Currency & Underworld Finances

The campaign uses standard **US Dollars ($)** at mid-century wage scales. Magic has not inflated the currency; instead, it has created a highly regulated, high-stakes black market where **healing is high-end** and **calculator magic is expensive and bad**.

### Daily Wage Standards
- **Unskilled Laborer (Tenement tenant - Human, Goblin):** $4.00 – $6.00 per day.
- **Skilled Craftsman (Union Longshoreman, Polish Dwarf Boiler-Smith):** $10.00 – $15.00 per day.
- **OCU Detective (First Grade):** $18.00 per day ($5,500/year).
- **Underworld Operator (On Ledger Retainer):** $50.00 – $100.00 per successful job.

## 2. Underworld Price Sheet & Suppliers

These are standard street prices for illicit spell-tech, bootleg elixirs, and underworld services in the five boroughs.

### A. Healing & Alchemical Restorations (Strong Lane — High End)
*Because life and death magic are highly advanced, prices scale up quickly. The feds strictly audit healing licensing to keep the underclass dependent on guild-approved temples.*

* **Bathtub Cure Wounds Elixir (1d8+2 healing):** **$15.00**. Leftover blue-glow on skin for 24 hours. Sold in paper bags at Mulberry Street bakeries (Moretti Halflings).
* **Bootleg Lesser Restoration Band:** **$45.00**. A copper wire wrapped around the wrist. Infused with purification runes by Lower East Side Jewish-Goblin (Erkling) alchemists. Burns out after stripping one curse or disease.
* **Back-Room Greater Restoration (Severe trauma/poison):** **$250.00**. Administered only by specialized Moretti "chaplains" in funeral-home back rooms.
* **Salon-Grade Neurological Work (Brain bleed, nerve repair):** **$500.00 – $1,200.00**. Administered in West Chelsea French Half-Elf salons. Scandalous if the FSA traces the client's magical signature.

### B. Capsule Arms & Ammunition (The Street Hardware)
*Chemical gunpowder is dead (B16). Weapons use magical capsules (spring-released pulse or kinetic sling loads).*

* **The Valenti-6 Revolver:** **$45.00**. A heavy, late-1800s style brass frame revolver manufactured in the Greenpoint foundries by Polish Dwarves. Holds six capsules.
* **Standard Pulse/Kinetic Capsules (Box of 6):** **$2.00**. Steel casings containing compressed force-runes. Sold by Irish Orc stevedores on the West Side docks (Valenti turf).
* **"Blue Kiss" Capsule (Box of 6):** **$5.00**. Infused with a flash-freezing cold pulse. Manufactured by Russian Shifters in Brighton Beach; highly effective at disabling clockwork loaders.
* **Silence Choke Capsule:** **$8.00 each**. Creates a 5-foot *silence* bubble at the impact point for 10 seconds. Highly illegal; manufactured by Lower East Side Goblins for wet work.
* **Cold-Iron Ammunition (Box of 6):** **$10.00**. Specialized kinetic slugs that shred magical shields and bypass *protection from normal missiles* wards. Smuggled by Russian Orc long-coat crews.
* **Pneumatic Kinetic Sling:** **$35.00**. A heavy, shoulder-mounted iron frame sling used by Greenpoint Gnomes to launch heavy steel slugs.

### C. Computation & Ledger Magic (Weak Lane — Expensive and Bad)
*Prediction and calculation are incredibly weak in this era. Mechanical gear-drift is common, and spirits hate math.*

* **Guild Brass Calculating Engine (Rental, one week):** **$200.00**. Heavy, loud, and constantly drifts after feast days. Requires a bound clerk-spirit who actively hates arithmetic. Families prefer human/Goblin bookkeepers.
* **Temple Augury Appointment:** **$75.00**. One business question answered in vague metaphors; wrong 30% of the time.
* **Chen-Okafor "Lucky Abacus":** **$50.00 session fee**. A warded jade abacus that can predict a numbers run, but is useless for auditing a real warehouse inventory.

### D. Smuggling & Contraband
* **Unlicensed Construct Core:** **$150.00**. Crude Jersey elemental cores used to power sweatshop sewing machines or Red Hook dock loaders.
* **Forged Locksmiths' Guild Stamp:** **$20.00**. A brass stamp used by Valenti forgers to mark contraband crates as "guild-cleared."
* **Speaking-Tube Tapping Clip:** **$35.00**. A brass clamp used by ward-breakers to tap into Midtown communication tubes.

## 4. Underworld Rackets & Payouts

### The Numbers Racket (Chen-Okafor & Harlem)
The numbers game is the financial backbone of the outer-borough wards.
* **The Bet:** Players bet pennies, nickels, or dimes on a three-digit number (000 to 999).
* **The Payout:** **600-to-1**. A dime bet wins $30.00 (minus a 10% cut for the runner).
* **The Margin:** The Chen-Okafor family keeps a steady 40% profit margin by using subtle probability-manipulation spells on the betting slips, printed in ink supplied by LES Erklings.

### Loan-Sharking (Jade-Contract Lending)
* **The Loan:** Small business owners borrow cash from Chen-Okafor Main Street grilles.
* **The Vigorish (Interest):** **"6-for-5"**. For every $5.00 borrowed on Monday, the borrower must pay back $6.00 on Friday (a weekly interest rate of 20%).
* **The Security:** Borrowers sign their name in blood on a **jade-focus contract**. If they default, the warded contract inflicts a constant, bone-deep joint pain, making default a terrifying prospect.
