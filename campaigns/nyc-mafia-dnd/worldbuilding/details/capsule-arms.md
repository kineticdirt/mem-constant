## Reload ritual (quick ref)

| Step | Failure |
|------|---------|
| Key twist open | Wrong twist → jam (action to clear) |
| Seat wax capsule | Dry seal → cap falls out on recoil |
| Patron whisper | Wrong name → **dud** |
| Close + double-tap iron | No iron nearby → 50% fizzle |

**Barry beat:** Reloading under fire is **socially humiliating** and **tactically fatal**. Enforcers carry **two** loaded frames or switch to knuckles.
