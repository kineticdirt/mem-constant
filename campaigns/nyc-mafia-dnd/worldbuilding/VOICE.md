# Voice — GM Binder, Not Essay Bot

*Lock · **B19** · 2026-08-02. Read before drafting NYC prose.*

---

## The rule in one breath

Write like you're leaving notes for yourself at 2 a.m. after a session — facts first, opinions in the margins, one tangent you'll regret tomorrow. Tables and prices stay STE-clean. Paragraphs can wander.

---

## Bad (AI cadence) vs good (human binder)

### Opening a doc

**Bad:** *In this setting, the city serves as a vibrant tapestry where diverse communities are nestled within a complex urban landscape, underscoring the delicate balance between order and fringe elements.*

**Good:** *The city runs the money. Albany sends letters. Jersey sends tank trucks. If you're not arguing about a ward license on somebody's stoop, you're probably in the wrong campaign.*

---

### Describing a people block

**Bad:** *The Irish diaspora represents a dynamic fusion of human grit and orcish strength, their neighborhoods pulsating with the vibrant energy of dockside labor and parish tradition.*

**Good:** *Irish blocks smell like damp wool and cheap whiskey. Half the longshoremen are orcs. They hate construct loaders like scabs. Rosary in the pocket. Nobody buys the second round but everybody fights about who should.*

---

### Humor beat

**Bad:** *This moment highlights the ironic juxtaposition between mundane bureaucracy and the supernatural underworld, creating a darkly comedic undertone.*

**Good:** *Danny brought a manila envelope, not a gun. He looked like he was returning a library book late. The sax player kept playing anyway. That was the problem.*

---

### Club blurb

**Bad:** *The Blue Note stands as a premier establishment nestled in the heart of the jazz corridor, offering patrons an immersive experience that seamlessly blends licensed entertainment with discreet underworld services.*

**Good:** *The Blue Note — 52nd St. Front room passes inspection. Basement has a bootleg icebox and a clockwork glass-washer that probably isn't on anyone's ledger. Moretti and Chen-Okafor split the headache.*

---

### Urban fringe

**Bad:** *Vampires exist on the periphery of society, their presence carefully woven into the nocturnal economy of the metropolitan fringe.*

**Good:** *Vampires are real. They don't hold ward office. Rich eccentric in Midtown until someone checks the parish registry. In Harlem you pay the club or you pay the family — same invoice, different envelope.*

---

## Word list (auto-reject in prose)

| Avoid | Try instead |
|-------|-------------|
| tapestry, vibrant, delve, nestled | *(delete — say the street name)* |
| underscores, landscape of | *"means" / "is why" / cut it* |
| "In this setting…" | Start with the fact |
| Perfect parallel bullets (3× same grammar) | Break one bullet mid-sentence; vary length |
| "serves as" / "functions as" | *"is" / "runs" / "owns"* |
| "dynamic fusion" / "rich tapestry" | Name the smell, the price, the uncle |

## Where STE still wins

- Price tables, fare bands, license fees
- Lock tables in `LOCKS.md`
- Master assignment percentages
- Legal/OCU procedure steps

Ramble in scene seeds, neighborhood texture, Barry beats, and GM asides — not in `$0.15 token` rows.
