## 1b. Culture layer (mid-century) × late-1800s street tech × capability asymmetry

**Play the gap:** Characters **sound and dress** like **mid-century New York** — bebop and early rock where licensed, sharkskin and church hats, commuter trains from Jersey, dock Irish banter, Mulberry Street wakes, Harlem rent parties, Flushing mahjong rooms that never closed. **Machinery** they touch is **older**: steam lift in a tenement, brass speaking-tube behind the club bar, guild ward on a roll-up door, foundry smoke over Red Hook. **Medicine** can be **frighteningly competent**; **ledgers and engines** are **frighteningly bad**.

**Cross-read:** `SETTING-CAPABILITY-ASYMMETRY.md` — uneven eras map, why healing outruns calculation, automata threat without superintelligence.

| Vice channel | Spell-tech hook |
|--------------|----------------|
| **Jazz club** | Horn or reed as **focus** for message cantrips and emotion-thief rituals; back room = bootleg healing |
| **Alcohol** | Licensed parish wine vs bathtub **alchemy** that dulls ward-sense |
| **Drugs** | Guild reagents sold as “vitamins”; divination burnout in numbers parlors |
| **Nightlife** | Automata **threat** shows up as unlicensed clockwork bartender or union-breaking golem on the docks |

Stereotypes are **voice and neighborhood texture**, not fantasy-race allegory maps.

## 2. Need → Magic fill → Iteration (agent grammar)

**Use this procedure** whenever think, Docs, Cursor, or a report invents a device, service, practice, or institution. **Do not skip steps.** If step 3 returns **forbidden**, do not invent around it without GM lock.

### Procedure

1. **Name the goal** — What civilization, city, family, or scene needs? (e.g. “move grain upriver,” “prove a contract,” “keep a factory running overnight.”)

2. **Break into needs** — List 1–3 concrete human/civic needs (labor, trust, speed, healing, binding, light, message, transport, entertainment, coercion).

3. **Magic fill** — Pick **D&D-plausible** spell, school, ritual, or item analog:
   - *Cantrip / common craft* — continual flame, prestidigitation, mending, message
   - *Temple / guild service* — lesser restoration bands, zone of truth ledgers, sending appointments
   * *Rare ritual* — teleport circle keys, fabricate batches, resurrection politics
   - *Forbidden or war-tier* — animate dead, mass suggestion, autonomous construct cores

4. **Cost class** — Sets rarity and who owns it:
   - **Street craft** (apprentice, parish, fixer)
   - **Guild secret** (formula, charter, stolen schematic)
   - **Temple patronage** (feast-day limits, saint license)
   - **Rare reagents** (supply raids, batch failure)
   - **Construct core** (artificer foundry, black-market scrap)

5. **Failure mode** — How it breaks **weird** (wrong voice on message, healing fixes bone but steals a week of memory, golem runs the wrong shift forever).

6. **Legal heat** — Licensed, taxed, bootleg, taboo, war-crime, or “polite denial” (Midtown pretends it does not exist).

7. **Control** — Guild, temple, family house magician, city ward-inspector, federal desk (names TBD), or black-market salon.

8. **Iterate** — How did this solution **develop along the path**?
   - Parish cantrip → guild batch → foundry brass → **clockwork + bound spirit** → **autonomous core** (threat tier)
   - Document **one generation back** and **one generation forward** so inventions feel path-dependent, not teleported.

9. **Invent** — Materials, hours, patron, sensory detail. **Never** smartphone, radio-as-default, chemistry-first industry, or “science discovers mana.”

### Worked example — Night message across wards

| Step | Answer |
|------|--------|
| **Goal** | Boss confirms a shipment without sending a runner |
| **Needs** | Speed, discretion, proof it was really the boss |
| **Magic fill** | **Message** cantrip via guild **speaking-tube** hub (sympathetic brass pair) + optional **sending** for VIP (appointment-only) |
| **Cost** | Guild secret on hub splices; **message** is street-craft; **sending** is rare ritual slot |
| **Failure** | Wrong tube pairing leaks to neighbor line; **sending** delivers boss’s voice but strips emotion |
| **Legal heat** | Licensed exchanges taxed; blind splice rooms bootleg |
| **Control** | Tube-Layer’s Union (guild); federal audit desk TBD |
| **Iterate** | Was runner + chalk signal → tube craft (18th c.) → hub exchanges (early industrial) → **automaton relay** (threat: unsupervised routing) |

## 5. Medical magic band

Healing is **real**, often **advanced** relative to street hardware — and shapes crime, labor, and law. It is **not** modern triage mass-market, but it **can** do things no brass-age street clinic could: close a brain bleed, strip a curse, **raise the dead** (with politics). See `SETTING-CAPABILITY-ASYMMETRY.md` §4.

| Band | What works | What fails / costs | Street texture |
|------|------------|-------------------|----------------|
| **Street / salon** | **Cure wounds**, **spare the dying**, cheap **herbalism**, stop bleeding, set bone; **niche potions** | Does not cure all disease; leaves **aura** bootleggers detect; addiction to pain-elixir | Blind salon parallel **void** — new contraband metaphor TBD |
| **Temple / parish** | **Lesser restoration**, **purify food and drink**, **restoration** (expensive queue); **field surgery-grade** miracles on feast days | Feast-day limits; saint refuses repeat sinners | Licensed lines; funeral-house tie-in |
| **Guild apothecary** | Tincture batches, **antitoxin** analog, prosthetics **plus** **mending**; **instant kill** reagents (assassin tier) | Batch failure = humoral wrong guess | Union vs temple price war |
| **Black-market salon** | Skip license; **greater restoration**; **regenerate** whispers; **brain / nerve** work | Scars that **glow** under **detect magic**; wrong patient memory | Family racket overlap — **high-end prices** |
| **Resurrection politics** | **Raise dead** exists; rare; scandalous | Who pays? Who stays dead? Press euphemism | Not casual; shapes assassination plots |

**Scars & disease:** Magic heals **wounds** more readily than **social stigma**. Chronic disease persists unless **restoration**-tier paid. **Resurrection** is political — not a respawn button.

## 6. Industrial growth path & automata threat

### Path (magic-powered industry, not chemistry-first)

```
18th-c parish cantrips & guild secrets
    → batch charms & fabricate presses (uniform parts)
    → brass clockwork + bound spirit contracts (foundries, docks)
    → rail syndicate + warded locomotives (early steampunk visible)
    → animate objects at industrial scale
    → **autonomous construct cores** ← existential inflection
    → (computation branch **stalled** — no stable symbolic engines)
```

**Computation failure (LOCKED):** Industry invested in **bodies, bindings, and brass** — not **abstract state machines**. Guild "calculating engines" exist as **prestige follies**; families run numbers with **clerks + divination**. Do not invent reliable spreadsheet magic without GM lock (`SETTING-CAPABILITY-ASYMMETRY.md` §4).

| Stage | Visible in city | Magic engine | Mundane parallel (allowed) |
|-------|-----------------|--------------|----------------------------|
| **Guild** | Brick workshops, parish halls | **Mending**, **light**, **message**, hand **fabricate** | Hand tools, sail, horse |
| **Early industrial** | Smokestacks, clock towers, rail yards | **Continual flame** forges, **unseen servant** lines, **animate objects** galleries | Brass, coal smoke, **capsule** revolvers & sling-rifles (no powder) |
| **Rising steampunk** | Ward-towers, foundry spires, rail choke points | **Bound air** cages, **fabricate** batches, **shield guardian** prototypes | No Tommy-gun identity; no radio-as-default |
| **Threat tier** | Runaway districts, siege rumors, empty factories that still run | **True automata** — purpose persists without operator | Law collapses to emergency commission |

### Automata as existential threat (**LOCKED**)

- **Not** `automaton=rare` spice. The city is **afraid** of what its own foundries bred.
- **Animate objects** on a factory line is **normal** industrial tension; **autonomous core** that rewrites its purpose is **civilization-scale**.
- **Not supercomputers:** threat is **labor, force, sentience** — modern-to-future automation panic, not silicon intelligence. Cores **do not** need to out-math a clerk.
- **Magic-biotech:** sigils, bindings, living patterns in consecrated vats — **not** CRISPR/lab aesthetics.
- Stakes: union extinction, military occupation, family arms race, divine judgment narrative, Below connection (mid-campaign).
- **Origin TBD:** foreign artificer power, domestic guild hubris, cult, Below breach — GM must lock before Phase G travel/automata prose.

**Ponytail:** When inventing a construct, ask: *operator-required* (labor) or *self-directed* (threat)? Only the second belongs in headline fear. When inventing **computation**, default **weak**.
