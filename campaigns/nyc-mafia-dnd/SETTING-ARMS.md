# Capsule Arms — NYC Mafia × D&D

*GM lock · **2026-08-02**. **SoT** for personal ranged weapons. Read after `LOCKS.md` (B16) and `SETTING-CAPABILITY-ASYMMETRY.md`. Catalog table: `worldbuilding/details/capsule-arms.md`.*

**Thesis in one line:** Guns do not use gunpowder. They use **sealed brass capsules** that discharge spell-craft — and because **medicine is strong** and **computation is weak**, the underworld arms race is about **how** you kill, not whether the body can be fixed.

---

## 1. No powder chemistry (VOID)

| Concept | Status |
|---------|--------|
| Gunpowder, black powder, smokeless chemistry, percussion caps as chemistry | **VOID** |
| "Brass cartridge" meaning **explosive propellant** | **VOID** — brass is the **capsule housing**, not a chemical round |
| Tommy guns, automatic powder weapons | **VOID** (B1b late-1800s band) |
| **Capsule arms** — warded frame + interchangeable spell-capsule | **LOCKED** (B16) |

Chassis look like late-1800s industrial hardware: six-shot **ward-revolvers**, lever **sling-rifles**, sawed **choke-shot** tubes. The **capsule** is the magic. The frame is guild brass.

---

## 2. Two base families (every capsule is one of these)

| Family | What it does | Spell analog | Street nickname |
|--------|--------------|--------------|-----------------|
| **Mana pulse** | Arcane **force** discharge — pressure wave, cold burn, thunder crack | *Eldritch blast* / *thunderwave* shard | "Popper," "blue kiss" |
| **Kinetic sling** | Launches a **rod, slug, or needle** at speed — real ballistics, real holes | *Magic missile* bolt as slug; mundane lead optional | "Spitter," "nail" |

**Pulse** leaves ward residue and aura. **Sling** leaves ballistics and blood. OCU builds cases on different evidence stacks.

Everything else is a **specialty capsule** — same brass form factor, different bound effect inside the wax seal.

## 4. Weak computation → dumb shots beat smart shots

| Works | Does not work |
|-------|----------------|
| Point, pray, **pulse** | "Smart" caps with divination guidance — **drift**, wrong target, diviner nosebleed |
| Sawed-off **sling** in a stairwell | Trajectory engines, rangefinder charms, "solve for wind" brass abaci |
| Specialty cap fired **at the door** not the guard | Autonomous aim that "knows" friend from foe — **computation lane is weak** |

**Play:** A capo with a plain popper is **more dangerous** than a warlock with a guided capsule that keeps landing in the radiator. Guild marketing promises smart caps; street results say **buy more dumb ones**.

---

## 5. Strong medicine → arms race of *how* you kill

Salons close bullet holes. *Spare the dying* buys time. *Raise dead* is politics, not myth.

So operators optimize:

- **Healing deny** caps and **spirit-tag** follow-up (finish before the salon)
- **Silence choke** + **sling** (no 911 tube scream)
- **Ward-pierce** then **pulse** through house-mage *shield*
- **Truth-sting** for sit-downs, not morgues

Violence is cheap until someone **cannot** be fixed the usual way. That is when the Commission sends a **specialty** loader, not a bigger gun.

---

## 6. Reload ritual (Barry awkward)

Reloading is **not** flicking a magazine. It is:

1. Break the ward-chamber with a **key twist** (specific click — wrong twist jams)
2. Slide a **wax-sealed capsule** on a brass rail (seal must be tongue-wet or it sticks)
3. Whisper the **patron name** or **guild stamp** (varies by maker — embarrassment if you say the wrong saint)
4. Close chamber; **tap the frame twice** on something iron or the cap **fizzles**

In a firefight this looks **ridiculous**. People die while reloading. Understatement after:

> *He got the third cap in. The first two rolled under the radiator. He shot the man anyway. Then he asked if anyone had seen his other capsules.*

## 7. Legal heat summary

| Tier | Examples | Typical sentence |
|------|----------|------------------|
| Licensed carry | Stamped **pulse** caps, registered frame | Fine + confiscation |
| Bootleg | Unstamped caps, stolen guild mold | 2–5 years audit cells |
| Class B specialty | Silence, binding, fog, truth-sting | 5–10 years |
| Class A | Ward-pierce, healing-deny, null pulse | Federal seizure + no parole |

OCU **ward residue** from pulse; **ballistics** from sling; **curse trace** from healing-deny. Cases are built like tax fraud — **paper and residue**, not "the capsule said so."
