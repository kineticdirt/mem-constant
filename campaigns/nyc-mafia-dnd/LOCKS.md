# LOCKS.md — NYC Mafia × D&D Locked GM Decisions

> **Era Lock (2026-08-02):** **Uneven temporal stack** — mid-century NYC/Jersey **culture** (jazz, vice, cold-war voice) × **late-1800s street hardware** × domains **ahead** (medicine/death) or **behind** (computation). **No exact calendar year** (VOID: "Year 1958").
> **Technology:** **late-1800s** brass/steam/guild spell-craft after **~300 years** of slow industrialization (Renaissance → early industrial). Technology is rooted in **D&D 5e spells/casting/rituals** (not science-first).
> **Capability asymmetry:** **Medicine/life/death magic = strong** · **Automata/sentience = modern/future threat** · **Computation/prediction = woefully weak**. See `SETTING-CAPABILITY-ASYMMETRY.md`.
> **Magic-biotech:** sigils, bindings, living patterns — **not** lab/CRISPR aesthetics.
> **Automata:** **Existential threat** at civilization scale (not rare union garnish; not supercomputers).
> **Gender:** Male-dominant diegetic society; women mostly domestic but can take suited male duties; somewhat sexual tone with some prudery.
> **Prose:** STE clarity with writing flair; concrete; NO AI slop (ban "tapestry", "vibrant", "delve", "testament").
> **Research-like Invention:** Prices, clubs, trades, districts — invent usable campaign facts fast.

**Status:** Active · **2026-08-02** (G0 + uneven eras + capability asymmetry)
